# Blind design (v4)

## 1. Premise

Long ago a civilization sank engines into the planet to hold its cracking crust still, learned the engines were grinding the world down faster than they held it up, shut them off, and walked away.

Their towers, rails, and half-buried machines are scattered across every region, and a few of them still hum on the little power that never drained.

{{ANTAGONIST}} has found the switches and is throwing them one by one, and the ground has started to move again.

## 2. The party

**{{HERO}}** — a sixteen-year-old mill-keeper's apprentice from {{HOME}} who wants to be trusted with something that matters; asks a lot of questions, ends sentences half-finished, and says the true thing a moment too late.

**{{FRIEND1}}** — a salvage diver who has been stripping old machines since she was nine and wants to be paid for once; talks in short flat orders, and only jokes when the situation is already bad.

**{{FRIEND2}}** — a young archivist sent from a fallen school to record the ruins, who wants proof that the old builders were right to leave; speaks in careful full sentences and reads plaques out loud whether or not anyone asked.

**{{FRIEND3}}** — a hired guard twice everyone's age, tired, with a bad knee and a debt at home, who wants the group to survive to the next town; says three words where six would do, and calls {{HERO}} "kid" until the point where he stops.

## 3. The antagonist

{{ANTAGONIST}} grew up in a region that the shutdown left barren, watched a hundred years of people scrape at dead soil, and found a working control room under it.

He is polite, arrives early, warns the party each time before he turns something on, and has never once lied to them about what he is doing.

**Want:** he wants the engines running again so his region stops starving.

## 4. Twelve regions

1. **{{HOME}}** — terraced green valley, water mill, a warm metal hatch in the mill floor that the elders told everyone to ignore; the party opens it and finds a lit room under the village.
2. **{{REGION1}}** — white salt flats ringed by leaning pylons, wind, one ranger's hut; the party follows the pylon line to the first engine and finds {{ANTAGONIST}} already switching it on.
3. **{{REGION2}}** — a stilt-town over a slow brown river, rope bridges, a market that sells scavenged parts by weight; the party trades salvage for a flat-bottomed river boat. **(grants the boat)**
4. **{{REGION3}}** — drowned forest, treetops above the water, old rail arches used as bird roosts; **boat required**; the party pulls a control rod out of a sunken station and learns the engines are linked in a chain.
5. **{{REGION4}}** — a mining escarpment of red shelves and dead cable towers; the party repairs a counterweight lift to climb it, and finds the miners already digging where {{ANTAGONIST}} told them to. **(grants the cable lift)**
6. **{{REGION5}}** — a glass desert fused smooth by whatever failed here, sun-glare, shade-tents of a trading caravan; the party crosses by night and reads the record of the shutdown vote on a standing wall.
7. **{{REGION6}}** — a coastal city built inside a wrecked engine housing, laundry strung between turbine blades, harbor traffic; the party is refused help by the council, and {{FRIEND1}} finds a deep-sea hull for sale.
8. **{{REGION7}}** — a scatter of green islands over open water, reefs, fishing floats, a seabed station blinking below; **deep hull required**; the party dives and cuts the chain's second link. **(grants the sea hull)**
9. **{{REGION8}}** — a black volcanic island shaking under a running engine, ash sky, evacuating villages; the party can shut it down or leave it, and either choice costs a region something.
10. **{{REGION9}}** — a cold high plateau of stone circles and wind, a hangar dug into the ridge; the party rebuilds an old lifting craft from parts gathered across the map. **(grants the lifting craft)**
11. **{{REGION10}}** — {{ANTAGONIST}}'s home, cracked gray pan with thin crops and hand-dug wells; **lifting craft required**; the party walks it with him and sees exactly what the shutdown did here.
12. **{{REGION11}}** — the anchor ring above the clouds, a quiet ribbed shell of corridors and windows, the master switch at its center; **lifting craft required**; the party climbs it while the planet shakes below.

## 5. The first hour, ten steps

1. The player carries three sacks of grain across the mill floor — the valley in morning light, the hatch ring scuffed clean by generations of boots.
2. The player trips the hatch latch with a dropped sack — warm air, a stairwell, blue running lights coming on one flight at a time.
3. The player climbs down and walks a corridor — a round room, dust, a wall of panels with one green dial still moving.
4. The player touches the moving dial — the panel lights the whole wall, and a map of the continent draws itself with eleven marks on it.
5. The player runs up and tells the elder — the village square, the elder already looking at the ridge, where the pylon line has begun to glow.
6. The player fights three field crawlers on the road out — small wheeled machines shaking dirt off themselves, slow, easy, loud.
7. The player reaches the ridge and meets {{FRIEND1}} cutting cable off a pylon — she has a bag of parts and no interest in a conversation.
8. The player follows the pylon line into {{REGION1}} with her — salt flats, heat shimmer, a black tower standing in the middle with its door open.
9. The player enters the tower and finds {{ANTAGONIST}} at the console — he turns around, greets them, finishes what he is doing, and leaves on foot.
10. The player walks out into a ground tremor — behind them the valley's river has changed color, and {{FRIEND1}} says she is coming along.

## 6. Three cutscenes

### A. The hatch

*INT. MILL — MORNING. Grain dust in the light. The hatch stands open.*

**ELDER:** That stair was sealed before my grandmother was born.

**{{HERO}}:** The lights came on by themselves. There's a room down there.

**ELDER:** Then it is drawing power, and it should not be.

**{{HERO}}:** I've walked over that floor every day of my life and it was just a floor.

**ELDER:** Take the ridge road. If the pylons are lit, keep going until you find who lit them.

### B. The ridge

*EXT. RIDGE — MIDDAY. {{FRIEND1}} is cutting cable from a humming pylon.*

**{{FRIEND1}}:** Stand back. This one's live.

**{{HERO}}:** They've been dead for centuries.

**{{FRIEND1}}:** They were. Line's hot from the flats, all the way out to that tower.

**{{HERO}}:** Then I'm going to the tower.

**{{FRIEND1}}:** You'll be there by dark, and you'll die in about a minute. I'll walk you.

### C. The tower

*INT. ENGINE TOWER — DUSK. {{ANTAGONIST}} stands at a lit console. He does not hurry.*

**{{ANTAGONIST}}:** You came faster than I expected. Give me a moment.

**{{HERO}}:** Whatever that is, turn it off.

**{{ANTAGONIST}}:** It is on. There are ten more, and I am going to reach each of them.

**{{ANTAGONIST}}:** The ground will shake for a day, then settle. Get your valley to high ground tonight.

**{{FRIEND1}}:** He's telling the truth. That's the part I don't like.

## 7. The ending

{{HERO}} shuts the chain down from the anchor ring with {{ANTAGONIST}} standing beside him, and the two of them agree to a slower plan that will not feed his region in his lifetime.

The flooded forest stays flooded, the volcanic island is gone, {{FRIEND3}} does not come down from the ring, and the valley {{HERO}} left is now one place among eleven that he is responsible for.
