# Design (blind, v4)

## 1. Premise

The old builders sank engines into the planet to hold it steady, learned the engines were grinding it
down, switched them off, and walked away so it could mend.

{{ANTAGONIST}} has found the switches and is throwing them, one region at a time, and the ground is
splitting again along the old seams.

{{HERO}}, a farm kid who can hear the humming ruins, follows the sound out of {{HOME}} and ends up the
one person who can shut all twelve engines down.

## 2. The party

- **{{HERO}}** — sixteen, orchard work, sleeps badly because the ruin under the trees hums; wants to
  know why the hum only speaks to them; talks in short flat statements and asks one question too many.
- **{{FRIEND1}}** — the village pump mechanic, four years older, missing two fingers to a gear; wants
  to keep every machine in {{HOME}} running, then every machine anywhere; explains things while doing
  them, never after.
- **{{FRIEND2}}** — a surveyor from the coast who maps ruins for money and keeps the good ones off the
  map; wants to reach a place no living person has stood in; dry, teasing, counts steps out loud.
- **{{FRIEND3}}** — a guard who quit {{ANTAGONIST}}'s work crew at the third engine; wants to be
  forgiven by people who do not know what they did yet; speaks rarely, and in apologies.

## 3. The antagonist

{{ANTAGONIST}} was a well-driller who lost a town to a dry season and dug until they found a builder
engine still warm under the salt, and it made rain for a year.

They are not hiding: they hand out the maps, hire the crews, pay the wages, and every engine they wake
feeds a region that was starving the month before.

**Want:** switch on every engine the builders left, so no town ever goes dry again.

## 4. Twelve regions

1. **{{HOME}}** — terraced orchards on a green step-valley, a stone hatch under the oldest tree that
   breathes warm air; the party opens the hatch and finds the first engine already spinning.
2. **{{REGION1}}** — a salt-flat market town under white glare, crane rigs and a pump crew in
   {{ANTAGONIST}}'s orange coats; the party trades the harvest for a rail cart and learns where the
   crews go next. *(Rail cart unlocks the eastern line.)*
3. **{{REGION2}}** — a drowned forest of grey trunks standing in flat water, walkways nailed between
   them; the party pulls the second engine's feeder rods and watches the water drop a hand's width.
4. **{{REGION3}}** — a cliff coast of stacked red rock and ladder towns, wind turning old builder
   vanes; the party repairs a cargo hoist and takes it down to the shore. *(Needs the rail cart.)*
5. **{{REGION4}}** — a desert where the sand has fused to green glass in long rays; the party walks a
   ray inward to its center and finds a shut engine that {{ANTAGONIST}}'s crew is one day from waking.
6. **{{REGION5}}** — a terraced mountain of switchback stairs and prayer bells; the party hauls a
   builder lifter back onto its cable to climb the last wall. *(Lifter unlocks the summit.)*
7. **{{REGION6}}** — an archipelago of black islands in warm water, fish gone belly-up in the shallows;
   the party buys a hull, fits a builder core to it, and sails. *(Boat unlocks all water routes.)*
8. **{{REGION7}}** — a mangrove swamp that steams where the buried pipes run hot, houses on stilts;
   the party carries {{FRIEND3}} to face the crew boss who hired them.
9. **{{REGION8}}** — three flat islands hanging a thousand feet up, roots dangling, wind constant; the
   party rides a lift column to reach them. *(Boat plus the lift column.)*
10. **{{REGION9}}** — an ice shelf split by a warm blue canyon that should not be warm; the party skis
    the canyon to the vent house and shuts the coolant engine, and the shelf stops calving.
11. **{{REGION10}}** — a volcanic caldera floored with builder plate, orange light coming up through
    the seams; the party crosses on a plate-runner sled to the control spine. *(Sled built in
    {{REGION9}}.)*
12. **{{REGION11}}** — the hollow under the sea floor where all twelve lines meet in one white room;
    the party takes the boat down a flooded shaft and stands where the builders stood. *(Boat refit
    for depth.)*

## 5. The first hour, ten steps

1. Carry water buckets down the orchard rows — the terraces, the valley below, the sealed stone hatch at
   the bottom row, warm air moving the grass around it.
2. Follow the hum to the hatch and put a hand on it — the stone is warm, and a light under it changes
   color when {{HERO}} touches it.
3. Fetch {{FRIEND1}} from the pump house — {{FRIEND1}} elbow-deep in a seized pump, orange-coated
   strangers on the road behind them.
4. Open the hatch together — a stair of dry white steps going down into blue light.
5. Walk down and fight two crawler drones — a round chamber, a spindle turning fast enough to blur,
   cable trunks running off in twelve directions.
6. Pull the feeder rod — the spindle slows, the light dims, and the whole valley outside goes quiet.
7. Climb out — the orchard's east terrace has slumped into a fresh crack, and the crack glows faintly.
8. Run to the village square — {{ANTAGONIST}} standing on a cart, handing out work contracts and
   rain-season maps to {{HERO}}'s neighbors.
9. Take the west road out with {{FRIEND1}} — the valley wall, a cut track, and a rail cart parked at
   the top of it with orange coats sleeping beside it.
10. Ride the cart down toward the salt flats — the white glare opening ahead, eleven cable trunks
    running under the track in the same direction.

## 6. Three cutscenes

**A. The hatch**

{{HERO}}: It's warm. It's been warm all week.

{{FRIEND1}}: Nothing down there should be warm. Stand back, I'm cutting the seal.

{{HERO}}: It got louder when I touched it.

{{FRIEND1}}: Then don't touch it while I'm holding a torch.

*(The hatch drops open. Blue light.)*

**B. The spindle**

{{FRIEND1}}: That's a feeder rod. Pull it and this thing stops.

{{HERO}}: And the crack in the east terrace?

{{FRIEND1}}: The crack is this thing. Pull it.

{{HERO}}: I've never broken anything this expensive.

*(The spindle slows. The lights go orange, then out.)*

**C. The square**

{{ANTAGONIST}}: Rain in the north flats by the fourth month. Signed and paid.

{{FRIEND1}}: You're waking the engines. They were shut for a reason.

{{ANTAGONIST}}: My town died waiting for the reason. Yours doesn't have to.

{{HERO}}: There are eleven more of those, aren't there.

{{ANTAGONIST}}: Twelve. Take the west road, kid, you'll see them all.

## 7. The ending

{{HERO}} shuts the last engine from inside the white room, and the planet keeps the scars it already
has: {{REGION2}} stays drowned, {{REGION4}} stays glass, the north flats go dry again and {{HOME}}
feeds them by cart.

{{ANTAGONIST}} lives, stripped of crews and maps, hauling water on the route {{HERO}} drew, and
{{HERO}} stays down in the white room a season longer than the others to learn which switches must
never be touched, and comes up older.
