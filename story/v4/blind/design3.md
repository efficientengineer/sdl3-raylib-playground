# Blind design 3

## 1. Premise

The old builders sank engines under the crust to hold a cracking planet together, found that the engines
were doing the cracking, shut them all down, and sailed away.

Their towers still stand in every corner of the world, and one of them is humming again, because
{{ANTAGONIST}} relit it and the ground under {{REGION2}} has been steady ever since.

{{HERO}}, a sixteen-year-old runner from {{HOME}} who has never crossed the valley wall, is carrying the
one key that shuts an engine off, and the rest of the keys are scattered across twelve regions.

## 2. The party

{{HERO}} — sixteen, runs messages between the terrace farms of {{HOME}} and wants to be handed something
that matters; speaks in short plain sentences and asks the question the adults are stepping around.

{{FRIEND1}} — a survey engineer's apprentice who wants to stand inside a live engine once before it goes
dark; talks in numbers, distances and lists, and drops jokes flat with no warning that one is coming.

{{FRIEND2}} — a caravan guard whose home town slid into a fault when she was nine, who wants every engine
dead and salted; loud, four words to a sentence, swears by the weather.

{{FRIEND3}} — the last student of the order that kept the shutdown keys, who wants proof the old builders
were right to leave; speaks formally to strangers and teases dryly when tired.

## 3. The antagonist

{{ANTAGONIST}} was chief surveyor of the quake service in {{REGION2}}, signed off on a valley as safe,
watched it fall in with four hundred people inside, and walked out of the inquiry into the ruins to find
a tower that could stop it happening twice.

He travels ahead of the party in a survey airship with four engineers, relighting towers in the order the
old builders shut them, leaving each region steadier, greener, and closer to the next fracture.

Want: "I will not stand over another buried town, so every engine goes back on."

## 4. Twelve regions

1. **{{REGION1}}** — {{HOME}}'s terraced valley of stone walls and wind pumps, with a sealed tower foot in
   the orchard; the party pulls the key out of it and watches the first airship pass overhead.
2. **{{REGION2}}** — a quake country of cracked highways and jacked-up houses, now unnaturally still; the
   party sees the relit tower, fails to reach its door, and is thrown off the ridge by the crew.
3. **{{REGION3}}** — a lake town on stilts with a drowned library beneath it; {{FRIEND3}} dives for the
   order's map of all twelve towers and the party recruits the ferry crew.
4. **{{REGION4}}** — a canyon of red shelves and rope bridges, reached only after repairing the old **cable
   ferry** in {{REGION3}}; the party races the crew to a key and loses it by a floor.
5. **{{REGION5}}** — a warm shallow coast of reed towns and fish terraces, reached by **river barge**; the
   party learns the relit engines are pulling the sea in and marks the water line on a wall.
6. **{{REGION6}}** — a mining plateau of spoil heaps and boiler towns, barge upriver; the party trades the
   barge for parts and sabotages a shipment of tower fuel.
7. **{{REGION7}}** — a salt desert with a buried builder city under the pan, crossed by the **sand crawler**
   built in {{REGION6}}; the party wakes a dormant tower's voice and hears the shutdown order read aloud.
8. **{{REGION8}}** — an ice shelf with a tower frozen into a cliff, reached by fitting the crawler with
   **runners and a steam breaker**; the party takes a key and {{FRIEND2}} nearly goes into a crevasse.
9. **{{REGION9}}** — a rainforest over a warm fault, where the ground steams and the trees are enormous;
   the party finds a town that loves its relit tower and has to argue instead of fight.
10. **{{REGION10}}** — a high volcanic rim of black terraces and cable stations, reached only by the
    **tunnel borer** started in {{REGION8}}; the party takes the borer through the rim and loses it there.
11. **{{REGION11}}** — a floating archipelago of rock islands held up by an old field, reached by the
    **captured airship**; the party disables the field's tower and lands the islands gently on a plain.
12. **{{REGION12}}** — the builders' control island, a flat grey disc of switch halls above open water,
    reached by the airship and opened by all twelve keys; the party and {{ANTAGONIST}} finish this there.

## 5. The first hour, ten steps

1. Run three letters across the terraces of {{HOME}} — see the valley, the wind pumps, and a hairline crack
   opening along the orchard wall.
2. Follow {{FRIEND1}} down into the crack — see a stone tower foot under the orchard with a slot in it.
3. Put a hand on the slot — see the key rise out of the stone and the tower's lights come up green.
4. Walk back up into town with the key — see an airship crossing the valley low enough to flatten the corn.
5. Stand in the square while the elders argue — see {{FRIEND2}} shoulder her way in and volunteer.
6. Ride the cart to the wall pass — see the first fight, three surveyors in matched coats turning back.
7. Cross the pass — see {{REGION2}}'s cracked highway and a town that has stopped shoring its houses up.
8. Interview the quake service clerk — see the log of the relight signed by {{ANTAGONIST}}.
9. Climb the ridge road to the relit tower — see the tower's rings turning and the ground steady underfoot.
10. Reach the tower door — see {{ANTAGONIST}} step out of it, take the key, read the name on it, and give
    it back.

## 6. Three cutscenes

### A. The orchard crack

*{{HERO}} and {{FRIEND1}} stand at the bottom of the crack. A stone face with a slot in it.*

**{{FRIEND1}}:** Four metres down, dry, and the wall is cut, not broken. This is built.

**{{HERO}}:** There is a slot. My hand fits it.

**{{FRIEND1}}:** Then do it before the elders get here and turn it into a meeting.

*{{HERO}} touches the slot. A key rises out of the stone. Green light climbs the wall.*

**{{HERO}}:** It knew me.

### B. The square

*The square in {{HOME}}. The airship's shadow has gone. Elders, {{FRIEND2}} at the back.*

**{{FRIEND3}}:** That ship is relighting the towers. Mine is the order that shut them, and I am what is
left of it.

**{{HERO}}:** So where do we go.

**{{FRIEND3}}:** Over the wall pass, into {{REGION2}}. The nearest tower is already running.

**{{FRIEND2}}:** Good. I have been waiting eleven years for a direction.

### C. The tower door

*The ridge above {{REGION2}}. Rings turning. {{ANTAGONIST}} steps out with two engineers.*

**{{ANTAGONIST}}:** You came a long way to hold a door handle.

**{{HERO}}:** We are turning it off.

**{{ANTAGONIST}}:** Look down the valley. No cracks, no braces, no funerals since spring.

*He takes the key from {{HERO}}'s hand, reads the name cut into it, and hands it back.*

**{{ANTAGONIST}}:** Go home. Come and find me at the next one if you cannot.

## 7. The ending

{{HERO}} shuts the last engine by hand while {{ANTAGONIST}} holds the vent open from the inside, and the
strain the engines were carrying goes where it was always going to go — the coast of {{REGION5}} slides
under the sea in one afternoon, with the reed towns emptied but the terraces, the boats and the water line
gone for good.

{{HERO}} walks home along a shoreline that is on no map, hangs the key on the wall of the order's ruined
office in {{HOME}}, and starts answering the letters that now arrive addressed to the keeper.
