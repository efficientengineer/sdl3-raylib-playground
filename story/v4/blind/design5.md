# Blind design (v4) — design5

## 1. Premise

An older people built engines to hold a cracking planet together, learned the engines were draining
it, shut them all down, and walked away so the ground could mend.

{{HERO}}, sixteen and good at fixing fences, is standing on the granary floor of {{HOME}} when a
stranger restarts the engine underneath it and the terraces slide into the river.

The party follows the restart across twelve regions, opening the road with a wind-sled, a rail car,
a dive bell and a flyer, and has to decide which engines to kill and who pays for it.

## 2. The party

- **{{HERO}}** — a repair apprentice from {{HOME}} who patches pumps and fences; wants to be handed
  something that matters and not drop it; speaks in short practical sentences and asks one question
  too many.
- **{{FRIEND1}}** — a caravan guard two years older, carrying her father's cracked shield; wants
  everyone she travels with to walk home; gives orders first and apologizes a day later.
- **{{FRIEND2}}** — a ruin-reader who copies old machine marks into a notebook; wants to read the
  shutdown log of the old people end to end; talks fast, in lists, and narrates while working.
- **{{FRIEND3}}** — a drifting engine-tender who kept one small machine quiet by himself for six
  years; wants the humming to stop everywhere; speaks rarely, flat, and it lands as a joke.

## 3. The antagonist

{{ANTAGONIST}} was the water warden of {{REGION3}} and watched every well in it go to salt across one
dry decade, with her name on the ledger that recorded each one closing.

She travels a week ahead with a crew of four, restarting engines in order of size, and she is
unfailingly polite, right about this season, and wrong about the next hundred.

**Want:** *"I want the wells full while the people who blame me are still alive to drink."*

## 4. The tour — twelve regions

1. **{{HOME}}** — terraced farm valley, stone pumps, wet green steps above a slow river; a hatch under
   the granary floor glows and the field above it slides; the party digs out farmers and follows the
   boot prints up the ridge road.
2. **{{REGION1}}** — pine ridge road with toppled relay towers and a burning trade caravan; the
   survivors have been paid to move inland; the party picks up {{FRIEND3}} and the caravan's sailcloth.
3. **{{REGION2}}** — a lake town on stilts where the water came up overnight and the top floors are
   the streets; the party pumps out a drowned ruin and takes the first engine key from it.
4. **{{REGION3}}** — white salt flat, dead well-heads, one glass road running straight; the party finds
   one well running full again after nine dry years, and {{ANTAGONIST}} standing beside it.
5. **{{REGION4}}** — red canyon of stacked shelves split by an updraft gap; **needs the wind-sled**
   built from the caravan sailcloth; the party crosses and pulls the first page of the shutdown log
   out of a wall.
6. **{{REGION5}}** — gray dunes over a buried rail depot, half a train standing out of the sand; the
   party repairs the rail car, **the machine that opens the next three stops**.
7. **{{REGION6}}** — a mining city on a slag terrace, **rail only**, furnaces fed by engine heat; the
   party shuts the engine, the furnaces go cold, and the city runs them off the terrace with rocks.
8. **{{REGION7}}** — flood plain gone jungle over a fallen tower, insects the size of dogs, **end of
   the rail line**; the party cuts to the tower's core and finds {{ANTAGONIST}}'s crew already gone.
9. **{{REGION8}}** — black sand coast, wrecked hulls standing upright in the shallows, a village of
   salvagers; the party trades the rail car for a hull and builds the dive bell in it.
10. **{{REGION9}}** — a lit hall on the sea floor, dry inside, fish pressing the windows; **needs the
    dive bell**; the party shuts a second engine and watches the coast tide drop a full meter by morning.
11. **{{REGION10}}** — a plateau above the cloud line, thin air, wind-farms of old blades still turning;
    **needs the ruin's lifting cage restarted**; the party strips the blades for a flyer.
12. **{{REGION11}}** — the polar seam, a black crack across the ice with the first and largest engine
    standing in it; **needs the flyer**; the party goes down the seam to end the network from inside.

## 5. The first hour, in ten steps

1. Carry three water buckets up the terrace for morning chores — the valley's pumps stall one after
   another, top to bottom.
2. Follow the sound into the granary — the floor stones are warm and a seam of green light runs under
   the grain bins.
3. Pry the hatch with a fence bar — a room-sized engine turning below, with a stranger's rope ladder
   still hanging into it.
4. Climb down and look at the housing — fresh boot prints in the dust and a chalk number, **1 of 9**.
5. Run for the surface as the shaking starts — the terrace wall splits and the top field goes into the
   river with the sheep on it.
6. Help {{FRIEND1}} pull two farmers out of the mud — four figures on the ridge watch, then turn and
   walk the hatch road north.
7. Search the wreck for tools — {{FRIEND2}} is already there sketching the split, notebook braced on
   her knee.
8. Fight two ruin-cleaners that crawl up out of the hatch, sorting people into the debris pile — the
   tutorial for swapping a party member mid-fight.
9. Take the elder's letter to the ridge road — from the top the river valley shows three more hatch
   lights glowing in fields downstream.
10. Step onto the pine road — the trade caravan is burning and {{FRIEND3}} is sitting on its roof with
    a wrench, in no hurry at all.

## 6. Three cutscenes

**A. UNDER THE GRANARY**

> {{HERO}}: The pumps stopped because this started.
>
> {{FRIEND2}}: Chalk's still soft. Someone numbered it. One of nine.
>
> {{HERO}}: Then they're out numbering the other eight.
>
> {{FRIEND2}}: I have wanted to stand in one of these since I was nine. I wanted it switched off.

**B. THE TOP FIELD, AFTER THE SLIDE**

> {{FRIEND1}}: Two more under the mud. Take the left arm and pull on three.
>
> {{HERO}}: Those four on the ridge — they're just watching us dig.
>
> {{FRIEND1}}: They came up the hatch road and they're going back down it. We follow at first light.
>
> {{HERO}}: I have never been past the ridge.
>
> {{FRIEND1}}: Good. Keep up, and complain quietly.

**C. THE BURNING CARAVAN, {{REGION1}}**

> {{FRIEND3}}: Caravan's finished. Drivers went downhill an hour ago.
>
> {{HERO}}: Did a woman with four others come through here?
>
> {{FRIEND3}}: Yesterday. She bought their horses and told them to move inland and stay there.
>
> {{FRIEND1}}: Then she knows exactly what she's starting.
>
> {{FRIEND3}}: She knows. I tended one of those for six years. It sounded better asleep.

## 7. The ending

{{HERO}} runs the shutdown from inside the seam engine and it takes the whole network down together —
the wells of {{REGION3}} go back to salt, {{REGION6}}'s furnaces stay cold, and {{ANTAGONIST}} stays
below holding the last switch steady so the sequence closes clean, and does not climb out.

The party walks home over a planet that is healing and poorer, and {{HERO}} — who was not trusted
with the granary pump in the opening scene — carries {{FRIEND2}}'s copy of the shutdown log town to
town, teaching the parts people can build and run themselves.
