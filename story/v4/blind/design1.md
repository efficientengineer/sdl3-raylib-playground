# Blind design 1

## 1. Premise

The old builders ringed the world with engines to hold it steady, learned the engines were grinding it down, switched them off, and walked away.
{{HERO}} lives in {{HOME}}, a terrace town built into a dead engine housing, and helps the salvage crews pull copper out of walls that still hum.
The day a stranger restarts the engine under {{HOME}}, the ground warms, the river runs the wrong way, and {{HERO}} leaves to find out how many more are being switched on.

## 2. The party

- **{{HERO}}** — sixteen, a salvage runner who can hear which ruin walls are live; wants to be believed by the adults who send them into holes; talks in short practical sentences and asks one question too many.
- **{{FRIEND1}}** — {{HOME}}'s pump mechanic, four years older, missing two fingers to a machine that woke up; wants every engine in reach shut down and sealed; talks in flat orders and small dry jokes at the worst moment.
- **{{FRIEND2}}** — a runaway archive keeper who can read the builders' notation; wants to finish the shutdown log the old builders never completed; talks in careful full sentences and quotes the log out loud when scared.
- **{{FRIEND3}}** — a caravan guard from the salt country who joined for the pay and stayed for {{HERO}}; wants a home that stays standing; talks loud, teases everyone, goes silent when the question is serious.

## 3. The antagonist

{{ANTAGONIST}} is a former engine-warden from a drowned coast town, old enough to remember when the wells still ran, patient, and completely willing to explain the plan to anyone who asks.
They are not raising an army; they are walking the ring with a key and turning things on in order, and each one they wake makes the next easier.

**Want:** make the land livable again in their lifetime, and take the price out of the planet's.

## 4. The twelve regions

1. **{{REGION1}}** — the terraced dead engine of {{HOME}}, ladders and laundry lines strung across a turbine throat; the party pulls {{HERO}} out of the flooding intake and finds the warden's key-marks scratched by the control ring.
2. **{{REGION2}}** — a reed delta gone hot, fish floating belly-up in water that steams at dawn; the party traces the new current upstream and takes the salvage skiff that carries them the rest of the way.
3. **{{REGION3}}** — a canyon of stacked stone terraces farmed by rope-and-pulley; the party warns the terrace elders, loses the argument, and watches the first terrace slide.
4. **{{REGION4}}** — a caravan city of tents around one standing builder tower that still lights at night; the party trades salvage for the wind-sled (**vehicle: wind-sled**, which opens the salt flats).
5. **{{REGION5}}** — white salt flats crossed by half-buried rails, mirage towers on the horizon; the party races {{ANTAGONIST}}'s work crew to a buried junction box and cuts it a day late.
6. **{{REGION6}}** — a pine highland with an engine grown through by roots, needles falling grey; the party learns from a hermit warden that the shutdown was deliberate, not a failure.
7. **{{REGION7}}** — a sunken coast, drowned streets, rooftops as docks; the party salvages a diving bell (**machine: diving bell**) and hears {{ANTAGONIST}}'s own town named as the one that went under.
8. **{{REGION8}}** — the seafloor works below the coast, glass corridors under pressure, lamps still lit; the party reaches the second-ring console and finds four of six engines already warm.
9. **{{REGION9}}** — a volcanic shelf of black glass and vent fields, the air tasting of iron; the party cools a runaway engine by hand and {{FRIEND1}} is burned badly doing it.
10. **{{REGION10}}** — a cold plateau of wind-scoured stone and standing antennae; the party repairs the lifting barge (**vehicle: lifting barge**, which opens the upper regions).
11. **{{REGION11}}** — a sky-anchored ring station above the cloud deck, gardens in vacuum-sealed bays gone to seed; the party confronts {{ANTAGONIST}} face to face and cannot stop the sequence from there.
12. **{{REGION12}}** — the core hall at the ring's hub, six columns of light under one hand-sized switch; the party throws the shutdown and decides what to do about the person still holding the key.

## 5. The first hour, ten steps

1. Player carries a coil of cable down a ladder into the intake — sees {{HOME}}'s terraces strung across a turbine throat, and the wall panel glowing faintly blue.
2. Player pries the panel and pockets the copper — sees a scratch mark beside the socket, fresh, not weathered.
3. Player runs the cable to {{FRIEND1}} at the pump house — sees the pump gauges climbing on their own.
4. Player follows {{FRIEND1}} to the intake floor — sees water rising against the current, warm enough to steam.
5. Player fights two wall-crawlers shaken loose by the vibration — sees the fight tutorial and the first loot: a builder's hand-lamp.
6. Player climbs to the control ring on the intake roof — sees a stranger in a warden's coat closing a panel and walking off calmly.
7. Player reports to the town council — sees the elders vote to wait and watch the water.
8. Player packs at home and takes the hand-lamp — sees {{FRIEND3}} already at the gate with two packs.
9. Player follows the steaming river downstream out of {{HOME}} — sees the delta of {{REGION2}} open up and the world map unlock to one road.
10. Player reaches the dead fish shallows — sees {{FRIEND2}} crouched over a builder's plate, reading it out loud, and the party becomes four.

## 6. Three cutscenes

### A. The intake floor

INT. INTAKE FLOOR — WATER RISING

{{FRIEND1}}: The river is climbing the wrong way. That means something under us is pulling.

{{HERO}}: The wall panel was lit. There was a mark by the socket.

{{FRIEND1}}: Show me on the roof. Now, before the floor goes.

{{HERO}}: My mother said this hole was dead for a thousand years.

{{FRIEND1}}: It was. Someone's opinion changed.

### B. The control ring

EXT. CONTROL RING — DUSK

{{ANTAGONIST}}: You have a good ear for live walls. I could use that.

{{HERO}}: What did you turn on?

{{ANTAGONIST}}: The one that feeds your town. Your wells will run by winter. Follow the river if you want to see the rest.

{{HERO}}: And after winter?

{{ANTAGONIST}}: After winter I will be five engines further along.

### C. The gate

EXT. {{HOME}} GATE — NIGHT

{{FRIEND3}}: Two packs. One of them is heavier, and it is not yours.

{{HERO}}: The council is going to wait.

{{FRIEND1}}: Then we go downstream and count the dead fish ourselves.

{{FRIEND3}}: I have never been downstream. I hear the food is terrible.

{{HERO}}: Keep the lamp out. I want to see the water.

## 7. The ending

{{HERO}} throws the shutdown and the ring goes dark; the terraces of {{REGION3}} stay fallen, {{FRIEND1}}'s hand does not come back, and the drowned coast stays drowned.
{{ANTAGONIST}} is left alive at the hub with the key snapped in two, and {{HERO}} — now the one who signs the seal orders — walks the ring for a year closing what is left.
