# Blind brief (v4)

You are designing a 16-bit JRPG. You have NOT seen any other material for this game and must not
look for any. Work only from this page.

Fixed points:
- A coming-of-age adventure with a light mix of wondrous fantasy and old science: an ancient, far
  more advanced civilization once built engines to hold a dying planet together, realized the engines
  were what was killing it, shut everything down, and left so the planet could heal. Their ruins are
  everywhere and some still hum.
- Now someone has found the engines and is waking them, because they work, and the planet is back on
  the path to destruction. The antagonist is a person or a small group with a want you can say in one
  line, not a faceless corporation.
- The hero is young and unproven at the start and is someone else by the end.
- It is an ADVENTURE: the party visits many different regions, each with its own look, and the map
  opens in stages (a vehicle, a route, a machine).
- Dialogue is short. A cutscene is three to six lines. Most lines tell the player what is happening
  or where to go next; one line per scene is a character quip, tease, or feeling said out loud.
- Plain words. Use tokens for names: {{HERO}}, {{FRIEND1}}, {{FRIEND2}}, {{FRIEND3}}, {{ANTAGONIST}},
  {{HOME}}, {{REGION1}}... Do not invent proper nouns.

Write, in this order, about 900-1,300 words total:
1. Premise in three lines.
2. The party: four members, one line each: who they are, what they want, how they talk.
3. The antagonist: two lines and a one-line want.
4. A tour of twelve regions, one line each, in the order visited: what it looks like, what is there,
   and what the party does there. Say which regions need a new vehicle or machine to reach.
5. The first hour as ten beats, one line each: what the player does, then what they see.
6. Three cutscenes from the first hour in screenplay form, three to six lines each, in the dialogue
   rule above.
7. The ending in two lines. Nothing is undone for free.

Be specific and concrete. Do not explain the theme. Do not use the words "nobody", "somebody",
"anyway", "beat", or "for the first time".
