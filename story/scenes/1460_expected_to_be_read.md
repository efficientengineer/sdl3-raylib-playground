# Scene 1460: Expected to Be Read

- type: talk
- location: a round chamber of dressed stone with a ring of stone seats rising in three tiers, a plain stone table at the centre, a closed stone door behind it, lamp panels in the ceiling, cold even light
- characters: Bron, Sefa, Hesk, Zeph, Ket, Pip, Ollo
- mood: tense, then sorrow
- backdrop: 1455_the_last_vote:5

## Beat
The argument the whole act turns on, had badly, in a cold room, by people who have not slept. Sefa
says it in filing terms and is correct and unbearable. Zeph defends the one thing he can defend: that
the record is whole. Hesk names what has been paying for it. Bron gets there last and flatly, in one
box, and nobody argues with him and nobody wins. Nobody in this scene states the theme, and nobody
says out loud what it means for the woman a day ahead of them, beyond Bron's ten words. Then Ollo,
who is the only one of them who has actually spoken to the result, asks a question about whether he
is allowed to have liked her, and the scene stops there.

## Dialogue
- Sefa {tense}: A deposit with no retrieval date is not a deposit. It is a disposal with better manners.
- Zeph: Registrar.
- Sefa: I have drafted the instrument for one. Twice. I know what it is when I am standing inside it.
- Zeph: The record is intact. A thousand years, and not a line of it has gone. That is not nothing.
- Sefa: It is not nothing. It is also not anything, until somebody comes down and reads it.
- Hesk: There are three hundred and forty of them up that street.
- Hesk: They're the interest. A thousand years of people, paid out a week at a time to keep the sum whole.
- Zeph: That is not what interest — ...no. No. That is exactly what it is.
- Bron: This is her plan. Already run. We're standing in it.
- Hesk: ...
- Sefa: Yes.
- Zeph: ...
- Ollo {sorrow}: Can I say a thing and not have it held against me.
- Ollo: I liked her. The woman at the oven. I liked her very much.
- Ollo: Is that allowed?
