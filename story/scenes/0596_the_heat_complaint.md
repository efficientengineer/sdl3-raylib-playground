# Scene 0596: The Heat Complaint

- type: talk
- location: a low stone room with a stone hearth, a long trestle table crowded with bowls, tools hung on every wall, a broad doorway looking out over stepped fields, firelight from the left, evening
- characters: Lyra, Pip, Ket, Hesk
- mood: comedy with a thousand years in it

## Beat
Back in the village after the stair. Hesk has the stove door open and is complaining at length about
the heat in a kitchen she banked herself, and will not be reasoned with. Ket, who does not register
heat, has taken the hottest corner in the room on purpose and says so. Then the two of them have
four lines that are about a stove and are not about a stove, and neither raises her voice, and both
of them are scrupulously polite, and Pip and Lyra sit there very carefully not making eye contact
with each other. Nothing is resolved. Hesk offers Ket the better chair and Ket declines it exactly,
and takes the offer as hospitality anyway, and Hesk lets her.

## Dialogue
- Hesk {tense}: This room is an oven. It has been an oven in here since Green.
- Pip: You banked it yourself. I watched you do it.
- Hesk: I banked it for you. That does not cool it down.
- Ket: I do not register heat. I have taken the hottest corner of the room on purpose.
- Ket: Nobody else wants it. A corner nobody wants is the cheapest thing anyone in this house can give me.
- Hesk: That's four things you've said about my stove, keeper.
- Ket: Five. And not one of them was about the stove.
- Hesk: No.
- Pip: I don't know what is happening and I am enjoying it enormously.
- Lyra: Eat, Pip.
- Hesk {hope}: Keeper. Take the good chair. It's the one by the window.
- Ket: Not correct. This chair is nearer the stove. Yours is better and colder. I will keep this one, and thank you.
