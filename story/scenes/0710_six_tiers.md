# Scene 0710: Six Tiers

- location: a three-hundred-foot quarry face cut into six stacked terraces of stone buildings, lit windows in every band, cable lines running up the face, a capped shaft head at the foot, cold blue dusk, warm window light
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip, Ket, Hesk
- mood: wonder, then dread
- staging: The city stands up-screen right and above in every panel; the open road and the dark fall away to the left. The party faces up-right at it throughout. The capped hall-mouth sits at the foot of the face, low and right

## Beat
Midnight at the foot of the switchback. The party has been three days in the dark on an empty road
and now the whole thing is standing over them: six shelves of lit windows in a cut face, cable lines
crossing between them with small cars on, and warm air coming down off it steadily, like breath off
a big animal. Pip has a joke ready and cannot get to it. Zeph counts the tiers out loud because
counting is what he does when he is impressed. Ket gives the metered roof figure without being
asked, flatly, because it spoils it, which is exactly why she says it. Hesk will not tilt her head
back and looks longer than anybody. And Bron is not looking at the city at all: he has found the
squat brass cap over the squared opening at the bottom of the face, and that is where he stays.
