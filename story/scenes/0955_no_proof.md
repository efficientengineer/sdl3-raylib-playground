# Scene 0955: No Proof

- type: talk
- location: a plank-walled office with a long counter, pigeonholes of paper folders behind it, a ledger open on a slope with a pen rest, a brass tally frame on the wall, flat daylight from a window at the left, morning
- characters: Bron, Pip, Hesk
- mood: paid in nothing

## Beat
Bron reports that he could not find them, which is a lie, and the first Line means he is not paid for
it, and he is not paid for it. The agent shrugs and writes the bill off, because men wander off on
the Flats. Hesk, who does not soften anything, points out exactly what it costs: Tovin-Caleth writes
the ledger wall at Sallowgate in her own hand, and there will be a line there with nothing beside it
for as long as the wall stands. Bron says fine. Pip buys the drinks with money they did not earn.

## Dialogue
- Bron {sorrow}: Couldn't find them.
- Agent: Couldn't find five men and a fire on a flat white pan.
- Bron: No.
- Agent: Right. Men wander off out here. I'll write it off. No proof, no pay — you know the wording.
- Bron: I know the wording.
- Hesk: You've just lied to a sealed bill.
- Bron: Yes.
- Hesk: Tovin writes that wall by hand. There'll be a line with your name and nothing beside it. For good.
- Bron: Fine.
- Pip {hope}: Right. I'm buying. With money we did not earn, which is the best kind there is.
