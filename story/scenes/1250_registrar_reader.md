# Scene 1250: Registrar, Reader

- type: talk
- location: a wide clerks' corridor of pale cut stone, numbered doors in a row on the left, a trolley of paper folders parked against the wall, hanging lamps in a line, flat even lamplight, no daylight
- characters: Sefa, Zeph, Bron, Hesk
- mood: tense

## Beat
Fourteen brass rings in one ear-fan and a trolley she is walking to the board floor herself because
she does not trust the porters. She is preparing for a meeting she expects to win, she is in the
best humour she has been in for years, and she is unbearable. She corrects two of Bron's numbers
and one of Hesk's, accurately. She mentions, out of professional pride and nothing else, that the
Tellwater roster came in yesterday and is now the best-documented town in the Basin. Hesk answers
in four words. Sefa writes them down. Zeph speaks once and it is a title.

## Dialogue
- Sefa {tense}: Reader. You've grown a coat.
- Zeph: Registrar.
- Sefa: Mind the trolley. Thirteen boxes and a case, and the porters drop the ninth every time, so I walk it myself.
- Bron: Four hundred names, eight days, and a hand we can't put to anybody.
- Sefa: Four hundred and seven. Nine days. And you have not once asked a Registrar. Three faults in one breath.
- Hesk: Fourteen boxes for one stair.
- Sefa: Thirteen and a case. A case is not a box. Do try. Precision is free and it buys a great deal.
- Sefa: I have a paper going in at four o'clock and it cannot be answered. I am in excellent humour, if it doesn't show.
- Sefa: Oh — and the Tellwater roster came in yesterday. Sealed, complete, cross-referenced, indexed by trade.
- Sefa: It is now the best-documented town in the Basin. Nothing about it will ever be in question again.
- Hesk: Nobody is in it.
- Sefa: ...Say that again. — No. I have it.
