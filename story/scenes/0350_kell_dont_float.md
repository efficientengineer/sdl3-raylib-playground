# Scene 0350: Kell Don't Float

- type: talk
- location: a half-sunk timber bore shed in standing brown water, its roof broken open, a winch drum rusted solid above the waterline, a plank ramp sliding in, flat grey daylight from the left, morning
- characters: Pip, Bron, Zeph, Lyra
- mood: hope, then tense
- backdrop: 0345_the_water_over_the_pasture:4

## Beat
Thirty seconds of comedy immediately before the worst place in the chapter. Pip is going in first
and is delighted about it. Bron is not going in the water, and the reason, delivered deadpan and
without the smallest embarrassment, is engineering: Kell are dense, Kell run hot, and a Kell in
deep water goes down like a dropped anvil. Zeph would like to test this. Lyra rations the lamps.
Nobody teases Bron about nerve, because the joke is the physics and not the man. They end with a
working plan that is almost entirely Pip's and that everyone agrees to call collective.

## Dialogue
- Pip {hope}: Me first. Don't argue. It's the only part of this I'm any good at.
- Bron: I'm not going in the water.
- Pip: You're not — right. Why aren't you?
- Bron: Kell don't float.
- Zeph: ...Say that again, slowly, I want all of it.
- Bron: We're dense and we run hot. Put me in deep water and I go down like a dropped anvil.
- Zeph: I would like to measure that. Under supervision. In a shallow place.
- Bron: No.
- Lyra: One lamp lit and one spare, and nobody lights the spare until the first one is out.
- Pip: Plank walk to the door, rope on the ring bolt, knots every arm's length, and I go first.
- Bron {tense}: That's the plan.
- Zeph: That is her plan.
- Bron: That's the plan.
