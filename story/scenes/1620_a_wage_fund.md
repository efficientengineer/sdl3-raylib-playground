# Scene 1620: A Wage Fund

- type: talk
- location: a graded gravel street between plank bunkhouses with tin roofs, a standpipe with a brass meter box, a stack of new crates under canvas, laid rail curving away at the end, flat noon light
- characters: Orrin, Hesk, Dov, Bron
- mood: sorrow, then hope

## Beat
Late Rime, at Cadder Step. Stripped, blacklisted and unemployable anywhere in the Basin, Orrin
Cadder has come home to the village he sold in 1081 and is running a wage fund out of his own
pension. About half of them draw off it. The other half walk past the gate, look at the wall, and go
on, and the game does not fix that and nobody in the scene tries to. Hesk Ninefold, who spat on his
boots at Ninefold Terrace, walks into his yard and asks him for the one thing he can still do, which
is get Kell onto a payroll: a standing paid watch at an open door with no employer behind it. He
writes it, says the terms once, and puts his own seal on it, which he mentions is the first seal he
has pressed into anything since a bill he burned the corner off. Dov Ninefold, whose bore crew was
disbanded when the Long Bore went cold, takes the nights. Bron stands there and says nothing at all.

## Dialogue
- Orrin {sorrow}: Hesk Ninefold. In my yard. Wipe your boots or don't. It's your business either way.
- Hesk: I've come for a bill.
- Orrin: I've no bills. I've a pension, and about half a village that'll draw off it.
- Orrin: The other half come past that gate, look at the wall, and go on. They've done it since Dust.
- Hesk: A door watch. The ninth door-mouth at the Terrace. Paid. Standing. No end date on it.
- Orrin: Who's the employer?
- Hesk: Nobody. That's the trouble.
- Orrin: A wage with no employer. That's the best thing anybody's asked me for in thirty years.
- Orrin: Two shifts of two, day and night. Four and six a week apiece, found in food. Say it back.
- Hesk: Two shifts of two. Four and six a week. Found in food.
- Orrin: Seal's mine. First one I've pressed into anything since a bill I burned the corner off.
- Dov: I'll take the nights.
- Dov: Off a blacklisted man's pension, working for my aunt. I want it said that I find that funny.
- Orrin: Standing watch on a door that's finally open. So the job's wide open. ...Because the door is.
- Dov: That's terrible.
- Orrin {hope}: You laughed. One out of four. I'll take it.
