# Scene 1035: The Wards

- location: a wide warm day room of cream plaster with forty plain chairs in loose rows, a stove with a guard rail, a shelf of identical enamel cups, tall windows down one side, flat daylight from the left, afternoon
- characters: Ovel, Lyra, Pip
- mood: warm, clean, wrong
- staging: The party comes in from the left and moves right through the building; Ovel walks at the left of frame facing right and the party follow him. The wards and the day room open off the right. The seated residents face left, toward the doorway the party are standing in

## Beat
The gate is not locked and the doctor comes out himself, because almost nobody ever visits and he
is proud of the place, and it is genuinely the best-run building any of them has ever been inside.
Warm rooms, scrubbed floors, wide windows, good food, nothing hurried. Ovel Marsh talks the whole
way with real tenderness, says "residents", slips into "patients", and corrects himself without
being asked. In the day room forty people are sitting in loose rows, healthy, well fed, upright and
entirely calm, and they look up at six strangers with mild friendly interest, and one of them asks
Pip whether she has come about the linen. Nothing here is a horror hospital: no restraints, no
bars, no filth, no staring, no noise. The party understand what they are standing in and say
nothing about it in front of him.

## Dialogue
- Ovel [1] {wonder}: Come in, come in. Almost nobody visits. Nineteen years, and I could count the visits on my two hands.
- Lyra [2]: How many are here?
- Ovel [2]: Forty-one patients. Residents. Residents — I slip, and I have been correcting myself for nineteen years.
- Zeph [3]: Eight beds to a ward and a number on every bed end. The numbers are on the beds and not on the people.
- Ovel [4]: Warm rooms, good food, nobody hurried. It is not a complicated profession. It is linen and patience.
- Ovel [5]: They sleep well. Every one of them sleeps better than I do.
- Pip [6]: ...
- Resident [7]: Have you come about the linen?
- Pip [7]: ...No. No. Sorry.
- Ovel [7] {dread}: They'll ask you that all afternoon and be pleased every time. They are the kindest people I have ever nursed.
