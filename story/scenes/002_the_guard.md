# Scene 002: The Guard

- location: the same cold stone corridor, the great door now standing open onto darkness
- characters: Lyra, Zeph
- mood: dread

## Beat
The door grinds open on its own. A skeleton in the rusted armor of an old hall guard stands
waiting on the other side. Zeph is fascinated. Lyra is not.
