# Scene 002: The Guard

- location: the same cold stone corridor, the great door now standing open onto darkness
- characters: Lyra, Zeph
- mood: dread
- staging: Lyra and Zeph stand on the left facing right. The open doorway and the skeleton guard are on the right, facing left toward them. Everyone's attention is on the guard

## Beat
The sealed door grinds open by itself. A skeleton in the rusted armor of an ancient hall guard stands waiting in the doorway, motionless. Zeph, the reckless scholar, is thrilled: he recognises very old magic and leans in to see it better. Lyra is alarmed and protective, raising her holy medallion against it. Both stare at the guard.
