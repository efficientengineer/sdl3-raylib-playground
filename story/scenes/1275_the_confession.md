# Scene 1275: The Confession

- location: a long panelled room with a single oval table and twelve chairs, a brass water carafe and glasses, a fireplace with a low fire at the left, tall shuttered windows on the right, evening lamplight
- characters: Crewe, Kerrow, Bron, Sefa
- mood: dread, then sorrow
- staging: Crewe stands at the head of the table on the left of frame and faces right down it. The directors sit along both sides, Kerrow nearest him; the clerk is at a side desk on the right; the party stands at the far right end of the table. Everyone's attention is on Crewe

## Beat
The chapter's centre. Crewe taps the cane once, stands, and puts the whole thing on the record: the
season order he signed in the spring of 1093, the density logs on his desk, the written objection
at 21:40, the answer at 22:02 quoting his own signature, the nine seconds, the four hundred and
seven, the reason, stated once and flatly, the decision to file it as a flood, who drafted the
instrument, the cost per head, the study nobody circulated, and then the names, read off loose
pages in his own hand. He is not a villain making a speech. He is a tired, courteous man reading a
document he wrote himself, and he is relieved to be doing it. He never justifies beyond the
arithmetic, never weeps, and never asks for anything. Two directors ask procedural questions. The
clerk keeps writing. Kerrow does not look up.

## Dialogue
- Crewe [1] {dread}: Before the first item, and not on the agenda. Extraction Bore One, Tellwater, the ninth of Fallow, ninety-three.
- Kerrow [1]: Clerk. Take all of it.
- Crewe [2]: I signed the season order that spring. It authorised the night run. That is my hand on the copy.
- Bron [2]: What day.
- Crewe [2]: The fourth of Thaw. The branch density logs had been falling two years. They came to my desk quarterly.
- Crewe [3]: At twenty-one forty the shift engineer entered a written objection and asked for the run to be stopped.
- Crewe [3]: At twenty-two oh two Vantage answered her. Continue, per season order. A duty clerk quoted my signature at her.
- Director [3]: Chairman. Is this minuted as an item, or as any other business?
- Crewe [4]: Nobody decided anything at twenty-two oh two. I should like that in the minute in those words.
- Crewe [4]: Twenty-two thirty-one. Nine seconds. Four hundred and seven residents, and four of the five crew.
- Crewe [5] {sorrow}: I was four years old in the last Hungry Winter. I did the arithmetic. I could live with the answer, and I still can.
- Bron [5]: What day was it filed.
- Crewe [6]: The ninth day. A flood and a subsidence. Sefa Quill drafted the instrument in two days, at nine marks and two a head.
- Crewe [6]: One more item. There is a study. Extraction, ninety-nine, twenty-six. It was completed and it was never circulated.
- Director [6]: Is that study before this board?
- Crewe [7]: These are the pages. My hand, never filed. Kettle, R. — Emberrow — tannery. Brae, A. — Windrow — laundry.
