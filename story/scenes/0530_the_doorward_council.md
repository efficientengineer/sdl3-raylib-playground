# Scene 0530: The Doorward Council

- type: talk
- location: a bare stone hall with a ring of nine worn stone benches, a shallow fire pit at the centre, nine iron door-keys hung in a row on the back wall, high slit windows, flat afternoon light from above
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Ket, Hesk
- mood: polite, immovable

## Beat
The council will talk to Ket, barely, and only because an Onn asking about a door is an old
complaint they know the shape of. They will not open anything, discuss what is behind anything, or
admit that anyone has ever asked before. What they have is work: a count of the nine door-mouths,
seals and mortar noted, for a fee so small it is an insult, and it is an insult, because everybody
on this cliff already knows all nine are shut. Ket asks one question about the Fifth and is told to
take the bill or go down the steps. Lyra notices that nobody answered her. From the wall, Hesk says
she will walk the path with them, and does not say why, and nobody asks her to.

## Dialogue
- Tarn {tense}: Keeper. You are welcome to sit. The hunters can stand.
- Ket: Correct. I will sit. Thank you.
- Tarn: You want the doors opened. Onn have wanted the doors opened since my grandmother's grandmother.
- Ket: Since before these terraces were walled. I have the dates if the council wants them.
- Tarn: The council does not want them.
- Ket: One question, then. The Fifth. What is the fill depth behind it?
- Tarn: Take the bill or go down the steps, keeper. Those are the two things on this table.
- Tarn: Two marks. A count of the nine door-mouths, seals and mortar noted, in order, on paper.
- Pip: Two marks. For nine doors on a cliff path.
- Tarn: It is not work. Everyone here knows all nine are shut. It is two marks and an afternoon and it is what there is.
- Bron: We'll take it.
- Lyra: You didn't answer her.
- Tarn: No.
- Hesk: I'll walk it with them.
