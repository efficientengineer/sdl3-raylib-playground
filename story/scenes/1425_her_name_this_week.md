# Scene 1425: Her Name This Week

- type: talk
- location: a small stone court off the street with an open water basin at its centre, a bread oven cut into the wall beside it, stacked clay bowls, a lamp column at the corner lighting from above, no daylight
- characters: Ollo, Bron
- mood: wonder, then sorrow

## Beat
Everyone has been asking the woman at the oven about the past and getting bread. Ollo Marrick, who
spent eleven years being warm to strangers for a living and is the only person here with any
practice at it, asks her about the bread instead, and she is delighted, exact, funny and entirely
present: she knows the oven, the mix, the heat, and the fact that the fat is wrong this week. She is
not pitiable, not vague, not slack; she is good at her job. She gives him a name because somebody
called her that this week, and next week it will be another one, and she does not mind and cannot see
why he would. Ollo tells her his own name, which he has said aloud perhaps four times in eleven
years, and she takes it carefully and thanks him for it. He never explains anything to her and never
will. Bron watches it from the doorway and does not understand how it was done.

## Dialogue
- Bron {wonder}: Zeph's asked her four times who built that oven. She keeps giving him bread.
- Ollo: That's a good heat you've got on. What are you running, three bakes?
- Nine: Four. And the last of them is a waste, because the fat's wrong this week.
- Ollo: Wrong how?
- Nine: Too soft. Goes into the crumb instead of sitting on it. Loaf's tired before it's cold.
- Nine: So I put them in high and drop the heat under them. It isn't right. It's better.
- Ollo: That is exactly what I'd have done.
- Nine: You bake?
- Ollo: I did. I do. — What do they call you?
- Nine: Nine. Somebody called me that this week and it stuck.
- Ollo: ...And next week?
- Nine: Something else, I should think. What do they call you?
- Ollo: Ollo. Ollo Marrick.
- Nine {sorrow}: Ollo Marrick. ...Ollo Marrick. Thank you for that.
- Bron: How did you know to do that.
- Ollo: Do what? I didn't want to be rude to the woman.
