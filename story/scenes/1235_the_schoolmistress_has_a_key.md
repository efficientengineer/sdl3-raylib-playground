# Scene 1235: The Schoolmistress Has a Key

- type: talk
- location: a children's dormitory corridor of cut stone with a bare wall on the left and a cable run humming behind it, a nightlight in a wall niche, a book-strap and a chair by the door, cold blue darkness, small hours
- characters: Idda, Bron, Zeph, Ket
- mood: hope

## Beat
The schoolmistress the party did a favour for in chapter seven has slept for three months and looks
like a different woman. She is delighted to see them and appallingly indiscreet about it. She hands
over a staff tier pass she is not supposed to lend and a cable house key she is not supposed to
have, and asks for one thing back: whether the noise is worse, because she thinks it is and she has
started counting it again. Ket puts her head to the wall for eleven seconds and gives her a figure
and will not round it, and Renn writes it down, and that small courtesy is worth more to her than
the favour was. Nobody works out what it means. Nobody says it should not be possible.

## Dialogue
- Idda {hope}: You came back. I told the caretaker you'd come back and he said nobody ever comes back.
- Idda: I've slept. Three months of it. I'd forgotten what my own face looks like when it isn't tired.
- Idda: This is a staff tier pass. I am absolutely not allowed to lend it, and I am lending it to you.
- Idda: And that's the cable house key. I'm not allowed to have that at all. Don't ask me how long I've had it.
- Bron: What do you want for them.
- Idda: Tell me whether it's got worse. That's all. Because I think it has, and I've started counting it again.
- Zeph: Counting. She said counting. — Ket. Wall. Go on, this is entirely your sort of thing.
- Ket: ...
- Ket: Two hundred and eight in the second, at this wall, tonight. Not two hundred and nine. Two hundred and eight.
- Idda: Slower. Say it slower, I'm writing it down.
- Ket: Two hundred and eight. I have no figure for Green and I will not guess one for you.
- Idda: ...Nobody has ever given me a number before. Thank you.
