# Scene 0110: The Ledger Wall

- type: talk
- location: the guild hall ground floor, bare sandstone walls, a black iron stove burning at the left, a pinned job board on the right, behind it a floor-to-ceiling wall of ruled columns of small handwriting, lamplit evening
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip, Tovin, Tibb
- mood: warm, then flat

## Beat
Home. The stove is going, Tibb Orsk is in the corner with his boots off, and behind the board is
fifty-four years of handwriting. Tovin-Caleth counts the proof out of the sack, refuses to round
anything in anybody's favour, pays on the spot and takes the tenth. The hall is funny and easy and
the joke is filthy and her face never moves. Then she says the other thing she has been holding,
in exactly the same voice, and the room stops. Lyra is not in it by then; she went up at ten past
four and nobody saw her go.

## Dialogue
- Tovin {hope}: Nine.
- Bron: There were ten.
- Tovin: There were ten on the bill. There are nine in the sack. I pay for nine.
- Tovin: Two marks and four. Less the tenth. Two marks, one, and a half.
- Bron: Call it two and two.
- Tovin: No.
- Tovin: Pay on completion. You completed. Hold out your hand.
- Tibb: Two marks! Four days on the road. I could have got you that standing still.
- Tovin: You have never got anything standing still, Orsk. These walls are one board thick.
- Pip: ...Oh. OH.
- Tibb: She does that. Don't look at her, it encourages her.
- Tovin {sorrow}: Board eats at six. Merrit Tack is upstairs and will not see the morning.
- Tovin: The sister is with him. She went up at ten past four.
- Pip: ...Lyra's already gone.
