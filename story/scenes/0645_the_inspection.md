# Scene 0645: The Inspection

- type: talk
- location: a timber derrick over a shaft collar on white salt, a winch house of corrugated iron beside it, a bank of valve gear on a plank stand, coiled cable, hard flat morning light, salt glare
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Ket, Hesk
- mood: comedy of procedure, played absolutely straight

## Beat
Ket walks into the gate office and claims a keeper's right of inspection over a shaft sunk through a
skywell foundation: a right a thousand years older than the company, never withdrawn, and therefore
— as the site supervisor establishes over about four minutes of honest searching — still in force.
She names her hands. There are six of them. The supervisor does not fold because he is frightened;
he folds because there is no procedure for refusing and he would rather not invent one on a Tuesday
with a shift on. Hesk objects to going down a company hole, on the record, in one sentence, and then
comes. The supervisor hands them a lamp each and tells them the night shift is working and to keep
off the north gallery gantry.

## Dialogue
- Ket {tense}: Keeper of Meddra, of the array. I am here to inspect a shaft driven through the foundation of Semmet.
- Supervisor: Right. Which form is that on.
- Ket: There is no form. The right is older than the form. It has never been withdrawn, so it stands.
- Supervisor: There'll be a form. There is always a form.
- Ket: I will wait. It took the clerk at Braid nine minutes and he found the same nothing you are going to find.
- Supervisor: ...There's an inspection book. It has got two entries in it and they are both from ten fifty-nine.
- Ket: Then mine is the third. Ket-Ossun, keeper, and six hands.
- Supervisor: Six.
- Ket: Six. They are mine, they go where I go, and I will bring all six of them back up. Write the number.
- Hesk: I want it written that I said no. I said no to a company hole and I am going down a company hole. Both.
- Supervisor: I'll... write that in the remarks.
- Hesk: Good.
- Lyra: Is there anything we should know before we go down?
- Supervisor {hope}: Night shift's on. Keep off the north gallery gantry, they're working it. And sign out the lamps, that one I do have a form for.
