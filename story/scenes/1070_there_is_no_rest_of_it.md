# Scene 1070: There Is No Rest Of It

- type: talk
- location: a windowless room of floor-to-ceiling pigeonhole shelving stuffed with paper folders, a rolling ladder on a rail, a bare table with one folder open on it, a hanging lamp overhead, night
- characters: Zeph, Hesk, Ket, Bron, Lyra, Pip
- mood: sorrow
- backdrop: 1065_four_marks:6

## Beat
Still at the desk, before the alarm. Zeph does the arithmetic out loud because he cannot stop
himself and because if he stops he will have to say something else, and everybody lets him. He gets
one figure wrong and nobody corrects it. Ket asks two flat questions about numbers and the answers
do not add up, and she says so, to nobody, in her ordinary voice, and that is the worst line in the
scene. Lyra says nothing at all. Nobody deduces cleanly, nobody makes a speech, nobody offers a
cure, and nobody tells Bron what he is feeling. Hesk says the hard thing in one sentence and does
not soften it afterwards, and then hands him something heavy, which is the kindest thing anybody
does in the chapter. Bron takes the box and carries it out, because it is heavy and he can carry
it.

## Dialogue
- Zeph {sorrow}: The field takes everything inside the ring and puts it in the ground. That is the whole of what it does.
- Zeph: What goes in that way goes in as nothing in particular. It is not filed. It is not anywhere you could go and look.
- Zeph: Three hundred feet of shaft, and a bell at the bottom of it with a woman standing at the bell.
- Zeph: She received it. All of it. That is the only reason four hundred people have a record at all.
- Zeph: He was standing at the top of the shaft.
- Ket: How many are held.
- Zeph: Four hundred and ten, and her own on top of that. Four hundred and eleven records.
- Ket: How many were inside the ring.
- Zeph: Four hundred and seven of the town. Four of the five on the crew.
- Ket: Four hundred and eleven. ...Then one of them is not held. Not correct.
- Zeph: ...
- Hesk: Nothing is coming back to you. Not one hour of it, and not ever.
- Hesk: Take that end. We're over the wall by four and you're carrying it.
- Bron: Which end.
