# Scene 1395: The Line Under the Hills

- type: narration
- mood: administrative

## Beat
Chapter end, text over black, over the sound of a rail car on laid track. What the maintenance rail
is, given as a timetable would give it: who laid it and when, how far it runs, what it links, how
long it takes end to end, and what the stations are called. Then three flat facts in a row, with no
comment on any of them, and a date.

## Dialogue
- Narrator {tense}: The maintenance rail was laid in 1071 by the Clement Works, along the hall-mouths, under the Rim Hills.
- Narrator: Two hundred and thirty-one miles of it. Eleven stations, each one named after the door it stands at.
- Narrator: End to end is nineteen hours with a crew on the crank. It reaches every sealed way into the hills.
- Narrator {dread}: The draw gear was shut down by hand and stood still for four hours. It will be turning again by morning.
- Narrator: A public-safety warrant sworn at Braid was discharged at Vantage at noon. The officer who swore it is suspended.
- Narrator: It is the fifth of Fallow. The ninth is four days off.
