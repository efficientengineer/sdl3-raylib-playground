# Scene 0492: The Rail South

- location: a low timber rail sledge under a single patched triangular sail running on laid rail across white seabed crust, its wake of dust behind, rails to a flat horizon, hard morning light from the left
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip, Ket, Cassa
- mood: hope -> tense
- staging: the sledge runs to the right in every panel and the Rim Hills stand ahead on the right. Cassa is at the crank at the stern on the left. Ket stands at the rail on the right facing right. Bron stands at the rail beside her, facing right

## Beat
First light at the siding below Braid. Five people and a sail, and Cassa counting them aboard and
saying the number twice, because the number has changed and she does not intend to mislay anybody.
Pip is helping and making it considerably worse. Then the salt runs east under them for four hours,
and Ket stands at the rail the whole way without sitting down, watching a hill line come up out of
the haze, and gives Bron the count that actually matters: nine doors, nine households, and a thousand
years of nobody getting in. Bron says the thing Bron says. Ket tells him it has been tried. The last
image is the cliff with nine squared black notches cut in it, seen from a moving deck.

## Dialogue
- Cassa [1] {hope}: First light, siding clear, wind off the north-west. We will be under the hills by ten.
- Cassa [2]: One, two, three, four, five — six. Six.
- Cassa [3]: Six. I am saying it twice because it has changed and I do not intend to mislay one of you.
- Ket [4] {tense}: Nine door-mouths in one cliff face.
- Ket [5]: One household living over each. Nine doors, nine households, and not one of them opened in a thousand years.
- Bron [6]: Then we ask nicely.
- Ket [7]: That has been tried.
- Ket [7]: Eleven times. By me.
