# Scene 1650: Above the Guild Hall

- type: talk
- location: a small plastered attic room, a cot, a survey satchel hung on a nail, a plank shelf of folded maps, a low square window looking down over flat roofs, early morning sun from the left
- characters: Bron, Maren
- mood: sorrow, then hope

## Beat
Fallow 1108, the day before the last one. Maren Ostry lives above the Even Hand hall and does small
survey work for it: cistern levels, a terrace wall, a disputed boundary out at Kettle. She is not
crowded. Her hands are her own, her accent stays where it starts, and she has no idea what happened
at Tellwater, or in a sanatorium, or on any road in the last two years, and she is not distressed
about it, which Bron finds a great deal harder than she does. They talk about a wall. She asks him
once, casually, whether they knew each other before, and takes yes for an answer, and asks what she
was like, and he tells her the truth, which takes one line. They like each other. It goes no further
than that and the game does not nudge it. She has never been to Braid and does not think she will.

## Dialogue
- Maren {sorrow}: That wall out at Kettle is going. Not this year. Three years, maybe four.
- Maren: The footing's on crust and crust moves. You can see it in the courses if you stand well back.
- Bron: Tull won't like that.
- Maren: Nessa Tull told me she had heard worse from more expensive people. I liked her enormously.
- Maren: I've put it at four years with a figure for the rebuild. Tovin wants it on one sheet.
- Bron: She'll want the figure not rounded.
- Maren: She said. Twice.
- Maren: ...Did we know each other? Before.
- Bron: Yes.
- Maren: That's nice. I thought we might have. You're an easy man to be in a room with.
- Maren: What was I like?
- Bron: Tired. Sorry about everything. You told me to sit down before you told me your name.
- Maren: ...That does sound like me. That is still me.
- Maren {hope}: They tell me there are eleven hours of it at Braid. Said out loud, every night.
- Maren: I've not been. I don't think I will.
