# Scene 1515: Four Hundred Voices

- location: an enormous wall of packed hair-fine filament, pale gold going white where it is densest, lying in strata like grain in wood, a stone ledge and iron rail along its foot, light coming only from the face
- characters: Maren, Bron, Pip
- mood: dread, then confront
- staging: Maren stands at the foot of the filament wall on the right of frame and turns to face left. The party is on the chamber floor at the left of frame facing right. The wall fills the back and the right. Everyone's attention is on Maren

## Beat
She turns round, and she is losing. Her hands arrive at the ends of gestures that belong to other
people; her accent walks through four Basin towns inside one sentence; her voice comes doubled and
then trebled and then as a crowd, all of them trying to give an address at once. She is not
threatening anybody and never will. She is a tired, courteous woman being drowned from the inside by
four hundred frightened people who all want to say where they live, and the worst thing in the scene
is that she keeps apologising for it. She says Bron's name correctly, and then three other people
who knew him at nine say it after her in her mouth, and she hears that happen and cannot get in
front of it. The wall behind her does nothing at all, the whole scene, exactly as it has done for
three thousand years.

## Dialogue
- Maren [1] {dread}: Sorry. I'm sorry. They all want to say where they live, and they all want to say it now.
- Maren [2]: The house by the well. Fourteen, Mill Row. Thirty-one. Thirty-one. Thirty-one.
- Bron [3]: Maren Ostry.
- Maren [4] {confront}: Bron.
- Maren [4]: Bron. Hesta's boy. The one that got lost above the fold. Bron.
- Maren [5]: That was four of us. I heard it happen and I could not get in front of it.
- Bron [6]: I'm here. Say it again.
- Maren [7]: I've kept four hundred people quiet for fourteen years. I can't do it standing here.
- Maren [8]: I can't hear which one of us is me.
