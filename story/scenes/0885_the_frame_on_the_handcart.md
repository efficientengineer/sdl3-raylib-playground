# Scene 0885: The Frame on the Handcart

- location: a steep street of dark stone housefronts with iron-railed steps, rope-walks strung overhead casting bars across the stones, a handcart chocked at the kerb with a long canvas-covered case on its bed, lamplight from open doors, night
- characters: Ovey, Bron, Lyra, Dalla, Ross
- mood: tense, then confront
- staging: The handcart, the frame-bearers and Ovey are at the right of frame, uphill. The party stands at the left facing right. Dalla is in her own doorway between them. Ross is further left again, still turning in the street

## Beat
An hour on, and the Works has a procedure for this, and the procedure is kind. A handcart comes down
the street with two frame-bearers walking it and a long canvas-covered case on the bed, and Recovery
Chief Ovey steps off it with a clipboard flat under his arm and asks which one is the wife before he
asks anything else. He is brisk to the point of rude and he is not unfeeling and he is not lying: it
is four minutes, it is legal and has been since fifty-two, the man will be comfortable and will
sleep, it will want doing again in a month and he will come back and do it. He does not pretend it
undoes anything. He does not draw and he does not threaten. Everybody standing in that street can
see that it would work, and that is the whole scene: Bron has to decide whether to put himself
between a man who will not stop asking for his house and the thing that would stop him asking. Ovey
asks Dalla for her word out loud, and she is standing right there, and she is entitled to give it.

## Dialogue
- Ovey [2] {tense}: Recovery. Chief Ovey. Which of you is the wife?
- Ovey [2]: Not you. You. Right. Four minutes standing up and he's comfortable before the cart's turned round.
- Bron [3]: And what's in him after four minutes?
- Ovey [4]: Somebody's good afternoon, sold and paid for. He'll be pleasant, he'll eat, he'll sleep the night.
- Ovey [4]: It doesn't take anything out. It puts something over. It'll want doing again in a month and I'll come back and do it.
- Lyra [5]: And the thing underneath it?
- Ovey [5]: Still there. I've not lied to anybody in this street tonight and I'm not going to start with a sister.
- Ovey [5] {confront}: Missus. It's your say and nobody else's in the Basin. I need it out loud.
