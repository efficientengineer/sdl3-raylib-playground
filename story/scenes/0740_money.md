# Scene 0740: Money

- type: talk
- location: a low stone-vaulted bar room, a long zinc counter, a wall of small kegs on racks, four round tables with mismatched stools, one hanging lamp over the counter at the left, night
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip, Ket, Hesk
- mood: hope

## Beat
The first night in the game the party has money and somewhere to spend it, and none of them knows
how to do it. Zeph orders things by name, specifically, and they arrive, and he is insufferable
about it. Hesk will not sit within eight feet of the fire and makes everybody move twice. Pip eats
four courses in a row and, between the third and the fourth, folds two rolls into her jacket without
breaking off what she is saying, and nobody comments and nobody ever will. Ket does not eat; she
counts the lamps in the room out loud, slowly, until she is asked to stop, and then gives the total
immediately, having had it all along. Lyra says the most frightening thing anyone says all evening
in exactly the voice she would use to ask for salt, and it goes straight past every one of them.
Nothing is decided. Nothing moves. It is the best hour any of them has had since Sallowgate.

## Dialogue
- Zeph {hope}: The dark one. Third cask from the left. And a plate of whatever that man has got, and bread. All of the bread.
- Pip: He can just do that. He says which one and they bring it.
- Zeph: They bring it because I said which one. Nobody ever says which one.
- Hesk: I'm not sitting there.
- Bron: It's a fire.
- Hesk: It's a wall with a fire in it. Take the end, I'll have the draught and be glad of it.
- Ket: Nineteen. Twenty. Twenty-one along the beam, and four behind the counter whose backs I cannot see.
- Pip: Keeper. Keeper. Please.
- Ket: Forty-one. I could have told you that at the door. I was checking.
- Pip: Four courses. Four. They just keep bringing them out and nobody's asked me for anything.
- Lyra: Everyone in this room draws their wage off the same man.
- Zeph: Mm. Was there a fourth cask? I want to say there was a fourth cask.
- Bron: Eat.
- Pip: I am eating. I have never stopped eating. This is the best night of my life and I'm furious about it.
