# Scene 0805: Two Days South

- type: narration
- mood: the road out of the warm air

## Beat
Two days down the ravine road from Vantage. The road leaves the metered air inside an hour and six
people feel it happen and none of them says anything about it. What is at the bottom is rope, hides,
lime, and a company care-house at the top of the street with glass in its windows. The narration
gives the town its trade, its stone and its year, flat, and then hands the player the smell, which
arrives half a mile before the picture does.

## Dialogue
- Narrator {tense}: South out of Vantage, and the warm air stops about an hour down. Nobody in the party mentions it.
- Narrator: Two days of cut country. Wind-dried air, rope-walks, and a road that only goes one place.
- Narrator: Emberrow. Nine thousand people, four hundred tanning yards, and smoke that sits in the cut because the cut has no wind.
- Narrator {dread}: Every wall in it was a skywell. Kestrin fell in ten oh two and was carted away in blocks, and that is the only reason anybody lives here.
- Narrator: You smell it half a mile before the road turns: bark, lime, and hides. Then the road turns.
