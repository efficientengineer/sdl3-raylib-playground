# Scene 1505: The Seam Face

- location: a great oval chamber of fitted stone with concentric stepped tiers going down to a central floor disc, stone standing posts at the rim, a seam face filling the back wall, faint even light from that face
- characters: Zeph, Ket, Sefa, Pip, Bron, Maren
- mood: wonder, then dread
- staging: The party comes out of the stair mouth onto the gallery at the left of frame and faces right and down. The stepped tiers drop away to the right. The filament wall fills the back and right of frame, and Maren is a small figure at its foot on the right. Everyone's attention goes right and down

## Beat
The biggest space in the game and nothing in it is moving. The party comes out onto a gallery above
an oval chamber the size of a town square, cut and fitted by people who measured, with a wall of
packed filament across the whole far side of it. The chord is loud enough to be felt in the jaw
rather than heard, and Pip keeps checking her own teeth with her hand. In the middle of the floor an
old brass draw head sits canted on a gimbal, and Ket puts an age on it that nobody wanted. Sefa,
who has been the most useful person in the party for two chapters, has nothing filed anywhere on
any of it and says so. At the foot of the wall there is one small dark figure with both bare hands
flat against the filament, and she has been there long enough that her shadow has not moved.
Nothing in the wall reacts to anybody. It never has.

## Dialogue
- Zeph [1] {wonder}: Nothing in here is moving. Not the dust, not the air. I do not like it at all.
- Ket [2]: The thing on the gimbal is a draw head. It was set in that floor a thousand years before this company.
- Ket [3]: The bands on the wall travel one way. They have travelled one way since before I was grown.
- Sefa [4] {dread}: I have no paper on any of this. Not a lease, not a licence, not one line in a return.
- Sefa [5]: Fourteen years of knowing where everything is kept, and here is a room nobody ever indexed.
- Ket [6]: It is running. It has run the whole time, and it is not looking at any of us. It never was.
- Zeph [7]: ...That is a woman. That is a woman standing with her hands on it.
- Zeph [8] {dread}: Her shadow has not moved since we came in.
