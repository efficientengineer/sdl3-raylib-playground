## Beat
The first minute of the game, at the end of a training session the player has just played and lost. {{MENTOR}} built these three machines out of wood, iron, rope and counterweights after his knee ended his hunting, and each one teaches a hunter one thing. {{HERO}} has beaten the other two and has spent all afternoon attacking the swing, which is a counterweighted arm on a pivot: every hit he lands winds it up, and it comes back harder. He has just walked into that arm and it has caught his wrist. {{HEALER}} watches from the wall between the two houses most days because it is the best show in {{HOME_TOWN}} — she keeps score, rates the falls and has an apple — and she has no idea how the swing is beaten and no interest in finding out. She comes down and closes the wrist anyway, quickly and easily, teasing him the whole time: magic here is ordinary and it does light work, and this is light work. {{MENTOR}} is at his bench and will not explain the machine he built.
