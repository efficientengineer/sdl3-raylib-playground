# Scene 0270: The Cutting Rig

- location: a plank working floor at the bottom of a sandstone cut, a cutting rig of iron frame and brass valve gear standing over a shaft collar, coiled hose and a barrow, naphtha lamps on poles throwing hard light downward, night
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip, Marek
- mood: nobody here is a villain
- staging: The party come down onto the plank floor at the left and face right. Marek stands between them and the rig; the rig and the working face fill the right of frame. Everyone's attention goes right, at the rig

## Beat
They come over the lip expecting a secret and find a workplace: eleven people on a night shift, lamps
on poles, and a rig biting into the rock. Marek Culm meets them with his own lamp in his hand, hears
them out, and is not hostile — he is busy. He will not stop the rig. He cannot: stopping is four days
of wage for eleven households and a repair bill off the crew's bonus, and he has no authority to give
that away and would not use it if he had. Lyra asks him plainly to stop and takes a plain no. Then he
goes back to work, and the crew go back to work, as though four armed strangers with a grievance were
weather, and the party have to decide what they are prepared to do to a man who has done nothing
wrong.

## Dialogue
- Marek [1] {tense}: You're on a working face. Stand on the planks or get off the hill.
- Zeph [2]: We came up for the man whose seal was burned off a bill in Sallowgate.
- Marek [2]: Then you want the superintendent, and he's down the hill till first light.
- Lyra [3]: Stop the rig.
- Marek [3]: No.
- Lyra [4]: I'm asking plainly and I'll take a plain no.
- Marek [4]: Eleven on this shift. Stopping is four days of wage I've no authority to give away.
- Marek [5]: If I had it I'd not spend it on you. Mind the hose on your way down.
- Pip [5]: ...They're all going back to work. Like we're weather.
- Bron [6] {confront}: Get them off it.
- Marek [7]: Don't you—
