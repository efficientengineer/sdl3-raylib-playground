# Scene 1065: Four Marks

- location: a windowless room of floor-to-ceiling pigeonhole shelving stuffed with paper folders, a rolling ladder on a rail, a bare table with one folder open on it, a hanging lamp overhead, night
- characters: Bron, Zeph
- mood: dread, then sorrow
- staging: The reading desk is at the right of frame under its lamp; Bron comes to it from the left and stays on the left of the desk, facing right. The rest of the party stand behind him on the left, outside the lamplight, and do not come forward

## Beat
The file is one page. The player reads it before anybody in the room speaks. It is a good clerk's
hand, ruled fields filled in neatly, a fair copy of nothing: a child of nine, a condition, a
disposal, a template number, two sleeves, a source letter, a price. Bron picks it up, reads it
twice, and puts it back down squarely on the blotter where it was. He does not shout, does not
weep, does not break anything and does not say a word about how he feels. He asks the only
question available to him, which is a question of fact, and the answer is that there is no rest of
it, because there was never anything to file. The five people behind him stay out of the light.
