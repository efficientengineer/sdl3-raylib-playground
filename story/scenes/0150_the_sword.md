# Scene 0150: The Sword

- map: hart_yard
- location: {{MENTOR}}'s yard in the last light of the second day: the four training machines, the last one scarred and standing, the workbench under the eaves, the low stone wall, {{HOME_TOWN}} laid out small on the flat below the hill
- characters: {{HERO}}, {{HEALER}}, {{MENTOR}}
- mood: exhausted, then astonished
- staging: the last machine stands on the right of the screen with {{HERO}} facing right at it. {{HEALER}} is up on the stone wall at the far left facing right. {{MENTOR}} comes out from the workbench at the back left and crosses towards the middle, facing right. - dialogue_box: no

## Beat
{{HERO}} has been at the fourth machine since before first light and has lost forty-one times, and the player has lost with him. His arms are gone, which is the only reason he finally stops swinging: he stands still, listens for the click of the counterweight dropping, turns the arm aside with the stick and hits the post while the arm is out of the way. {{HEALER}}, who has called that arm from the wall since he was twelve, is knocked quiet for a moment and then spoils it on purpose. {{MENTOR}} has watched from the bench and is genuinely surprised — he brings out the sword he finished two winters ago and says exactly what it means, because the boy would not work it out on his own. While he is saying it the evening bell starts down in the town, and the board closes on its last ring.

## Dialogue
- {{HEALER}} [1] {tense}: That's forty-one. You've been out here since before it was light.
- {{HERO}} [2]: Don't count today. Please don't count today.
- {{HEALER}}: Left! It's always the left. I've been telling you since you were twelve.
- {{HERO}} [3]: My arms are gone. I can't swing at it again.
- {{HEALER}}: Then don't swing at it. Stand there and watch the arm come.
- {{HERO}} [4]: There's a click first. The weight drops, and then the arm comes.
- {{HERO}}: It's been clicking at me all day.
- {{HERO}} [5] {confront}: Come on, then. Come on!
- {{HEALER}} [6] {wonder}: He turned it aside. He turned it aside and then he hit it.
- {{HEALER}}: I had a very good thing ready to say and now I'm not saying it.
- {{HERO}}: Say it!
- {{HEALER}}: No. You'd have it framed.
- {{MENTOR}} [7] {hope}: Put the stick down. Both hands.
- {{HERO}}: Did you see it? The arm went nowhere!
- {{MENTOR}}: Saw it. Take this. I made it the winter before last.
- {{HERO}} [8]: You've had a sword for me this whole time?
- {{MENTOR}}: Finished and on its hook. Waiting for you to stop swinging.
- {{HERO}}: Why today? What changed?
- {{MENTOR}}: A hunter who only attacks dies out there. Today you waited.
- {{MENTOR}}: A first sword comes from your teacher. That is the sign.
- {{MENTOR}}: Take it to the clerk. He will write your name in the book.
- {{HERO}}: I can sign? Tonight, I can sign?
- {{MENTOR}}: You can sign. You're ready.
- {{HEALER}} {tense}: {{HERO}}. That's the evening bell. It started while you were shouting.
- {{HERO}}: The board closes on the last ring — hold the gate open!
