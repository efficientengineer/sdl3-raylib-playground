# Scene 0150: The Sword

- map: hart_yard
- location: {{MENTOR}}'s yard in the last light of the second day: the three training machines, the swing scarred and standing, the workbench under the eaves, the low stone wall, {{HOME_TOWN}} laid out small on the flat below the hill
- characters: {{HERO}}, {{HEALER}}, {{MENTOR}}
- mood: exhausted, then astonished
- staging: the swing stands on the right of the screen with {{HERO}} facing right at it. {{HEALER}} is up on the stone wall at the far left facing right. {{MENTOR}} comes out from the workbench at the back left and crosses towards the middle, facing right. The town and the bell tower lie below and behind, on the left
- dialogue_box: no

## Beat
{{HERO}} has been at the swing since before first light and the player has lost with him all day. His arms are gone, which is the only reason he stops swinging: he stands still, lets the weight drop, turns the arm aside with the stick and hits the post while the arm is out of the way. **Nobody tells him to do it and nobody says afterwards what he did.** {{HEALER}} has watched him lose to that machine since he was twelve and she has never once known how it was beaten; she is knocked quiet for a moment and then spoils it on purpose. {{MENTOR}} has watched from the bench and is genuinely surprised — he brings out the sword he finished two winters ago and says the one thing about it the boy needs: take it to the clerk. The sword gets a held beat in his two hands with nobody talking. Then the evening bell starts down in the town, and the board closes on its last ring.
