# Scene 1580: Eleven Hours

- location: a great oval chamber of fitted stone with concentric stepped tiers going down to a central floor disc, stone standing posts at the rim, a seam face filling the back wall, faint even light from that face
- characters: Maren, Zeph, Ilven, Cassa, Pip, Kerrow
- mood: sorrow, then hope
- staging: The broken half-ring of Vess is on the floor disc at the centre and left. Maren sits on the stone nearer the wall at the right. The filament wall fills the back and the right. The stair head and the company are above and behind at the left, and Kerrow turns away from there

## Beat
The last name is said and answered, and then the chamber is quiet, and none of them has heard quiet
like it. Zeph gets about halfway to standing and does not make the rest of it; Cassa and Pip take an
arm each and carry him, and he lets them. Maren sits down on the stone and looks round a room full
of people she does not know, and she is not crowded: her hands are her own, her accent has stopped
moving, she is the only one in there, and she has nothing at all to say about the 9th of Fallow,
1093. Nothing has been healed and nothing has been undone. At the head of the stair Kerrow reads a
figure, gives the order to stand the charges down because her window is two hours gone, and does not
change her mind about a single thing, and asks for that to be written down as well. Behind all of
it the wall is exactly as it was, still brightening very slightly in slow bands, still travelling
the same way.

## Dialogue
- Maren [1] {sorrow}: Ostry, Maren. Chief surveyor, third season. She went down the shaft because the gauge disagreed with itself.
- Zeph [2]: Ostry, Maren. Chief surveyor, third season. She went down the shaft because the gauge disagreed with itself.
- Ilven [3]: Held. — That is the whole of it. Four hundred and eleven, in order, once.
- Zeph [4]: ...Sit me down. I've gone as far as the knees and there's nothing past the knees.
- Ilven [5]: Eleven hours and nine minutes. It is in the line now. It will be said again tonight, and every night.
- Kerrow [6]: Stand them down. Crate them, count them, sign for them.
- Kerrow [6]: I have not changed my mind about any of it. I'd like somebody to write that down as well.
- Maren [7] {hope}: It's very quiet in here.
- Ilven [7]: Ostry. The ninth of Fallow, ninety-three.
- Maren [8]: ...Nothing. It's a date. It goes straight through.
