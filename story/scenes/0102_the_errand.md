# Scene 0102: The Errand

- map: hart_yard
- type: talk
- location: {{MENTOR}}'s yard in the middle of the afternoon: the workbench under the eaves with a new iron catch clamped in the vice, a cloth bundle tied on the bench beside it, and the gate down to {{HOME_TOWN}} standing open
- characters: {{HERO}}, {{HEALER}}, {{MENTOR}}
- mood: chores, and an old man asking the same thing he asks every week
- backdrop: 0101_the_dummies:1

## Beat
The wrist is wrapped and the afternoon has to be got through. {{MENTOR}} asks {{HEALER}} to eat at
his table tonight, which he has done every week for six years and still says in full, and sends
{{HERO}} down into {{HOME_TOWN}} with bread money and a bundle for the guild hall: the catch he cut
for the well winch, and post of his own with his seal on it. What is in the post is the water run and
thirty coin of his own money, and he is sending it because he is certain the boy cannot take it. He
says none of that. {{HERO}} carries his own job down the hill without knowing.

## Dialogue
- {{MENTOR}} {wonder}: {{HEALER}}. Eat here tonight. Sundown.
- {{HEALER}}: You have asked me that every week for six years.
- {{MENTOR}}: And you have come every week for six years. Sundown.
- {{HEALER}}: Sundown, then. I am at {{TOWN_HEALER}}'s room until it gets dark.
- {{MENTOR}}: {{HERO}}. Town. Bread for three, and this bundle goes to the guild hall.
- {{HERO}}: What's in the bundle?
- {{MENTOR}}: The catch off the well winch. It sheared. I cut a new one.
- {{HERO}}: And the rest of it? That's a letter.
- {{MENTOR}}: Post. Give the whole bundle to the clerk and come home.
- {{HERO}}: It's sealed, and that's your mark on the seal. What's in it?
- {{MENTOR}}: Mine. Bread for three. Go.
- {{HEALER}} {hope}: He will not tell you. He has never told me either, and I eat at his table.
- {{HERO}}: Then I'll ask the clerk when he opens it!
