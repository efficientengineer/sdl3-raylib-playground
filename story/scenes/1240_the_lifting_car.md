# Scene 1240: The Lifting Car

- location: a small riveted iron car with wooden bench seats along both sides, a brass hand rail, a lattice gate at the front, a lamp in a wire cage on the roof, lit tiers passing outside the gate, night
- characters: Zeph, Pip, Ket
- mood: cold, high, wondrous
- staging: The car climbs. The cut quarry face and the sliding bands of lit windows are outside the gate on the right; the open drop and the black Basin are on the left. Everyone inside faces right toward the gate

## Beat
Six of them in a car built for six, going up the outside of a three-hundred-foot quarry face in the
dark with the whole cold Basin below. Pip is frightened of the height and will not say so, so Zeph
talks for the entire ascent, about cable gauges and about the people who laid the first rail and
about nothing at all, and she lets him, and neither of them says why. Ket watches the cable and
gives the two numbers that matter. Ollo says the obvious thing about the view and it is not a
clever line. Nobody mentions that the car is exactly full.

## Dialogue
- Zeph [1] {wonder}: Eleven-strand cable. My people laid the first ones. It is not on the plate anywhere.
- Pip [2]: ...
- Zeph [2]: Four hundred miles of laid rail out there before anybody cut a road up here.
- Zeph [2]: My line held the gauge for the western run. In their heads. No paper anywhere.
- Ket [3]: Rated at eight hundred pound. We are six and the gear: four hundred and ten. Both figures are fine.
- Pip [4]: Keep going, Zeph. You hadn't finished about the gauge.
- Zeph [4]: I had not. I have barely started on the gauge.
- Ollo [5] {tense}: That is a very long way down.
