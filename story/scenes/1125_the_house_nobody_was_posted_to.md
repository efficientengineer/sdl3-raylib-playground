# Scene 1125: The House Nobody Was Posted To

- location: a row of identical sandstone cottages along a swept lane with green shutters, the last house at the end standing apart with its door open, scrubbed steps, a hand pump, flat grey daylight from the left, morning
- characters: Pip, Zeph, Hesk
- mood: dread
- staging: The lane runs left to right and the last house stands at the right end of it. The party comes up the lane from the left and stops at the open door on the right. Everything faces right, into the house

## Beat
Four or five houses into the morning's crating work, the last house on the south row is furnished,
swept, aired and has never been lived in. A posting was written for it in 1093 and never filled,
so for fourteen years somebody has been paid to keep it ready. Inside every cupboard door there is
a small nailed card listing what the cupboard should hold, all in one hand, all dated the same
week. Zeph counts the cards and cannot leave off counting them. Pip, who has picked her way into
other people's lives all game, puts a hand flat on the doorframe and does not go in. Hesk says the
thing nobody wants said.
