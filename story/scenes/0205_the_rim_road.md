# Scene 0205: The Rim Road

- type: narration
- mood: two days north on their own money

## Beat
Two days up the rim road out of Sallowgate, paying for it themselves, with the terraces on one hand
and four hundred miles of white salt on the other. The weather is arriving earlier than the almanac
says and nobody on the road thinks that is worth remarking on. The tram spur comes alongside for the
last six miles, and every tram on it is full.

## Dialogue
- Narrator {wonder}: Two days up the rim road. Their own money, their own days. Nobody had posted a bill for it.
- Narrator: Terraces on the right hand. On the left, salt, white to the horizon, and the wind coming off it.
- Narrator {tense}: The almanac gave the first frost a fortnight off. It had come already. Nobody on that road remarked on it.
- Narrator: For the last six miles the tram spur ran beside them and passed them four times.
- Narrator: Every one of them was full of people going to work.
