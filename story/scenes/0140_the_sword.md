# Scene 0140: The Sword

- map: hart_yard
- type: talk
- location: {{MENTOR}}'s kitchen that evening: one room, the door propped open on the yard, supper cleared to one end of the table and the guild ledger open at the other, the sword lying on the boards with a stone beside it
- characters: {{HERO}}, {{HEALER}}, {{MENTOR}}
- mood: a shop, and then not
- dialogue_box: no

## Beat
Supper is over and the sword has been on the table the whole time, which is how {{MENTOR}} sells
anything. He charges for it, and he charges for it while the ledger is still open in front of him,
because he puts a price on every favour he has ever done. Then {{HERO}} asks about the notch and gets
the truthful answer: the father waded into the sheets north of the river instead of walking round,
for twenty years, with {{MENTOR}} behind him to cut him out, and the year he walked alone there was no
one. The notch stays in the blade. {{HEALER}} ends it by telling {{HERO}} to pay the man.

## Dialogue
- {{MENTOR}} {hope}: Take your father's sword. Six coin.
- {{HERO}}: Six coin? You're selling me my own father's sword!
- {{MENTOR}}: Kept it eleven years. I want paying for the keeping.
- {{HERO}} {tense}: There's a notch in the blade. How did he put a notch in it?
- {{MENTOR}} {sorrow}: Sheets, north of the river. One drops on you and you're held until somebody cuts you out. He'd wade into them instead of walking round. Twenty years I was there to cut him out. Year he walked alone there was no one. Notch stays.
- {{HEALER}} {hope}: Pay him, {{HERO}}. He will stand there all night at six coin an hour.
