# Scene 1525: The Fifteen Questions

- location: an enormous wall of packed hair-fine filament, pale gold going white where it is densest, lying in strata like grain in wood, a stone ledge and iron rail along its foot, light coming only from the face
- characters: Bron, Maren
- mood: confront, then sorrow, then hope
- staging: Maren stands and then sits at the foot of the filament wall on the right of frame, facing left. Bron comes in from the left of frame and faces right. The wall fills the back and the right. The rest of the party is back at the rim behind him at the left and stays there

## Beat
Bron walks up to her, gives his name and his trade first because a doorward told him to an hour ago,
and then asks the first question and waits for an answer before he asks the second. He is not
performing a rite. He does not know he is running a maintenance procedure a thousand years old, and
he has never been told that the questions are a check on whether a thing has arrived whole. It is
also, and only, a man asking a frightened woman who she is, in order, until she can hear herself
over the rest of them. The chorus does not stop; it thins. Each voice is asked something it can
answer, answers it, and then has nothing further to say. He takes one gauntlet off to hold his hand
out. Time passes between the third question and the seventh and the scene shows it with the wall and
not with a caption. By the seventh she is down to two voices and one of them is hers.

## Dialogue
- Bron [1] {confront}: My name is Bron Sallow. I'm a hunter, out of Sallowgate. I came down the stair this morning.
- Bron [2]: Do you know where you are?
- Maren [3]: ...Under the hills. The room with the wall in it. Yes. I know where I am.
- Bron [3]: Do you know who is holding your hand?
- Maren [4] {sorrow}: Nobody is. — No. Sorry. You are. Both of them.
- Bron [4]: What is your name?
- Maren [5]: Maren Ostry. ...Maren Ostry. That one was only me.
- Bron [6]: What happened the day before this one?
- Maren [7]: I came down a mile of stair with a lamp and I did not sit down. Nobody else in here did that.
- Bron [8] {hope}: What did you do that you would do again?
