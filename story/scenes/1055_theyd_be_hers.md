# Scene 1055: They'd Be Hers

- type: talk
- location: a cold storeroom of shallow wooden drawers pulled half open, each tray holding rows of paper sleeves with glass threads inside, a brass tally frame on the wall, one lamp above the table, night
- characters: Pip, Bron, Zeph, Lyra
- mood: tense, then quiet

## Beat
Two in the morning, on the way to the file room, and the night clerk assumes they are a transfer
detail because nobody else would be here. He is helpful, unremarkable, and pleased to have somebody
to explain the indexing to. The room is drawn stock waiting on sale or disposal, indexed by source.
He offers a lookup the way a man offers to check a timetable for you. Pip gives her own name before
she has decided to, and he has the drawer open inside a minute because it is a well-kept index, and
there they are, in rows, in season colours. He offers to sign them out. She looks at them for a
long time. Then she says no, and shuts the drawer herself, with her own hand, and asks Bron to
move. Nobody in the party says anything about it, then or ever.

## Dialogue
- Clerk {tense}: Transfer detail? You're early. I've nothing down for tonight, but they never send the paper down.
- Zeph: What is this room?
- Clerk: Holding stock. Drawn stock waiting on sale or disposal. Indexed by source, then by year.
- Pip: By source. Meaning by who it came out of.
- Clerk: By who it came out of, yes. Want a lookup while you're here? It's no trouble. It's what an index is for.
- Pip: ...Rime. P. Emberrow care-house.
- Clerk: Rime, P. Here we are. Care-house contributions, they send down every quarter. Well-kept drawer, that one.
- Clerk: Eleven years of it. Sixty-two sleeves. I can sign them out to you now if you want them.
- Pip: ...
- Bron: Pip.
- Pip: No. They'd be hers. I'm me.
- Pip: Bron. Move.
- Clerk: Right you are. I'll put the drawer back.
