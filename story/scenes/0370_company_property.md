# Scene 0370: Company Property

- type: talk
- location: a stony road climbing a ridge above a sandstone town, dry-stone walls on both sides, a leaning stone mile post, the flooded hollow and low roofs below on the left, hard low sun from the right, late afternoon
- characters: Hanna, Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip
- mood: tense, then confrontation
- backdrop: 0365_the_pale_on_the_ridge:1

## Beat
Sergeant Kesk does this properly, which is far worse than doing it badly. She has their full names
off a list that was written in Windrow the day before yesterday, and she says so without making a
point of it. The flooded ground is company ground, the works under it are company plant, they were
on it without a chit, and there is a schedule with the plate on it as an item. She does not
threaten anybody and does not need to. Zeph argues the law well and she agrees with him about the
law and holds her hand out anyway. Once, half-turned, she says one word to the man behind her,
asking for an instruction, and gets nothing back at all. Bron gives her the plate, because he is
right that she is right, and takes the receipt. Her hand is still out when the scene ends.

## Dialogue
- Hanna {tense}: Bron Sallow. Lyra, of the Order of the Late Hour. Zeph Quill. Pip Rime.
- Hanna: Off a list. Made at Windrow, the day before yesterday.
- Zeph: ...
- Hanna: The low ground is company ground. The works under it are company plant.
- Hanna: You were on it without a chit and you are carrying a fitting off it.
- Zeph: You cannot own a flood.
- Hanna: No. You can own what is under one. There is a schedule of it and the plate is item nine.
- Zeph: The schedule is dated after the flood. A list of a thing you drowned is not a title to it.
- Hanna: That's a good argument. Put it in writing to the Registry at Vantage. They read everything.
- Hanna: The plate, please.
- Bron: And if I say no.
- Hanna: Then I write down that you said no, and somebody else comes, and it isn't me.
- Lyra: ...
- Hanna: ...Sir.
- Bron {confront}: Receipt.
- Hanna: Yes. Take the number off it as well. The number is the only way anything ever comes back.
