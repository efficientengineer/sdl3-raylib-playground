# Scene 0340: Can a Town Be Wrong

- type: talk
- location: the back room of the town inn, a plank table with the plates pushed back, a small coal fire in a plain grate, a row of clean empty hooks by the door, a shuttered window, lamplight from the left, night
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip
- mood: tense, then sore

## Beat
The first real fight the four of them have had. Pip says it out loud, badly and frightened and
joking through the middle of it, because she is the one who watches people. Zeph is the worst of
them without meaning to be, because he is counting rather than accusing and his count is exact.
Bron takes it hard, and his argument is the good one: these people were kind to him this afternoon
and a town being odd is not evidence of anything. Lyra will not take a side and asks one question
too many, gently, and it lands like a slap. Nobody wins, nobody apologises, and nobody says the
word wrong about Bron himself. It ends with the only thing left that anyone can put their hands on.

## Dialogue
- Pip {tense}: Right. I'm saying it, then I'm going to bed, and nobody has to like me for it.
- Pip: Three people called you Coppertop. Three. Three different mouths.
- Bron: It's a name. People had names for me.
- Pip: Did they have that one?
- Zeph: Two of them said the same sentence. "You've got her hands." Word for word, four minutes apart.
- Bron: That's a thing people say.
- Zeph: It is a thing one person says. It is not a thing two people say identically.
- Pip: And a man asked if you ever got another dog.
- Bron: I don't remember a dog.
- Bron {confront}: I left here at thirteen. I've forgotten most of a town. That isn't the town's fault.
- Bron: A town can be odd. A town can't be wrong. It's a place. It's houses.
- Lyra: Bron. Have you ever told anybody here that name?
- Lyra: Coppertop. Since you came through the gate this morning. Have you said it to anyone.
- Bron {sorrow}: ...What is that. What is that a question about.
- Lyra: I don't know yet. That's why I asked it.
- Lyra: I'd like to stop arguing and go in. I'd like that to be because we decided.
- Bron: We'll look at the flooded ground in the morning. That's something you can put your hands on.
