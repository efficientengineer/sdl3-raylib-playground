# Scene 0155: Rule One, Touch It

- type: talk
- location: a long dressed-stone gallery with a row of square floor plates set flush, brass studs at their corners, an iron trip bar sprung out of one wall, dust disturbed in a line, one carried lamp from the right
- characters: Pip, Zeph, Bron
- mood: banter in the dark

## Beat
Between fights, on the second floor. Pip has picked something up off the floor. Zeph wants to know
what it is considerably more than he wants her to put it down, and says so, and Bron comes back
along the gallery to find the two of them holding it between them. The argument is whether a thing
a thousand years old belongs to whoever is carrying it. Everyone is slightly wrong and nobody
minds. It is a spoon.

## Dialogue
- Pip {hope}: Rule one: touch it. Rule two: tell Bron after.
- Zeph: Do not touch that. — No. Do touch it. I want to see what happens.
- Pip: It's a spoon.
- Zeph: It is a spoon that is a thousand years old, which makes it not a spoon.
- Bron: It's a spoon.
- Zeph: It belongs to whoever this hall belongs to.
- Bron: It's mine because I carried it. That's what having something is.
- Zeph: You have not carried it. She has carried it. You have been standing there.
- Bron {tense}: ...Fine. It's hers.
- Pip: It was always mine.
