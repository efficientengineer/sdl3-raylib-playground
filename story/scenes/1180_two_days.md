# Scene 1180: Two Days

- type: narration
- mood: flat

## Beat
Time passing, text over black. Two days given in three registers at once — the weather, the
schedule, and the paperwork — with one domestic fact at the end and no comment on any of it.

## Dialogue
- Narrator {sorrow}: Two days. Warm for Dust, and no wind on either of them.
- Narrator: The postings at Tellwater closed on the 11th, as scheduled.
- Narrator: The roster was sealed on the 12th and went down to the Registry at Vantage with the rest of the papers.
- Narrator: A field commission was suspended pending review. The review is listed for Rime.
- Narrator: The Order of the Late Hour was written to at its house in Sallowgate, concerning Sister Lyra. The letter took four days.
- Narrator: Her mace went into the cart with the tools, because nobody could think where else to put it.
