# Scene 0630: The Bunk-Row Supper

- type: talk
- location: a long shed of double plank bunks with hanging blankets between them, salt dust drifted along the duckboard gangway, a row of tin basins, a stove at the far end, low sun entering from the right, evening
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip, Ket, Hesk
- mood: six people, four bunks, one lamp

## Beat
A rented room built for four with six in it, a bad supper, and the party being a party for the first
time since it became six. Hesk has the window wide open and is complaining about the heat while
everyone else eats in their coats. Ket does not eat and explains what she does instead, in figures,
which finishes Zeph's appetite for him. Pip has lifted something small off every person in the room
and gives it all back as a bit, which takes some time because she has to remember whose was whose.
Lyra is asked for something wise and supplies something extremely unwise in exactly the same voice
she uses for everything else, and nobody is certain for three seconds. Bron makes a list of tomorrow
out loud, because that is how he goes to sleep. Nothing in this room is a clue. It is the last time
all six of them are comfortable.

## Dialogue
- Hesk {hope}: Window's open because this room is an oven. Nobody touch it.
- Pip: It is four degrees out there and there is salt blowing onto my food.
- Hesk: Then eat faster.
- Zeph: Keeper. You have not eaten anything. Not this evening and not last.
- Ket: Correct. I take about eleven hundred grains of dry salts in eleven days and four hours of rest with them.
- Ket: The rest of it comes out of the reserve. That is what the reserve is. It is going down and I am spending it on this room.
- Zeph: ...I have finished with my supper.
- Pip: Right. Hands where I can see them. Zeph, pin. Lyra, glove. Hesk, that's a mortar wedge and I want you to know it was easy.
- Hesk: Give it back.
- Pip: I am giving it back. That's the whole bit. The bit is the giving back.
- Bron: What's mine?
- Pip: Nothing. You've got nothing. It's tragic.
- Zeph: Sister. Say something wise. You are the only one of us with a medallion.
- Lyra: Never sit down beside a man at a fair who has already told you how many drinks he has had.
- Pip: ...Is that wise?
- Bron {hope}: Gate office at seven. Ket does the talking. Boots dry by six, Zeph, I mean it. Somebody blow that lamp out.
