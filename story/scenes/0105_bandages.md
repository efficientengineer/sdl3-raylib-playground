# Scene 0105: Bandages

- map: hart_yard
- type: talk
- location: {{MENTOR}}'s yard on the hill above {{HOME_TOWN}}, early afternoon: the practice posts in the grass, the ladder already against the eaves, {{HEALER}} sitting on the water barrel with a roll of bandage across her knees
- characters: {{HERO}}, {{HEALER}}
- mood: ordinary afternoon, one boy in a hurry

## Beat
The first person the player ever talks to, in the yard of the house {{HERO}} lives in, on an ordinary
afternoon. She tells him the one thing he needs to know today — the guild board has jobs on it and it
closes when the bell stops — and in answering his questions she says what a job is, what she does,
and what she is to him. Nothing else happens. She is not going anywhere and is not asking to; he is
already half out of the gate.

## Dialogue
- {{HEALER}} {wonder}: There you are. They put the jobs up on the guild board this morning.
- {{HERO}}: Jobs? Is there one I could sign for?
- {{HEALER}}: Whatever the clerk has left. Sign your name before the bell stops.
- {{HERO}}: And then what — I walk out of the valley on my own?
- {{HEALER}}: You walk it, you come back, he counts out your coin. I patch up whoever needs it.
- {{HERO}} {hope}: {{MENTOR}} keeps telling me I'm not ready. Watch me sign anyway!
