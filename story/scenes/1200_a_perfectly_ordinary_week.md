# Scene 1200: A Perfectly Ordinary Week

- type: narration
- mood: administrative

## Beat
Chapter opening, text over black. Four days of a city having an ordinary week, given as a company
circular: a notice of a special meeting with its date, hour, floor, quorum and items, then the
week around it, then the price of warmth, then the price of water four days out on the salt. No
irony in the voice. The last line is there for scale and the register should be very slightly
funny, which makes it worse.

## Dialogue
- Narrator {tense}: A special meeting of the board is given for the sixteenth of Dust. Four o'clock, sixth floor, quorum seven.
- Narrator: Items: the returns for Dust, Tier One ventilation, the western schedule, and any other business.
- Narrator: That week on Tier Three: the fair, a new piece at the theatre, and the school term opening on the fourteenth.
- Narrator: Warmth is metered at threepence the night this month. A Tier One wage buys twenty-six of the forty-five.
- Narrator: At Kettle, on the drove road, the cistern is nine marks a month between eleven people.
