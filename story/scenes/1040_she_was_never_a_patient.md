# Scene 1040: She Was Never A Patient

- type: talk
- location: a small office off the sanatorium corridor, a desk with a blotter and a tea tray, a wall of cream plaster, a long shelf of ledgers, one tall window with the blind half down, flat daylight from the left
- characters: Ovel, Lyra, Zeph, Bron
- mood: dread, then sorrow
- backdrop: 1035_the_wards:2

## Beat
Marsh pours tea and answers everything, because there is nothing here he has ever thought of as
irregular. The woman they are asking about was not a patient; there was nothing wrong with her; she
was a record, and the Works does not destroy records, and there was nowhere else to keep one. The
door had no lock and never had. She never asked to leave. He was fond of her and would like that
said out loud. He mentions the file name in passing, the way a man says a ward number, and it does
not touch him at all. Then Lyra asks him one question more than she needs to, and he answers that
too, and does not notice what it does to him, and offers more tea.

## Dialogue
- Ovel {dread}: The woman who left. Twelve years here, two years gone this Rime. There was nothing irregular about it at all.
- Ovel: She was never a patient. There was nothing wrong with her. She was a record.
- Zeph: A record.
- Ovel: Held record, Windrow. The fair copy. The Works does not destroy records, and there was nowhere else to keep her.
- Bron: Was the door locked?
- Ovel: It has no lock. It never had one. She walked out on a Tuesday and told the porter on her way past.
- Zeph: Did she ask to leave?
- Ovel: Not once in twelve years. I would have written to Vantage for her. I'd have written that same day.
- Ovel: I was fond of her. I should like that said out loud.
- Ovel: She wrote, you know. Forty books, front to back, in her own hand.
- Ovel: They are still on her shelf, where she left them.
- Lyra: Have you read them?
- Ovel: Certainly not. They're hers.
- Lyra {sorrow}: Doctor. Did anybody ever ask her what she wanted?
- Ovel: ...I don't believe anybody did. There was no form for it. Will you take more tea?
