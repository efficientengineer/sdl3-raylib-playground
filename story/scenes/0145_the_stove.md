# Scene 0145: The Stove

- type: talk
- location: the guild hall ground floor, bare sandstone walls, a black iron stove burning at the left, a pinned job board on the right, behind it a floor-to-ceiling wall of ruled columns of small handwriting, lamplit evening
- characters: Pip, Zeph, Tibb, Sela
- mood: easy

## Beat
Boots drying, the stove going, the other pair of hunters off duty. Tibb Orsk lost the burned-seal
bill by three seconds and is sour about it for one line and generous about it from the next.
Sela Marrin is drier than he is and better at the work and they both know it and neither of them
minds. They trade the worst bill each has cleared. Sela wins, easily, with a councillor, a cistern
and a cat, and the room is still laughing when Tibb asks for the only thing he wants out of it.

## Dialogue
- Tibb {hope}: Three seconds. I had my hand on the pin.
- Sela: You had your mouth open. Different thing.
- Tibb: Fine. Good money, and she was quicker. I hope it's horrible.
- Tibb: Worst I ever cleared: nine goats out of a flooded culvert above Windrow. In the dark.
- Sela: Eight. One of them was a dog.
- Tibb: It was a very goat-shaped dog.
- Sela: Councillor's cistern, second terrace. Something in the water eleven days. It was his cat.
- Sela: Alive, too. He wanted it out quietly and he wanted to keep the water. I made him pick.
- Pip: Which did he pick?
- Sela: He picked. It took him the whole afternoon.
- Zeph: I have it as the best thing said in this hall all year.
- Tibb: Whatever's down there — bring the seal back. I want to know who burned it.
