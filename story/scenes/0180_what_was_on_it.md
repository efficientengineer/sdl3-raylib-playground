# Scene 0180: What Was On Him

- type: panels
- map: high_pasture
- location: the {{HIGH_PASTURE}} in the last hour of the night and then the first grey light: trodden grass, the broken wall, a long shaggy animal lying dead in the open, sheep lying all around it and beginning to stand
- characters: {{HERO}}, {{HEALER}}, Distel, Garbe
- mood: quiet, wrong, and then morning
- staging: the dead animal lies across the middle of the screen with its head to the left. Distel kneels at its head on the left facing right. {{HERO}} stands beyond it on the right facing left, sword down. {{HEALER}} kneels between them with the lamp. Garbe comes up the slope from the bottom right
- dialogue_box: no

## Beat
The plan worked and then it stopped working: Distel held her {{GUARD_BEAST}} with her voice for a moment and he did not know her, and when he went for her {{HERO}} put the sword through him. His first kill is a mercy and he is not proud of it. **The player has already walked up to the body and found the ring of nine small healed holes behind the left ear themself** — this scene is the three of them with what the player found, and nobody explains it, because nobody on this hill knows. Then the sheep stand up all at once and walk, and {{SHEPHERD}} comes up the slope and pays {{HERO}} five coin, the first money he has ever earned, for a job that is finished on paper and not finished at all. A {{GUARD_BEAST}} does not forget its handler, so something was done to this one, and Distel is going to find out what. {{HEALER}} takes her with them without asking her.
