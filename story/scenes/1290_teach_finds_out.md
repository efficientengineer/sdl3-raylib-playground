# Scene 1290: Teach Finds Out

- location: a small Pale post room of grey brick at night, a black iron stove with a kettle, a plank bench under the window, a duty board of iron hooks on the wall, grey coats on pegs, lamplight from a bracket above
- characters: Hanna, Ivo
- mood: sorrow
- staging: Hanna stands in the doorway on the left of frame facing right. Ivo stands by the stove on the right facing left. The duty board with its hooks is on the wall behind him at the right, and nobody looks at it until he does

## Beat
No party member is present. The player watches. Kesk has come in off the Tellwater detail with her
cap in her hands to report a death and a suspended field commission, which is a form he has to
sign. He signs it correctly. Then he asks who was on the road, and she tells him, and he asks why a
boy on a two-day commission was standing in a road alone, and she says one word in a particular
tone and does not answer, because the answer is on a duty sheet on the hook behind him with one
line struck through and initialled in his own hand. He looks at it. Then he asks one more question,
about a stranger's last hour, and is told the one thing he wanted, and he says it is good, and sits
down on a bench too small for him with a cup he has not drunk from. He says nothing else about any
of it to anybody for a chapter and a half.

## Dialogue
- Hanna [1] {sorrow}: Sir. Off the Tellwater detail. One death on the road, and a field commission suspended.
- Ivo [2]: The suspension wants my signature. Bring it here.
- Ivo [3]: ...Who was on the road, sergeant.
- Hanna [3]: Constable Wen Arden, sir. Nineteen years old. Two-day commission.
- Ivo [3]: Why was a boy on a two-day commission standing in a road by himself.
- Hanna [4]: ...Sir.
- Ivo [5]: Was anybody with her at the end.
- Hanna [5]: Yes, sir. I wasn't there. I haven't asked for the details.
- Ivo [5]: That's good.
