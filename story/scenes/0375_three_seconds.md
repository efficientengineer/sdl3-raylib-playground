# Scene 0375: Three Seconds

- location: a stony road climbing a ridge above a sandstone town, dry-stone walls on both sides, a leaning stone mile post, the flooded hollow and low roofs below on the left, hard low sun from the right, late afternoon
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Ivo
- mood: confrontation, then nothing at all
- staging: The party stands at the bottom of the road on the left of frame facing right and uphill. Ivo comes down the road from the right and stops in front of Bron, facing left. The line of grey coats stays across the top of the road on the right

## Beat
The big man comes down the road himself, which nobody expected, and stops in front of Bron. He
does not look at the axe and he does not look at the others. He looks at Bron's face, and he keeps
looking at it for three full seconds, which is far too long, and there is nothing in his own face
while he does it. Bron looks back and has no idea what is happening and braces for a fight that
never arrives. Then the man says three words, and the line opens in the middle, and nobody is
arrested and nobody is charged. Bron is left standing in a road being angry at someone who has
just done him a kindness, and none of the four of them can say what any of it was.
