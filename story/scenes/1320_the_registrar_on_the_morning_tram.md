# Scene 1320: The Registrar on the Morning Tram

- type: talk
- location: a short stone halt above a terrace town where a single rail line ends at a buffer, an iron bench, a trolley of paper folder boxes, a lamp post, cold grey morning light from the right
- characters: Sefa, Zeph, Bron, Hesk, Pip, Ollo
- mood: tense

## Beat
Eight in the morning at the terrace halt. The Registrar of the Clement Works has been passed over
for the chair by a woman twenty-three years her junior, and she is not sulking; she is furious, and
in her the fury comes out entirely as efficiency. She steps down with a seal, a case of forms, a
trolley of boxes and a pistol she has never fired and does not intend to, offers her services in
the flattest possible terms, and inside the hour she will cut a lead seal that has held eleven
years, because she is entitled to and because nobody stops a woman holding the correct form. She
reorganises their kit without being asked, corrects their counts, tells Ollo his posting was closed
improperly and what it is worth, and calls her son Reader. Then Hesk says what she is, in four
words, in front of everybody, and Sefa corrects one of them, agrees with the rest, and writes it
down. Nobody welcomes her. Nobody objects either.

## Dialogue
- Sefa {tense}: I have been passed over. I am therefore available. Where are you going, and what needs signing?
- Bron: ...
- Sefa: Reader. Take the trolley. The ninth box goes on top or the lid springs.
- Zeph: Registrar.
- Zeph: The barred shelf at the back of the crating floor. The seal on it is the Registry's.
- Sefa: The office's, not mine, and that distinction has fed me for fourteen years. One form and forty minutes.
- Sefa: You are carrying four days of food and eleven days of rope. Reverse that before you go anywhere.
- Pip: ...Seven. We're seven now, are we.
- Sefa: Seven if somebody asks me. Nobody has. — Mr Marrick. Your posting.
- Ollo: Closed on the eleventh. There's nothing owing, I did write and ask.
- Sefa: Notice posted the second, postings closed the eleventh. Nine days. The instrument requires thirty.
- Sefa: You are owed four months at the Rim rate. I will get it. Do not thank me; it is a fault in their paper.
- Ollo: ...Right. Thank you.
- Hesk: You wrote Tellwater down.
- Sefa: ...Out. I wrote it out. Down implies there was something there to copy from.
- Sefa: Say it again the way you said it. — No. I have it.
- Hesk: Write it small.
