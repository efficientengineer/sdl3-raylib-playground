# Scene 1385: The Inner Doors

- location: two great cut-stone doors closed together across a squared opening, a bronze relief of plain interlocking rings on their faces, iron sconces on both jambs, a wide threshold step worn hollow, one guttering torch from the right
- characters: Maren, Bron
- mood: confront, then dread
- staging: The doors and the two hall-guards are at the right of frame, facing left. The party comes up the gallery from the left and faces right. Maren stands on the far side of a hand's width of gap, with her lamp, at the right

## Beat
They come up the last of the gallery at a run and they are minutes late. The doors are shut and the
guards are back across them and the party cannot pass, because the transcript is still folded in
Sefa's case and nobody has said it out loud yet and a guard does not wait while you find your page.
There is a hand's width of dark between the doors and Maren is on the other side of it with a lamp,
and she is not running, and she stays to answer. She tells them her plan, plainly, the way a person
gives testimony: she is going to put that night into every living person in the Basin at once, all
of it, so that it cannot be filed or seized or argued with afterwards. She apologises for it. She
says what it costs her and that she is glad of the cost. Bron asks the one question that matters,
which is arithmetic, and she does not have an answer, because nothing in this world is ever copied
and she has not got that far and cannot, and she says so. Hesk says the doorward thing. Then Maren
picks up the lamp and the light goes away down the corridor and the guards do not move.

## Dialogue
- Bron [1] {confront}: Maren!
- Maren [2]: I know. I'm sorry. They come back across it the moment you're through. They always do.
- Bron [3]: Open it.
- Maren [4]: I can't. I could say it again and they'd lift again, and I'd be this side and you would not.
- Maren [4]: Everybody in the Basin. All of it, into all of them, at once. The whole of that night and everyone in it.
- Maren [5] {dread}: Then it isn't a file. It's testimony. Nobody puts testimony in a drawer and nobody says it didn't happen.
- Maren [5]: It costs me all of it and I'm glad. I've carried a town about for two years and I'd like to set it down.
- Bron [5]: How many people can have it?
- Maren [5]: ...
- Maren [5]: I don't know. I'm sorry. I haven't got that far. I've had two years and I can't.
- Hesk [6]: That's no door of mine. Nine in my cliff, and every one shut from the inside. You've not asked them why.
- Maren [6]: No. I haven't.
