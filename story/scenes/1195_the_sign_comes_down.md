# Scene 1195: The Sign Comes Down

- location: the same swept square at dusk, the stone well black against the paving, lamps lit in three windows out of forty, empty benches along the walls, a dark ring tower above the roofs, cold green sky
- characters: Bron, Ollo
- mood: sorrow
- staging: The well and the leaning town board are centre-right of the square; the party stands on the left facing right toward them; the handcart and the road north are off to the right of frame. Everyone faces right

## Beat
The carts are gone, the town board is down and leaning against the well, and six people are
standing in a square built for four hundred. Tibb is taking the cart back to Sallowgate, which
means he is the one who will stand in front of the ledger wall and say why they came back short,
and he asks what to have written. Zeph has been through the roster and reads the authority line on
the decommission order out loud. Bron looks at the lit tiers of Vantage on the skyline and says
what they are doing next, and it is not a vow.
