# Scene 0107: The Sword

- map: hart_yard
- location: {{MENTOR}}'s yard at the end of a long day, the light going orange and low: the four training machines in the cut grass, the workbench under the eaves with the workshop book open on it, and the roofs of {{HOME_TOWN}} small down the hill behind
- characters: {{HERO}}, {{HEALER}}, {{MENTOR}}
- mood: worn to nothing, then wide awake
- staging: the last machine stands on the right of the screen with its weighted arm high. {{HERO}} faces it from the left. {{HEALER}} is on the stone wall at the far left facing right. {{MENTOR}} comes out of the workshop door at the back left and crosses to the right. Everything points at the machine until the sword comes out, and then everything points at the sword
- dialogue_box: no

## Beat
{{HERO}} has been at the last machine since before first light and the player has failed with him all day. His arm is finished; he cannot swing again, so for once he does not swing. He stands and watches the arm come, hears the counterweight drop, turns the swing aside with his stick — and the machine is wide open. {{HEALER}} has been calling that swing since he was twelve and goes quiet. {{MENTOR}}, who has watched him walk into that arm forty-one times, puts down his file, goes into the workshop and comes out with a sword he made the winter before last and has had finished on its hook ever since, waiting for exactly this. He says in plain words what it is for: a hunter who only attacks dies on the road, and this sword is what the clerk looks for. And the evening bell is already ringing down the hill, because the board has been open all day while they were up here.

## Dialogue
- {{HEALER}} [1] {tense}: That is the forty-first time. You have been at it since before it was light.
- {{HERO}}: Don't count today. Please don't count today.
- {{HEALER}}: Left. Left! It is always the left, {{HERO}}. I have said so since you were twelve.
- {{HERO}} [2] {sorrow}: My arm's gone. I can't swing at it again.
- {{HEALER}}: Then don't swing at it. Stand there and watch the arm come.
- {{HERO}}: There's a click. The weight drops, and then the arm comes.
- {{HERO}}: It's been clicking at me all day.
- {{HERO}} [3] {confront}: Come on, then. Come on!
- {{HEALER}} [4]: He turned it aside. He turned it aside and then he hit it.
- {{HEALER}}: I had a very good thing ready to say to you and now I am not saying it.
- {{HERO}}: Say it!
- {{HEALER}}: No. You would have it framed.
- {{MENTOR}} [5] {wonder}: Put the stick down. Both hands.
- {{HERO}}: Did you see what it did? The arm went nowhere!
- {{MENTOR}}: Saw it. Forty-one times I watched you walk into that arm.
- {{MENTOR}}: Take this. I made it the winter before last.
- {{HERO}}: The winter before last? You've had it the whole time?
- {{MENTOR}} [6]: Finished and on its hook. Waiting for you to stop swinging.
- {{HEALER}}: The grip is dark in the middle. He has had that down off the hook a hundred times.
- {{HERO}}: Why today? What changed?
- {{MENTOR}}: A hunter who only attacks dies out there. I was not burying {{FATHER}}'s son as well.
- {{MENTOR}} [7]: You're ready. A first sword comes from your teacher — show the clerk and he will let you sign.
- {{HERO}}: Sign? The sheets went up at dawn!
- {{HEALER}} {dread}: {{HERO}}. That is the evening bell. It started while you were shouting at a machine.
- {{HERO}} {confront}: The board closes on the last ring. Hold the gate open!
