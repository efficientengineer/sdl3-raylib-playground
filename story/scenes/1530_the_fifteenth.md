# Scene 1530: The Fifteenth

- type: talk
- location: an enormous wall of packed hair-fine filament, pale gold going white where it is densest, lying in strata like grain in wood, a stone ledge and iron rail along its foot, light coming only from the face
- characters: Bron, Maren
- mood: sorrow, then hope
- backdrop: 1525_the_fifteen_questions:4

## Beat
Two people on a stone ledge, everybody else out of earshot. He has worked down the list and is near
the end of it, and the last three are the ones nobody ever asked Lyra. He asks the thirteenth and
does not follow it up, because following it up is not on the list. He asks the fourteenth and gets
one word. Then he gets to fifteen, where the sheet he was taught off has a blank line and an
instruction, and he asks it anyway. She answers honestly, which means laying out her own plan and
then taking it to pieces herself, out loud, in four sentences, because nothing in this world is ever
copied and she has finally had the quiet to notice what that means. She is not talked out of
anything. She has not been able to follow one thought to the end in fourteen years and she has just
followed this one, and it took about eleven seconds. Then she says what she wants done, and the city
she names was evacuated yesterday.

## Dialogue
- Bron {sorrow}: Is there anything you would rather nobody knew?
- Maren: Yes. Four hundred somethings. I'm not telling you what they are.
- Bron: All right.
- Maren: ...You're not going to ask?
- Bron: It isn't one of the questions.
- Bron: Is there anything you want said out loud now, while there are people?
- Maren: Yes. Sorry — yes. That's the only thing I've been sure of since ninety-three.
- Bron: What do you want done?
- Maren: I meant to put it into everyone. All of it, into everybody, so that nobody could file it away.
- Maren: Nothing is ever copied. It would divide. A hundred thousand people with nine minutes each.
- Maren: Not one of them could put it together, and there'd be no whole one left anywhere after.
- Maren: I'd have burned the only record of four hundred and eleven people in order to publish it.
- Maren: I'm sorry. Fourteen years and I couldn't get to the end of that until just now.
- Maren {hope}: Say it once. Whole. Out loud, to people who keep a thing by saying it. Then I'd like to sleep.
- Bron: Who.
- Maren: Braid.
