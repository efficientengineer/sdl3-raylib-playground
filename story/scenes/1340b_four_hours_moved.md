# Scene 1340b: Four Hours — Moved

- type: talk
- optional: yes
- location: a laid-rail siding on white crust at dusk, a sledge with its sail furled on the spar, a small fire in a salt-brick ring, bedrolls laid out on the deck, firelight from the centre, cold sky
- characters: Threnn, Domma, Zeph, Pip, Cassa
- mood: tense

## Beat
**Branch: plays when the side contract "Walk the line" (`1335`) ends with the party moving the
chant-line onto the sledges before the passage is finished. One of two; see `1340a`.**

It is quick, it is lawful, nobody is hurt, the sledges run on time, and a line that has held its
record since year 700 goes out onto the seabed having lost four hours of it. Cassa has it in the
book as no delay, which is the true entry and is the worst thing in the scene. Nobody blames the
party, which is worse than being blamed; Threnn says so and would rather somebody were angry. The
four hours are gone and no later scene gets them back. Neither outcome of this contract is punished
and neither is praised. The last beat is the chant carrying on down the line ahead of them in the
dark, four hours shorter than it was.

## Dialogue
- Cassa {tense}: Lawful, fast, nobody hurt, the run on time, and I have it in the book as no delay.
- Cassa: They came off at the third hour of the passage. They begin tomorrow on the far side of it.
- Threnn: You did what the order allows. Nobody here is angry with you. I would rather somebody were.
- Domma {sorrow}: Thank you for walking the line. That was the work and you did the work. Go back before the cold.
- Zeph: ...
- Pip: It's still going. Listen. It's gone on down the line ahead of us.
