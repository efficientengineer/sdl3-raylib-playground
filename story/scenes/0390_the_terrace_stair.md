# Scene 0390: The Terrace Stair

- type: narration
- mood: time passing, downward

## Beat
Four hundred feet of cut steps down the face of the old shoreline terrace, and the Basin turned
inside out at the bottom of them. No character speaks. Nothing is promised and nothing is
restated.

## Dialogue
- Narrator {sorrow}: Four hundred feet of cut steps down a terrace face, with an iron rail bolted on in sections.
- Narrator: The Vess cut them, back when the water came up to the top of them.
- Narrator: Nobody who cut a step here expected the sea to leave.
- Narrator {wonder}: At the bottom: salt. Flat and white to the horizon, and two lines of laid rail running out into it.
- Narrator: From underneath, the Basin is not the same country at all.
