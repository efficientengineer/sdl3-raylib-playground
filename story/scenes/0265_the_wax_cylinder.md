# Scene 0265: The Wax Cylinder

- location: a single stone room with a plank bench of brass switchgear and dials, coiled wire on hooks, a wax cylinder machine with a brass horn on the table, dust in the air, lamplight from the left, no window
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip
- mood: delight, then something colder
- staging: The party work at the left of the stone room facing right. The instrument bench, the crates and the cylinder machine stand along the right wall, the brass horn turned left toward them. Everyone's attention goes right, at the horn

## Beat
A telegraph relay a hundred years older than the company, which the company has been using as a
store for sixty years: crates, stores lists, and a wax-cylinder recorder with a horn and a hand
crank. Zeph cranks it and a voice comes out — flat, slow, unhurried, nothing human in the cadence —
saying a short formal passage at a door-mouth, recorded by somebody standing close enough to be
killed who was not. He plays it again. And again. Sixty-one words, identical to the syllable, every
pause in the same place. Zeph is thrilled by the machine. Lyra understands what it means about the
thing in the corridor at Sallowgate, and it frightens her considerably more than the thing did.

## Dialogue
- Zeph [1] {wonder}: A relay hut. Telegraph line, lightning mast, and a company store sat inside it.
- Zeph [2]: Sixty years of stores lists on that shelf. The earliest hand on it is sixty years old.
- Pip [3]: And a box with a horn on it.
- Zeph [3]: A recorder. Somebody carried this up to a door-mouth and cranked it at what was standing there.
- Voice [4] {dread}: The post is taken. The watch is set at the sixth hour. The way below is barred, at this door and at every door.
- Voice [4]: Bar the way. Give warning. Do not pursue. Do not strike first.
- Voice [5]: And stand aside for a voice that knows the words. The post is held until the relief is spoken, and not one hour past it.
- Zeph [5]: ...Again.
- Zeph [6]: Three times. Sixty-one words. Every pause in the same place. Every breath.
- Lyra [7] {dread}: Then it isn't saying it. It's being played.
