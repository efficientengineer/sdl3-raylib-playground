# Scene 0487: Domma Sath

- location: a low back room behind a chant hall, pale violet-grey stone walls, woven mats laid in a ring on the floor, one small iron stove, a stack of clay bowls, warm stove light from the left, evening
- characters: Pip, Bron, Domma
- mood: sorrow
- staging: Domma sits at the centre-right of every panel, facing left. Her chant-line sits in a ring around her. The stove is at the left. Pip and Bron stand in the doorway at the left, facing right

## Beat
Domma Sath posted the bill with no seal on it because she has no seal and no more time. Eleven years
of saving bought one retail sleeve of one afternoon of her daughter's, who went into the water at
Wick, sold by the company that bought the afternoon from somebody who needed the money more. She
knew before they came in; the shop wrote to her. She takes the flat grey thread in both hands anyway
and holds it a while, because holding it was the whole of the plan. Her line keeps up a low hum
underneath her the entire time and never stops for a word of it. She does not weep, does not accuse
the company of anything, and thanks them at length and by name. Then she tells Pip to sit down and
eat. Pip does not look at the sleeve once.

## Dialogue
- Domma [1] {sorrow}: Come in, both of you. Sit where it is warm.
- Domma [2]: I know what it is. The shop wrote to me when it was found. Give it to me anyway.
- Domma [2]: Eleven years I put by for that. I would like to hold it for a while.
- Domma [3]: Thank you. Pip Rime, and Bron Sallow, of the Even Hand at Sallowgate. I have your names and I will keep them.
- Domma [3]: My daughter was Ansa Sath. She went into the water at Wick on the fourth of High.
- Pip [4]: ...
- Domma [4]: Sit down. Eat something. You came a long way with a small thing.
