# Scene 1170: The Road

- location: a straight dirt road leaving town between two low sandstone walls, wheel ruts, a leaning stone mile post, dry grass on the verges, flat overcast morning light, the town roofs low behind
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Arden
- mood: tense
- staging: The party comes up the road from the left of frame with a handcart, facing right. The thin line of grey-coated constables stands across the road on the right, facing left, with one figure standing forward of them in the middle of the road. The town is behind the party at the left

## Beat
A nineteen-year-old in a coat a size too big for him is alone in the middle of a road with a rifle
held wrong and an order to hold the road until his sergeant comes back. Bron is armed, walking,
and not going to stop, so Lyra does what she has always done and steps past him with her hands
open and empty and starts saying something ordinary and slow. At the far end of the street a
cart-horse bangs a trace against a shaft. The rifle goes off once. Nobody charges him. For a long
moment nothing else happens at all, and the road is very wide and very quiet.
