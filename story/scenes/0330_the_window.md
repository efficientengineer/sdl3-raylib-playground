# Scene 0330: The Window

- location: the swept square at night, the raised stone well black at its centre, one lit kitchen window across forty dark housefronts throwing lamplight over the frosted flagstones from the right, the dark ring tower above
- characters: Bron, Pip
- mood: quiet, warm
- staging: Bron stands alone in the middle of the square facing right toward the one lit window on the right-hand side. The inn door is behind him on the left. Everything in the scene points at the window

## Beat
A man alone in a cold square in front of the house he grew up in, and it is exactly right — the
window, the sill, the angle the light falls at — and he is happy about it. He says so out loud to
nobody. Another family is moving about behind the glass and he does not mind in the least; he
never expected the house to be kept empty for him. He is not uneasy, he does not doubt anything,
and nothing here troubles him. He stands in a wind he cannot feel. Pip comes to the inn door
behind him, watches for a while, says nothing at all, and goes back inside.
