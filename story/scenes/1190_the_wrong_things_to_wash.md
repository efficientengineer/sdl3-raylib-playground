# Scene 1190: The Wrong Things to Wash

- type: talk
- location: a small warm bakery, a brick oven arch at the back with its iron door open, a flour-dusted plank counter, a long wooden peel leaning by the jamb, firelight from the oven at the left, early morning
- characters: Pip, Ollo
- mood: sorrow

## Beat
After dark in the bakery. Pip has washed the same three trays four times and is going to do it
again. Ollo, who knew her for one day and therefore has no right to say anything about her, talks
instead about the oven: the flue, the brick, and the fact that the caretaker contract will let it
go cold, and that a cold oven takes two days to bring back. Pip makes a joke and it lands wrong
and she keeps going anyway. He sets a plate down beside her and says nothing about eating it. She
asks him one question about Denner and changes the subject before he can answer it, and he lets
her.

## Dialogue
- Pip {sorrow}: These are clean. I know they're clean. I'm doing them again.
- Ollo: The flue draws badly on the left. Always has. You want the door open a finger, not a hand.
- Ollo: The caretaker contract lets her go cold on the 12th. Two days to bring an oven back, if the brick's sound.
- Pip: Two days for a fire. I've known Zeph take longer than that to find his own boots.
- Pip: ...That was funnier in my head. They usually are. I'm going to keep going.
- Ollo: Keep going.
- Ollo: Tray's got a warp in it. That one. You can wash that one nine times and it'll still sit crooked.
- Pip: Then I'll do it nine times.
- Ollo: Right you are.
- Pip: Who was Denner?
- Ollo: I don't—
- Pip: The left of the flue, you said. Show me which brick.
- Ollo: This one. And the two under it. You'll feel where it's cold.
