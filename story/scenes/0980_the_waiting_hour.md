# Scene 0980: The Waiting Hour

- type: talk
- location: the curved inside of a toppled stone ring as a sloping floor and wall, fallen course blocks stacked at angles, salt drifted in the low side, a small fire of crate wood, lamps turned down, night
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip, Ket, Hesk
- backdrop: 0975_a_mile_of_rings:3
- mood: comfortable, and then not

## Beat
They cannot move until the cordon moves, so they sit in a dead machine two-thirds of the way in and
wait it out. Lamps down, a small fire of crate wood, salt drifted along the low side. Hesk, standing
inside something her people did not build, works out how she would have laid the courses and is
grudging and admiring in the same sentence. Zeph asks Ket a question about her own manufacture that
is appallingly rude, and she answers it in full, with figures, and is entirely unbothered, and Pip
nearly dies. Bron says almost nothing and cleans the axe. Then he cleans it again. Nobody remarks on
it. Lyra watches him do it and says nothing at all. Let this run long enough that the player relaxes.

## Dialogue
- Hesk {wonder}: I'd have laid these narrower at the shoulder. Less stone, same load. Whoever cut this had stone to spare.
- Hesk: And it's lain here eight hundred years on its side. So they were right, and I'm talking.
- Zeph: Keeper. May I ask you a rude question?
- Ket: You may ask any question. Rudeness is a thing you do. It is not a thing I receive.
- Zeph: How were you made?
- Pip: ZEPH.
- Ket: Grown. Four thousand of us in the last muster, over twenty years. I came up in the fourteenth of them.
- Ket: Six hundred and eleven are left. I have one thousand two hundred and sixty-one years, and I am not the oldest.
- Pip: I'd like it on record that I tried to stop this.
- Zeph: And the lights?
- Ket: The reserve. They go out in order, they do not come back, and that is the whole of what they are.
- Zeph: Thank you. I have wanted to ask that since Braid.
- Ket: I know. Ask sooner. There is a limit on how long I am available.
- Bron {tense}: Four hours before the near flank comes round the north end. Sleep if you can.
