# Scene 003: The Warning

- location: a cold cut-stone corridor deep underground, a great stone door standing open onto darkness, one guttering wall torch
- characters: Bron, Lyra
- mood: tense standoff
- staging: Bron and Lyra stand on the left side of the corridor facing right. The open door and the skeleton guard are on the right side, facing left toward them. Everyone's attention is on the guard

## Beat
An undead guard in ancient armor blocks the open doorway. It does not attack: it lowers its halberd across the gap and points back up the corridor, ordering them to leave. Bron is angry and wants to force his way through. Lyra is frightened, because she realises it is still keeping its oath and is warning them away from whatever is inside. Both of them keep their eyes on the guard the whole time.

- Lyra [2] {dread}: No. Look at it. It's pointing us back the way we came.
