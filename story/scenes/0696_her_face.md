# Scene 0696: Her Face

- location: a labour town of plank bunk rows on white salt crust at the foot of a broken stone ring stump, a bore head derrick above the roofs, a crawler depot fence, cold pink first light from the right
- characters: Bron, Lyra
- mood: sorrow, then hope
- staging: The dead ring stump and the stopped derrick stand on the right of every panel, behind the two of them. The open salt runs away to the left. Bron and Lyra stand side by side at the town edge facing left out at the salt; the other four wait a long way off to the left

## Beat
First light at the edge of the town, where the plank walls stop and the crust starts. The headframe
behind them is not turning. Lyra asks him to describe his mother's face, and he does it easily and
warmly, the way he does everything about home, and what comes out is a list of duties: a widow, one
son, a green apron, a kitchen, the autumn fair. There are no eyes in it. There is no mouth in it.
There is not one particular morning in it. He does not hear that. She asks again, for one thing that
was only hers, and he starts three times, and the third time he stops in the middle of a sentence
and stands there with his hand half up, puzzled, like a man who has mislaid a tool he was holding a
moment ago. He is not frightened. He thinks he is tired. Lyra does not comfort him, does not explain
and does not tell him what she thinks; she keeps the promise he asked of her on the salt below
Tellwater, in his own words and not one syllable more, and then she keeps the rest of it to herself.
The last thing he asks her for is a job, because that is where he puts everything, and she takes it.

## Dialogue
- Lyra [1] {sorrow}: Describe your mother's face to me.
- Bron [2]: A widow. One son. Green apron, flour to the elbow, and a kitchen too warm to stand in.
- Bron [3]: She took me to the autumn fair every year of my life and never once went on anything.
- Lyra [4]: That's what she was. I asked what she looked like.
- Bron [5]: Her hands were —
- Bron [5]: She used to say —
- Bron [5]: When she came in off the square she'd put her —
- Lyra [6]: You told me to say it straight away. Not kindly.
- Bron [7] {hope}: Ask me again tomorrow. And the day after that. Don't stop asking.
- Lyra [7]: I will.
