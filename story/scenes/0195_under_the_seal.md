# Scene 0195: Under the Seal

- type: talk
- location: the guild hall ground floor, bare sandstone walls, a black iron stove burning at the left, a pinned job board on the right, behind it a floor-to-ceiling wall of ruled columns of small handwriting, lamplit evening
- characters: Zeph, Bron, Tovin, Lyra, Pip
- mood: tense, then stubborn

## Beat
Mid-morning, the hall half empty, the stove going. Tovin pays them in full on the spot at triple
rate and enters it in her own hand, and it is more money than any of them has held at once, and
nobody is pleased. Zeph has been quiet for an hour, which is not like him. He takes the bill to the
open stove door and holds it up against the light: the burn only took the top layer of the paper,
and under it the seal is still pressed into the sheet, and enough of it survives to name a survey
office. Tovin says where that office is, and then says she is not posting a bill for it, because
nobody is paying them to be curious.

## Dialogue
- Tovin {tense}: Triple rate. Eleven marks and two. Less the tenth. Ten marks and one.
- Tovin: Entered. Signed. It is the largest single fee on that wall since ten ninety-eight.
- Pip: That's more than I have ever held.
- Pip: I don't want to hold it.
- Zeph: ...
- Zeph: Bron. Bring it here. Open the stove door.
- Zeph: The burn only took the top layer. Underneath, the seal is still pressed into the paper.
- Zeph: I have it as a survey office. There is a number, and there is a place, and the place is legible.
- Bron: Read it.
- Zeph: Windrow.
- Tovin: Two days up the rim road. I am not posting a bill for it.
- Tovin: No patron. That is the second Line. Nobody is paying you to be curious.
- Lyra: Then it's our own money and our own days.
- Bron {hope}: I'm going anyway.
