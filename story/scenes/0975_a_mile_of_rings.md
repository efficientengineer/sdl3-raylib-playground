# Scene 0975: A Mile of Rings

- location: the curved inside of a toppled stone ring as a sloping floor and wall, fallen course blocks stacked at angles, salt drifted in the low side, shafts of moonlight entering through joint gaps from above
- characters: Zeph, Pip, Ket
- mood: wonder, and give it air
- staging: The fallen column runs away to the right. The party comes in from the left and moves right, lamps up, and everything anyone looks at is up and to the right

## Beat
They drive the crawler in through the broken cap and get out, and the world changes shape. This was a
tower three hundred feet high; it went over around the year 300 and it is now a mile of stacked stone
rings lying on their sides, so that what were horizontal courses are walls and what was up is a mile
away down a tunnel. Salt has drifted through every joint in long white fans. There are birds in here.
In the ring cores there is machinery that has been dead so long it has gone to powder and blows off
under a finger. Nothing in here is awake and nothing is hostile. Ket walks the first two hundred feet
without saying one word, which from her is enormous, and the scene ends on her face and not on the
architecture.
