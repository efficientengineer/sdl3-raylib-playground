# Scene 1345: The Sealed Shelf

- location: a barred iron shelf-gate across a stone alcove at the back of a hall, a lead seal on its hasp, folder boxes behind the bars, a pair of shears on a stool, one lamp from the left, no daylight
- characters: Sefa, Zeph
- mood: wonder, then dread
- staging: The barred alcove is at the right of frame and everyone faces right toward it. Sefa stands nearest the gate, Zeph two paces behind her at the left, the rest of the party behind him. The lamp is on a trestle table at the left

## Beat
It takes a form, a signature, and forty seconds with a pair of shears. Behind eleven years of lead
is the hall-guard material Zeph was put out of this building for reading: the Thurn oath in three
recensions, a duty roster of a corps a thousand years dead, and the relief formula — the words a
relieving watch says when it takes a post over, which is why a guard stands aside for anybody who
can say them. Zeph reads it silently and is helplessly, completely delighted, and then ashamed of
being delighted, because the door opened on his mother's signature. Ket, who has heard the oath
spoken at four wells across nine hundred years, puts a finger on one word of the transcription and
says the word is wrong, and she is right, and nobody else in the room hears which word it was.
Then she reads the roster off and counts the posts, and the count is the last thing said.

## Dialogue
- Sefa [1] {wonder}: One form. One signature. A Registrar's authority is not a great mystery, it is simply nobody else's.
- Sefa [2]: Eleven years. Your first chanter asked four times, correctly each time. Nobody holding the shears was listening.
- Zeph [3]: ...
- Zeph [4]: Three recensions. The oath, a duty roster, and the relief — the words a watch says when it takes a post over.
- Zeph [4]: That is why they stand aside. It was never a password. It is a handover.
- Zeph [4]: I was nineteen and I read four pages of this and they put me on the step, and it is the best day of my — ...
- Ket [5] {dread}: That word is wrong. I have heard this spoken at four wells across nine hundred years. Change it.
- Ket [5]: The roster is complete. Thirty at the council door. Four on the stair. Two at the inner doors.
- Ket [5]: They are all still being kept. That is not a guess. That is what a roster is for.
