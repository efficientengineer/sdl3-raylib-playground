# Scene 0125: The Burned Seal

- location: the guild hall ground floor, bare sandstone walls, a black iron stove burning at the left, a pinned job board on the right, behind it a floor-to-ceiling wall of ruled columns of small handwriting, lamplit evening
- characters: Tovin, Bron
- mood: tense, level
- staging: Bron stands at the left of the board facing right. The board, its timber frame and the ledger wall behind it fill the right of every panel. Tovin stands in front of the board facing left. Everyone's attention is on the pinned sheet

## Beat
Supper is called and there is a new sheet on the board, chalked up off a paper that came in on the
tram. It says clear the entrance, ask no questions, at triple rate, and where the poster's seal
should be there is a ragged hole burned straight through. The board's own timber frame carries four
short rows of scorched marks burned into it, one to a side, older than the paint: the guild's four
Lines. Tovin says the third one out loud, as a fact, and then says she is posting the bill anyway
because the water is metered now and the hall needs the tenth. She is not tempting him. She tells
him to his face that it stinks and lets him choose. Tibb wants it and is three seconds slow.

## Dialogue

- Pip [2]: There's a hole burned in it.

- Tovin [4]: I am posting it. The water is metered now and the hall needs the tenth this winter.
