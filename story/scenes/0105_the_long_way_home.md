# Scene 0105: The Long Way Home

- type: talk
- location: the same stacked sandstone terraces at dusk, roof cisterns black against a cold green sky, lamps lit in a few windows, brass meter boxes bolted beside each cistern tap, smoke going straight up
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip
- mood: tired, fond

## Beat
Four people coming down the rim road at dusk at the end of a small job, with the proof of it in a
sack that nobody wants to hold. The whole argument is about the sack. Pip wants it known she has
carried it longest; Zeph corrects her by a distance he has actually measured; Lyra refuses to
adjudicate; Bron takes it off her without being asked, and that ends it. Below them Sallowgate is
lighting its windows and there is smoke going straight up off the guild hall stove.

## Dialogue
- Pip {hope}: Four miles I've had this sack. Four. And it's started leaking.
- Zeph: Four. It is two and a half. There are mile posts. Counting them is the whole point of them.
- Pip: Sack miles are longer. Ask anyone who's carrying a sack.
- Lyra: I am not settling this.
- Pip: You're a sister. Settling things is basically the job.
- Lyra: The job is sitting with the dying. You are all offensively alive.
- Pip: So what's it pay, this, after Tovin's tenth?
- Zeph: A bed each, and one of us eats. Assuming she accepts what is in the sack.
- Bron: Give it here.
- Pip: I've carried it the whole way.
- Bron: I know. Give it here.
- Pip {hope}: ...That's smoke off the stove. That's the stove. Come on.
