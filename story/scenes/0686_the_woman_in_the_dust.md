# Scene 0686: The Woman in the Dust

- location: a white salt street with tools and buckets dropped where people stood, a handcart standing loaded and unattended, dust hanging motionless in the air, hard flat afternoon light, no shadows
- characters: Zeph, Pip
- mood: dread
- staging: The bore gate is at the right of every panel and the care-house is at the left. The crowd moves from right to left across frame. Zeph and Pip stand at the left, facing right, and the single figure moves right, against everyone

## Beat
Every person in the yard is walking away from the bore. One person is walking toward it: a woman in a
long grey travelling coat with a canvas satchel and bare ungloved hands, unhurried, not pushing, her
lips moving because she is counting her paces. She stops at a mark in the dust, turns and looks back
the way she came, and writes something down against the satchel. Zeph sees her and says nothing for a
moment, because he is working out what is wrong with the picture, and what is wrong with the picture
is the direction. By the time Pip has turned round the crowd has closed. No face, no name, nothing
said. The player connects this to the card on the board at Braid, or does not.

## Dialogue
- Zeph [1] {dread}: Everybody here is walking away from that hole.
- Zeph [2]: Except one. And she is counting.
- Pip [3]: Counting what?
- Zeph [4]: Her own paces. From the gate. She stopped at a mark she had already put there.
- Pip [4]: Well, the card in Braid said don't talk to it, so that's us off the hook. Where'd she go?
