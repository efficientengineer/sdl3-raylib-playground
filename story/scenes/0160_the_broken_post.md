# Scene 0160: The Broken Post

- location: a cut-stone corridor collapsed at one end into a heap of squared blocks, a doorway that now opens on solid rubble, one guttering torch on the right wall, no daylight
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip
- mood: dread, then sudden
- staging: The party is on the left of the rubble facing right. The standing doorway and the two armoured figures are on the right, facing left toward the party. Everyone's attention goes right

## Beat
A junction whose roof came down centuries ago. A squared doorway still stands in the heap, and it
opens onto forty feet of fallen stone and nothing else. Two figures in ruined segmented armour
stand either side of it with rusted halberds, absolutely still, holding a way that stopped existing
before anyone alive was born. They do not move when the party come into the room. They do not move
when Zeph talks at them. They do not move when Lyra says to go round. There is no body inside the
armour and the empty wrist of one gauntlet shows it plainly. They move the instant Pip steps into
the doorway, because stepping into the gap is taking the way, and that is the one thing the oath
lets them stop. The only sound in the whole scene is armour.
