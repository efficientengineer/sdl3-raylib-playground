# Scene 0985: The Recitation

- location: the flat upper surface of a fallen stone ring section lying on salt, its edge worn round, a rusted iron cramp still in a joint, an open satchel set down on the stone, cold moonlight from the left
- characters: Maren, Bron, Hesk, Pip
- mood: a confrontation that does not happen
- staging: Maren sits on the right on a fallen ring section, facing left. The party comes in from the left and faces right, Bron in front of them. Everything anyone looks at is on the right

## Beat
The far end of the fallen column, open to the sky, moonlight, ring segments lying about like benches.
She is sitting on one of them with her boots off and her satchel open beside her, and when six armed
people come round the stone she does not stand up and she does not run. Bron comes in with the axe up
and asks her what she did to him. She tells him she did nothing, that she has been trying since
Emberrow to work out why she recognised him, and that she has it now and he will not like it. Then
she tells him the winter he got lost above Tellwater — in the first person, because it is hers. The
timing is right. The small thing in the middle is right. Her voice arrives doubled exactly once, on
the word *bird*, and then goes back to normal and neither she nor anyone else remarks on it. She does
not gloat, does not raise her voice, does not stand, and does not touch anybody. Bron does not put
the axe down and does not swing it. His face stops. She works the rest of it out while she is sitting
there, and apologises twice, and means it. Nobody explains anything. The last picture is the axe
still up and her two hands open on her knees, palms empty.

## Dialogue
- Maren [1] {confront}: I'm not running and I'm not going to fight you. My boots are off.
- Bron [2]: What did you do to me.
- Maren [3]: Nothing. I've never touched you. I've been trying to place you since Emberrow. I have it now.
- Maren [4] {sorrow}: I was nine. Two nights out in the drifts above the fold. The first night I wasn't cold. I was bored.
- Maren [5]: I counted stones. There was a dead bird in a drift on the way up, and its wings were open.
- Maren: Then it went dark, and I sat in a sheep scrape and shouted at a rock until my voice went.
- Bron [6]: Anyone could know that.
- Maren: Nobody knows that. You never told it. It's too small to tell.
- Maren: The day before that, my father put a dog down and I wouldn't speak to him at supper.
- Maren: The day after, I was sent back up with grease for a shepherd's boot, and I cried the whole way.
- Maren: Tell me either of those. Either one. Take your time.
- Bron [7]: ...
- Hesk [8]: Kell don't feel cold.
- Maren {sorrow}: Somebody took two hours off me in ninety-three and put them in a child. I didn't know. I'm sorry.
- Maren: I'm sorry. They kept me twelve years at Windrow. All of it written down. If there's more of you, it's there.
