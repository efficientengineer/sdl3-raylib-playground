# Scene 0345: The Water Over the Pasture

- location: a shallow flooded hollow east of town, still brown water over drowned fence lines, the broken roof of a shed above the surface, reeds at the edges, cold flat daylight from the right, no wind
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip
- mood: dread
- staging: The party stands on the near bank at the left of frame facing right out over the water. The shed roof and the leaning winch drum stand out in the water on the right. Everyone looks right

## Beat
A lake the wrong shape. The water does not move at all: no ripple, no birds, nothing working in
it. The cut hedge lines run straight down the bank and keep going underneath, and there is a mark
on the terrace wall that is level the whole way round, and nothing above it was ever wet. Zeph
sees what that means before anyone and gets no pleasure at all out of being right. Nobody in the
party has a word for what is sunk out there — they are looking at a company works of a kind none
of them has seen. Lyra says the quiet frightening thing in five words. Then Bron finds the door.

## Dialogue
- Pip [1] {dread}: It's a lake.
- Zeph [1]: It is not a lake. A lake has a shape. This has a line.
- Zeph [2]: The hedge goes in and keeps going. Somebody cut that hedge, and then it was under water.
- Zeph [3]: And there is a mark on the terrace, level the whole way round. Nothing above it was ever wet.
- Zeph [3]: Water does not fill a bowl and stop.
- Lyra [4] {dread}: Then somebody filled it.
- Bron [4]: ...There's a door.
