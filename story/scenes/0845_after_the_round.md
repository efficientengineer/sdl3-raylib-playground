# Scene 0845: After the Round

- type: talk
- optional: yes
- location: a town packed into a narrow ravine of stacked stone housefronts on both walls, rope-walks strung across the gap overhead, smoke lying in a flat layer below the roofline, low evening sun striking only the upper stones from the right
- characters: Bron, Hesk, Lyra, Pip, Zeph
- mood: sorrow

## Beat
The evening after the sixth door, on the wall above the town where the ravine road comes over the
lip. Somewhere to put it, and three ways it went. The game passes no judgement in any of them.
Nobody says they had no choice. If they paid, the party is broke and Hesk says nothing for the whole
scene and then says one sentence that is not thanks. If they refused, a company clerk reached the
hall before they did, and Bron has to say out loud what he has cost a woman with a stove and no
wood. If they did the job, nobody speaks for three boxes, and then Zeph starts working out how the
interest was calculated and cannot finish it, and Hesk stops him, and he stops. Nobody in the party
defends it and nobody condemns anybody else either.
