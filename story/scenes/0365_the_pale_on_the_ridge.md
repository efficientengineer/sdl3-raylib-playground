# Scene 0365: The Pale on the Ridge

- location: a stony road climbing a ridge above a sandstone town, dry-stone walls on both sides, a leaning stone mile post, the flooded hollow and low roofs below on the left, hard low sun from the right, late afternoon
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip, Ivo, Hanna
- mood: tense, then dread
- staging: The party is at the bottom of the road on the left of frame, facing right and uphill. The line of grey coats stands across the top of the road on the right, facing left and downhill. Everyone looks uphill to the right

## Beat
They come up off the flooded ground wet to the waist and the ridge is occupied. It is not a trap
and not an ambush: it is a formation standing in a road, in numbers so far in excess of four tired
hunters that the number is itself the message. Nobody draws anything and nobody raises a voice.
One constable at the end of the line is looking at the wall instead of the road and would plainly
rather be anywhere else. At the centre of it is a very big slow-moving man in a coat that has
never fit him, who says nothing at all, for the whole scene. A sergeant steps out of the line to
do the talking, and she is not enjoying it either.
