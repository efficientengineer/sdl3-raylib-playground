# Scene 1145: The Keeper Who Was Never Asked

- location: a stone platform at the foot of a tall dark ring tower, swept clean, a plain broom leaning against the stonework, a stack of stone chips squared off, lamplight from the square below at the left, night
- characters: Sem, Ket, Bron
- mood: dread
- staging: Ket and Bron come up the stair on the left of the platform and face right. Sem stands at the platform rail on the right, facing left toward them, with the dark tower behind her and the town below and behind the camera. Everyone faces the rail

## Beat
Ket climbs the platform because she wants to know the state of a dead well, and asks the keeper a
professional question, and gets a professional answer. Then Bron asks a second question and
Sem-Dree answers that one the same way: she was standing here on the 9th of Fallow, 1093, and she
watched all of it, and she has the hour, the count and the number of seconds. She is not bitter
and she is not tragic. She has told this to anyone who asked for fourteen years. Nobody has asked.
Nobody in the party says sorry, because there is nothing in what she says that invites it.

## Dialogue
- Ket [1] {dread}: Sem-Dree. What is the state of your well?
- Sem [2]: Dark since ten thirty-nine. The root shaft is sound. The third ring spalls on the north face, a hand's width a decade.
- Sem [2]: I sweep it. There is nothing else that can be done with it.
- Ket [3]: Two a side. Four of eight.
- Sem [3]: Four of eight. Correct.
- Bron [4]: You were up here. On the ninth of Fallow.
- Sem [5]: Twenty-two thirty-one. Nine seconds. The stillness went out across the pasture in a ring and stopped past the wall.
- Sem [5]: Nineteen people were standing in the street. None of them fell. Two walked to the end of the lane and stopped there.
- Sem [6]: A kettle boiled dry in the third house. Nobody came for it. I could hear it from here until about midnight.
- Sem [6]: Riders at twenty past one. Eleven constables. They carried the ones who would not walk.
- Bron [7]: Why did you never come down and tell somebody?
- Sem [7]: I have been asked for the meter readings twice. I have not been asked for anything else.
