# Scene 0970: Lamps in a Line

- location: a line of grey-coated figures spaced out across white salt with a parked crawler behind them, a folding table with a lamp and a clipboard, hard low sun from the right, long shadows, dust hanging
- characters: Orrin, Ivo, Arden, Ket
- mood: competent, patient, closing
- staging: The cordon and its lamps run left to right across the frame and the party watches it from up-screen right, looking left; the Ilder stump stands on the horizon at left. On the company's own deck Orrin stands at the left of Ivo and the two of them face each other

## Beat
Dusk on the pan. A cordon eleven miles wide, driven from both ends, crawlers on the flanks, a lamp
every two hundred yards, and it is being run properly, which is Cadder, and patiently, which is
Teach. Neither of them is cruel and neither of them is enjoying it; they are two good professionals
doing a job well, and one of them bought this party dinner five chapters ago. Somewhere down the line
a nineteen-year-old constable is holding a lamp out in front of him and being told to keep the
spacing. Ket counts the lamps and gives the number, the closing rate and the hours, and corrects her
own approximation halfway through. There is one piece of cover on the entire Flats, and the cordon
knows that too.
