# Scene 0815: Up That Street

- type: talk
- location: a steep street of dark stone housefronts with iron-railed steps, rope-walks strung overhead casting bars across the stones, a handcart of bark chocked at the kerb, hazy afternoon light from above, smoke at knee height
- characters: Pip, Bron, Lyra
- mood: easy, then not
- backdrop: 0810_smoke_in_the_cut:7

## Beat
The bottom of the main street, first afternoon. Bron does the sensible thing and starts carving the
town up between six people, and he gets as far as saying which end he will take himself before Pip
has the other end, and the yards, and the rope-walks, and three jokes stacked on top of them, which
for Pip is a scream. Bron is not clever about people but he is not stupid either. He hears something
he cannot name, and instead of asking her what it is he simply keeps the top of the street, which is
what she wanted and cannot say. Lyra stands two paces off and watches the whole thing happen and
does not say one word about it, then or ever.

## Dialogue
- Bron {hope}: Six of us, four hundred doors. We split it. I'll take the top of the street and work down —
- Pip: Bottom.
- Pip: Bottom of the street. And the yards. I'll do the yards, they like me in yards.
- Pip: And the rope-walks. Somebody's got to do the rope-walks and it can't be Ket, she'd measure them.
- Bron: ...
- Pip: I've a system, see. You knock, you look hungry, they talk. You knock and they think it's the rent.
- Pip: Hesk knocks and they move house.
- Bron: I'll take the top of the street.
- Pip: Grand. All yours.
- Pip: Mind the hill. It's a hill.
