# Scene 0395: Camped on the Salt

- type: talk
- location: a camp on white salt crust at the foot of a terrace wall, a small fire of scavenged timber, bedrolls laid on canvas, two lines of laid rail running west into the dark, cold clear night
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip
- mood: sorrow, then hope

## Beat
Four people who have lost the roads, the fee, the plate and the plot, eating a baker's bread on a
white plain at the bottom of the world. Pip does the inventory and it is very funny and there is
almost nothing in it. Zeph corrects the count upward, over-reaches, and concedes the correction on
the spot, which is the most Vess thing he does all act. Then Bron, who has been quiet since the
ridge road, asks Lyra for one thing, very quietly, in the middle of other business, and she
agrees, and nobody at the fire makes anything of it whatsoever. Nobody swears an oath. Nobody
makes a speech. Pip has the last word and it is the worst joke of the act.

## Dialogue
- Pip {sorrow}: Inventory. Eleven loaves, nine of them left, and I'm not sorry.
- Pip: No fee — it went in Windrow. No plate — the Pale have it and I've a receipt with a number on it.
- Pip: No road. And a lamp with about an hour in it. That's everything. That took four seconds.
- Zeph: Five things.
- Pip: I said everything, not — what five things?
- Zeph: A name. An office. A date. That is three you did not count.
- Pip: That's three, not five.
- Zeph: ...It is three. I have it as three. I was showing off.
- Lyra: We've eaten. Somebody bank that fire before it eats the rest of the timber.
- Bron: ...Lyra.
- Lyra: Mm.
- Bron: If I'm ever wrong about something I remember. Tell me.
- Lyra: All right.
- Bron: Straight away. Not kindly.
- Lyra: All right.
- Pip {hope}: Good news, anyway. Nobody can follow our tracks out here.
- Zeph: Everybody can follow our tracks out here. They are the only tracks.
- Pip: ...That was the joke.
