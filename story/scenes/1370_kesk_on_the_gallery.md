# Scene 1370: Kesk on the Gallery

- type: talk
- location: a broad stair of square-cut stone between dressed walls, iron handrails bolted on both sides, a landing with a lamp bracket, coils of cable stacked against the left wall, lamplight from above, no daylight
- characters: Hanna, Bron, Sefa, Hesk
- mood: tense
- backdrop: 1365_the_long_bore:1

## Beat
The last gate before the draw gear, and a Pale company on the stair above it under a sergeant who
was standing in a town three weeks ago and is not going to talk about it. She recognises Bron at
once. She tells them what she has been told to do, and asks them not to make her do it, and it is
not a threat and there is no edge in it anywhere. Sefa produces a form that is very nearly good
enough and Kesk reads it properly, right through, and says no, and is a little sorry about it.
Then she says the only personal thing she will ever say to Bron: two clauses, about a stove
inventory and where she was standing, given as fact and not as a defence. Bron says one word. Then
they fight, and she fights well, and nobody on either side wants to, and nobody says a name.

## Dialogue
- Hanna {tense}: Sergeant Kesk, Pale, holding this gate. You are Bron Sallow and there are seven of you, which I knew.
- Hanna: My orders are the draw gear stands and nobody goes past this landing. That is the whole of my orders.
- Hanna: I am asking you not to make me do it. That is not a threat. I have nothing to threaten you with.
- Bron: We're going down.
- Sefa: Before anybody does anything. Registry warrant of inspection, countersigned, current, and in order.
- Hanna: ...Read it out to me. No, I'll read it. Give it here.
- Hanna: It is in order. It inspects a store of records. This is a winch floor. It does not reach.
- Sefa: It reaches far enough that you had to read it twice.
- Hanna: It did. I'm sorry. The answer is still no.
- Hesk: You've thirty on that stair and four of us can climb it.
- Hanna: I have forty-one, and you are right, and that is not the difficulty either of us has.
- Hanna: I was at the south end of that town arguing about a stove, and I had left the road to a boy.
- Bron: Yes.
- Hanna: Right. Batons, not blades. Anybody who draws on the stair answers to me afterwards.
