# Scene 0520: A Meeting in the Yard

- location: a bare stone hall with a ring of nine worn stone benches, a shallow fire pit at the centre, nine iron door-keys hung in a row on the back wall, high slit windows, flat afternoon light from above
- characters: Orrin, Hesk, Bron
- mood: tense, cheerful, losing
- staging: The nine seated councillors fill the right and back of the hall. Orrin stands at the left of the fire pit facing right toward them. Hesk comes in from the left and goes to him. The party stand in the doorway at the far left. Everyone's attention is on Orrin

## Beat
A Kell in a company field coat with both sleeves cut off is running a meeting that nine people
refuse to look at. Orrin Cadder is warm, fast and funny, uses every councillor's first name, and has
brought a survey rule, a wage schedule and a tin whistle. He offers the Sixth Door on a lease, a
clinic, and wages through the winter, and nobody answers him, and the silence is the loudest thing
in the room. Hesk Ninefold crosses the floor, spits on his boot, and stands there. He looks down at
it, says something to her that is not an insult, finishes his meeting, and gets most of what he came
for, because nobody said no. Then he walks out past the party, delighted to see them.

## Dialogue
- Orrin [1] {tense}: Mave. Tarn. Ordo. I said it long in Green, so today I'll say it short.
- Orrin [2]: The Sixth Door, on a lease. Twenty years, wages through the winter, and a clinic in the top lane.
- Orrin [2]: Say no and I'll go. I'll be back in Thaw with less. That isn't a threat, that's the schedule.
- Bron [3]: ...Who's that?
- Orrin [4]: ...
- Orrin [5]: Hesk.
- Orrin [6]: Your father did that to me in this room in eighty-one. He had the better aim.
- Orrin [7] {confront}: Nobody's said no. I'll write it down as nobody said no, and I'll send the schedule Thursday.
- Orrin [7]: Four hunters and a keeper on my hill. Stay there. I'm two minutes.
