# Scene 1045: Over The Wall

- type: talk
- location: a long blank wall of grey brick topped with iron spikes, a single barred gate with a lamp bracket above it, a gravel road along its foot, cold blue night, no windows
- characters: Pip, Bron, Zeph, Hesk, Lyra, Ket
- mood: tense
- backdrop: 1010_the_wall:6

## Beat
Behind a laundry, in the dark, an hour before they go. This is Pip's scene and Pip has been
wrong-footed since the day room; she has the plan and it is a good plan, and she is delivering it
a beat slower than she has ever delivered anything, and twice she starts a sentence again. Nobody
mentions it. Nobody asks if she is all right, and she does not say. Hesk asks the sensible
questions about heights and about coming back out with something heavy. Zeph has the building's
layout room by room off the Registry index — the first time his mother's map has been any use at
all — and he does not make a joke about that, which is how everyone knows. Bron gives the order in
the flattest voice he has used in three chapters.

## Dialogue
- Pip {tense}: Right. The gate's watched on the hour and not between. The gate's no good to us anyway.
- Pip: Over the wall behind the coppers, across the drying green, in at the east end. The east end. That's where we go in.
- Hesk: How high.
- Pip: Nine feet. Eight and a bit where the buttress is.
- Hesk: And coming out. Same wall, in the dark, carrying a box.
- Pip: ...Same wall. We want the barrow out of the laundry yard.
- Zeph: File room is the far end of the north corridor. Two doors past the treatment hall, which we do not go into.
- Hesk: How do you have that?
- Zeph: It's listed as a company holding. Room by room. It was on the map.
- Hesk: Your mother's map.
- Zeph: Yes.
- Bron: Two o'clock. Go.
