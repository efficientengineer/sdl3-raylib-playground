# Scene 1050: The Room With The Window

- location: a small clean room with a cot, a plain chair, a stack of forty identical notebooks on a shelf, a window with no bars looking at a brick wall, one lamp set on the table, night
- characters: Lyra
- mood: quiet, empty
- staging: The party come in through the door at the left of frame and face right. The table, the chair and the window are on the right wall; the window is the thing everybody ends up looking at

## Beat
The first room the building gives them, and it is not a cell. A cot, a table, a chair, a kettle,
forty notebooks on a shelf, and a window that opens on the hill. The door has no lock and no plate
where a lock was taken off. What is in the room is wear: twelve years of one person sitting in the
same chair, the paint gone off the sill in the shape of two forearms, the hinge of the window clean
and bright from use. There is nothing to fight and nothing to solve and nothing to pick up. Lyra
sits down in the chair without thinking about it and stands up again fast. Nobody says the word
prisoner, because she was not one, exactly, and that is the worse thing. Play it slow.
