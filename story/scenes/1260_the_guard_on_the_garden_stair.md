# Scene 1260: The Guard on the Garden Stair

- type: talk
- location: a deep square stone shaft with a plain iron stair zigzagging up it, a lamp bracket at each landing, a coil of hose on a hook, a handrail bolted to cut stone, lamplight from above, night
- characters: Bron, Hesk, Ollo
- mood: tense

## Beat
Four house guards on the stair up from the machinery to the Cap, and they are not the Pale: private
staff, older, better paid, and extremely calm. The captain states the position, gives the time, and
says what he would prefer, which is that nobody goes over the rail. Ollo, on eleven years of pure
employee instinct, produces his posting badge and tries to explain himself, and it very nearly
works, and the captain is decent about it. Then the fight, which is short, and which is stopped in
the middle by a courteous voice from the top of the stair.

## Dialogue
- Captain {tense}: Far enough. You are above Tier Five, and there is no above Tier Five.
- Captain: The board sits in forty minutes. I'd sooner do this without anybody going over that rail.
- Bron: Agreed. Nobody goes over the rail.
- Ollo: Hold on — hold on, I've a badge. There. Brass, numbered. It's a posting badge.
- Captain: ...That's a Rim district badge.
- Ollo: Tellwater. Eleven years. The number's on the back, you can take it down.
- Captain: Your posting closed on the eleventh, Mr Marrick. I read the circulars. It's a quiet job, this one.
- Hesk: He nearly had you.
- Captain: He very nearly did. At the rail, then, and not on the steps.
- Crewe {confront}: Captain. Stand off, please. — And have somebody bring chairs up, and the lamps on in the walk.
