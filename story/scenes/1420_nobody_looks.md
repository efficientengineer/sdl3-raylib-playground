# Scene 1420: Nobody Looks

- type: talk
- location: a wide swept street of fitted stone flags between stone housefronts with square doorways, tall lamp columns at intervals, a stone water trough running with clear water, warm steady light from the columns above head height
- characters: Pip, Zeph, Ket, Hesk, Bron, Sefa, Ollo
- mood: dread
- backdrop: 1415_the_lit_street:1

## Beat
The first thirty seconds of the hub, and every tool the party has fails at once. Pip throws a joke
down the street and it lands on nobody, which frightens her more than the street does. Zeph counts
and corrects his mother twice without looking at her. Ket agrees with a figure nobody wanted
confirmed. Hesk will not say the name of the place, and then says it, and that is the whole scene.
Nobody deduces anything. Bron asks the only kind of question he ever asks, and it is factual, and it
stops the scene because there is no answer to it yet.

## Dialogue
- Pip {dread}: Watch this. — Excuse me! Which way to the nearest bar?
- Pip: ...That's a good one. That is one of my good ones.
- Sefa: A few hundred of them, I should think. It is a long street.
- Zeph: Three hundred and forty.
- Sefa: A few hundred, then.
- Zeph: Three hundred and forty. I counted the doorways, and then I counted the people standing in them.
- Ket: Correct. I have the same figure.
- Pip: Stop it. Both of you, stop doing that now.
- Hesk: ...
- Hesk {sorrow}: Thurn.
- Hesk: My grandmother told it like a story. It's a street. It's got a trough down the middle of it.
- Bron: How long have they been down here.
