# Scene 0481: The Standing Notices

- location: a dry stone gallery above the water line with company shelving bolted to old walls, paper folder boxes in ranks, a trolley, a hanging lamp in a wire cage, flat lamplight from above, no windows
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip
- mood: tense -> dread
- staging: the cork board is on the right wall of every panel. The stove and the door out are on the left. Bron, Lyra, Zeph and Pip stand on the left facing right at the board

## Beat
An ordinary office at night, over the stacks, with nobody in it and the stove banked. On the board: a
lost-property list, a duty rota, a metering notice, a handwritten reminder about the stove, and one
stiff printed card with a wax seal at its head. Pip reads the card out as a joke, because everything
here is a joke until it is not. Lyra reads it, and then reads it again, and stops joining in. The
card is pinned at the same height as the reminder about the stove and has been there long enough for
the cork to have gone pale around it. Nobody speculates. Nobody says the obvious thing. They stand in
a warm room dripping on the flags and then they go.

## Dialogue
- Pip [1] {tense}: Lost property. A rota. And somebody keeps leaving the stove in.
- Pip [2]: Recovery notice. Is that us? Have we got a notice?
- Lyra [3] {dread}: ...Read it again.
- Bron [4]: R, ten ninety-three, four. Standing recovery notice. All districts.
- Bron [4]: No description. No name. Three words of instruction.
- Bron [4]: The Fair Copy. Do not converse.
