# Prologue: Beneath the Hills

- type: narration
- mood: legend told over darkness

## Beat
Opening narration over black, in the manner of a 16-bit JRPG prologue. Sets up the Ancient
Halls, the oath of its guards, and the bill that brings the party there. The party is never
split: four hunters sign, at triple rate, and nobody asks why.

## Dialogue
- Narrator {wonder}: Long ago, the people of the hills built their halls downward, toward something that sang to them in the dark.
- Narrator {wonder}: They grew wise there. They grew rich. And then, in a single season, they sealed every door from the inside.
- Narrator {dread}: No one came out. The guards who held those doors swore an oath that outlasted their lives.
- Narrator {dread}: A thousand years later, a bill went up on the board in the water-house at Sallowgate.
- Narrator: Clear the entrance. Ask no questions.
- Narrator {tense}: Triple rate. Four hunters signed for it, and nobody in the hall asked why.
