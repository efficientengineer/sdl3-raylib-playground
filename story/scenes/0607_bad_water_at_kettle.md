# Scene 0607: Bad Water at Kettle

- type: talk
- location: three low plank buildings alone on white salt beside a rutted drove road, a raised stone cistern on timber legs between them, a brass meter box at the tap, a hitching rail, hard flat noon light
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip, Ket, Hesk
- mood: hope, then the arithmetic

## Beat
The bill Nessa Tull walked three days and paid tram fare to post in Windrow, cleared four chapters
later by six people who happened to be going past. The fouling is something dead wedged in the open
inflow channel and eleven residents' worth of standing water on top of it, and the work is an hour
of filth in a plank shed at noon, and Hesk is the only person present who enjoys it. There is a man
living in the cistern house and has been for nine years; Nessa mentions him the way she would
mention a leaning post. Nobody looks any further at him and nobody in the party thinks to. Then she
pays, on completion, out of her own purse, in coin she counts twice, and she is genuinely angry when
Bron tries to take less. The sting is the sum she does out loud afterwards, and the last thing she
says is about a letter.

## Dialogue
- Nessa {hope}: You'll be off the board, then. Three days I walked to put that up, and the tram fare on top of it.
- Bron: We're on it. Tell us what's wrong with the water.
- Nessa: If I knew that I'd have had it out myself and saved the fare.
- Hesk: Inflow's stopped, and stopped a while. Lid off. Give me a bar and a bucket and stand well back.
- Nessa: Mind the man, then. He sleeps in the cistern house. Nine years now. Washes in there. He's no trouble.
- Pip: Where's he from?
- Nessa: Asked him twice. He doesn't know and it doesn't bother him, so I left off asking.
- Nessa {sorrow}: Four marks. Stand still while I count it. I'm not counting it twice for you.
- Bron: Take two back. It was an hour.
- Nessa: Pay on completion. That's your line, not mine, and I'll not be the one who broke it. Four.
- Nessa: Four marks out of my box, for an hour. The meter has the eleven of us at nine marks a month, every month.
- Nessa: For water that fell out of the sky onto my own roof and went down my own channel into my own tank.
- Nessa: Here. Did the hall at Sallowgate ever get my second letter?
