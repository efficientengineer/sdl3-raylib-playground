# Scene 0285: The Dinner

- type: talk
- location: a low chop-house room of dark plank walls, one long scrubbed table down the middle crowded with dishes, a coal stove at the back, a steamed window, warm lamplight from a bracket at the left, night
- characters: Orrin, Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip
- mood: the best hour they have had in weeks, with a man who used them

## Beat
He orders for five people without asking anybody and gets it right. He admits the whole thing before
he is properly accused, and he is not sorry — he is pleased, and he says so, and it is somehow not
obnoxious. He pays: the bill goes back to the hall at full rate and the damages go on the table in
cash, the rig, the crew's four days, the door, because pay on completion is the Line and he knows
the Line better than they do. He asks Bron which hold he is out of, hears the answer, says "Ah" and
moves straight on. He tells a joke before the bad news and another one after it. And then he asks
the question nobody in this party ever answers, in this chapter or any other, and Pip changes the
subject as badly as it is possible to change a subject, and he lets her, because he has already won
and he likes them.

## Dialogue
- Orrin {hope}: Four chops, the barley soup, all the bread you've got, and beer for the tall one.
- Pip: You didn't ask what any of us wanted.
- Orrin: No. I know what's good here. Be cross with me after.
- Orrin: Right. Before anybody makes a speech at me: I posted it. Burned the seal myself, made a mess of it.
- Bron: You sent us in to trip the wards.
- Orrin: I sent *someone* in. You lot were cheap and very keen.
- Lyra: And if it had killed us?
- Orrin: It didn't. The door's open, nobody's dead, and I'm pleased. I'll not sit here pretending otherwise.
- Orrin: Bill's gone back to your hall at full rate. What's on this table is the rig, the crew's four days, and the door.
- Pip: ...Nobody's ever put money on a table at me before.
- Orrin: Pay on completion. I didn't write the Line, and I'll not be the man who breaks it in a chop-house.
- Orrin: Which hold are you out of, Bron?
- Bron: Tellwater. My mother was human.
- Orrin: Ah. — Have the pie. It's better than it looks, and that isn't difficult.
- Zeph: You are taking these hills apart in order.
- Orrin: We are. Nine doors on that face and I'd like all nine before I'm finished.
- Orrin {tense}: And you'd like us out of the hill country. Grand. Say it's done tomorrow.
- Orrin: Who pays the terraces this winter? Not you. You're leaving on Thursday.
- Lyra: ...
- Pip: What's in the pie?
- Orrin {hope}: That's the spirit. Anybody for pudding?
