# Scene 0105: The Wrist

- map: halm
- type: talk
- location: {{TOWN_HEALER}}'s room on the square in {{HOME_TOWN}}, late morning: one long bench against the wall for whoever is waiting, a shelf of stoppered jars, a basin, and {{HEALER}}'s bandage roll and satchel on the end of the bench where she works
- characters: {{HERO}}, {{HEALER}}, {{TOWN_HEALER}}
- mood: an ordinary morning, two neighbours, one sore wrist

## Beat
The second thing the player does, after training. {{HERO}} has come down the hill with a wrist he hurt
on the sixth practice post, to the room where {{HEALER}} works — she lives next door to {{MENTOR}} and
he has known her all his life. She heals it in front of the player, which is how healing is taught,
and then she is finished for the morning, which is how its limit is taught. {{TOWN_HEALER}} says the
plain thing about her that she will not say herself. Then {{HEALER}} sends him at the board, because
the guild put its job sheets up this morning and she wants him to go and look.

## Dialogue
- {{HEALER}} {wonder}: Sit on the bench. What have you done to it this time?
- {{HERO}}: I hit the sixth post wrong and the post won. It's my wrist.
- {{HEALER}}: Let go of it, then. I cannot look at a wrist you are holding.
- {{HERO}}: It isn't broken. {{MENTOR}} says it isn't broken.
- {{HEALER}}: {{MENTOR}} says that about his own knee. Hold still.
- {{HERO}}: That's warm — what are you doing to it?
- {{HEALER}}: Closing it. A small hurt I can close in a minute.
- {{HERO}}: And a big one?
- {{HEALER}}: A big one takes everything I have and then I have to stop. There. Turn it.
- {{HERO}} {hope}: It turns! Do the other one, it's been stiff since the spring —
- {{HEALER}}: No. That is me finished until this evening.
- {{HERO}}: One wrist and you're finished for the day?
- {{TOWN_HEALER}}: She is still building her strength. That comes with the years and the work.
- {{HERO}}: Then why isn't she a full healer yet? She's been at this since I was small.
- {{TOWN_HEALER}}: I give full standing after a season of work away from home. She has never had one.
- {{HERO}}: A season away? {{HOME_TOWN}} is the only place she has ever lived!
- {{TOWN_HEALER}}: I know. She was nine when she came to me, and she has not been past the bridge since.
- {{HERO}}: Nine? Why did you take her at nine?
- {{TOWN_HEALER}} {sorrow}: Her mother and father took a sickness that winter. She tried to heal them herself.
- {{HERO}}: At nine years old?
- {{TOWN_HEALER}}: She was not strong enough. I saw what she had managed at that age and I went and got her.
- {{HEALER}}: {{TOWN_HEALER}}. He came in here about a wrist.
- {{TOWN_HEALER}}: So he did. Off you go, the pair of you.
- {{HERO}} {tense}: {{MENTOR}} told me twice this morning I'm not ready.
- {{HEALER}}: He says that to me as well, and I am six years older than you.
- {{HEALER}}: The guild put its job sheets up on the board this morning. Go and look at them.
- {{HERO}}: Job sheets? Could I sign for one?
- {{HEALER}}: Sign for whatever the clerk has left. The board closes on the last ring of the evening bell.
- {{HERO}} {hope}: Then I'm going down there before he takes them all down!
- {{HEALER}}: Take the bandages I rolled you. You will want them before the week is out.
