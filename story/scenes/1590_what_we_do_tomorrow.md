# Scene 1590: What We Do Tomorrow

- type: talk
- location: a short plank platform on a cut-stone ledge beside laid rail, an iron lamp stanchion, a stacked pile of sleepers and spikes, a squared ancient doorway in the wall behind, lamplight from above, no daylight
- characters: Bron, Pip, Ket, Ollo, Hesk, Sefa, Tovin, Zeph
- mood: hope, then sorrow, then hope

## Beat
The quiet the player has earned. The party comes apart on a rail platform, on purpose, in the open,
while other people carry crates and rope past them and nobody stops to make an occasion of it. Ket
asks Tovin-Caleth to come and bring Vintry up with her — two Onn of the same muster, six lights lit
between them and no more coming — and Tovin says no, because she has a wall half copied and does not
leave a thing part done, and neither of them is wrong and neither of them makes a speech about it.
Ollo is going back up to Tellwater, where sixty people stopped being employees on the eleventh of
Dust and somebody has to be there who knows where the flour is kept. Zeph is on the platform boards
because he still cannot stand. Pip says nothing whatever about staying and picks up Bron's pack as
well as her own. Nobody says goodbye in words, and the last thing anyone says is about work.

## Dialogue
- Ket {hope}: Tovin-Caleth. Vintry is eleven days of work and I have the sheet for it. Come and do it with me.
- Tovin: No.
- Tovin: Nine hundred and six lines copied. I do not leave a wall part done. You know that about me.
- Ket: ...Correct.
- Ket: I am at two. You are at four. That is six between us and neither of us gets another one.
- Tovin: Then go and spend two of them on a tower. I am spending four on a wall.
- Ollo {sorrow}: I'm going up to Tellwater. Sixty of them stopped being employed by anybody on the eleventh.
- Ollo: Somebody has to be there who knows where the flour's kept. That's me. It's been me eleven years.
- Zeph: ...Has anybody seen my legs. I had them at the start.
- Pip: In the pack. I've got the pack.
- Pip: I've got both packs. Don't make a thing of it.
- Bron: Pip.
- Pip: Don't.
- Bron {hope}: Hesk. What's the job tomorrow?
- Hesk: Same as today.
- Hesk: Hold that. I've the door to get.
