# Scene 0525: Who Pays the Terraces

- type: talk
- location: stepped barley terraces walled in dry stone, half of them bare brown earth, hand-dug water channels and stacked hoes, a low stone hut at the field corner, hard clear morning light from the left
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip, Orrin
- mood: friendly, unanswerable
- backdrop: 0520_a_meeting_in_the_yard:7

## Beat
He knows exactly who they are — four hunters who cleared an entrance for him in Turn and then would
not stay cleared — and he is delighted. He apologises for the door again, asks whether the hall paid
out, and offers them work at a rate none of them has ever been paid. He is not fishing and he is
not sorry about the Sixth Door. When Bron says the Works should be out of the hills, Cadder agrees
it would be grand and asks who pays the terraces this winter, and then answers it himself. Nobody
has anything. He goes down the path playing four bars badly, and it is funny, and it fixes nothing.

## Dialogue
- Orrin {hope}: Bron. Zeph. Pip. And — Lara.
- Lyra: Lyra.
- Orrin: Did the hall pay out? Eleven marks and two, less the tenth. I costed that bill myself.
- Bron: You burned your own seal off it.
- Orrin: I did, and I made a mess of it. I'm sorry about the door. I'll go on being sorry, it costs me nothing.
- Orrin: Come and work for me. Twelve marks a week. Each. I'll pay your hall its tenth on top of it.
- Pip: ...Each?
- Orrin: Each. Boots found.
- Bron: The Works should be out of these hills.
- Orrin: Grand. Say it's done and we're gone by Thaw. Who pays the terraces this winter?
- Orrin: Not you. You're leaving on Thursday.
- Bron: ...
- Lyra {tense}: When they sit there and don't answer you, what do you write down?
- Orrin: That they didn't answer.
- Zeph: ...He is whistling. Four bars, and two of them are wrong.
