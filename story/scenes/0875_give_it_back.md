# Scene 0875: Give It Back

- location: a low vaulted stone cellar with a bedroll and a crate table, a survey satchel open on it, an oil lamp and a tin cup, a drain grate in the floor, warm lamplight from the left, no daylight
- characters: Maren, Ross
- mood: tense, then sorrow
- staging: Ross sits at the left of frame facing right; Maren sits at the right facing left. The stair and the street are away to the left, and everything in the last page moves left and up. The lamp is on the crate between them the whole time

## Beat
Ross Kettle came down a stair in the dark to ask a stranger for his own life, and she makes him say
back to her what he is actually asking for before she will touch him, because everybody hears it
once and does not hear it. The years are gone. He does not get to have lived them. What he gets is
all of it at once, tonight, at full strength. He says yes, because a man who cannot remember his own
childhood has wanted this for fourteen years and has thought about nothing else since Tuesday. She
takes both his hands, ungloved, skin on skin, and for about a minute two people sit on crates in a
cellar holding hands and there is absolutely nothing to see. Then a fifty-eight-year-old tanner
finds out, in one piece, that he had a wife before Dalla, and a daughter, and a town, and a night,
and he does not scream. He gets up, goes past everybody as though they were furniture, walks out
into the street, and starts politely asking people where his house is.
