# Scene 0605: The Crossing

- type: narration
- mood: two days on the crust

## Beat
Two days down off the hill paths and out onto the Flats. White crust that takes a boot print and
holds it until the wind comes for it, plate tracks a yard wide, a drove road, a metered cistern at
Kettle, and nothing else at all between here and the horizon in any direction. At the end of it, a
town that is one hole with five thousand eight hundred people arranged around it. The last thing the
narration gives is a figure off the board at the gate, and it gives it flat and does not compare it
to anything.

## Dialogue
- Narrator {wonder}: Two days west off the hill paths, on crust that takes a boot print and keeps it until the wind comes.
- Narrator: Plate tracks a yard wide. A drove road with dung dried in the ruts. A metered cistern at Kettle.
- Narrator: Nothing else on it. No shade at any hour, and no water that does not belong to somebody.
- Narrator {tense}: Saltmouth at the end of it. Five thousand eight hundred people, one bore, one payday.
- Narrator: Everything in the town comes up the same hole: the wage, the water, the work, and the reason to stay.
- Narrator: On the board at the gate, the depth of Extraction Bore Four. Nine hundred feet.
