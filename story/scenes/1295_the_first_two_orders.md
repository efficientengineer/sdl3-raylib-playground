# Scene 1295: The First Two Orders

- type: talk
- location: a walled roof garden on the topmost shelf, clipped hedges and gravel walks, warm pipes running under iron grilles in the paths, a stone bench facing the drop, lamps on posts, the sky over the parapet going grey before dawn
- characters: Bron, Zeph, Ket, Hesk, Ollo
- mood: tense
- backdrop: 1265_the_warm_gardens:1

## Beat
Dawn on the top tier with a copy of the night's minutes that Zeph took off a trolley and does not
intend to give back. Two resolutions carried under the new chair, to go out as orders on the
twentieth: evacuate Braid, and raise the Long Bore to full. The first is the most humane document
the company has produced in sixty years and the party knows it, which is what makes the second
intolerable. Ket does the arithmetic out loud, and it is the first time in the game that a number
frightens her. Hesk goes very quiet and then says the doorward thing. Ollo asks the practical
question and Zeph answers it, and that is the chapter out. Nobody says a word about the woman who
signed either of them.

## Dialogue
- Zeph {tense}: The minutes of the special meeting. Off a trolley. I have no intention of giving them back.
- Zeph: Two resolutions carried under the new chair. They go out as orders, dated the twentieth.
- Zeph: The first evacuates Braid on the company's account. Sledges, a schedule, a hardship payment, a named officer.
- Hesk: That's the right order.
- Zeph: It is. It's the best piece of paper anybody in that building has written in sixty years.
- Zeph: The second raises the Long Bore to full extraction.
- Ket: ...
- Ket: At full, the Long Bore takes the branch under the Rim. Not a part of it. The branch.
- Ket: Every sealed door in the hills stands on that branch. I would like one of you to tell me I have it wrong.
- Hesk: ...
- Hesk: Ninefold's nine sit on it. And Dov's crew will be down the hole when it turns.
- Ollo: Right. How do we get to Braid ahead of four thousand people leaving it?
- Zeph: We don't go to Braid. We go to Saltmouth and take the rail up, and we leave as soon as the crawler's fuelled.
