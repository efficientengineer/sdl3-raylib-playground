# Scene 0180: The Tent

- location: a squared cut-stone chamber with a canvas company tent pitched inside it, a folding table with a lamp and a clipboard, crates stencilled without words, a stack of survey rods, lamplight from the table, no daylight
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip, Emmet
- mood: anticlimax, then cold
- staging: The party come out of the side passage at the left and face right. The tent, the folding table and Emmet are on the right, Emmet facing left toward them. Everyone's attention goes right

## Beat
They come round the guard with weapons up, ready for whatever a thousand-year oath was holding
back, and what is in the chamber is a man at a folding table doing paperwork by lamplight. The
ancient room has already been stripped, measured, numbered and staked out around him. Emmet Sark
looks up with mild annoyance, not fear, because people are not supposed to come in that way. He is
not a trap and not an ambush: he is a duty clerk on a night shift who would like his kettle to
boil, and he would rather like to know where their entry chit is. Bron's anger has nowhere to
land. Lyra is the one who works out what they have just been paid triple to do.
