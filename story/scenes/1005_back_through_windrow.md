# Scene 1005: Back Through Windrow

- type: narration
- mood: flat, administrative

## Beat
Chapter opening, text over black. The town they slept in once, given its real figures. No irony
and no threat: a relay town, its employer, and one fact the party is carrying that the town does
not know it is standing next to. It ends on the tower, because nobody in Windrow looks at that
either.

## Dialogue
- Narrator {tense}: They came through Windrow in the second week. A relay station, a boarding house, a man on a bench.
- Narrator: Three thousand four hundred people. One tram yard. Four laundries, and a wall at the top of the town.
- Narrator: Behind the wall is the Clement Works sanatorium. It has stood there longer than anybody now living in the town.
- Narrator: Nobody in Windrow calls it anything but a good employer. It pays on the first and it has never once been late.
- Narrator {dread}: A woman was kept behind that wall for twelve years, and walked out of it through a door nobody had locked.
- Narrator: Over the roofs stands a tower three hundred feet high that has not turned since the year 944. Nobody looks at that either.
