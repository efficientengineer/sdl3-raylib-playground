# Scene 0545: The Cold Walk

- location: the broken lower rings of a stone tower standing eight feet high on a bare ridge, its interior floor filled with stacked stone offerings, dry grass at the base, hard low sun from the left, cold dusk
- characters: Bron, Hesk, Pip
- mood: wonder, then something wrong
- staging: The children, the lamp and the broken stone rings are on the right. Hesk stands at the left of the path with Bron beside her on her right, both facing right. Pip is further back down the path at the left

## Beat
The party comes up onto the ridge at dusk and there are two six-year-olds out on it in the wind with
one adult and one lamp, being walked in the cold on purpose. Hesk explains it the way she explains
everything: flat, and without offering it as remarkable. Kell cannot feel cold coming, so at six you
are taken out into it and taught to count your fingers and go in when you cannot make the number.
Bron says he was never taken, and tells her about the winter he got lost above Tellwater, and he is
easy and faintly pleased with the telling. Hesk looks at him and says nothing, and goes on saying
nothing, and then goes down the path. Bron is not troubled. Pip, further back, is.

## Dialogue
- Hesk [1] {wonder}: Caleth's been dead since fifty-eight. People still carry stones up to it.
- Hesk [2]: That one's six. Hand up, count the fingers. When she can't make the number she goes in.
- Hesk [3]: We can't feel it coming. Nothing in us says cold. So we're taught a number instead.
- Bron [4]: Nobody ever took me out.
- Bron [5]: I got lost above Tellwater at nine. Two nights out, in the drifts above the fold.
- Bron [5]: Second night I was shaking too hard to hold a stone. Shook the whole way down after she found me.
- Hesk [6] {dread}: ...
- Bron [6]: Say the rest of it.
- Hesk [7]: I've nothing to say. Eat your food.
- Pip [7]: ...Bron. Come down. I saved you the good end of the bread.
