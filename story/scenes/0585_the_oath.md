# Scene 0585: The Oath

- type: talk
- location: a wide cut-stone landing with a barred stone doorway at the back, three empty armour niches in the left wall, fallen iron segments on the floor, a guttering torch in a bracket on the right, no daylight
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Ket, Hesk
- mood: dread, then something like wonder

## Beat
Three niches, three sets of segments on the floor, and nothing at all inside any of them. Hesk gives
the doorwards' explanation, and it is not supernatural and she does not find it interesting. The
corps said one oath four times a day for years in a place that keeps whatever is said often enough,
and the oath is still being said, and the only thing left to say it in is the armour. Zeph connects
it to the wax cylinder at the Windrow relay station, where the words came out identical every time,
and is thrilled, and Hesk puts him down flat. Bron asks the practical question. Hesk has one line of
the relief formula, it is the back half of something, and it opens a side passage and nothing else.

## Dialogue
- Hesk {dread}: Three niches. Three sets of segments on the floor. There was a post here and it has come apart.
- Zeph: There is nothing inside the armour. I checked the wrist. I always check the wrist now.
- Hesk: There never was. The corps said one oath. Four times a day, for years, in a place that keeps what's said often enough.
- Hesk: The men are a thousand years gone. The oath is still being said. The only thing left to say it in is the armour.
- Zeph: The cylinder at the Windrow relay station. It played a guard's oath, identical every time. Every pause. Every breath.
- Zeph: That is a thousand-year-old recording, and it stands up, and it holds a door. Do you know what that is worth to—
- Hesk: It isn't interesting. It's a promise that outlived the men who made it. Those are two different things.
- Lyra: What are its terms?
- Hesk: Bar the way. Give warning. Do not pursue. Do not strike first.
- Hesk: And stand aside for a voice that knows the words.
- Bron: So the words can be learned.
- Hesk: They could be. They are not. What I have is one line of the relief, and it is mangled.
- Hesk: The post is relieved. The way is spoken for. Stand aside and let them by.
- Hesk: That's the back half of something. Nobody living has the front. It unbars a side passage and nothing more.
- Ket: Say it again. Slower. I want to have it right.
