# Scene 0110: The Board

- map: halm
- location: the guild hall of {{HOME_TOWN}}: a long low stone room with a plank counter, a double doorway standing open on an evening street, and a bare job board on the wall behind the counter
- characters: {{HERO}}, Guildclerk
- mood: out of breath, then decided
- staging: {{HERO}} stands at the counter on the left of the screen facing right. The Guildclerk stands behind the counter on the right facing left. The job board with its one last sheet is on the wall behind him, on the right. Both of them keep coming back to that sheet
- dialogue_box: no

## Beat
The bell has stopped. {{HERO}} has run the whole width of the town from the hill with an hour-old sword on his back and come in on the last ring to find the board stripped: the sheets went up at dawn and went all day while he was in the yard, and {{RIVAL}} signed the good one at noon. The clerk is closing the ledger and is not hurrying for anybody — until he sees the sword, and then he stops arguing, because that is the whole of the rule he recited yesterday. One sheet is left, a water run no one wanted. When {{HERO}} hears that the job came in yesterday in the bundle he carried himself, that {{MENTOR}} is paying the thirty coin out of his own pocket, and what the road is, he takes it standing at the counter.

## Dialogue
- Clerk [1] {tense}: You made it on the last ring of the bell.
- {{HERO}} [2]: The last ring? I came the whole width of the town for that!
- Clerk: Then look at the board. There is one job sheet left.
- {{HERO}}: One? The board was full of job sheets at dawn!
- Clerk: The others went all day. {{RIVAL}} signed for the {{COAST_ROAD}} job at noon.
- {{HERO}} [3]: At noon I was in the yard with a stick in my hand!
- Clerk: You were. You have a sword on your back now.
- {{HERO}} [4] {confront}: {{MENTOR}} put it in my hands an hour ago. He said to show you.
- Clerk {wonder}: Then I will write your name. The last job sheet is the water run.
- {{HERO}}: What does the water run pay?
- Clerk: Thirty coin. Two days out and two days back.
- {{HERO}}: Out to where?
- Clerk: Out to the {{STAIR}}. A jar of our well water goes there sealed, every year.
- {{HERO}}: What for?
- Clerk: No one has ever told me. It goes all the same.
- {{HERO}} [5]: Who put it on the board?
- Clerk: You did. It came in yesterday, with {{MENTOR}}'s post.
- {{HERO}}: I carried it in? I stood right here while you opened it!
- Clerk: You did.
- {{HERO}}: {{MENTOR}}? But the carters take the jar out. They take it every year.
- Clerk {dread}: There is no convoy this year. Every carter in the valley has walked west.
- {{HERO}}: Then who is paying the thirty coin?
- Clerk: {{MENTOR}} is. His own money.
- {{HERO}} [6] {confront}: A man who walks that road alone dies out there.
- {{HERO}}: He was my father.
- {{HERO}}: I'll take it! Write my name down now!
