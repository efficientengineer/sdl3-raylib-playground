# Scene 1105: The Postings Come Down

- location: a neat sandstone town square with a raised stone well at its centre, swept flagstones, whitewashed housefronts with green shutters, a painted board over the bakery with no words, bright clean midmorning sun from the left
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Hesk
- mood: tense, orderly, wrong
- staging: The party comes in from the left of frame and faces right into the square. The raised well and the dark ring tower stand centre-right; the bakery front is on the left; the road out runs off to the right. Everyone's attention is on the square

## Beat
The town that welcomed Bron wrong five months ago is coming apart politely. Employees are lifting
their own posting notices off their own front doors and carrying them to a clerk with a tally
board, and queueing to do it. Nobody is crying and nobody is angry. Bron came here wanting
something to hit and there is nothing in the square to hit; Hesk is the only one who asks a useful
question, which is who owns four hundred houses on the twelfth. Above the roofs the dark ring
tower stands with one small figure on its platform, as it has every day for fourteen years, and
nobody looks up at her.

## Dialogue
- Bron [1] {tense}: Doors open. Carts half loaded. Nobody's shouting.
- Pip [1]: They're queueing. Nobody queues to be put out of their own house.
- Lyra [2] {sorrow}: That's his own notice, off his own door. He's carrying it over to be ticked off a board.
- Hesk [3]: Four hundred houses here. Sixty people. The other three hundred and forty were never anybody's.
- Hesk [4]: So who holds them on the twelfth. Nobody in this square has asked that once.
- Bron [4]: Ask the clerk.
- Hesk [4]: I did. She's writing it down to find out.
- Bron [6]: Somebody here has to be in the wrong. I can't find them.
