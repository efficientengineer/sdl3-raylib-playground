# Scene 0990: Eat That

- type: talk
- location: the curved inside of a toppled stone ring as a sloping floor and wall, fallen course blocks stacked at angles, salt drifted in the low side, a small fire of crate wood, lamps turned down, night
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip, Ket, Hesk, Maren
- backdrop: 0975_a_mile_of_rings:3
- mood: somewhere to put it

## Beat
Back at the small fire in the ring core, hours before dawn, cordon still out there. Nobody analyses
anything and nobody tells Bron what he is feeling. Hesk builds the fire up, heats something, puts it
into his hands and tells him to eat it, and that is the entire extent of what she offers. Zeph tries
to be useful by being precise about dates and is stopped by three people in a row and takes it well.
Pip cannot look at Bron, so she says things to the fire instead, and the last one is funny, and
nobody laughs, and she looks like she has been slapped. Maren sits apart with her satchel on her
knees and says one line, to nobody, and it is an apology for something else entirely. Lyra gives one
practical instruction about the morning. Bron eats it. That is all he does.
