# Scene 0310: The Welcome

- location: a neat sandstone town square with a raised stone well at its centre, swept flagstones, whitewashed housefronts with green shutters, a painted board over the bakery with no words, bright clean midmorning sun from the left
- characters: Bron, Ollo, Ista, Pip
- mood: warm, delighted, one degree off
- staging: The party comes in from the left of frame and faces right into the square. The well is centre-right and the town comes out toward them from the right. Ollo stands on the right of Bron in every shot they share. Everyone in the square turns toward Bron

## Beat
The square fills to meet them and the welcome is completely genuine. Nobody here is lying and
nobody here is frightening: they are sixty decent people who have read a town file and want to do
the job well, and every single beat of it is a half-degree too accurate and a half-beat too quick.
Ollo Marrick comes out with flour to the elbow, takes Bron by the shoulder with a good rehearsed
grip and calls him Hesta's boy in a voice that has said it before. A weaver uses a childhood name
on him that he has never had in his life, and two more people use it after her: a townsman in a
carter's apron who also asks whether he ever got another dog, and a townswoman leaning out of an
upstairs window with washing over her arm who calls it down the square like good news. Bron has not been here since he was thirteen and is honestly pleased to
be back, and works visibly hard to look as pleased as everyone expects, and cannot quite manage
it, and feels bad about that. Pip is watching the crowd instead of the reunion and says nothing
about it.

## Dialogue
- Pip [1] {hope}: Every gate's painted. Every single one.
- Ollo [2]: There now. There he is.
- Ollo [3] {wonder}: Hesta's boy. Hesta's boy, back in the square.
- Bron [4]: ...You had the bakery. I remember the smell of it.
- Ollo [4]: Eleven years of it. And you've got her hands.
- Ista [5]: Coppertop! I said it was him. I said so at the pump.
- Townsman [6]: Coppertop. Did you ever get another dog, after?
- Bron [6]: ...No. Not yet.
- Ista [7]: He's got her hands, hasn't he. Look at him.
- Townswoman [8] {dread}: Coppertop's home!
