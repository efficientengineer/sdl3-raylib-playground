# Scene 1455: The Last Vote

- location: a round chamber of dressed stone with a ring of stone seats rising in three tiers, a plain stone table at the centre, a closed stone door behind it, lamp panels in the ceiling, cold even light
- characters: Hesk, Zeph, Bron, Sefa
- mood: wonder, then dread, then sorrow
- staging: The party enters from the left and works rightward round the chamber wall. The cut wall face is at the right and back of every panel; the table and the bowls are centre. Everyone looks right or down

## Beat
A round council chamber in perfect repair, the wall cut floor to ceiling in close ruled rows of
shallow marks, nine stone bowls of tally stones standing on the table where they were counted and
left. Hesk reads it in her grandmother's language, of which she has about sixty words, and gets
quieter with every line. The last council did not die of anything and nobody took them: they wrote
everything they were into the Seam for safekeeping, sealed the doors on the bodies from the inside so
that no one could walk out and do it to the rest of the world, and left the machine below running so
the record would stay coherent until somebody came down and read them back out. Nobody came. Twenty-
eight generations of their own descendants have been living on top of it, a week at a time, ever
since. They are not villains and not martyrs; they were record-keepers whose town had become an
argument anyone could edit. No legible words anywhere in the art — the cut rows are texture.

## Dialogue
- Hesk [1] {wonder}: It's the old tongue. My grandmother had forty words of it. I've maybe sixty.
- Zeph [2]: Nine seats, nine bowls. One of them is nearly full and the other eight are not.
- Hesk [3]: It's a vote. They cut the vote into the wall, and then cut what it was for underneath it.
- Hesk [4] {dread}: They weren't taken. Nobody did this to them. They put everything they were into it themselves.
- Hesk [4]: Then they barred the doors from in here, so none of them could walk out and do it to the rest.
- Zeph [5]: There is a date on the last of it. That is not an epitaph. That is an instruction with a date on it.
- Hesk [6] {sorrow}: Come down and read us back out.
- Sefa [6]: And nobody came.
- Bron [7]: There's a street of them over our heads who've never been told there's a door.
