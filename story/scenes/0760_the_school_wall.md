# Scene 0760: The School Wall

- type: talk
- location: a cramped stone machinery room behind a dormitory wall, a cable running through floor and ceiling on brass sheaves, a bank of valve wheels, a greased drum, a lamp in a wire cage above, night
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Pip, Ket
- mood: dread, then wonder

## Beat
Optional, and the answer is boring on purpose. Crossing the school terrace at two in the morning on
the way to the staff stair, Ket stops walking, which Ket does not do, and the party stops behind
her. There is an unlocked machinery door in the terrace wall, and behind it the lifting-car cable
comes down through the floor on brass sheaves, and the stone it is bolted to is the back wall of the
children's dormitory. The note comes and goes with the cars. Ket puts her hand flat on the stone and
gives the pitch as a figure, and it is the Ossun head's note, carried a hundred and eighty feet down
a cable and into a wall sixteen children sleep against. Nothing wakes up. Nothing attacks. The
children are entirely fine and always have been, and the only person in six years who has been hurt
by this is the woman asleep in a chair two tiers below. Pip makes the joke and then keeps her hand
on the wall a while longer than the joke needs.

## Dialogue
- Ket {dread}: Stop.
- Pip: What. What is it. Say the thing.
- Ket: Put your hand flat on that. Not the door. The stone.
- Pip: ...It's going. It's going, and then it isn't, and then it is.
- Ket: Thirty-seven a second. That is the Ossun head. I have known that note for a thousand years and it is in this wall.
- Lyra: And the children are on the other side of it.
- Ket: Sixteen beds. The cable comes down on the sheaves and the sheaves put the note into the stone.
- Ket: Nothing is wrong with them. They are sleeping inside a machine, and the machine is working correctly.
- Pip {wonder}: That is the least frightening thing anybody has ever paid me for.
- Pip: ...I'm going to stand here a minute.
