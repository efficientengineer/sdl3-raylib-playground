# Scene 0295: The Whistle

- location: a low tram town of grey brick and sandstone in a shallow valley, washing lines strung between roofs, a tall dark ring tower standing above it, steam drifting flat, overcast morning, light from the left
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip
- mood: liking him, and not knowing what to do with that
- staging: The party stand on the boarding-house step at the right of frame facing left. Orrin and his crew walk away up the middle of the street to the left. The road east lies off to the left

## Beat
The morning they leave for Tellwater. He goes up the middle of a wet street toward the tram yard
with a survey crew strung out behind him, playing the tin whistle badly, and one of his crew is
laughing at him and he does not care in the slightest. On the step behind him are four people who
cannot say what happened to them this week. The last thing in the chapter is a junction, a road
sign, and the four of them walking east underneath it.
