# Scene 1150: The Fifteenth Question

- type: talk
- location: a flat grassless field at the town edge, four bare timber fair frames standing in a row, trodden circles of ground, a coil of bunting rope on a post, low evening sun from the right, long shadows
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Ollo
- mood: tense
- backdrop: 1155_the_fair:1

## Beat
Dusk at the fair ground with the trestles going up around them, and the two of them having the
argument they always have, properly: whether taking the roster is theft, and whether that matters.
She loses it on the merits and is not bothered about losing it. Then, sideways, she tells him what
she worked out at the Ilder stump — that the fifteen questions she has asked at two hundred
deathbeds were written by somebody as a procedure, to make sure a thing arrived in the condition
it left in, and that this makes the prayer better and not worse. He does not follow her and says
so. She quizzes him to make her point and he gets to fourteen. Then she is called away to hold a
trestle up and she goes.

## Dialogue
- Bron {tense}: It's theft. I'd rather we said so out loud before we did it.
- Lyra: It's theft. Pell signs for that book. When it goes, she's the one who's short.
- Bron: A copy says whatever the man copying it wants. That book's had her hand in it since ninety-three.
- Lyra: ...Mm. I haven't a better answer than that one.
- Lyra: Ket read my fifteen questions off a maintenance manual at the stump. She stopped halfway down.
- Lyra: They're a check. Somebody wrote them so a thing would survive being moved.
- Bron: And you're pleased about that.
- Lyra: I'm extremely pleased about it. Two hundred hours, and I'd had it the wrong way round the whole time.
- Bron: I don't follow you.
- Lyra: No. Say them back to me, then. Eleven.
- Bron: What is unfinished. Twelve, what should be told, and who to.
- Bron: Thirteen. Is there anything you would rather nobody knew.
- Bron: Fourteen. Is there anything you want said out loud now, while there are people.
- Lyra: Fifteenth question. Ask me the fifteenth.
- Bron: I don't know it.
- Lyra: You will.
- Ollo: Sister! This end, before it goes over!
- Lyra {hope}: Coming.
