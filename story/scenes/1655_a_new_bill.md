# Scene 1655: A New Bill

- location: the guild hall ground floor, bare sandstone walls, a black iron stove burning at the left, a pinned job board on the right, behind it a floor-to-ceiling wall of ruled columns of small handwriting, cold bright morning light through the open door at the far end
- characters: Tovin, Bron, Pip, Tibb
- mood: hope, then wonder
- staging: Facing into the hall from the door end. The stove is on the left, the board and the ledger wall are on the right, and Tovin stands in front of the board facing left. The hall door stands open at the far left of frame with the cold terrace beyond it. Bron faces right at the board, and left at the door

## Beat
The 9th of Fallow, 1108. A cold bright autumn morning and a hall full of people eating. Tovin-Caleth,
at two lights a side, chalks a new bill onto the board in her own hand: two days' walk, bad water,
pays badly, a woman with a well and no money — and this one has a seal on it. Bron takes it down and
signs the ledger, and the wall behind him carries fifty-five years of names in her handwriting,
including one line with a single ruled bar through it and a date in Dust, and nobody looks at it and
nobody says anything about it. Pip signs underneath him without being asked. The door is standing
open and the wind coming off the terrace is cold — properly cold, the kind a Kell can feel once it
is actually on him — and on the way out Bron stops in it for a second because he wants to. Then two
people walk out into a bright cold morning and there is an empty space on the board behind them.

## Dialogue
- Tovin [1] {hope}: Two days' walk. Bad water. Pays badly.
- Tovin [2]: A woman with a well and no money. Seal's on it.
- Bron [3]: Fine.
- Pip [4]: Who does she rent the upstairs room off, anyway?
- Tovin [4]: Me. Room directly above mine. She walks about at night.
- Pip [5]: Bron visits.
- Tovin [5]: I am aware. I hear the ladder.
- Bron [6]: It's a staircase.
- Tovin [6] {wonder}: I know what I heard.
