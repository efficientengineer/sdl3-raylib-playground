# Scene 0165: Nobody In It

- type: talk
- location: a cut-stone corridor collapsed at one end into a heap of squared blocks, a doorway that now opens on solid rubble, two rusted halberds crossed on the floor, one guttering torch on the right wall, no daylight
- characters: Zeph, Lyra, Bron, Pip
- mood: dread

## Beat
The armour is down in pieces across the rubble and there is nothing in it. Zeph counts the pieces,
twice, and both counts agree, and that is what bothers him: no bone, no ash, nothing that was ever
a person. Lyra is not frightened by that; she is sad about it, and says so once, flatly. Pip makes
a bad joke and hears how bad it was. Bron wants one thing only, which is whether the other one down
there will do the same, and nobody can tell him.

## Dialogue
- Zeph {dread}: Forty-one pieces. I counted twice. Forty-one both times.
- Zeph: No bone. No ash. No teeth. Nothing in there that was ever a person.
- Pip: So they were... very tidy skeletons?
- Pip: That was worse out loud than it was in my head.
- Lyra: Nobody ever came back for them. That's the part I mind.
- Bron: The one at the bottom of this. Will it do that?
- Zeph: I do not know.
- Bron: Guess.
- Zeph: I have it as a procedure. Not a person.
- Zeph: I do not know yet why that is worse.
