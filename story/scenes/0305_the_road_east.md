# Scene 0305: The Road East

- type: narration
- mood: wonder, then flat

## Beat
Two days east of Windrow. The country gives up and then thinks better of it, and the road drops
into a bowl of good fields under a dead tower. The town at the bottom of it drowned fourteen years
ago and was built again on the high side of the water, and it is the best-kept place any of them
have ever walked into. The last two lines are said as a fact about a small town, not as a warning.

## Dialogue
- Narrator {wonder}: Two days east of Windrow the country gives up. Then, without warning, it thinks better of it.
- Narrator: Thin grass. Then hedges, cut square. Then a bowl of good fields under a tower that has been dark since ten thirty-nine.
- Narrator: The low ground drowned in the autumn of ten ninety-three. They built the town again on the high side of the water.
- Narrator {tense}: Four hundred houses. Sixty people.
- Narrator: Every gate is painted.
