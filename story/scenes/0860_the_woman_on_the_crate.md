# Scene 0860: The Woman on the Crate

- location: a low vaulted stone cellar with a bedroll and a crate table, a survey satchel open on it, an oil lamp and a tin cup, a drain grate in the floor, warm lamplight from the left, no daylight
- characters: Maren, Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip, Hesk, Ket
- mood: tense, then wonder
- staging: The stair and the way out are at the left of every panel; the cellar runs deeper to the right. The party comes in from the left facing right. Maren is at the right facing left. Everything that arrives, arrives from the left

## Beat
Six armed people come round the last corner of a dungeon expecting the most dangerous person in the
Basin. What is there is a tired woman in a travelling coat sitting on a crate with bread in her lap,
who says hello, apologises for the state of the room, and gets up slowly with her hands open where
they can be seen, because she has done this before and it saves time. Then she looks past all of
them at the smallest one and tells Pip to sit down, because Pip has been walking on a split seam in
her left boot since Windrow and it is taking her foot sideways. Her accent moves three towns in four
sentences. Her hands make a gesture half a beat before she says the line it belongs to, and it is
not always her gesture. Lyra recognises the profession before anyone else does and says nothing
about it. And then Maren looks at Bron a second too long, and something arrives behind her face that
she does not explain and will not explain, and before anybody can ask there are boots on the steps
and a man's voice calling down into the dark.
