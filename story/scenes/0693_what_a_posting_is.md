# Scene 0693: What a Posting Is

- type: talk
- location: a labour town of plank bunk rows on white salt crust at the foot of a broken stone ring stump, a bore head derrick above the roofs, a crawler depot fence, hard flat noon light, no shadows
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip, Ket, Hesk
- mood: tense, then sorrow

## Beat
Five people arguing on the walk out to the salt edge, around a sixth who does not say one word from
the first line to the last. Zeph does the arithmetic out loud and keeps arriving at a number of
postings that will not divide into a town. Hesk says the unwelcome thing first, as she always does,
and does not soften it afterwards, and does not stop when Pip tells her to, because she has never
once stopped. Ket, asked straight, gives what is documented and declines twice to go past it, and
the refusal frightens everybody more than a theory would have. Nobody says the true answer. Nobody
asks the obvious question. Lyra says nothing at all until the end, and then asks the other four to
walk on ahead.

## Dialogue
- Zeph {tense}: Sixty names. Sixty. Tellwater is four hundred houses and I counted sixty people in the square.
- Zeph: So the sixty are the town. There is no second list. I have looked at the back of it twice.
- Pip: People move. Towns empty out. My gran's village had nine people in it and a hundred roofs.
- Zeph: Your grandmother's village did not have a baker on a three-year renewable.
- Hesk: A place where every soul in it draws a wage for being there isn't a town. It's a shift with houses.
- Pip: Shut up, Hesk.
- Hesk: No. Somebody's got to say it out loud before it gets dark.
- Pip: It doesn't help. Saying it doesn't help.
- Hesk: I'm not saying it to help.
- Ket: The page says sixty postings, sixty numbers, one entry without a number, and a role note against twelve of them.
- Zeph: And what does a role note mean?
- Ket: Not known. I will not say what it means. I will say what is written and I have said it.
- Zeph: Keeper, I am asking you to guess.
- Ket: No.
- Lyra {sorrow}: Walk on ahead, all of you. Go and see about the crawler. We'll come.
