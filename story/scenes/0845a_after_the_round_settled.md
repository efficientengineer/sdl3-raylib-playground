# Scene 0845a: After the Round — Settled

- type: talk
- optional: yes
- location: a town packed into a narrow ravine of stacked stone housefronts on both walls, rope-walks strung across the gap overhead, smoke lying in a flat layer below the roofline, low evening sun striking only the upper stones from the right
- characters: Bron, Hesk, Pip, Zeph
- mood: sorrow

## Beat
**Branch: plays when the side contract "Collection at Emberrow" (`0820`, `0840`) ends with the party
settling the sixth door's debt out of their own money. One of three; see `0845b` and `0845c`.**

The evening after the sixth door, on the wall above the town where the ravine road comes over the
lip. Somewhere to put it. The party is broke — fifty-two marks and four is most of what chapter
seven paid them — and Pip cannot stop saying the figure, which is how she says everything else.
Hesk says nothing at all for the whole scene and then says one sentence that is not thanks and is
not an apology either, and is the nearest thing to either that she has. The game passes no
judgement. Nobody says they had no choice. Nobody in the party defends it and nobody condemns
anybody else.

## Dialogue
- Pip {sorrow}: Fifty-two marks and four.
- Zeph: Yes.
- Pip: Fifty-two marks and four.
- Bron: We know, Pip.
- Hesk: ...
- Hesk: I'd have had to pay that out of the Fifth's box and then stand in front of the council and say why. You didn't have to say anything to anybody.
