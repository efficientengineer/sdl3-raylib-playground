# Scene 0140: Paid in Beans

- type: talk
- location: the guild hall ground floor, bare sandstone walls, a black iron stove burning at the left, a pinned job board on the right, behind it a floor-to-ceiling wall of ruled columns of small handwriting, lamplit evening
- characters: Bron, Pip, Tovin
- mood: matter-of-fact

## Beat
The hatchling is out of the tank and gone down the terrace. Bex pays on completion, because that is
what a bill is, and what she has is beans, because the water bill took the money. She offers them
without apologising, because a Kell does not apologise for a true count. Bron takes them, because
taking them is the polite thing and refusing would be an insult, and he says nothing else about it.
Back at the hall Tovin enters the bill as cleared and the fee as nought, out loud, and takes a
tenth of it, out loud, and does not round anything up to be kind.

## Dialogue
- Bex {hope}: Four and six. I haven't got four and six. The water bill had it off me on the first.
- Bex: I've got beans. Two pound. They're good beans.
- Bron: Right.
- Bex: You'll want to soak them.
- Bron: I know.
- Tovin: Bill cleared. Bex Caleth, third terrace. Fee: nought.
- Tovin: The tenth of nought. Entered.
- Pip: What's a tenth of nothing?
- Tovin: Nothing. I have entered nothing, in ink, in the book, and it is straight.
