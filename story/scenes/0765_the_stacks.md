# Scene 0765: The Stacks

- location: a narrow aisle between iron shelf runs packed with labelled folder spines, a rolling ladder hooked at the end, a wheeled trolley half loaded, a lamp on a bracket at head height lighting from the left, night
- characters: Bron, Zeph, Pip, Hesk
- mood: wonder, then dread
- staging: The aisles run away to the right and the party moves right, deeper in. The stairwell and the way out are at the left of every panel. Everyone's attention goes right, down the aisle

## Beat
Out of the staff stairwell onto the Registry floor, and the floor goes back further than the lamp
does. The best-lit rooms in the Basin, unlit, at three in the morning: rolling ladders on their
rails, trolleys parked square against the shelf ends, a bank of drawer fronts covering an entire
room from the boards to the ceiling. Nothing here is dusty, nothing is crumbling, nothing has been
forgotten. It is all correct. Zeph, who was thrown out of an archive for reading what he was not
given, is the only one who understands the scale of what he is standing in, and it frightens him
much more than it frightens the rest of them, and he does not make one joke in the whole scene. Hesk
wants out of the room and cannot say why and does not try. Nobody is guarding any of it, because
nobody has ever needed to.

## Dialogue
- Pip [1] {wonder}: How far back does it go?
- Zeph [2]: Past the lamp. I have been holding it up for a while now and that is still the answer.
- Hesk [3]: I want to be out of this room.
- Pip [3]: You've stood in a live bore. You've stood in a flooded hall.
- Zeph [4] {dread}: Braid's archive is four rooms and a drowned stair. This floor is one of six, and it is not the big one.
- Bron [5]: What's in the drawers.
- Zeph [6]: People. One town to a drawer, and every one of them in the right place.
