# Scene 1550: The Refusal

- type: talk
- location: a short plank platform on a cut-stone ledge beside laid rail, an iron lamp stanchion, a stacked pile of sleepers and spikes, a squared ancient doorway in the wall behind, lamplight from above, no daylight
- characters: Sefa, Ilven, Cassa, Zeph, Bron, Hesk
- mood: sorrow

## Beat
The line comes off the rail from the seabed camp — eleven of them, stiff from nine hours on a
sledge — and Sefa Quill does the asking, because she is the only person on the platform who knows
the correct form of the request. She does it perfectly. She gives her line name and her office, she
acknowledges before she answers the way you are supposed to, she wastes nothing, and she offers
herself to be checked. Ilven Tessen refuses her in front of eleven of her own people and four of the
party, and the reason is procedure and not sentiment: a line does not accept a record, it accepts a
chanter, a chanter has to be checkable, and thirty-one years of Sefa's second tone going flat are on
the public record in that woman's own hearing. Ilven is not cruel and is not enjoying it. Sefa does
not argue, does not plead, does not make a joke, and does not ask again. She says thank you, steps
back two paces with a case of forms nobody needs, and hands her son the floor by using his title.

## Dialogue
- Sefa {sorrow}: First chanter Tessen. Sefa Quill, of the line Quill. Registrar of the Clement Works.
- Sefa: I acknowledge you and I will not waste your hour. I am asking your line to take a record.
- Sefa: Four hundred and eleven accounts. Once, whole, tonight, from the woman who holds them.
- Ilven: Record. — A line does not take a record, Registrar. A line takes a chanter.
- Sefa: Chanter. Then I offer myself to be checked.
- Ilven: You have been checked. Thirty-one years of it, in the hall and in the annex and at the lease table.
- Ilven: Your second tone goes flat. I have it flat in my own hearing eleven times, on the public record.
- Ilven: I will not bond eleven of mine to a voice that cannot be checked. That is the procedure.
- Ilven: It is not a judgment of you. It would be the same procedure for my own daughter.
- Sefa: ...
- Sefa: Thank you. That is the correct form of it.
- Zeph: Registrar.
- Sefa: ...
- Sefa: Reader.
