# Scene 1415: The Lit Street

- location: a vast cut-stone cavern holding a city of stacked stone housefronts on terraces, lit stone lamp columns down the middle of a swept street, a still water channel, warm even light from the columns, no sky
- characters: Bron, Pip, Ket, Hesk
- mood: wonder, then dread
- staging: The street runs left to right and away from camera; the party comes out of the stair at the left and looks right down it. The people of Thurn all face the same way, right and away, and never toward the party

## Beat
A mile of dark, and then this: a street that is lit, swept, warm and full. Old clothes in perfect
repair, bread somewhere, clean water running, a child being carried, a man mending a shutter. Every
person in it is upright, healthy, unhurried and pleasant — never slack, never staring, never a
zombie — and every one of them is facing the same way, and not one turns to look at seven armed
strangers coming out of a doorway that has been shut since year 0. The place is in better repair
than anywhere on the surface. Zeph counts because he cannot not count. Pip has a joke ready and does
not get to use it. Hesk is furious and will not say why yet. Nothing is ruined; nothing is
frightening; nobody looks up. That is the whole horror and the panels must carry it without one
cobweb.

## Dialogue
- Pip [1] {wonder}: ...There's people.
- Zeph [2]: Two hundred and six lamp columns down this street, and not one of them out.
- Zeph [3]: And no soot on that roof. A thousand years of lamps and not a mark on it.
- Pip [4] {dread}: Friend? You want a hand with that shutter? — Friend. I'm talking to you.
- Pip [5]: He's not deaf. He looked at my boots when we came past him.
- Ket [6]: He is not troubled by you. That is not the same as not hearing you.
- Ket [6]: Nobody has turned round. I have been watching since the stair and it is nobody.
- Hesk [7] {sorrow}: ...
- Hesk [8]: It's warm. Somebody has been keeping it warm.
