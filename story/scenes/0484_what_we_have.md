# Scene 0484: What We Have

- type: talk
- location: a Vess town on a terrace lip stepping down onto white dry seabed, narrow tall stone houses with shuttered openings, laid rail running to a flat horizon, a patched stone ring tower behind, morning sun from the left
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip, Ket
- mood: tense -> hope

## Beat
Dawn on the terrace lip, the salt going grey below them, everything they own drying on a wall. Bron
lays out what they have, which is a word, a card and no way onto the rail, because the sheds will not
sign an unsealed party onto the line. Ket solves it in one move and does not appear to think it
clever: she will enter four hunters on the Meddra maintenance manifest as her hands, and the Works
can only refuse that by writing down that Meddra runs. She then states her price, which is passage
east and a doorward who will talk, and Zeph gives her the geography she is asking about without being
asked twice. Bron asks the only question worth asking about a new partner, and gets the answer
straight, with nothing taken off it. This is where Ket-Ossun joins the party.

## Dialogue
- Bron {tense}: What we have. A word for the thing under the hills. A company card about something called the Fair Copy.
- Bron: And no way onto the rail.
- Zeph: The sheds will not sign an unsealed party onto the line. I asked twice. The second time in Vess.
- Pip: And?
- Zeph: The second time was worse.
- Ket: I will sign four hunters onto the Meddra maintenance manifest. As my hands.
- Lyra: Will the Works take that?
- Ket: They will take it, or they will refuse it in writing. To refuse it they must write down that Meddra runs.
- Ket: They have one working skywell printed on everything they own. It would cost them a sentence.
- Ket: What I want is passage east under the hills, and a doorward who will talk to me.
- Zeph: Nine door-mouths in one cliff face. All nine mortared. One household living over each.
- Zeph: The hold above them is Ninefold Terrace. They will not talk to you either.
- Bron: What are you going to want off us later?
- Ket: Thurn. The door-mouth, and the specification kept behind it. When a door opens I go through it first and I take it.
- Bron {hope}: Fine.
