# Scene 1160: The Last Plate

- type: talk
- location: the same swept square at dusk, the stone well black against the paving, lamps lit in three windows out of forty, empty benches along the walls, a dark ring tower above the roofs, cold green sky
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip, Ket, Hesk, Ollo
- mood: hope
- backdrop: 1155_the_fair:1

## Beat
Midnight, one trestle still standing in the square, six people and a great deal of leftover bread.
Zeph tries to explain how a chant-line keeps its books and is heckled for it. Hesk complains about
the heat off an oven that has been dead four hours. Pip lifts something off Ollo's belt in front
of everybody and he lets her. Ket announces her own state without embarrassment and asks whether
anyone objects to her sleeping in the road. Then Ollo comes over with a bundle he packed before he
asked, and asks, formally and badly, whether a man whose posting closes on the 11th can come with
them. Bron asks him one practical question. Hesk says the unwelcome thing and then moves up the
bench.

## Dialogue
- Zeph {hope}: A chant-line's books are not books. There is no page. The count is held between the pair and rehearsed daily.
- Pip: So it's two people arguing about a number for a hundred years.
- Zeph: It is two people not arguing about a number for a hundred years. That is the entire innovation.
- Hesk: That oven's been dead four hours and I can feel it from here. Door and stone.
- Ollo: I'll open the back if you like.
- Hesk: Open the back, open the front, and take the roof off it.
- Ket: I am at four lights. I intend to sleep in the road, where it is flat. Does anybody object?
- Bron: No.
- Ket: Good. I have never understood why that question makes people uncomfortable.
- Pip: That was easy. That was insultingly easy. You didn't even shift.
- Ollo: It's a dough scraper. Keep it, I've four. Two of them are Denner's and I can't tell which.
- Ollo: ...Right. I've been saying this wrong in my head all evening, so I'll say it wrong out loud.
- Ollo: My posting closes on the 11th. I've nowhere to be on the 12th. I can cook. I'd like to come.
- Bron: Can you walk twenty miles in a day?
- Ollo: I can walk it. I'll be poor company for the last four of them.
- Hesk: He'll slow us and he'll eat twice. ...Move up, then.
- Lyra: You'd already packed.
- Ollo: ...I had.
