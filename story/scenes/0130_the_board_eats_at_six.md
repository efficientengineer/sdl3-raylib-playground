# Scene 0130: The Board Eats at Six

- type: talk
- location: a cramped back kitchen with a sandstone hearth, a blackened pot on an iron arm, a scrubbed plank table with mismatched stools, bunches of dried beans on hooks, warm stove light from the left, night
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip, Tovin
- mood: warm

## Beat
Supper, bowls on knees, the bill folded in Bron's jacket. The vote that is not really a vote. Zeph
is delighted by one word on the sheet and has already worked out which hill-mouth it has to be. Pip
is against it for exactly one sentence and then asks what triple rate comes to. Lyra says the
frightening thing plainly and quietly and then says she will go. Tovin passes through with a
grievance about the hour. Then the cold comes up through the stone floor, Pip complains about it,
and Bron tells the story of the winter he got lost above Tellwater when he was nine — warmly, well,
at length by his standards, and funny in the middle. Nobody comments on it. Lyra asks one more
question about the contract than the table wants, gets nothing, and lets it go.

## Dialogue
- Zeph {hope}: Entrance. It says entrance. There is one entrance above this town and it has been shut a thousand years.
- Pip: I don't like it. ...How much is triple rate, though?
- Zeph: Eleven marks and two. Between four. After the tenth.
- Pip: I don't like it slightly less.
- Lyra: Somebody took a hot iron to their own seal, and then offered three times to keep it that way.
- Lyra: I'd like that in the room before we say yes.
- Bron: It's in the room. I'm going.
- Tovin: You are eating at twenty past six. I want that noted.
- Pip: This floor is stone. Actual stone. I have lost my feet.
- Bron: You want cold. I got lost above Tellwater when I was nine. Two nights out.
- Bron: First night I wasn't frightened. I was bored. There's nothing in the drifts above the fold. I counted stones.
- Bron: Then it went dark and I was shaking, and I sat in a sheep scrape and shouted at a rock till my voice went.
- Bron: She found me. Hands under my arms. Carried me the whole way down and told me off the entire way.
- Bron: Two nights lost, and what I remember is her saying I'd ruined my boots.
- Lyra: Who burns their own seal off a bill they're paying triple for?
- Lyra {hope}: ...Mm. Pass the bread.
