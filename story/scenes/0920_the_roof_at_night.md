# Scene 0920: The Roof at Night

- type: talk
- location: a smaller battered survey crawler on plate tracks with a ladder to a roof rail, survey rods lashed along one side, a stencilled company panel with no words half scraped off, under a black sky thick with stars, night
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip, Ket, Hesk
- mood: warm, sprawled, no plot at all

## Beat
Bedrolls on the roof deck, the engine ticking as it gives its heat back, salt glowing faintly all the
way out. Nothing happens. Pip has lost an argument about where she sleeps and is relitigating it;
Hesk wants to know whose turn it was to run the boiler down and gets an answer she does not enjoy;
Zeph tries to teach the party the names of things overhead and is corrected out of existence, so Pip
starts inventing constellations out of spite, and Ket writes two of them down because nobody told her
they were invented. Hesk, who has slept under a roof every night of her life, lies there furious.
Lyra does not sleep and does not mention it. Bron takes the last watch without being asked.

## Dialogue
- Pip {hope}: I'm not sleeping by the stack pipe again. I woke up cooked on one side and frozen on the other.
- Zeph: You chose the pipe. Your words were: the pipe is the best bunk on this machine.
- Pip: I was wrong in a warm way.
- Hesk: Whose turn is it to run the boiler down? It isn't mine. It wasn't mine last night either.
- Ket: It was. Two hours and ten minutes after we stopped. I was awake.
- Hesk: ...
- Zeph: That group of six, overhead. The annex calls them the Sleeve.
- Ket: Not correct. Nine. Three are faint and you are not counting them because you cannot see them.
- Zeph: I am counting the ones a person can see.
- Ket: Then say that.
- Pip: Fine. That one's the Bucket. Those four are Hesk's Bad Mood. The long one is A Man Owes Me Money.
- Ket: A Man Owes Me Money. Spell the first word.
- Hesk: I have slept under a roof every night of my life. There is no roof here. There's no anything.
- Lyra: There is a great deal. It is all simply very far away.
- Bron {hope}: I'll take the last watch. Go to sleep.
