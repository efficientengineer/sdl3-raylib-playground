# Scene 0930: Ostry Bar

- location: a low salt bar with a broken stone ring cap protruding from the crust, patched canvas shelters leaning against it in a crescent, standing figures spaced apart at the ring, cold pink dawn light from the right
- characters: Bron, Ket
- mood: friendly, and the player works out why that is worse
- staging: The cap ring and the camp fill the right of frame and the open pan runs off to the left. The party arrives from the left; they look right at the camp and left at the standing figures out on the salt

## Beat
Arrival. A ring of dressed stone the size of a town square standing two feet out of the crust, with
three hundred people's shacks built against its outer face, and eleven Onn spaced far apart out on
the open pan doing nothing whatever, some of them for days. The camp is a good place: washing on
lines, a cook fire, a child with a hoop, somebody singing badly. Everyone waves. Ket says what the
standing Onn are doing in one sentence and will not be drawn any further. The last thing the scene
shows is a row of faces turned toward the party with mild, friendly, unhurried interest, and Bron has
seen that expression before, at Saltmouth, and does not say so.

## Dialogue
