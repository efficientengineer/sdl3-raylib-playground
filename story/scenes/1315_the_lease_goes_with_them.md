# Scene 1315: The Lease Goes With Them

- type: talk
- location: a high stone hall with its shelf runs stripped bare, packing crates in rows on the flags, straw and cut cord on the floor, one lamp on a trestle table, flat lamplight from above, no daylight
- characters: Ilven, Zeph, Bron, Hesk
- mood: tense

## Beat
The first chanter of Braid is seventy and is standing in a leased building watching company porters
crate her people's records for shipment to a store at Vantage, which the schedule entitles them to
do when a site is evacuated, and which runs to 1187. She is courteous and precise and entirely
unmoved by sympathy, because sympathy is not what is in front of her; an inventory is. She will not
let a wrong count stand and she corrects Bron twice without any pleasure in it. What the chant-lines
are doing about it is the only thing they have ever done about anything, and she gives it as a
schedule. When Zeph asks about the barred shelf at the back — the one he was put out of this
building for reading — she answers him exactly as she would answer a stranger, which is worse than
anger, and tells him the truth, which is the only useful thing anyone says all day.

## Dialogue
- Ilven {tense}: Crates. Yes. Two hundred and forty of them, and they are entitled to every one.
- Bron: How much are they taking?
- Ilven: Taking. They are not taking. They are removing leased contents to a store, which the schedule permits on evacuation.
- Ilven: The term runs to the forty-fifth of Deep, eleven eighty-seven. I have eighty years of being courteous left in me.
- Bron: Eighty.
- Ilven: Eighty years and four months. You have rounded twice now. I allowed the first one.
- Hesk: What do you do about it.
- Ilven: Do. We rehearse harder. Nine lines have gone to two passages a day. Four have taken on a fifth line's work.
- Hesk: On a sledge.
- Ilven: On a sledge, in the cold, at four miles an hour. It is not a good way to hold a passage. It holds.
- Zeph: First chanter. The shelf at the back. The barred one.
- Ilven: The barred one. You read behind it in eleven oh six and this building put you out on the step for it.
- Zeph: It did.
- Ilven: It is a Registry seal. Only the Registrar's office cuts one. I have asked four times in eleven years.
- Ilven: And the Registrar is on the eight o'clock tram, up at the halt. You may have that for nothing.
