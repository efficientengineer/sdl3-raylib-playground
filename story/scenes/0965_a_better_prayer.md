# Scene 0965: A Better Prayer

- type: talk
- location: a crescent of patched canvas and plank shelters on salt, a shared fire pit of packed salt bricks, a water barrel on a sled, washing on a rope, cold blue dusk, firelight from the centre
- characters: Lyra, Ket
- mood: quiet, and it goes the other way

## Beat
Outside, on the crawler's track frame, with the camp fires behind them and salt to the horizon. Lyra
has sat two hundred and eleven last hours asking questions she believed were a prayer, and has just
been handed the specification they were copied off. Ket is factual about it, because she cannot be
anything else, and because being factual is the only kindness she has. She does not say anything
comforting and she does not intend the two sentences in the middle to land the way they land. In the
middle of it, without changing register at all, she says she is at four lights now and they should
plan around it. Lyra says she knows. Neither of them makes anything of it, then or ever. Lyra tells
nobody else in the party about any of this, and never does.

## Dialogue
- Lyra {sorrow}: Two hundred and six. That's how many hours I have sat. I said those words over every one of them.
- Ket: Then somebody has been running that check correctly, in order, without the sheet. For how long?
- Lyra: Longer than anyone wrote down. And it was made for a thing, Ket. Not for a person.
- Ket: It was made for whatever was being moved. The sheet does not say what. It says: ask in order, ask all of it.
- Lyra: Does that make it less?
- Ket: Not correct.
- Ket: It was written by people who wanted something to arrive whole. That is the entire purpose of the sheet.
- Ket: You have been asking the dying whether they arrived whole. I do not see the lesser version of that.
- Lyra: ...
- Ket: I am at four lights now. Plan around it.
- Lyra: I know. I was looking at you when it went.
- Lyra {hope}: Read me the rest of the page tomorrow. Not tonight. Tomorrow.
