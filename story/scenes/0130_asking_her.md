# Scene 0130: Asking Her

- map: hart_yard
- type: talk
- location: the low stone wall between the two houses, late: {{HEALER}}'s house dark behind her with one chair at the table, {{MENTOR}}'s lit window behind him
- characters: {{HERO}}, {{HEALER}}
- mood: asked at last, and an easy yes
- backdrop: 0101_the_dummies:1

## Beat
{{HERO}} comes over to the wall and asks her in a plain sentence, which no one has ever
done. Her yes is instant and she covers it at once with a joke about bandages. Then the rest comes
out, because he asks: {{TOWN_HEALER}} gives full standing after a season of work away from home,
{{HEALER}} has never been further than the bridge in her life, and she is the one who sat with a
carter she could not save. She is not going because he asked. She is going because she wants it —
and being asked is the part she does not mention.

## Dialogue
- {{HERO}} {hope}: {{HEALER}}. I'm walking the water run west at dawn. Come with me.
- {{HEALER}}: Yes.
- {{HERO}}: That was fast.
- {{HEALER}}: My bag has been packed since the bell. Someone has to carry the bandages you will need.
- {{HERO}}: You packed it before I asked?
- {{HEALER}}: I packed it because {{TOWN_HEALER}} told me to pack it.
- {{HERO}}: {{TOWN_HEALER}} wants you to go west?
- {{HEALER}}: She gives full standing after a season of work away from home. I have never had one.
- {{HERO}}: A season away? You've healed this whole town since I was small!
- {{HEALER}}: In this town. I have never been further than the bridge in my life.
- {{HERO}}: Four days is not a season.
- {{HEALER}}: It is four days more than I have. She said to start.
- {{HEALER}} {sorrow}: Two winters ago they carried a carter in off that road. I could not close what he had.
- {{HERO}}: What happened to him?
- {{HEALER}}: He died on {{TOWN_HEALER}}'s bench with my hands on him. I want to be out where it happens.
- {{HERO}}: You never told me any of that.
- {{HEALER}}: You have never asked me anything until tonight.
- {{HERO}}: I'm asking now. Come west with me.
- {{HEALER}} {hope}: I said yes the first time. Be at the gate before it is light.
- {{HERO}}: I'll be there before you are!
- {{HEALER}}: You will not.
