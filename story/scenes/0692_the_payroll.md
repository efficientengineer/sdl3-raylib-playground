# Scene 0692: The Payroll

- location: a narrow gap between two plank buildings on salt crust, a stack of empty crates against one wall, a standpipe with a brass meter box, wind-driven salt dust along the ground, hard flat afternoon light
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip, Ket, Hesk
- mood: tense, then sorrow
- staging: The counting house wall is on the right of every panel and the open salt is at the far left end of the alley. Zeph and Bron stand at the right facing left into the wind, holding the page between them; the other four stand further down the alley to the left

## Beat
One page out of the Rim district payroll, held open in the wind in the lee of the building it came
out of: the Tellwater station, sixty names, ruled in a clerk's hand, with a trade and a role note and
a posting number against every one of them. The baker is a posting. The well-keeper is not, and is
the only name on the page with no number beside it. And two thirds of the way down, in the column of
closed entries, is a line that stops Zeph in the middle of reading it out. He does not finish it and
does not look at the page again. Bron takes it out of his hands by the edge and finishes it himself,
out loud, correctly, and then reads it a second time without saying anything, and the wind takes the
corner of the page and he flattens it with his thumb. Nobody in the alley moves toward him. Nobody
touches him.
