# Scene 0950: They Will Not Be Moved

- type: talk
- location: the far side of the sunken cap ring, bare salt to a flat horizon, five patched shelters built against the stone, a fire pit of salt bricks with a kettle on it, cold blue dusk, firelight from the left
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Hesk, Corrow
- mood: settled, immovable

## Beat
Five men camped against the far side of the ring, three hours out from the Bar, with a kettle on and
nothing wrong with any of them. Three weeks ago one of them met a woman on the drove road and got his
life back in one piece: a name that is not the one on the payroll, a wife eleven days' walk away who
married somebody else in 1099, and thirty-four years arriving in an afternoon. He has thought about
it a great deal and he is not going. The other four stayed because he was in a bad way for a
fortnight and somebody had to, and they are the actual point of the scene. He says the important
thing twice so it cannot be softened, and asks the party not to put "grateful" or "sorry" in his
mouth. There is no bill in the world that moves them.

## Dialogue
- Crewmate {tense}: Kettle's on. Sit if you're sitting. We're not going back, so you may as well have the tea.
- Bron: The company's paid out on a bill for you. Five of you, back at work.
- Corrow: Then somebody's wasted nine marks. Sit down.
- Corrow: Three weeks ago a woman came along the drove road and gave me my life back. All of it, in one go.
- Corrow: The name on that payroll isn't mine. I've a wife eleven days' walk from here who married a man in ninety-nine.
- Lyra: How long were you—
- Crewmate: Fortnight. Bad. Didn't eat for four days and shouted at the stone half the night.
- Crewmate: Somebody had to sit with him. So we sat with him. That's the whole of why we're out here.
- Corrow: I'm not grateful. Say it back to me.
- Bron: ...You're not grateful.
- Corrow: And I'm not sorry. Don't put either of those on me. Everybody puts one or the other on me.
- Lyra: What will you do?
- Corrow: Sit here. Think about it. I've had it three weeks. I had the other thirty-four years to get used to.
- Corrow {sorrow}: I'm not walking eleven days to stand in a doorway and ruin a woman's afternoon.
