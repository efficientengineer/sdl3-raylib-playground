# Scene 0455: Tovin at Braid

- type: talk
- location: a side room off a Vess chant-hall, a low plank ceiling, a chalked job board on the right-hand wall, a squat iron stove with a kettle on it at the left, a short ledger wall of ruled columns of small handwriting behind the board, lamplight from the left, evening
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip, Tovin, Mir
- mood: tense -> hope

## Beat
The guild master of Sallowgate has walked three days down a terrace stair and out onto the salt to a
hall that is not hers, and she has come to hand back money. The poster of their bill has not paid and
will not, so the tenth the hall took is not a tenth of anything; it is the hall paying them, and a
hall that pays a hunter is a patron. So she returns it, in coin, out of a box that needed it, and
says why in one sentence and does not dress it. Then she tells them what came to her door afterwards
and declines to tell them what to do about it. Mir Lirr is at the stove through all of this and only
moves once. Tovin does not stay to eat.

## Dialogue
- Tovin {tense}: Three days and four hours. I will not call it three days.
- Tovin: One mark and one. That is the hall's tenth off your bill.
- Bron: That's the hall's winter.
- Tovin: The poster has not paid and will not. There is no seal on it to make him.
- Tovin: The hall paid you out of its own box. If the hall keeps a tenth of that, the hall is your patron.
- Tovin: No patron. That is the second Line. One mark and one. Hold out your hand.
- Tovin: Two constables at my door on the ninth. No warrant. A list of four names. They put a request to me and I declined it.
- Lyra: Is there an order out on us?
- Tovin: No. If there were, I would have carried it down here and read you all of it, including the back.
- Tovin: Bron. Are you on a bill?
- Bron: No.
- Tovin: Noted. — Mir. Your bunks are over your stove. In my hall they have the decency to be quiet about it.
- Tovin {hope}: The board eats at six. I am not staying for it.
