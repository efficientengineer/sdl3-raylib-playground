# Scene 0540: Dov at Dinner

- type: talk
- location: a low stone room with a stone hearth, a long trestle table crowded with bowls, tools hung on every wall, a broad doorway looking out over stepped fields, firelight from the left, evening
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip, Ket, Hesk, Dov
- mood: too warm, too loud, good food

## Beat
Hesk's nephew Dov is home for four days off a company bore crew: cheerful, twenty-six, and on the
wrong side of every argument at his own dinner table. He has boots that fit, a clinic card and a
wage out of the Saltmouth counting house, and he says all three out loud because he is proud of
them. Hesk says the unwelcome thing first and does not soften it after. Dov's defence is not
ideology, it is arithmetic: the barley figures, and who ate last winter. The room is far too warm
and only the humans are comfortable. The household eats straight through the whole argument, and
somebody keeps putting food into Pip's hands.

## Dialogue
- Dov {hope}: Humans at the fire end. Keeper where she likes. You and me on the door side, cousin, or we'll cook.
- Pip: First warm room in nine days. I am being buried here.
- Hesk: It is too warm in here. It has been too warm in here since Green.
- Dov: You banked it, aunt.
- Hesk: For them.
- Dov: Boots. Look at them. They fit. I never once had to cut the sides out.
- Dov: Clinic card. Wage on the ninth, out of the counting house at Saltmouth. They hold the whole Rim district. Never late.
- Hesk: Six households on this cliff eat on company money. You are one of the six. I'd rather say it than sit on it.
- Dov: Seven, since Thaw.
- Hesk: Seven. Worse.
- Dov: What did the Fifth's fields make this year, aunt?
- Hesk: Four hundredweight.
- Dov: What did they make when your father walked them?
- Hesk: ...Eleven.
- Pip: Somebody keeps putting food in my hands and I can't work out who it is.
- Dov: Bron. You've the shoulders for it and a hall ledger behind you. Say the word and I'll have you on a crew by Green.
