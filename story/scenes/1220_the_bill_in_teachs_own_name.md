# Scene 1220: The Bill in Teach's Own Name

- type: talk
- location: an old stone hall with a high beamed roof, a burned-edged job board on the back wall, long benches, a cold stove with a pile of unswept ash, a company notice board with no words, grey daylight from the left
- characters: Bron, Pip
- mood: tense

## Beat
The Even Hand's founding hall, which the Works has been trying to lease out from under it for six
years. One bill on the board has been up nine days: sealed, in a private name, paid out of a
private purse. Sit the last hour with a man at the Quiet House in Windrow. The hall keeper explains
why three hunters have read it and hung it back up, states who posted it without editorialising
beyond the fact, and lets the tone do the rest. Bron reads the poster's name and takes it down
without discussion. Pip does not argue.

## Dialogue
- Stobb {tense}: That one's been up nine days. Three have read it and put it back on the hook.
- Bron: It's sealed.
- Stobb: It's sealed, it's in a private name, and it pays out of a private purse. Nothing wrong with the paper.
- Stobb: Sit the last hour with a man at the Quiet House at Windrow. That's the work. That's all of the work.
- Pip: Then why's it hung nine days?
- Stobb: He's retired Pale. He was one of the eleven that went up to Tellwater in ninety-three.
- Stobb: And the Order of the Late Hour have written back declining to send anybody.
- Pip: ...
- Stobb: Poster's name is at the foot of it, if you want to know who's paying. Warden Ivo Teach. His own money.
- Bron: Book it out.
