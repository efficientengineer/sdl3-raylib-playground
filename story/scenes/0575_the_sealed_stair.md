# Scene 0575: The Sealed Stair

- location: a dressed stone wall covered in shallow cut marks in fifteen even ruled rows, a rubbed-bare patch at hand height below them, dust on the floor, one lamp held close from the right, no daylight
- characters: Zeph, Lyra, Hesk
- mood: wonder, then quiet dread
- staging: Down the stair is to the right and away. The marked wall runs along the right-hand side at hand height for the whole descent. The party goes down left to right in file with the lamps carried at the front

## Beat
Cold air comes up before anything else. The stair is cut, swept and in perfect repair, and a band of
shallow marks in ruled rows runs down the wall beside it as far as the lamps reach — the same band
that is cut over the face of all nine doors. Zeph reads a little of it and is disappointed: it is
not a warning, it is a numbered list, and the numbering is wrong at the end. Hesk watches the stair
and not the wall, because that is her job. Lyra walks with one glove off and her hand travelling
along the band, and she does not speak in this scene at all, and nobody looks at her.

## Dialogue
- Zeph [1] {wonder}: Cold air, and it is coming up. There is a great deal of hall under us.
- Zeph [2]: It isn't a warning. It's a list. Numbered, ruled, and cut by one hand.
- Narrator [2]: What happened the day before this one?
- Hesk [3]: Same band as the doors. All nine carry it. It has never had to mean anything.
- Zeph [4]: Fifteen entries. Fifteen is cut, and then there is nothing after it. The numbering is wrong.
- Narrator [5] {dread}: Is there anything you would rather nobody knew?
- Zeph [6]: I was promised a warning and I have been given a form.
- Hesk [7]: Wall side. It's a long way down and the treads are true.
