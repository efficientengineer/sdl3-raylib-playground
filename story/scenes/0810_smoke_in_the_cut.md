# Scene 0810: Smoke in the Cut

- location: a town packed into a narrow ravine of stacked stone housefronts on both walls, rope-walks strung across the gap overhead, smoke lying in a flat layer below the roofline, low evening sun striking only the upper stones from the right
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip, Ket, Hesk
- mood: dread, then sorrow
- staging: The party stands on the rim path at the left of frame, facing right and down into the cut. The town, the rope-walks and the care-house roof are all to the right and below. Smoke drifts right to left across every panel

## Beat
The party comes over the lip at the end of two days and looks down into a town with a lid on it.
Ket puts one hand on a block in the wall beside the path and names the well it was cut from and the
year it fell, in her ordinary voice, and it is the first time in the game she has sounded angry.
Hesk has spent five chapters demanding the Works get out of the hills; she looks at nine thousand
people living inside a dismantled one and cannot finish the sentence she has been saying since
Ninefold Terrace. Pip has already found the care-house from a quarter of a mile away without
appearing to look anywhere near it, and makes a joke about the smell instead. Nobody notices where
she is not looking. The care-house is the best-kept building in the cut and its roof is the only
clean one.
