# Scene 0210: Windrow Under a Dead Well

- location: a low tram town of grey brick and sandstone in a shallow valley, washing lines strung between roofs, a tall dark ring tower standing above it, steam drifting flat, overcast morning, light from the left
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip
- mood: cold, ordinary, wrong in a way nobody notices
- staging: The party stand at the left of the tram gate facing right. The constable stands at the right facing left, with the town and the dark ring tower behind him. Everyone is looking at the papers, not at each other

## Beat
A checkpoint that is not a threat. A cold, bored, polite constable of the Pale reads four sets of
guild papers out loud because that is the procedure, and one of the things he reads is a name, in a
list, between two other things, and nobody in the frame reacts to it — not the constable, not the
party. He wishes them a good stay and asks the small one to keep the daggers out of sight. Then the
gate opens on steam and tram bells and a town of people who have never once looked up at the
three-hundred-foot dark tower standing over their washing.
