# Scene 0140: Supper

- type: talk
- map: hart_yard
- location: {{MENTOR}}'s kitchen at sundown: a scrubbed table with three places laid, a fire, and a workshop book open on the bench by the door
- characters: {{HERO}}, {{HEALER}}, {{MENTOR}}
- mood: warm, ordinary, one argument that will not close
- dialogue_box: yes

## Beat
Three people who eat together most nights. {{HERO}} has come back from the guild hall knowing the rules and has decided he is signing tomorrow; {{MENTOR}} says he is not ready and, pushed, says the sentence the whole chapter turns on and then refuses to explain it. {{HEALER}} walked over from the house next door, which is dark, and eats here because there is nobody in it — a fact {{MENTOR}} states in four words while she is holding a spoon, and which she gets out of by turning it back on {{HERO}}. She is somebody's apprentice too, which is a formality and not a lack: {{TOWN_HEALER}} wants her to work a season somewhere other than {{HOME_TOWN}} before she gives her full standing, and {{HEALER}} has never been further than the bridge. The night ends with {{HERO}} announcing he will be at the machines before light.

## Dialogue
- {{HERO}} {wonder}: Job sheets go up at dawn. The clerk told me himself.
- {{MENTOR}}: Heard you the first time.
- {{HERO}}: And the board's open all day! I could sign and be gone by noon.
- {{MENTOR}}: You could not. You are not ready.
- {{HERO}} {tense}: I beat three of your machines! Three out of four!
- {{MENTOR}}: The fourth one is the one that is out there.
- {{HEALER}}: He's right about the fourth one. You never wait, {{HERO}}.
- {{HERO}}: I hit it eleven times! What is there to wait for?
- {{MENTOR}}: It isn't about hitting it.
- {{HERO}}: Then say what it is about!
- {{MENTOR}}: You hit everything as hard as you can. Every single time.
- {{HERO}}: That's how you hit things!
- {{MENTOR}}: Eat.
- {{HEALER}}: He won't tell you. He's never told me either, and I eat here every night.
- {{HERO}}: Who decides when you stop being an apprentice?
- {{HEALER}}: {{TOWN_HEALER}} does. The town healer. She's in no hurry about it.
- {{HERO}}: You've healed this whole town since I was small!
- {{HEALER}}: I have. She still has to say the word, and it's hers to say.
- {{HERO}}: What's she waiting for?
- {{HEALER}}: A season's work somewhere that isn't here. Pass the pot.
- {{HERO}}: You're here every single night. Does no one cook next door?
- {{HEALER}}: There's no one next door to cook for. My house is dark and this one has a fire.
- {{MENTOR}}: Fever took her mother and her father in one winter. She was nine.
- {{HERO}}: Nine?
- {{HEALER}}: {{MENTOR}}. He asked about my cooking.
- {{MENTOR}}: He did.
- {{HERO}}: I didn't know that. I've known you my whole — I didn't know that.
- {{HEALER}} {sorrow}: You never ask me anything. Eat your supper.
- {{HERO}} {hope}: I'm going out to the machines before it's light.
- {{HEALER}}: He means it, {{MENTOR}}. He'll be out there in the dark hitting a post.
- {{HERO}}: I'll still be out there when you come and laugh at me!
