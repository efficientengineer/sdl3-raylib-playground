# Scene 0795: Wet Paste

- location: a narrow cut-stone street on a quarry shelf, a blank rendered wall down one side pasted over with layer on layer of torn company notices, an iron downpipe, a paste bucket and brush left at the foot of it, grey daylight from the left, dawn
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip, Ket, Hesk, Idda
- mood: hope, then tense
- staging: The guild board is at the left of the hall panels and the pasted wall is at the left of the street panels. The party stands to the right of both and everyone's attention goes left

## Beat
First light on Tier Two, with the Pale three tiers behind them and the retreat over. The bill gets
closed the way a bill gets closed: the noise was real, the noise was a cable in a wall, and the
keeper chalks the line on the burned-edged board and counts out a small fee on the spot, because
that is the first Line and it does not bend for anybody's night. Idda Renn is asleep in a chair
against the wall with her coat still buttoned, because she walked down two tiers again at four in
the morning to hear somebody say it out loud, and did not make it back up. Nobody wakes her. Then
Pip notices that the paste on the wall outside is still wet, and Zeph reads what the bill-poster has
just been through and put up: the same standing recovery notice they found at Braid, word for word,
and freshly overprinted under it, a town. Bron says the name of the town and starts walking, and
nobody asks him anything.

## Dialogue
- Stobb [1] {hope}: It's a cable in a wall. Right. Noise found, noise named, cleared. That's the line.
- Bron [1]: Pay on completion. One mark and two, and she put it in an envelope with the nights written on the back.
- Stobb [2]: Leave her. She came down those two tiers again at four, to hear it said.
- Pip [3]: Paste is still wet.
- Zeph [4] {tense}: It is the Braid notice. The same one. No description, no name, three words of instruction.
- Zeph [4]: The Fair Copy. Do not converse.
- Zeph [5]: And a town overprinted under it, and that ink is wetter than the rest of the sheet.
- Bron [5]: Emberrow.
