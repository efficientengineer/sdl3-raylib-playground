# Scene 1520: Everything We Are Good At

- type: talk
- location: a great oval chamber of fitted stone with concentric stepped tiers going down to a central floor disc, stone standing posts at the rim, a seam face filling the back wall, faint even light from that face
- characters: Pip, Zeph, Hesk, Sefa, Ket, Ollo, Bron, Maren
- mood: confront, then tense
- backdrop: 1515_four_hundred_voices:7

## Beat
Everybody tries the one thing they are best at, in turn, and every one of them fails for a reason
that belongs to them personally. Pip makes a joke and two voices out of four hundred laugh at it,
which is far worse than silence and she knows it immediately. Zeph runs the edges test, the trick
that has never once let him down, and gets four different true days back in a single breath. Sefa
gives her name and her rank, which has worked on everyone she has met for thirty years, and is
answered with a docket number that turns out to be one of her own. Ket asks for figures and is given
figures that are all correct and all belong to different people. Then Ollo, who has spent eleven
years being warm to strangers for a living and is not trying anything, asks whether she has eaten,
and there are four seconds of one woman. Bron is standing behind all of them and is watching when
it happens.

## Dialogue
- Pip {confront}: Nobody panic. I've already panicked. There's none left for you.
- Pip: ...Two of them laughed. Two. Why is that worse. That's worse.
- Zeph: Maren. Yesterday, then. And the day before that one?
- Zeph: Four days. Four different days, and every one of them a real day.
- Zeph {tense}: That has never failed me. It has just failed me four times in one breath.
- Sefa: Registrar Sefa Quill, of the Clement Works. Answer me.
- Sefa: ...She has given me a docket number. It is a real docket. It is one of mine.
- Ket: Maren Ostry. How deep is the well at Tellwater.
- Ket: Eleven feet. Nine marks. The ninth of Fallow. Every one correct. Not one of them hers.
- Ollo: Have you eaten anything today?
- Ollo: ...
- Ket: Four seconds. She was one person for four seconds.
- Pip: Do that again.
- Ollo: Do what again?
- Ollo: I only asked her if she'd eaten.
