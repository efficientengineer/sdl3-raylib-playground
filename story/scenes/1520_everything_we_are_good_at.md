# Scene 1520: Everything We Are Good At

- type: talk
- location: a great oval chamber of fitted stone with concentric stepped tiers going down to a central floor disc, stone standing posts at the rim, a seam face filling the back wall, faint even light from that face
- characters: Pip, Zeph, Hesk, Sefa, Ket, Ollo, Bron, Maren
- mood: confront, then tense
- backdrop: 1515_four_hundred_voices:7

## Beat
Everybody tries the one thing they are best at, in turn, and every one of them fails for a reason
that belongs to them personally. The player watches three of them do it. Pip makes a joke and two
voices out of four hundred laugh at it, which is far worse than silence and she knows it
immediately. Zeph runs the edges test, the trick that has never once let him down, and gets four
different true days back in a single breath. Sefa gives her name and her rank, which has worked on
everyone she has met for thirty years, and is answered with a docket number that turns out to be
one of her own. Between those and the last of them, Ket asks for figures and gets figures that are
all correct and all belong to different people, and by then it is not worth a box. Then Ollo, who has spent eleven years being warm to strangers for a living and is not trying
anything, asks whether she has eaten, and there are four seconds of one woman. Bron is standing
behind all of them and is watching when it happens.

## Dialogue
- Pip {confront}: Nobody panic. I've already panicked. There's none left for you.
- Pip: ...Two of them laughed. Two. Why is that worse. That's worse.
- Zeph: Maren. Yesterday, then. And the day before that one?
- Zeph: Four days. Four different days, and every one of them a real day.
- Zeph {tense}: That has never failed me. It has just failed me four times in one breath.
- Sefa: Registrar Sefa Quill, of the Clement Works. Answer me.
- Sefa: ...She has given me a docket number. It is a real docket. It is one of mine.
- Ollo: Have you eaten anything today?
- Ollo: ...
- Ket: Four seconds. She was one person for four seconds.
- Pip: Do that again.
- Ollo: Do what again? I only asked her if she'd eaten.
