# Scene 0820: The Collection

- type: talk
- optional: yes
- location: a single low room over a rope-walk, dark stone walls, a burned-edged timber job board on the end wall, four pinned paper bills, a stove pipe crossing to a window, warm lamplight from a bracket at the left, evening
- characters: Bron, Hesk
- mood: hope, then dread

## Beat
The Emberrow hall is one room, one board and four bills, and three of the four are rats and a wall.
The fourth is sealed properly by the Clement Works accounts office and pays four times the rest of
the board together: walk a collector round for one day. The collector is in the room, because the
hall is where he waits — a pleasant tired man with a satchel and a ruled schedule who has done the
round alone for eleven years and would like not to. He answers Hesk's question accurately and
without embarrassment, because it is legal work and he is not ashamed of it, and the figure he gives
her is nine years old and has more than quadrupled since it was written. The hall-keeper has a stove
and no wood and will not tell them to leave it. Nobody in this room is doing anything wrong. Pip is
not in the room; she took the rope-walks and the yards and has not come up.

## Dialogue
- Hall-keeper {hope}: Four bills. Three of them are rats and a wall. The fourth one's the company.
- Hall-keeper: Sealed properly, accounts office at Vantage, and it pays four times the other three put together.
- Collector: Escort. One day, my round, up the street and back. I've walked it alone eleven years and I'd like to stop.
- Collector: It isn't the debtors. It's the stairs and the dogs. Mostly the dogs.
- Hesk: What's the debt.
- Collector: Nineteen of them on the schedule. Bark accounts, two shop books, and one intake fee that's been running nine years.
- Collector: Twelve marks and six at the start. Interest at the schedule rate. It stands at fifty-two and four.
- Hesk: Intake fee for what.
- Collector {dread}: The care-house. Bed, board, schooling, a bed kept warm. It's the same paper everybody signs.
- Hesk: ...
- Hall-keeper: I'll not tell you to leave it. I've a stove up here and no wood in it.
- Bron: Seal's on it. We'll take it.
