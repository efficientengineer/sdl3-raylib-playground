# Scene 0790: Flagged at Five

- location: a deep square stone shaft with a plain iron stair zigzagging down it, a lamp bracket at each landing, a coil of hose on a hook, a handrail bolted to cut stone, lamplight from above, night
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip, Ket, Hesk, Ivo
- mood: dread, then confront
- staging: The stair drops away down-screen right. The lamps, the detail and Ivo are always below and to the right; the party is above and to the left. Everyone in the party looks down-right; Ivo looks up-left

## Beat
Lamps on the stair, three landings down, coming up at a walking pace and not hurrying. The very big
man in the badly hanging grey coat does not draw anything, does not raise his voice, and does not
send anybody up ahead of him. He reads all six of them off a docket by their full names, including
the two names that were never on any paper the party made, and lets that sit without pointing at it.
He tells them, mildly and accurately, that their permits were flagged at five o'clock by the
Director of Extraction, who reads the gate baskets herself. He offers them a warm room two tiers
down in the tone of a man offering a cup of tea. Then he asks a question he already knows the answer
to, gets the honest answer, and says nothing about it at all. Nobody in the party believes for one
second that this ends in conversation. He takes his time. Bron is already moving.

## Dialogue
- Ivo [1] {dread}: Bron Sallow. Zeph Quill. Pip Rime. Hesk Ninefold. Sister Lyra of the Late Hour. Ket-Ossun, of Meddra.
- Ivo [2]: Your papers were flagged at five o'clock by the Director of Extraction. She reads the gate baskets herself. She is thorough.
- Pip [3]: She's got Ket's post off a permit we wrote. That was never on anything we wrote.
- Ivo [4]: Come down with me. There's a room on Two, it's warm, and nobody has to be carried anywhere.
- Ivo [5]: Have you got something under your coat, Bron Sallow?

- Ivo [5]: Mm.
- Bron [6] {confront}: Up. All of you. Now.
