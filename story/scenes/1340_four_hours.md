# Scene 1340: Four Hours

- type: talk
- location: a laid-rail siding on white crust at dusk, a sledge with its sail furled on the spar, a small fire in a salt-brick ring, bedrolls laid out on the deck, firelight from the centre, cold sky
- characters: Threnn, Domma, Zeph, Pip, Cassa
- mood: tense

## Beat
The contract resolves one of two ways, and neither is punished and neither is praised.

**If the party waits:** four hours on the salt in the cold beside eleven people chanting a river
system that dried up six hundred years ago. Cassa comes up the line at speed with the exact cost of
the delay, says all of it, and then, having said it, sits down and waits too. Pip learns eight words
of the passage badly and is told that badly is fine, badly is data.

**If the party moves them:** it is quick, it is lawful, nobody is hurt, the sledges run on time, and
a line that has held its record since year 700 goes out onto the seabed having lost four hours of
it. Nobody blames the party, which is worse. The four hours are gone and no later scene gets them
back.

Either way the last beat is the same: the chant carries on down the line ahead of them in the dark.

## Dialogue

**If the party waits.**

- Cassa {tense}: Eleven sledges, ninety-four people, four hours. That is sixteen miles they do not make tonight.
- Cassa: The siding water at the first stop is measured for tomorrow. Tomorrow it will be measured for two days.
- Cassa: I have said all of it. I said it fast on purpose. — Right. Where do I sit.
- Pip: You came four miles out here to sit down.
- Cassa: I came four miles out here to say the figure. Sitting is a separate thing.
- Domma {hope}: Come to the fire, both of you. You cannot join it. You may hold the count of it if you want a job.
- Pip: I'll get it wrong.
- Domma: You will get it wrong. Getting it wrong where somebody can hear you is how it is put right.
- Pip: ...That's eight words. I've got eight words of a river.
- Threnn: Eight words badly. Badly is not nothing. Badly is a thing to correct in the morning.
- Zeph: Go on. I have the count if Domma wants to drink something.

**If the party moves them.**

- Cassa {tense}: Lawful, fast, nobody hurt, the run on time, and I have it in the book as no delay.
- Cassa: They came off at the third hour of the passage. They begin tomorrow on the far side of it.
- Threnn: You did what the order allows. Nobody here is angry with you. I would rather somebody were.
- Domma {sorrow}: Thank you for walking the line. That was the work and you did the work. Go back before the cold.
- Zeph: ...

**Either way.**

- Pip: It's still going. Listen. It's gone on down the line ahead of us.
