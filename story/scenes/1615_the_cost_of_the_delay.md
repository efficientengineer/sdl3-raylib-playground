# Scene 1615: The Cost of the Delay

- type: talk
- location: a plain hired office of cut stone over the workshop yards, a desk with a shaded lamp, a small iron stove with a half-full scuttle, two chairs, a shuttered window over the drop, warm lamplight from the left, evening
- characters: Kerrow, Bron
- mood: tense, then hope

## Beat
Rime, on Tier Two, in a room she pays the rent on. Bron came up with a surveyor's question about a
boundary at Kettle and has been given tea he does not want and is not going to be allowed to leave
without drinking. Kerrow won the technical argument completely — the study is out under her name,
large bores are suspended across the Basin, and every person who read the mathematics accepted it —
and her own board served notice on her for what the delay at Thurn cost, using the same clause and
the same count she used on Crewe, which she finds funny and nobody else does. She is warm for the
first time in her life, because she is paying for the stove herself and keeps it high. She has not
changed her mind about a single thing and says so. She is the most likeable she has ever been and
she concedes nothing. Nobody scores a point off anybody.

## Dialogue
- Kerrow {tense}: Sit down. There is tea. You will not want it and you will drink it, because I have poured it.
- Kerrow: The study went out under my name on the eleventh. Large bores are suspended across the Basin.
- Kerrow: My board served notice eleven weeks after I took the chair. Article nine. Eight to three.
- Kerrow: The same clause and the same count I used on Crewe. I found that funny. Nobody else did.
- Bron: What was the reason?
- Kerrow: The cost of the delay at Thurn. Eleven hours at full, and a cap that never happened.
- Kerrow: They are not wrong about the figure. I checked it twice. It is a very large figure.
- Bron: And you're here.
- Kerrow: Consulting. Fourteen councils who cannot agree a water rate, and they pay on the day.
- Kerrow: I am better at this than I was at the chair. I pay for that stove myself and I keep it high.
- Kerrow {hope}: I have not changed my mind. The Basin bought eleven hours and a year. It will pay for both.
- Kerrow: A bore will invert inside the decade. I would like it minuted that I said so.
- Bron: Do you want the coat back where it came from?
- Kerrow: No.
