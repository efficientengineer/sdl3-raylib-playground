# Scene 0250: One Light

- type: talk
- location: a tall stone ring tower standing whole and dark over a tram town, birds circling the upper rings, a swept stone apron at its foot, a broom and a stacked pile of squared stone chips, grey overcast morning light
- characters: Arro, Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip
- mood: cold, swept, funny

## Beat
Everything up here is swept. An Onn with one light left at his temple is keeping a machine that has
been dead since the year nine hundred and forty-four, and greets four strangers without any surprise
at all, because people do occasionally come up. He answers exactly what he is asked and nothing
beyond it, and they mostly ask the wrong things. He corrects Zeph's count of the birds twice. He
states that they are not a fault. He announces his own state without embarrassment and tells the
party to plan around it, and then makes a joke he has had since the year nine hundred and forty-four
to get right. Then he goes back to sweeping while they are still standing there.

## Dialogue
- Arro {wonder}: The stair is swept. You will not have slipped.
- Pip: We didn't slip.
- Arro: Correct.
- Zeph: There are — about a hundred birds up there.
- Arro: One hundred and forty-one.
- Zeph: About a hundred and forty, then.
- Arro: One hundred and forty-one.
- Bron: Are they hurting the well?
- Arro: They are not a fault. They are on it. Those are different words.
- Lyra: Do you mind them?
- Arro: That is not a question about the well.
- Arro: I am at one light. You should plan around it.
- Pip: ...Plan around it how, exactly?
- Arro: Around whatever you were going to ask me to do next week.
- Zeph: ...
- Arro {hope}: It was a joke. I have had since the year nine hundred and forty-four to get it right.
- Arro: You are still here. This side of the apron is not swept.
