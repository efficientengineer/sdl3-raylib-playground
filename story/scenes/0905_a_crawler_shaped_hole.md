# Scene 0905: A Crawler-Shaped Hole

- type: talk
- location: a wire-fenced yard on white salt holding three survey crawlers nose to tail, a stencilled company plate bolted on the nearest one, a plank watch hut, one lamp on a pole lighting from the right, night
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip, Ket, Hesk
- mood: cheerful crime

## Beat
Half a mile off the drove road, flat on their fronts in the dark, watching a company depot. They came
down out of Emberrow this morning and nobody has said Ross Kettle's name once, which is how you know.
Maren is walking north and walking is faster than they are. Pip lays the job out and enjoys herself
more than anyone has enjoyed anything in three chapters; everybody has a correction; Hesk refuses on
principle and then asks which gate. Lyra offers one piece of practical dishonesty, pleasantly, and
the rest of them go quiet, and nobody asks her where she got it.

## Dialogue
- Pip {hope}: Three crawlers, two watchmen, one lamp on a pole. I've done worse with a nail and a wet evening.
- Ket: Not correct. Three vehicles. One of them is a water bowser and weighs eleven tons empty.
- Pip: Two crawlers, then.
- Ket: Correct. That is what I said.
- Bron: We can't walk it. She's two days out and she doesn't stop properly.
- Hesk: I'll not be part of a theft. My family keeps a door. You don't take what isn't yours.
- Zeph: Theft. I have it as a recovery. Their plate is on it and we have a standing grievance.
- Hesk: That is the worst thing anybody has ever said to me.
- Pip: Lovely. Zeph does the lamp, Bron does the noise, and I do the part with my hands.
- Ket: Take the near one. Working boiler, re-plated tracks. I have been looking at it since we sat down.
- Lyra: Watchmen sign off a sheet at the end of a shift. Wake one an hour early and he'll sign it and go home.
- Zeph: ...Sister.
- Lyra {hope}: Pass me the lamp oil.
- Hesk: ...Which gate.
