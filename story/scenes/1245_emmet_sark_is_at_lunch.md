# Scene 1245: Emmet Sark Is at Lunch

- type: talk
- location: a vast low room of desks in ranks under a glazed roof, every desk with a lamp, ledger trays, and a clipboard, brass message tubes running along the ceiling, flat clean daylight from directly above, midmorning
- characters: Emmet, Bron, Pip, Ollo
- mood: tense

## Beat
The mildly annoyed man from the folding table behind the ancient door now has a desk on Tier Five,
one of four thousand, and he remembers them without warmth and without alarm as the people who did
not have an entry chit. He is eating his lunch. He answers every question they ask, accurately,
because none of it is confidential, and he declines the one thing he cannot do and explains why in
one sentence that is not an apology. He is neither helping them nor obstructing them. Ollo, who has
spent eleven years being an employee, likes him immediately, and they discuss the canteen.

## Dialogue
- Emmet {tense}: You're the entry chit people.
- Pip: We are not the entry chit people.
- Emmet: You are, on my recollection, the entry chit people. This is my lunch. Ask what you came to ask.
- Bron: The board meeting.
- Emmet: Four o'clock. Sixth floor. Up the east stair from the end of this room; the west stair is shut for the sheaves.
- Bron: Can we get in.
- Emmet: Visitors sign the book at the stair head. Somebody with a floor number signs them in.
- Pip: Would you sign us in?
- Emmet: No. I have a desk number. A desk number is not a floor number. I'm not being difficult, I'm being accurate.
- Ollo: Is that the canteen down there? The one with the two doors?
- Emmet: That is the canteen. The pie is better on a Thursday. I have never established why and I have stopped enquiring.
- Ollo: Ha. Right. Right. It's the same everywhere.
