# Scene 1430: A Week Is a Long Time

- type: talk
- location: a squared stone archway at the low end of the street, its stone bar still across it, a stair dropping into darkness beyond it, a threshold step worn hollow, a lamp column on the right lighting from above, no daylight
- characters: Zeph, Ket, Sefa, Hesk, Bron
- mood: dread, then tense

## Beat
At the barred archway the chord is loud enough to feel in the jaw, and Zeph and Ket put it together
the only way this game allows: by arguing and correcting each other, each holding half of it. Ket
gives the numbers, Zeph gives the shape. There is a draw head at the bottom of that stair, the
smallest either of them has heard of, and it has been running since the doors shut, and it takes a
very little continuously out of every living thing standing in the city and holds them at a level,
the way a cistern is held at a level. Nobody down here was ever emptied. And the chord is mostly
them: twenty-eight generations of this street, rehearsing. Ket says plainly, once, that it is a
machine that was switched on and left. Sefa closes the scene by filing it.

## Dialogue
- Zeph {dread}: It is not louder here. It is exactly as loud as it was at the door. We are nearer to it.
- Ket: There is a draw head under that stair. Small. The smallest I have ever heard run.
- Ket: A bore head at Saltmouth is rated at four hundred. This is rated to take very little and never stop.
- Zeph: Never stop since when?
- Ket: Since the doors were shut. There is no wear on it anywhere for being off.
- Zeph: Then it has not emptied anybody. It cannot have. An inversion is nine seconds and it is done.
- Zeph: This takes the top off. Continuously. Off everything alive standing in that street.
- Ket: A cistern held at a level. Nobody notices a cistern being held at a level.
- Bron: How much does it take.
- Ket: About a week. I do not round, and I am saying about. About a week, and then about a week again.
- Zeph {tense}: ...Then the singing is not the builders. It is that street. Twenty-eight generations of it, going round.
- Ket: It is a machine. It was switched on and left. It has never wanted anything from anybody.
- Sefa: Built to hold a record, left running, and nobody ever came back down to read it.
- Sefa: Can anybody here name me a second institution that does that.
