# Scene 0694: Pip Cannot Joke

- type: talk
- location: a fenced salt yard with two salt-crawlers parked on wide plate tracks, a fuel tank on a timber stand, a heap of chain and hooks, a plank watch hut, hard flat afternoon light, salt glare
- characters: Bron, Pip
- mood: sorrow, then hope

## Beat
Pip sits down on the step of a parked crawler with two cups and makes a joke, and it does not work.
She makes another one and it does not work either. She keeps going, because for her stopping has
always been worse than failing, and then she runs out of jokes and there is nothing after them. What
she says instead is small and practical and entirely about footwear, and it is the closest she has
ever come to answering a question about herself out loud, and she does not explain why she is saying
it to him. Bron says one sentence about the boots that happens to be exactly the right thing, and he
does not know that it is, and he does not notice what she has told him. The cups go cold between them.

## Dialogue
- Pip {sorrow}: Tea. It's mostly tea. There's a man in the hut who says it's tea and I'm inclined to believe him.
- Pip: So a Kell, a sister and a very tall man walk into a counting house —
- Pip: ...no. That one's got nowhere to go either. I had three and they were all rubbish.
- Pip: ...
- Pip: My boots.
- Bron: Your boots.
- Pip: Care-house issue. You get one pair at six and one pair at twelve and that's the lot.
- Pip: I've had these since. Resoled four times. Fourth one was a man in Windrow who wouldn't take the money.
- Pip: Sixteen years and one pair of boots. They were very good boots.
- Bron: ...
- Bron: Four soles on one pair. Whoever made them knew exactly what they were doing.
- Pip {hope}: ...Yeah.
- Pip: Yeah. They did.
- Pip: Your tea's cold. Drink it anyway, it was a mark.
