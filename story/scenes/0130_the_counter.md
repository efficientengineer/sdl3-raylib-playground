# Scene 0130: The Counter

- type: talk
- map: halm
- location: the guild hall of {{HOME_TOWN}} in the afternoon: a long low stone room with a plank counter, an open double doorway on the street, and an empty job board on the wall behind the counter
- characters: {{HERO}}, Guildclerk, {{RIVAL}}
- mood: matter of fact, then stung
- dialogue_box: yes

## Beat
{{HERO}} has carried {{MENTOR}}'s repaired catch across town and put it on the counter, and while he is there he asks the question he has been asking all year. The clerk does not recite a list: he states the one rule that stops {{HERO}}, and when he is cut off he starts the sentence again from the beginning, which is what he does. {{RIVAL}} is at the counter with a new coat and a year's advantage, and he is kind about it out loud. The board on the wall is bare, because it is not dawn; what governs tomorrow comes out of {{RIVAL}} gloating, not out of a lecture, and the player is meant to remember it tomorrow. {{HERO}} leaves holding the rival's money.

## Dialogue
- Clerk (neutral) {wonder}: The catch goes on the counter. Is there anything else?
- {{HERO}} (resolve): Yes. When can I take a job?
- Clerk (neutral): No sword, no signature.
- {{HERO}} (concern): I don't have a sword.
- Clerk (neutral): The rule is that your teacher gives you your first sword —
- {{HERO}} (angry) {tense}: I've trained four years!
- Clerk (annoyed): The rule is that your teacher gives you your first sword. Then I write you in the guild book.
- {{HERO}} (resolve): I'll buy one, then.
- {{RIVAL}} (smile) {confront}: You can't buy one. Four years now, is it? Mine took two.
- {{HERO}} (annoyed): {{RIVAL}}. How long have you been standing there?
- {{RIVAL}} (neutral): Sheets go up at dawn. Don't be up a hill when the bell rings.
- {{RIVAL}} (smile): Here — three coin for your bread.
