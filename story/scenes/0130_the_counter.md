# Scene 0130: The Counter

- type: talk
- map: halm
- location: the guild hall of {{HOME_TOWN}} in the afternoon: a long low stone room with a plank counter, an open double doorway on the street, and an empty job board on the wall behind the counter
- characters: {{HERO}}, Guildclerk, {{RIVAL}}
- mood: matter of fact, then stung
- dialogue_box: yes

## Beat
{{HERO}} has carried {{MENTOR}}'s repaired part across town and put it on the counter, and while he is there he asks the question he has been asking all year. The clerk does not recite a manual: he gives the one answer that matters and then defends it, in order, without opinions, while {{HERO}} argues with every word of it. {{RIVAL}} is at the counter with a new coat and a year's advantage, and he is kind about it. The board on the wall is bare, because it is not dawn; the two rules that govern tomorrow come out of {{RIVAL}} gloating, not out of a lecture, and the player is meant to remember them tomorrow.

## Dialogue
- Clerk {wonder}: The repaired part goes on the counter. Is there anything else?
- {{HERO}}: Yes. When can I take a job?
- Clerk: No sword, no signature.
- {{HERO}}: Say that plainly.
- Clerk: An apprentice signs the guild book when his teacher says he is ready.
- {{HERO}} {tense}: I've trained four years!
- Clerk: A hunter's first sword comes from his teacher. It is the sign, and I look for it.
- {{HERO}}: Then I'll buy one myself.
- Clerk: You do not buy one. You are given one. That is the whole of the rule.
- {{HERO}}: {{RIVAL}}. Of course you're standing there.
- {{RIVAL}} {confront}: He's right. Mine took me two years to get.
- {{RIVAL}}: Job sheets go up at dawn and the board shuts on the last ring of the evening bell.
- {{RIVAL}}: So don't be up a hill at supper time. Here — three coin for your bread.
- {{HERO}} {hope}: I'll have a sword before you've spent it!
