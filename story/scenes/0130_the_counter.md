# Scene 0130: The Counter

- map: halm
- location: the guild hall of {{HOME_TOWN}} in the afternoon: a long low stone room with a plank counter, an open double doorway on the street, and an empty job board on the wall behind the counter
- characters: {{HERO}}, Guildclerk, {{RIVAL}}
- mood: matter of fact, then stung
- staging: the counter runs across the screen with Guildclerk behind it facing left and the bare job board on the wall behind him. {{HERO}} stands at the counter on the left facing right. {{RIVAL}} comes in from the street doorway at the right and takes the counter beside him, facing left
- dialogue_box: no

## Beat
{{HERO}} has carried {{MENTOR}}'s repaired catch across town and put it on the counter, and while he is there he asks the question he has been asking all year. The clerk does not recite a list: he states the one rule that stops {{HERO}}, and when he is cut off he starts the sentence again from the beginning, which is what he does. {{RIVAL}} is at the counter with a new coat and a year's advantage, and he is kind about it out loud. The board on the wall behind them is bare, because it is not dawn. {{HERO}} was sent out for bread with nothing in his pocket, and {{RIVAL}} puts the coin down for him where the clerk and the street can both see it. {{HERO}} leaves holding the rival's money.
