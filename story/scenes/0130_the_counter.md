# Scene 0130: The Counter

- type: talk
- map: halm
- location: the guild hall of {{HOME_TOWN}} in the afternoon: a long low stone room with a plank counter, an open double doorway on the street, and an empty job board on the wall behind the counter
- characters: {{HERO}}, Guildclerk, {{RIVAL}}
- mood: matter of fact, then stung
- dialogue_box: yes

## Beat
{{HERO}} has carried {{MENTOR}}'s repaired part across town and put it on the counter, and while he is there he asks the question he has been asking all year: when can he take a job? The clerk answers it the way he answers everything, in order, without opinions — the board's two rules, and then the one that stops {{HERO}} dead. {{RIVAL}} is at the counter with a new coat and a year's advantage, and he is kind about it, which is worse. Nothing is on the board: it is not dawn. This is the only place in the chapter where the rules are said, and the player is meant to remember them tomorrow.

## Dialogue
- Clerk {wonder}: The repaired part. Put it on the counter.
- {{HERO}}: {{MENTOR}} cut a new catch for the well winch. He says this one is the right size.
- Clerk: I will tell the hall keeper. Is there anything else?
- {{HERO}}: Yes. When can I take a job?
- Clerk: The rule is that job sheets go up on that board at dawn.
- {{HERO}}: At dawn. And then what happens?
- Clerk: The board stays open all day. It closes on the last ring of the evening bell.
- {{HERO}}: One bell, at the end of the day. I can hear that bell from the yard.
- Clerk: You can. The board is bare now because it is not dawn.
- {{HERO}}: Then I'll be standing here at dawn and I'll sign for the first sheet up!
- Clerk: You will not. I cannot write your name in the book.
- {{HERO}} {tense}: Why not? I've trained four years!
- Clerk: No sword, no signature.
- {{HERO}}: What does that mean? Say it plainly.
- Clerk: An apprentice signs for a job when his teacher says he is ready.
- {{HERO}}: My teacher is up a hill. How would you know what he says?
- Clerk: A hunter's first sword comes from his teacher. It is the sign. I look for the sword.
- {{HERO}}: So I go and buy a sword.
- Clerk: You do not buy one. You are given one. That is the whole of the rule.
- {{RIVAL}} {confront}: He's right, {{HERO}}. Mine took me two years to get.
- {{HERO}}: Two years?
- {{RIVAL}}: Two. I've had it a year now, so I sign for whatever I like.
- {{HERO}}: You're one year older than me!
- {{RIVAL}}: Here. Four coin for your bread. Pay me back when you sign for something.
- {{HERO}}: I don't want your four coin, {{RIVAL}}!
- {{RIVAL}}: Take it. I'd rather you owed me than went home short.
- {{HERO}} {hope}: I'll have a sword before you've spent it!
