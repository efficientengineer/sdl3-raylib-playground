# Scene 1225: The Quiet House

- type: talk
- location: a small boarding-house bedroom upstairs, an iron bedstead against the right wall, a black stove with a full scuttle beside it, a chair with a folded coat, one sash window shuttered, warm stove light from the left, night
- characters: Rask, Bron, Ivo
- mood: sorrow

## Beat
An old man in a narrow bed who has been awake two days and would like the company. One other of the
party is in the room and does not speak. He talks about a dog, a tram strike and a wife who died
first, and then, because it is late and because nobody has ever let him, about a street at four in
the morning in 1093. He does not know who he is talking to and never will. Bron files what he hears
and asks nothing about it. Then Bron does the Tally from memory, gets a question out of order,
corrects himself and keeps going, and it works anyway, because a man did not die on his own.
Afterwards a very large man pays on the doorstep, full rate, on completion, and asks nothing.

## Dialogue
- Rask {sorrow}: Sit where I can see you. I've been awake two days and I've run out of ceiling.
- Rask: I had a dog would open that door. Not a clever dog otherwise. One trick, nine years.
- Rask: My wife went first. Eleven years back now. That's the wrong way round and nobody warns you.
- Rask: Were you in for the tram strike? Three weeks. I stood a line at Windrow yard in the rain and I was happy.
- Bron: I was a boy.
- Rask: ...I'll tell you a thing, then. Nobody's ever let me, and it's late enough for it now.
- Rask: Four in the morning, ninety-three. Four hundred people stood in a street looking at us. Pleasant. Every one of them.
- Rask: The grown ones walked when you told them. The children wouldn't. So you picked them up and you carried them.
- Rask: My warden carried one the whole four hundred yards himself. Wouldn't set him in the cart till the end of it.
- Rask: I've not seen that man carry so much as a lamp since.
- Bron: ...
- Bron {hope}: Do you know where you are. Do you know who is holding your hand.
- Bron: What did you eat last. What was the weather. — No. What is your name. What name did your mother use.
- Bron: That's out of order. I'll start again and go slower. I learned it off somebody.
- Rask: Go slow. I've nowhere to be.
- Ivo: Full rate, on completion. Count it before you go down the step.
- Ivo: ...Thank you for the hour.
