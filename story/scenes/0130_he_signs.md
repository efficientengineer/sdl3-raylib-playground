# Scene 0130: He Signs

- map: hart_yard
- type: talk
- location: the same yard after the practice posts, near dark: the guild book held open on {{HEALER}}'s forearm and {{MENTOR}} down off the roof at last, sitting on the bottom rung of the ladder
- characters: {{HERO}}, {{HEALER}}, {{MENTOR}}
- mood: worn down, settled
- backdrop: 0120_youre_not_ready:1

## Beat
An hour of practice posts later, and {{MENTOR}} has come down. {{HEALER}} has the guild book open and
does the arithmetic out loud until he signs, because the point she is making is that she is going too.
He signs, gives the whole job in two sentences, and then hands over the only two rules that matter at
the far end of it. {{HERO}} has never been out that far and says so, and the thing he asks about is
the woman, not the road. {{HEALER}} has been waiting to be asked along since the first line of the
scene.

## Dialogue
- {{HEALER}} {tense}: Sign it, {{MENTOR}}. Four days, not two — two west and two back — and I am the one who patches whoever goes.
- {{MENTOR}}: Jar on the stone shelf under the bottom step. Straight back.
- {{HERO}}: What's at the step? I've never been out that far.
- {{MENTOR}} {wonder}: A staircase the ancients built. A woman lives under it and takes the jar once a year. Don't touch the stone. Don't go past her shelf.
- {{HERO}}: A woman lives under it? {{HEALER}} — you've walked that road twice. Come with me?
- {{HEALER}} {hope}: Yes! And he has never once asked me to come anywhere until today.
