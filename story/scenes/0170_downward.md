# Scene 0170: Downward

- type: narration
- mood: time passing underground

## Beat
Hours of descent between the second floor and the great door. The halls get older as they go down
and the work gets better, not worse. And under everything there is a pitch none of them mentions.

## Dialogue
- Narrator {wonder}: Three floors down, the cut changed. The blocks got bigger and the joints got finer.
- Narrator: Nobody above ground builds like that. Nobody above ground ever has.
- Narrator {wonder}: The air got warmer as they went down, which is the wrong way round, and none of them said so.
- Narrator {dread}: And under everything, at the edge of hearing, there was a pitch you feel in your teeth rather than hear.
- Narrator {dread}: None of them mentioned it to the others for four hours.
