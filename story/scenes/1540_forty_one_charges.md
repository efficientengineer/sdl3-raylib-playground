# Scene 1540: Forty-One Charges

- location: a long flight of broad cut-stone steps between dressed walls, iron handrails on both sides, a lamp stanchion at the head, cable coils stacked against the left wall, faint even light rising from below at the right, no daylight
- characters: Kerrow, Hanna, Bron
- mood: tense, then confront
- staging: The stair descends from the upper left toward the chamber at the lower right. Kerrow and the company come down from the left and above, facing right and down. Bron and the party stand lower on the stair at the right, facing left and up. The handcarts and the lamps are behind Kerrow at the left

## Beat
She comes down the stair with an engineering party, a Pale company under Sergeant Kesk, and
forty-one shaped charges on two handcarts. She is the coldest-looking person in a cold place and
she is also, visibly and badly, cold, and she will not mention it. She explains what she is doing
before anyone asks, because she does not lie and can see no use in a pause. The charges are dull,
plain, industrial objects in straw. There is no braid on her coat, no escort flourish, no speech.
She gives her window in hours rather than days and gives the reason for it in one sentence, and the
reason is correct. Sergeant Kesk says one word in a particular tone and it is the only sound anyone
on the company side makes. Then Kerrow asks the party a question, and the rest of the chapter is
the party failing to answer it.

## Dialogue
- Kerrow [1] {tense}: You will want to know what this is. I'll tell you and save us both the pause.
- Kerrow [2]: Forty-one shaped charges. Roof beams the length of this flight, and the lintel at the Ninth Door.
- Kerrow [3]: Two thousand feet of hill onto both approaches, and onto the face.
- Kerrow [3] {confront}: After that nobody draws anything out of the Seam again. Not this company. Not anyone.
- Bron [4]: How long have you got.
- Kerrow [5]: Hours. When the branch inverts the head is scrap, and I can't place a charge off a scrap head.
- Kerrow [6]: Sergeant. Nobody fires first and nobody fires at all. Put that in the book and initial it.
- Hanna [6]: ...Sir.
- Kerrow [7]: Now. Which of you is going to tell me something I haven't already costed.
