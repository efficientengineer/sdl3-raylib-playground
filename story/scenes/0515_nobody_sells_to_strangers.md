# Scene 0515: Nobody Sells to Strangers

- type: talk
- location: stepped barley terraces walled in dry stone, half of them bare brown earth, hand-dug water channels and stacked hoes, a low stone hut at the field corner, hard clear morning light from the left
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Pip
- mood: closed doors, no rudeness

## Beat
The village will not sell them food, will not rent them a room, and will not be rude about any of
it. A woman carrying two water yokes answers Bron the way a Kell answers: the yield in weights, nine
years of it, and then the reason. Half the young are down on company crews and the money comes up
the hill and the barley does not. She clocks Bron as Kell, asks which hold he is out of, and does
not make anything of the answer. Then she gives him half her water, because he is Kell and he is
carrying none, and tells him where the council sits and that they will not talk to him either.

## Dialogue
- Kell woman {tense}: No. There is nothing to sell you. The barley came in at four hundredweight.
- Kell woman: It was eleven when my father walked these steps. That is the reason, and now you have it.
- Bron: We can pay.
- Kell woman: Paying is not the trouble. Having it is.
- Kell woman: Half the young are down on company crews. The money comes up the hill. The barley does not.
- Kell woman: You're Kell. Which hold are you out of?
- Bron: Tellwater.
- Kell woman: That is not a hold. That is a town.
- Bron: It's where I'm from.
- Kell woman: Mm. Hold that out.
- Kell woman: Half. You're Kell and you're carrying no water at all, which is the stupidest thing I have seen this month.
- Bron: Thanks.
- Kell woman: Council sits in the stone hall at the top lane. They will not talk to you either.
