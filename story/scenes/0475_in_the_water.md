# Scene 0475: In the Water

- type: talk
- location: a broad Vess stone stair descending between carved plain pilasters, a company handrail bolted crudely over the old stonework, a lamp on a bracket at the turn, water sound below, lamplight from above, no daylight
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip
- mood: hope
- backdrop: 0470_the_drowned_stacks:4

## Beat
Four cold wet people on a dry landing halfway down, and nothing happens for a quarter of an hour.
Bron runs hot and is the only one comfortable, which Pip takes as a personal insult and argues at
length and loses. Zeph is trying to dry a page by waving it, which is not working, and will not stop.
Lyra takes her boots off and wrings out a stocking and says something in a completely level voice
that nobody is sure about for three seconds. Bron says ten minutes. Nobody moves for twenty, and
none of them minds.

## Dialogue
- Pip {hope}: You're not even shivering.
- Bron: No.
- Pip: You went in the same water. Exactly the same water. To the same height.
- Bron: I did.
- Pip: And you're sat there like a bread oven while I lose the feeling in my face.
- Bron: You can sit nearer if you want.
- Pip: I don't want to sit nearer, I want you to suffer. It's about fairness.
- Zeph: If I move it fast enough the water leaves. This is established.
- Zeph: ...It is not established.
- Lyra: There's a hundred years of Vess scholarship in that water, and a good pint of it is in my boot.
- Pip: ...
- Bron: Ten minutes. Then we go on.
