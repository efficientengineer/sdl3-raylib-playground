# Scene 1575: What Was in the File

- type: talk
- location: a high stone hall lined with iron shelf stacks of paper folders to the ceiling, a rolling ladder on a floor rail, a long counter across the front, hanging lamps in a row, flat even lamplight, no windows
- characters: Emmet
- mood: tense, then hope

## Beat
Two hundred miles away and eight hours into the chant, the best-lit room in the Basin is almost
empty, and Emmet Sark is clearing the last docket of his shift because it is his docket. In it is a
relay station log for the night of the 9th of Fallow, 1093, with two entries on it, and the second
one is timed 22:02 and says six words. It has been in that drawer for fourteen years because nobody
ever had a reason to take it out. A junior clerk, who has been reading the same rumours as everyone
else, suggests holding it until they see which way things go. Sark does not deliberate, does not
have an opinion about the Clement Works, and does not know what he has. He states the document's
class, indexes it, copies it to the inquiry office because that is where a document of that class
goes, and puts his coat on. Then he asks about the lift, and he means it entirely sincerely.

## Dialogue
- Emmet {tense}: Docket eleven of eleven. Relay station log, Tellwater line, ninth of Fallow, ninety-three.
- Emmet: One sheet. Two entries. Twenty-one forty out. Twenty-two oh two in.
- Clerk: What does the second one say?
- Emmet: Continue, per season order.
- Clerk: ...That's the night everybody's talking about.
- Emmet: It's a relay log. It's been in that drawer fourteen years because that's the drawer it goes in.
- Clerk: Shouldn't we hold it? Until we see which way it all goes.
- Emmet: Which way what goes?
- Clerk: ...All of it. Vantage. The whole thing.
- Emmet {hope}: The file is the file. Class four. Cross-index to the inquiry office, two copies, one retained.
- Emmet: If it were not that class I would not index it that way. It is that class.
- Emmet: Right. Coat.
- Emmet: Have you an entry chit for the lift? After ten the rules about the lift are very clear.
