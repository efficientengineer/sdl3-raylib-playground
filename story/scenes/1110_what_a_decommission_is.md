# Scene 1110: What a Decommission Is

- type: talk
- location: a small sandstone office on the square with a plank counter across it, a trestle desk set outside the propped door, a rack of pigeonholes behind, a tally board with chalk marks, flat grey noon light from the left
- characters: Ondra, Bron, Zeph, Hesk
- mood: tense
- backdrop: 1105_the_postings_come_down:1

## Beat
The clerk closing the town explains the closing of the town the way you would explain a tram
timetable: dates, schedules, the word personnel. She is not hiding anything and she is not sorry,
because as far as she can see there is nothing to be sorry about yet. Zeph, asking about paper the
way other people ask about weather, gets her to say out loud that one book holds all sixty names
and what each of them was hired to be. Hesk asks the question nobody has asked. Pell answers it
honestly, which is that she does not know.

## Dialogue
- Ondra {tense}: Postings close on the 11th. The roster's sealed on the 12th and goes down to the Registry at Vantage.
- Ondra: Buildings pass to a caretaker contract. Personnel are redistributed against vacancies.
- Hesk: Against vacancies. Meaning wherever there's a hole.
- Ondra: Meaning wherever there's a hole, yes. I'd not have put it that way, but I'd not argue with it.
- Zeph: The roster. Is that one book, or several?
- Ondra: One book. Sixty names, sixty trades, date of posting, date of close.
- Zeph: Sixty names, and what each of them was hired to be. In one book. With a clasp on it.
- Ondra: With a clasp on it. There's tea inside if anyone wants it, the urn's still hot.
- Bron: Does the bakery go?
- Ondra: The oven stays. Ovens are fixtures. The baker doesn't; he's personnel.
- Hesk: What happens to a town that stops being a town?
- Ondra: I've never done one before. I wrote and asked. The answer was four pages and I still don't know.
