# Scene 1020: The Man On The Bench

- type: talk
- location: a public bench of grey planks against a brick wall, a bent lamp post beside it, pigeons on the cobbles, a folded coat left on the seat, flat grey noon light with no shadows
- characters: Aldo, Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Hesk
- mood: warm, then cold

## Beat
Aldo Fessel is seventy-one, forty-one years on the spur, nineteen of them as inspector, and he has
the coat on because he puts it on to sit out. He is delighted to be asked anything at all. The
autumn of ninety-three is the clearest fortnight of his working life and he tells it the way a man
tells a story about a hard job done well: the carts, the batches, the rows of quiet people, the
clerks working out of the goods shed with a table and a box of surnames off a survey map. He was
seconded to sign because they were short of hands and he had the best copperplate on the spur. He
is not defensive, because nobody has ever accused him of anything. He says twice that it was all
done very kindly. He has no idea who Bron is, and Bron does not tell him, and nobody in the party
works out what they are listening to.

## Dialogue
- Aldo {hope}: Forty-one years on this spur. Inspector the last nineteen of them. Sit down, sit down, the bench holds four.
- Lyra: The autumn of ninety-three. Were you working then?
- Aldo: Ninety-three. Busiest fortnight of my life. Forty carts down from the north in three batches — fourteen, fourteen, twelve.
- Bron: Carts of what?
- Aldo: People. Sat in rows with their hands in their laps, waiting to be called. Not one raised voice in a fortnight.
- Hesk: Who was doing the calling?
- Aldo: Clerks, out of the goods shed. A table, a chair, and a box of surnames taken off a survey map.
- Aldo: They were short of hands, so they had me in to sign. I've a good hand. Copperplate. My mother saw to that.
- Lyra: You signed.
- Aldo: Where I was told, and glad to. They fed everybody. Hot, twice a day, at the company's expense.
- Hesk: ...
- Aldo: It was all done very kindly. I've always been glad I could help. You don't often get to be useful at that size of a thing.
- Lyra {dread}: Has anybody asked you about that fortnight before?
- Aldo: In fourteen years? Not once. It's been a pleasure.
