# Scene 0625: Tibb and Sela

- type: talk
- location: a long shed of double plank bunks with hanging blankets between them, salt dust drifted along the duckboard gangway, a row of tin basins, a stove at the far end, low sun entering from the right, evening
- characters: Bron, Pip, Lyra, Tibb, Sela
- mood: the best hour of the week

## Beat
Two hunters off the Sallowgate board, ninety miles from home on a bill of their own, eating badly on
the end of a bunk and delighted to see faces they know. Tibb takes the bills the party turns down and
says so proudly and at length, the near-fatal one included. Sela does the thing a partner of four
years does: she lets him tell it and then supplies the one fact that ruins it, and every number he
gives she quietly gives back one smaller. They argue about whose round it is by reference to a ledger
entry from four years ago and neither of them will look it up. Nobody mentions the bore, the payroll
or anything else that matters. At the end Sela looks at Bron properly for the first time and asks him
a question that is not about the work, and does not accept his answer.

## Dialogue
- Tibb {hope}: No. No. Tell me you didn't clear it. Tell me it went to somebody else.
- Bron: We cleared it.
- Tibb: Triple rate.
- Sela: He has said "triple rate" since Turn. Twice in his sleep. I have a witness and the witness is me.
- Tibb: Because it was! Never mind. We've had four since. Four bills. One of them nearly had me.
- Sela: Three. The fourth is tomorrow and it is a drain.
- Tibb: The culvert one nearly had me.
- Sela: A ladder nearly had him. He fell off a ladder.
- Tibb: Off a ladder. Into a culvert. There is a difference and it is the culvert.
- Pip: Whose round is it?
- Tibb: Hers. Ledger, Sallowgate, second of Rime, eleven oh-three. It is written down and it is hers.
- Sela: It says I owe him a round. It does not say when. I have read it more carefully than he has.
- Sela {sorrow}: Bron. You look tired. Are you eating?
- Bron: Yes.
- Sela: That isn't what I asked.
