# Scene 0320: The Fair Poles

- type: talk
- location: an open-sided timber pole store at a fair ground, bare fair frames stacked in racks along one wall, a plank loft above with coiled bunting rope on it, trodden bare ground outside, hard afternoon sun from the right
- characters: Ollo, Bron, Pip
- mood: hope

## Beat
Side contract, scene one of three. Ollo wants to hire them directly and will not be talked into
calling it a favour, which produces a small crisis about the third Line: a bill needs a seal, and
the only seal Ollo owns is the one he presses into the loaves. He offers it without a flicker of
embarrassment and Bron accepts it without a flicker of amusement, which is what makes it funny.
The job is to fetch the frames and the bunting out of the store and carry them in, because the
autumn fair is three weeks earlier than last year, which was a fortnight earlier than the year
before. Everyone in the room finds this entirely ordinary and nobody remarks on it. The store is
full of neatly labelled poles and the field outside has nobody in it.

## Dialogue
- Ollo {hope}: I'll pay. I'm not asking a favour off you. I'll pay.
- Bron: Then it's a bill, and a bill needs a seal. Third Line. No seal, no bill.
- Ollo: A seal.
- Pip: Anything that leaves a mark and is yours and nobody else's.
- Ollo: ...Will this do? It's for the loaves. Wheat ear, and a chip out of the top of it.
- Bron: That's a seal.
- Pip: That's the best seal I have ever seen on a bill and I want it noted.
- Ollo: Frames are in the racks. Long ones out first. Rope's in the loft and it wants two of you.
- Bron: When's the fair?
- Ollo: Nine days. Early this year. Three weeks up on last year.
- Pip: Three weeks?
- Ollo: Last year was a fortnight up on the one before. You want it warm, you have it early. Everyone does.
- Bron: And the fee.
- Ollo {hope}: Bread. Paid on completion — I do know the rules, there's a board at Windrow and I read the whole of it while I waited.
