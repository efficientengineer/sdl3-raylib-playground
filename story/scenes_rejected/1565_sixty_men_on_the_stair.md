# Scene 1565: Sixty Men on the Stair

- location: the top of a broad cut-stone stair entering a chamber through a squared doorway, iron handrails, stacked crates of company cap charges under canvas, lamp stanchions on the landing, lamplight from above
- characters: Orrin, Ivo, Tibb, Kerrow, Bron
- mood: tense, then hope
- staging: The stair runs down to the right toward the chamber. Cadder's crew stand across it in three ranks facing left and up. Kerrow, the stopped handcarts and the squared doorway are at the top of the stair on the left, and Teach fills that doorway. Everyone on the stair has their back to the chamber

## Beat
Two hours into the chant, and Kerrow's window closes because of people and nothing else. Orrin
Cadder comes down the Long Bore shaft with sixty of his own crew, having shut the bore head off on
the way past against a direct order, which is the second time in three days he has been relieved of
a post he no longer holds. He puts his crew across the stair in three ranks because that is how you
hold a gallery, and he tells the chairman of the Clement Works so, cheerfully, by her first name.
Warden Teach, dismissed, unarmed and holding nothing, stands in the doorway at the head of the
flight and does not threaten anybody, which as always is very much worse. Tibb Orsk comes off the
maintenance rail with eleven hunters and a bill in his fist with a company seal on it, and asks the
only question a hunter asks, and gets the wrong answer, and stays. Nobody fights. The Pale are under
orders not to fire first and every one of them knows it.

## Dialogue
- Orrin [1] {tense}: Three ranks across, and sit down, front rank. It's a gallery. You hold a gallery by being in it.
- Orrin [2]: Ottoline. I shut your bore head off on the way past. Valve's chained and I've got the chain.
- Kerrow [3]: You were relieved of your post on the fifth.
- Orrin [3]: I was. Nobody's told the crews and I'm not going to be the one that does.
- Ivo [4]: Superintendent Orrin Cadder. Chairman Ottoline Kerrow.
- Ivo [4]: No warrant, nothing in my hands. I'm standing in a door. That's all I'm doing.
- Tibb [5]: Who's paying for this? There's a company seal on it and the fee line says nothing.
- Bron [6]: Nobody's paying. That's what it says.
- Tibb [7]: ...Right. Shove up, then. Eleven of us. Tovin sent the whole board.
- Orrin [8] {hope}: I burned a seal off a bill once. Got four hunters, a wrecked rig and a dinner. Poor trade.
