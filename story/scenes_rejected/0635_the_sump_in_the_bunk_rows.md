# Scene 0635: The Sump in the Bunk Rows

- type: talk
- location: a sunken plank-lidded drain sump between two bunk sheds, grey water standing in it, a standpipe with a brass meter box above, a dropped tin bucket on the salt, hard flat noon light from overhead, no shadows
- characters: Bron, Pip, Tibb, Sela
- mood: a good week

## Beat
Tibb and Sela's bill is a fouled drain sump under the bunk rows. It pays badly, it stinks, and Tibb
describes it as a career highlight without a trace of irony. The four of them work it for an hour and
talk complete rubbish for all of it. Sela does the one competent thing anybody does in the scene and
does not point it out. Then Tibb, who has clearly been working up to this since yesterday, asks Pip
whether she has ever thought about partnering with somebody, and makes a mess of it, and means every
word. Payday is the ninth, which is in two days, and Sela says so, and nobody in the yard thinks
anything of it at all.

## Dialogue
- Tibb {hope}: Now this. This is a bill. Six marks, a day's work, and the smell is free.
- Pip: You have been down there eleven minutes and you have not stopped smiling.
- Tibb: Nine minutes.
- Sela: Eleven. Rod's in the wrong way round, Tibb. Hook goes under.
- Tibb: The hook was under.
- Sela: The hook is under now.
- Bron: Bar or bucket?
- Sela: Bucket. You'll want the bar in a minute and you'll want it dry.
- Tibb: Pip. Can I ask you a thing. Not a bill thing.
- Pip: That is the worst opening anybody has ever used on me and I have been in taverns.
- Tibb: Have you ever thought about going out with somebody. Partnered. Signed together, both names on the line.
- Tibb: Four out and four back is easier with two. That's all. That's the whole of the thing I'm asking.
- Pip: ...
- Sela {hope}: Leave her be. Payday's the ninth. We'll stand the lot of you a drink and he'll spill it.
