# Scene 0560: I Should Have Noticed

- type: talk
- optional: yes
- location: a squared door-mouth in sandstone filled with fitted cut-stone blocks, an iron door-key ring set in the centre stone, worn steps in front of it, a stacked cairn to one side, flat noon light
- characters: Bron, Ket, Hesk
- mood: sorrow, going hard
- backdrop: 0555_company_mortar:1

## Beat
Hesk reports the count to nobody, out loud, twice, and gets the same number both times. Three of
nine, on her cliff, in her year, with a company seal pressed in at knee height. She says she would
have noticed and then she says she should have noticed, and the second one is the one that lands.
Bron does the only useful thing he knows, which is to ask what she needs. She has no answer, which
is new for her. Ket says one flat sentence about a thousand years of asking politely, and neither
Hesk nor the player can tell whether it is comfort or the worst thing anyone has ever said to her.

## Dialogue
- Hesk {sorrow}: Three of nine. Third, Seventh, Ninth.
- Hesk: ...Three of nine. Third, Seventh, Ninth.
- Hesk: Forty feet of fill and a company seal. By the Fifth, I'd have noticed. I should have noticed.
- Bron: Say what you need and I'll carry it.
- Hesk: ...
- Hesk: I don't know. That's new.
- Ket: My people asked politely for a thousand years and were answered politely every time.
- Hesk: Is that meant to be a comfort, keeper?
- Ket: I do not know. I have had eleven hundred years to decide and I have not.
- Hesk: ...
- Hesk {tense}: Council sits at first light. They'll have the count off me before they've eaten.
- Hesk: They'll thank me for it.
