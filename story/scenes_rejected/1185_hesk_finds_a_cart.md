# Scene 1185: Hesk Finds a Cart

- type: talk
- location: a walled yard of swept flagstones behind a sandstone office, a two-wheeled handcart standing on its shafts, stacked empty crates under a canvas sheet, a water butt at the wall, low evening sun from the right
- characters: Hesk, Ondra, Bron, Zeph
- mood: sorrow

## Beat
Hesk is the only one functioning, so Hesk does everything: finds a cart, argues the price down,
gets the address of the house at Sallowgate off the clerk, and sets people tasks in the order the
tasks need doing. She is not comforting anybody and she is not gentle about any of it; her whole
kindness is in the allocation of work. Pell, who has signed forty forms this week, is the one who
cannot get to the end of a sentence, and she hands over the roster unsealed and does not write
down that she did. When Bron asks for something to do he is given something slightly beneath him
and entirely necessary, and he does it.

## Dialogue
- Hesk {sorrow}: Cart and a mule to Sallowgate. He wanted nine marks. It's a four-mark road; he's taking six.
- Hesk: Zeph. Names and times, in order, starting at the cordon. Don't make them pretty.
- Zeph: ...Nine forty. I have it as nine forty.
- Bron: Nine forty. It goes in the letter. That was asked for.
- Hesk: Write it down. Then the address of the house at Sallowgate.
- Ondra: It's — the Order's at the — I had it copied out for you, I put it somewhere, I—
- Ondra: I'm sorry. I've signed forty forms this week and I can't get to the end of this one.
- Ondra: Take the roster. It isn't sealed. I won't be writing down that you have it.
- Hesk: Then you can't sign the transfer on the 12th.
- Ondra: No. I'll say the book went down on the cart, and I'll be a signature short, and they'll ask me about it in Rime.
- Bron: What do you want me to do.
- Hesk: Water the mule and go over his feet. Both of them wrong and we're walking.
