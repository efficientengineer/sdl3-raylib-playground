# Scene 0750: The Empty Fair Ground

- type: talk
- location: the market shelf after the fair has closed for the night, timber stalls shuttered under striped awnings, the strung lamps taken in, bunting still up overhead, a plank stage standing empty, cold dark
- characters: Lyra, Pip
- mood: sorrow, then hope

## Beat
Optional, and nothing in it moves the plot. Pip is sitting on a shuttered stall in the dark, on her
own, a long way from the bar. Lyra comes and sits down beside her without asking and without asking
anything. They talk about boots for a while, properly, the way you talk about boots. Then Lyra says
the one thing she has been carrying since Tellwater, plainly and once, and makes no offer and no
comfort out of it and does not look at her while she says it. Pip takes a moment and then does what
Pip does. Lyra laughs at the joke, out loud and properly, which almost nobody has ever heard her do.
Then they sit on the crate in the dark under the bunting and neither of them says anything else.

## Dialogue
- Pip {sorrow}: They take the lamps in and they leave the bunting up. That's optimism, that is.
- Lyra: May I sit down?
- Pip: It's a crate.
- Lyra: It's a very good crate.
- Pip: These are good boots, though. Four soles, same uppers, and the uppers will see me out.
- Lyra: I've known since Tellwater. I've said it to nobody, and I won't say it again after tonight unless you want it said.
- Pip: ...
- Pip: Nobody leaves a care-house with anything but the boots. At least I got the good ones.
- Lyra: Ha. ...That is very good.
- Pip {hope}: Don't. You'll only encourage me.
