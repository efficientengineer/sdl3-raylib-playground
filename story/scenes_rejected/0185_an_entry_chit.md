# Scene 0185: An Entry Chit

- type: talk
- location: a squared cut-stone chamber with a canvas company tent pitched inside it, a folding table with a lamp and a clipboard, crates stencilled without words, a stack of survey rods, lamplight from the table, no daylight
- characters: Emmet, Bron, Zeph, Lyra, Pip
- mood: dread, then tense

## Beat
The argument, which Emmet Sark wins without raising his voice, because he has the forms and they
have a bill. He is not evasive: he answers everything he is allowed to answer, and the list of
things he is allowed to answer is short, and he is not ashamed of it. He cannot say who posted
their bill. He can say the ward trip was logged at ten past four, which is the hour they set it
off, and that the survey party comes down at seven, and that four hunters have just saved them four
days. Then he does the party a kindness, because their bill wants proof of completion and he has a
pen and a stamp. Lyra asks one more question than he can answer and he does not answer it.

## Dialogue
- Bron {dread}: Who posted our bill?
- Emmet: That didn't come through me. I'm not able to say.
- Zeph: The seal. Somebody burned the seal off it. Who?
- Emmet: You'd have to ask the poster. I am sorry.
- Bron: What time did we set the wards off?
- Emmet: Ten past four. It's logged. The survey party comes down at seven.
- Emmet: Six of them and a theodolite. A clearance of this kind was determined to be four days' work.
- Emmet: You've saved them the four days.
- Zeph: Determined.
- Pip: Zeph.
- Lyra: Who is it that decides a door like that gets opened?
- Emmet: ...
- Emmet {tense}: Your bill will want proof of completion. May I?
- Emmet: Countersigned. Stamped. That's your clearance. Good walk up, all of you.
