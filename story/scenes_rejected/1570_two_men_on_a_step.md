# Scene 1570: Two Men on a Step

- type: talk
- location: a wide cut-stone landing at the foot of a stair, a stone bench along the back wall, a dropped lamp on the flags, the chamber doorway open at the right with faint even light coming through it, no daylight
- characters: Orrin, Ivo, Bron
- mood: sorrow
- backdrop: 1565_sixty_men_on_the_stair:7

## Beat
Hour six of eleven. The chant carries on below and is audible through the doorway and nobody
mentions it. The two men who between them put a nineteen-year-old in a road at Tellwater are sitting
on the same step and do not say so. They talk about the stair, the air, the water channel, and
whether the tin whistle is in tune, which it is not and has not been since eighty-nine. Teach asks
a question he already knows the answer to, the way he has asked questions his whole career, and
Cadder answers it honestly and without a joke in front of it, which is his entire character. The
only time either of them comes near the thing itself, Cadder repeats the last sentence he said to
the boy that morning and then offers Bron bread, and Bron takes it. All the way down the stair
behind them food is going hand to hand between company crew and hunters who have never met, and
that is the whole of what the game has to say about what these people are doing for each other.

## Dialogue
- Orrin {sorrow}: Air's better down here than it is up at the head. I'd not have guessed that.
- Ivo: It's the channel. They cut it to move air. A thousand years on and it still moves air.
- Ivo: That's flat.
- Orrin: It's been flat since eighty-nine. Went in a sump. I've grown fond of it flat.
- Ivo: Orrin. Whose order put a two-day constable in a road by himself?
- Orrin: Mine. There was nobody else on the sheet and I never asked for anybody else.
- Ivo: ...Mm.
- Orrin: Last thing I said to him was, nobody has to get hurt. Just stand in the road.
- Orrin: Ten words. I've had them in my mouth for a month and I can't put them down.
- Orrin: Bread?
- Bron: Yes.
- Orrin: Three days old and carried a mile down a stair. Eat it anyway. It's what there is.
- Bron: ...
- Orrin: ...Whistle's still flat.
- Ivo: Constable Wen Arden.
