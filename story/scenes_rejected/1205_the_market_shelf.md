# Scene 1205: The Market Shelf

- location: a wide shop-lined shelf under a stone overhang, awnings in company colours, barrels and crates in ranks, hanging lamps strung along the shopfronts, a low stone parapet over the drop, warm evening lamplight from the right
- characters: Bron, Pip
- mood: warm, crowded, wrong
- staging: The party crosses the shelf from the left of frame toward the right. Up-tier, the lit stairs and the shopfronts are on the right and above; the parapet and the drop are on the left. The crowd moves both ways across the frame

## Beat
The warmest, brightest, busiest street in the Basin, on an ordinary evening, full of people
enjoying themselves. Ollo has never been anywhere like it and is openly delighted for about four
minutes, until he prices a loaf. Hesk hates the heat and says so continuously, which is normal for
her and therefore restful. Bron stops dead in the middle of the crowd for no reason he could name
and the crowd goes round him. The party's walking order is one wide, and Pip is holding the shape
of it without looking at it, and nobody says a word about that. Then they set the plan in three
lines and go up.

## Dialogue
- Ollo [1] {hope}: Nine kinds of salt in one window. Nine of them. Who is buying the ninth?
- Hesk [1]: They have roofed a street and lit it. Forty thousand people living in an oven.
- Ollo [2] {tense}: ...That is a better loaf than mine. And it costs a third of a night's warmth.
- Pip [2]: Eat it anyway. You'll tell us what's wrong with it and we'll all enjoy that.
- Bron [3]: ...
- Pip [4]: He does that. Walk round him, he catches up.
- Bron [5] {tense}: Tier Six. That's the top one. The old man's up there.
- Hesk [5]: Then we get him in a room and he says it out loud with the names in it.
