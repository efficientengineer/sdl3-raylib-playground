# Scene 1645: Registrar and Reader

- type: talk
- location: a tall narrow stone hall with a stepped floor of shallow tiers, long seating ledges cut into the walls, a shallow central water basin, high slit openings along the right wall, cool daylight falling in bars, midday
- characters: Zeph, Sefa, Ilven, Cassa
- mood: hope

## Beat
Dust 1108, the second checking of the year, which takes two days and is extremely boring, which is
the point. On the second day a bonded pair in the third line says the wrong verb in the account of
22:02 and Ilven Tessen sends the whole line back to the top of the hour over one word. Nobody is
embarrassed and nobody argues; that is what checking is. Sefa Quill, struck off the Registry and now
teaching eleven Vess clerks to file in a room on the floor above, comes down to listen from the
doorway and does not come in and is not invited. She and her son address each other by title, with
total correctness and no warmth at all, and both of them find it privately funny and neither will
ever say so. The annex lease still runs to 1187 and nobody has got it back. Cassa, who counts
everything, has the total.

## Dialogue
- Cassa {hope}: Second day, hour nine. You are at Kettle, R. That is about two thirds.
- Zeph: Two thirds. It is eleven twentieths. You know quite well it is eleven twentieths.
- Ilven: Third line. Stop.
- Ilven: Twenty-two oh two. You said the relay replied. The word is answered.
- Ilven: From the top of the hour. All of you.
- Zeph: ...The whole hour?
- Ilven: One word out is one word out. Nobody here is embarrassed. From the top.
- Sefa: Reader.
- Zeph: Registrar.
- Sefa: They have sent back an hour for a word.
- Sefa: I am upstairs until the fourth. Second door. Eleven of them, learning to file.
- Zeph: Registrar.
- Sefa: Reader.
- Cassa: Three hundred and fifty-one passages since the ninth of Fallow. None of them missed.
- Cassa: A hundred and forty-four thousand, two hundred and sixty-one sayings. I have them all counted.
