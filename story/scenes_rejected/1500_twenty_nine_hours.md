# Scene 1500: Twenty-Nine Hours

- type: narration
- characters:
- mood: dread

## Beat
Text over black before the player takes the first step down the Choir stair. A clock being wound,
in numbers, with no opinion attached to any of them. The date is the only thing in it that carries
any weight and nothing points at it.

## Dialogue
- Narrator {dread}: The Long Bore has run at full for twenty-nine hours.
- Narrator: Under Thurn the branch is thinner this morning than it was last night.
- Narrator: Thinning is what causes an inversion. There is no other cause.
- Narrator: Above ground it is the seventh of Fallow. Two days short of fourteen years.
- Narrator: Below it, a woman has stood at an open face for a day and a half and has not sat down.
