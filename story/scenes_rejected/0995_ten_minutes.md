# Scene 0995: Ten Minutes

- location: a mile of enormous toppled stone rings lying half sunk in white salt in a broken line to the horizon, drifted salt banked against them, birds on the highest ring, cold pink dawn light from the left
- characters: Ivo, Hanna, Bron
- mood: no fight, and no explanation
- staging: The crawler and the party come out of the stump on the left and move right. Ivo and Hanna stand on the right of frame facing left at them; the open pan and the walking figure are far off to the right

## Beat
First light. They come out fighting and they do not have to for long. Teach walks his half of the
line down to the crawler on his own, stands in front of it with his hands empty, and looks at six
armed people and then out past them at a grey coat already half a mile out on the pan going the other
way. Then he gives Sergeant Kesk an order about re-forming the near flank on the north end of the
stump, and says how long that will take, and she says "sir" in a particular tone and does it. He does
not say one word to the party. Nothing about this is explained here or anywhere in the act. The last
picture is a mile of fallen rings on white salt at dawn, one small grey figure walking away, and a
dust trail running north.
