# Scene 0775: The Wrong Thing

- type: talk
- location: a narrow aisle between iron shelf runs packed with labelled folder spines, a rolling ladder hooked at the end, a wheeled trolley half loaded, one lamp standing on the boards, night
- characters: Zeph, Pip, Lyra, Hesk
- mood: sorrow

## Beat
Thirty feet outside the office door, in the dark between two shelf runs, with the roll under Bron's
arm and nobody ready to unroll it yet. Pip cannot leave a silence alone and reaches for the kindest
thing available, which is that the woman is extremely good at her job, and it is a perfectly
reasonable thing to say, and she hears herself say it. Zeph lets it go past him and then says four
words, which is the only completely sincere sentence he has produced since Sallowgate, and none of
them is about himself. Nobody has an answer. Hesk changes the subject to the weight of the roll and
how Bron is carrying it, and goes on about it, because that is the only help she has got. Lyra does
not say one word from the first line of this scene to the last.

## Dialogue
- Pip {sorrow}: She's good at it, though. Whatever else she is. You'd have to be clever to run a room like that.
- Pip: ...I heard that. I heard myself do that. Sorry.
- Zeph: ...
- Zeph: She didn't say mine.
- Pip: ...
- Hesk: That roll's heavy. Three pounds, she said, and she'll have used the good scale.
- Hesk: He's carrying it wrong. Under the arm it creases at the middle and you lose a line off every sheet.
- Zeph: ...Yes. Under the arm.
