# Scene 0555: Company Mortar

- location: the same squared door-mouth with grey company mortar packed into the joints, fresh drill dust on the steps, a brass survey pin driven at the threshold, hard raking light from the right, afternoon
- characters: Hesk, Pip, Zeph
- optional: yes
- mood: quiet, and very bad
- staging: The cliff and the door-mouth are on the right. The ledge path and the drop run away to the left. Hesk stands at the door-mouth facing right, Pip and Zeph are stopped further along the ledge at the left. Everyone's attention is on the mortared joint

## Beat
The fill in the Third Door is not the fill. It is newer, greyer, and laid in courses instead of
packed, and somebody has done a tidy professional job of it inside the last year. Hesk puts her hand
flat on it and holds it there and says nothing for a long time. Ket gives the mix and the cure time
without being asked, because that is what she has instead of sympathy. Further along the ledge they
find the same at the Seventh and the Ninth: three of nine, opened and closed again, with a company
seal pressed into the mortar at knee height where nobody looks. Nobody here is to blame and nobody
here can do anything, and Pip and Zeph stay where they are.
