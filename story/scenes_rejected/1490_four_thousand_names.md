# Scene 1490: Four Thousand Names

- location: a long vaulted stone room lined with empty glass-fronted tanks on brass frames, their pipework cut through and capped, a floor drain channel, one tank shattered inward, dim lamp panels overhead, no daylight
- characters: Ket, Bron
- mood: sorrow, then hope
- staging: Ket sits on a bay step at the right of frame facing left with the roll across her knees. Bron sits on the floor at the left facing right. The lamp stands on the flags between them and burns down over the whole scene

## Beat
Ket reads the four thousand names of her own muster aloud — the batch that ran from year minus one
hundred and sixty-one to minus one hundred and forty-one, hers and Tovin's — and it takes six hours,
and Bron sits on the floor and hears every one of them. She does not perform it: flat, exact,
unrounded, no pauses except the iris clicking. He does not speak until she has finished. The six
hours must be shown by the lamp burning down and by his posture changing, never by a caption. When
she stops he asks her for the Order's fifteen questions, because the last thing Lyra said to him was
that he would know the fifteenth, and he does not. Ket has them in the maintenance manual she has
carried since before there were years. Fourteen are on the page. The fifteenth is a ruled line with
nothing in it. She must not thank him and he must not say why he stayed.

## Dialogue
- Ket [1] {sorrow}: There is a roll in that cabinet. My muster. Four thousand grown, and it has never been read out.
- Bron [2]: Read it.
- Ket [3]: It will take most of a day, and I will not hurry any of them.
- Bron [4]: I've nowhere to be.
- Ket [5]: Ess-Ilder. Pel-Ossun. Han-Meddra. Vay-Kestrin. Tovin-Caleth.
- Ket [6]: ...Ovv-Sarn. Ket-Ossun. That is the four thousand.
- Bron [7] {hope}: The sister's list. The fifteen questions. Is it in your manual.
- Ket [7]: Fourteen are on the page. The fifteenth is a ruled line with nothing in it. Under it: ask the holder.
- Bron [8]: Start me at the first one.
- Ket [8]: Do you know where you are?
