# Scene 0945: The Crawler That Came Back Empty

- type: talk
- location: a plank-walled office with a long counter, pigeonholes of paper folders behind it, a ledger open on a slope with a pen rest, a brass tally frame on the wall, flat daylight from a window at the left, morning
- characters: Bron, Pip, Hesk
- mood: routine

## Beat
The salt company's shed at the Bar: a counter, a tally frame, one bored agent. A freight crawler came
in on Tuesday with the load still lashed and nobody aboard. The crew of five are not dead — two
drovers have seen them since — and the company would simply like them back at work. Sealed bill,
decent fee, proof as usual. The agent is mildly embarrassed about the whole thing and assumes drink,
because it is always drink. Nothing in this scene points at anything.

## Dialogue
- Bost {hope}: That bill's mine and it's sealed. Crawler came in Tuesday, load still lashed, nobody on it.
- Bron: Dead?
- Bost: No. That's the awkward part. They've been seen twice out past the ring, by two different drovers.
- Pip: Then walk out and fetch them.
- Bost: I've a desk and a tally board. You've six people and a crawler and a token on your wrist.
- Bron: What's the fee?
- Bost: Nine marks for the five, proof as usual. I'd have offered four, but the season's on.
- Pip: Why'd they walk off?
- Bost: Drink. It's always drink. Or a woman. It's drink.
- Bron {tense}: We'll take it.
