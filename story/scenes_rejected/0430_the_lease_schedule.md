# Scene 0430: The Lease Schedule

- type: narration
- mood: read out flat

## Beat
Text over black. The current schedule to the Braid annex lease, read out in the order it is printed,
without emphasis and without comment. Five clauses and a signature. Nothing is explained and nothing
follows the last line.

## Dialogue
- Narrator {dread}: The demised premises: the archive halls at Braid, together with the lower works and all contents.
- Narrator: The term: to the forty-fifth of Deep, eleven eighty-seven.
- Narrator: The rent: one mark, annually, payable on demand.
- Narrator: The lessee shall admit readers two hours daily, twelve at a time, by card.
- Narrator: The lessee may reorganise the contents at its discretion.
- Narrator: Countersigned: Quill, S. — Registrar, the Clement Works.
