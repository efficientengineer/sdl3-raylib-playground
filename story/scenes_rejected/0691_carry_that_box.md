# Scene 0691: Carry That Box

- type: talk
- location: a plank-walled office with a long counter, pigeonholes of paper folders behind it, a ledger open on a slope with a pen rest, a brass tally frame on the wall, flat daylight from a window at the left, morning
- characters: Bron, Zeph, Emmet
- mood: hope, then dread

## Beat
The counting house is moving its working files out to the relief tent, because the clerks have been
put on the count and the files have to be where the clerks are. Emmet Sark, who two days ago refused
these people a payroll page on the correct form and would refuse it again this afternoon, comes down
the steps with a box, sees a man standing in his doorway with arms on him, and asks him to carry it
forty yards. It is not a favour and there is no wink in it. It is the same accuracy pointed a
different way: he is four clerks short and Bron is in the way. On top of the box is a fresh printed
notice that came down over the chairman's seal this morning with four names on it, and Sark mentions
it in exactly the tone he would use for the weather, because it is in the file and it concerns them.
Zeph takes one page out of the box on the walk. Nobody says anything about it and nobody is pleased.

## Dialogue
- Emmet {hope}: Counting house is going to the tent. Clerks are on the count, so the files follow the clerks.
- Emmet: You. You've got arms on you. Forty yards, third trestle, the end with the urn.
- Bron: ...
- Emmet: It isn't a favour. I'm four clerks short and you are standing in my doorway.
- Zeph: Doorway. Understood. Give me the light one.
- Emmet: There is no light one.
- Emmet: Mind the notice on the top, it isn't pinned. That came down this morning over the chairman's seal.
- Bron: Saying what?
- Emmet: Four names. Yours is first, which I would say was alphabetical, except that it isn't.
- Bron: You didn't have to tell me that.
- Emmet: It's a notice. Telling people is the whole of what it's for.
- Emmet: Left at the urn. Mind the guy rope, three of them have gone over it this morning and one of them was me.
- Emmet {dread}: Thank you. I'll take it from here.
