# Scene 1340a: Four Hours — Waited

- type: talk
- optional: yes
- location: a laid-rail siding on white crust at dusk, a sledge with its sail furled on the spar, a small fire in a salt-brick ring, bedrolls laid out on the deck, firelight from the centre, cold sky
- characters: Threnn, Domma, Zeph, Pip, Cassa
- mood: tense

## Beat
**Branch: plays when the side contract "Walk the line" (`1335`) ends with the party leaving the
chant-line where it is until the passage is finished. One of two; see `1340b`.**

Four hours on the salt in the cold beside eleven people chanting a river system that dried up six
hundred years ago. Cassa Tessen comes up the line at speed with the exact cost of the delay, says
all of it fast and on purpose, and then, having said it, sits down and waits too. Domma gives Pip a
job rather than an explanation, and Pip learns eight words of the passage badly and is told that
badly is fine, that badly is a thing to correct in the morning. Neither outcome of this contract is
punished and neither is praised. The last beat is the chant carrying on down the line ahead of them
in the dark.

## Dialogue
- Cassa {tense}: Eleven sledges, ninety-four people, four hours. That is sixteen miles they do not make tonight.
- Cassa: The siding water at the first stop is measured for tomorrow. Tomorrow it will be measured for two days.
- Cassa: I have said all of it. I said it fast on purpose. — Right. Where do I sit.
- Pip: You came four miles out here to sit down.
- Cassa: I came four miles out here to say the figure. Sitting is a separate thing.
- Domma {hope}: Come to the fire, both of you. You cannot join it. You may hold the count of it if you want a job.
- Pip: I'll get it wrong.
- Domma: You will get it wrong. Getting it wrong where somebody can hear you is how it is put right.
- Pip: ...That's eight words. I've got eight words of a river.
- Threnn: Eight words badly. Badly is not nothing. Badly is a thing to correct in the morning.
- Zeph: Go on. I have the count if Domma wants to drink something.
- Pip: It's still going. Listen. It's gone on down the line ahead of us.
