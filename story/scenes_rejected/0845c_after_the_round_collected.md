The evening after the sixth door, on the wall above the town where the ravine road comes over the
lip. Somewhere to put it. Nobody speaks for three boxes. Then Zeph, who deals with a thing by
getting the arithmetic of it straight, starts working out how nine years of interest turns twelve
marks and six into fifty-two marks and four, and cannot get there, and starts again, and Hesk stops
him, and he stops. The game passes no judgement. Nobody says they had no choice. Nobody in the
party defends it and nobody condemns anybody else either.
