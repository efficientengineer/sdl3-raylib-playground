# Scene 0595: You Cannot Pass a Promise

- type: talk
- location: a dressed-stone hall packed to the roof with squared blocks laid by hand in courses, a narrow gap at the top left unfilled, dust on the last course, one carried lamp from the right, no daylight
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip, Ket, Hesk
- mood: the end of a road

## Beat
They get past the guard and there is nothing to get to. The hall beyond the gate has been filled by
hand, floor to roof, in courses, from the far side, and it goes on past the reach of the lamp. Ket
measures it and gives the volume and the labour in person-days, and that number is the only thing
this chapter has actually found out. Hesk is savagely vindicated and takes no pleasure in it at all.
The only way under the Rim Hills is somebody else's hole, and the nearest one is ninety miles west,
and Hesk's nephew draws his wage from the counting house beside it. Ket says she will come, and
gives her terms again, and they are the same terms.

## Dialogue
- Zeph {confront}: That isn't a wall. That's the hall. The hall is full.
- Ket: Dressed block, laid in courses, floor to roof. It was laid from the far side.
- Ket: Nine feet by fourteen, and it goes on past the lamp. Call it a hundred feet, and I am being kind.
- Ket: Twelve thousand person-days. I have rounded down, and I do not round.
- Pip: Twelve thousand days. Of people. Doing that.
- Hesk: They didn't want out.
- Hesk {sorrow}: Everything my household has ever said about this cliff is true. I have never had that before and I don't want it.
- Lyra: Hesk.
- Hesk: Don't.
- Bron: Then the road under these hills isn't ours.
- Bron: It's somebody else's hole.
- Zeph: The company has one. Ninety miles west, under Saltmouth. Bore Four. Three times the depth of anything on this cliff.
- Hesk: My nephew's wage comes out of the counting house beside it. On the ninth. He'll tell you twice.
- Ket {tense}: I will come.
- Ket: My terms are the terms I gave at Braid. Passage east under the hills, and a doorward who will talk. Neither has moved.
