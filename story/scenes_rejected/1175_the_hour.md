# Scene 1175: The Hour

- location: a straight dirt road leaving town between two low sandstone walls, wheel ruts, a leaning stone mile post, dry grass on the verges, flat overcast morning light, the town roofs low behind
- characters: Lyra, Bron, Pip
- mood: sorrow
- staging: Lyra is down in the ruts across the middle of the road with her head to the left. Bron kneels on her left, Pip on her right. The seated grey-coated figure and the rifle are apart behind them at the right of frame, and nobody looks at him. Everyone's attention is on Lyra's face

## Beat
Continuous with the road. Lyra starts the Tally on herself, out loud, out of plain professional
habit, and gets a little way down it and cannot go on. Pip has her other hand and is saying the
wrong things very fast and nobody stops her. Lyra asks Bron to take the hour, which is the only
thing her order does: be there, ask the questions in order, and carry it afterwards because you
were there. He says he does not know the fifteenth. She tells him it. He puts his forehead against
hers with both hands on her face and asks her the questions in order, and she answers the ones
there is time for. There is no device here, nothing changes hands but a medallion, and nobody
makes a speech. Behind them a young officer sits down in the dirt beside his rifle, and later, as
they go, a wordless man puts an apron round the boy's shoulders.
