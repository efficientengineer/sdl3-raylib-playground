# Scene 0489: The Company Shop at Lomm

- type: talk
- location: a shop interior of dark shelving stocked with tins and cloth, a glass-topped counter with trays of paper sleeves in seasonal colours, a brass till, a lamp on a bracket at the right, evening
- characters: Pip, Lyra
- mood: hope -> tense

## Beat
The first strand counter the player can shop at: trays under glass, sorted by category, priced in
plain figures, in this season's colour. The clerk is warm, helpful, and genuinely proud of the stock
rotation, and explains the whole line without being asked twice. Lyra asks who sells them and gets
the answer every employee in the Basin believes, and the clerk believes it completely, because he has
never once been in a position to find out. Pip picks one up. Pip puts it down. She does not say why,
and Lyra does not ask her, then or later.

## Dialogue
- Clerk {hope}: Afternoon. Sorry — Neve's off today, so it's me on both counters.
- Clerk: New stock in on the ninth, all in this year's mulberry, sorted by category.
- Clerk: Comfort's your evening's wage. Good weather, a good meal, a walk somewhere pleasant.
- Lyra: Who sells them?
- Clerk: Older people, mostly. Somebody eighty with a good afternoon they're not using, and grandchildren who eat.
- Clerk: It's a kindness both ways, I always think. They get the winter and you get the afternoon.
- Lyra: And you've seen them come in and sell.
- Clerk: Me personally? No. Stock comes down from Vantage. But that's where it's from.
- Pip: How much is that one.
- Clerk: Evening's wage. Shall I wrap it?
- Pip {tense}: ...No. Leave it.
- Clerk: Right you are. — Oh, we take the permit office queue number here as well. Saves you walking back.
