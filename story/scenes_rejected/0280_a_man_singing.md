# Scene 0280: A Man Singing

- location: a raw stepped cut sliced into a sandstone hillside, spoil heaps and plank walkways, a canvas company tent lashed at the top step, a standing derrick with brass gear, cold grey dawn light from the left
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip, Marek, Orrin
- mood: braced for a villain, handed a friend
- staging: The party and Marek stand at the left of the cut facing right. The dead rig fills the centre. Orrin comes up the path at the right, beyond the rig, and stays on the right facing left. Everyone's attention goes right, at him

## Beat
They hear him before they see him, because he is singing, badly, on the walk up. He comes over the
lip at first light: short, very wide, brass-coloured hair standing straight up, a company field coat
with both sleeves cut off, burn scars up both bare forearms, a brass rule through his belt like a
sword and a tin whistle on a cord. He looks at the wrecked rig, and the first thing out of his mouth
is whether anybody got hurt. Told no, he loses interest in the machine entirely, first-names his
crew chief, asks which of the four is Bron, and puts a hand out. Four people came up this hill to
accuse him and he is delighted to see them.
