# Scene 1310: Cassa Counts the Sledges

- type: talk
- location: a rail head on the dry seabed below a terrace town, four loaded sledges strung out on laid rail, a chalked board on a post beside the first, flat overcast daylight from the left, dust raised low
- characters: Cassa, Pip, Bron, Ollo
- mood: hope
- backdrop: 1305_braid_is_leaving:1

## Beat
Nineteen, running a sledge, running a running total, and correcting anybody who rounds. Cassa is
evacuating her own town at speed and enjoying the work, which is the point of her: the busyness is
not a mask over grief, it is how a competent person gets seven thousand people onto rails in
fourteen days. She is thrilled to see Pip, appalled at how thin she is, and feeds her out of a
sledge locker without breaking stride. She and Ollo establish in about ninety seconds that they are
the same person. Then, counting heads the way she counts everything, she comes up one short and
says so, and Pip gives her a number, and she takes it and puts it down and does not ask, and
nobody comes back to it. Then she chalks work on the board, because she is short a body and there
are two hunters standing in front of her doing nothing.

## Dialogue
- Cassa {hope}: Forty-three loaded, forty-four on the crank, and you are standing where forty-five goes. Move. — Pip!
- Cassa: Pip. You have got thinner. That is not an opinion. That is about four pounds and I have carried you.
- Cassa: Locker under the bench. Flat one first, the round one keeps. Do not argue with me, I am counting.
- Ollo: Is that a proving box? Under the sail there?
- Cassa: Sail locker. There is dough in it, and I will not be told it is the wrong box by a man off a hill.
- Ollo: I'd not dream of it. What are you giving it, four hours?
- Cassa: Three and a half. It is cold out here, so four. ...You are going to be a great deal of trouble.
- Cassa: Six. — Six of you. I had six off the crust at Saltmouth and one of these is new.
- Cassa: That is five and a new one. Pip. Where is the sister.
- Pip: Ninth of Dust.
- Cassa: ...Ninth of Dust. I have it.
- Cassa: Right. Board. I am chalking this and one of you is taking it, because I cannot spare a body.
- Cassa: Walk the line two miles west before tonight's run. Anything on the rail, take it off the rail.
- Cassa: Tessen, sledge nine, and a mark under it. That is a seal where I come from.
