# Scene 1445: Say the Words

- type: talk
- location: a broad stone landing before double stone doors at the back right, stone weapon racks along the left wall, a shallow step worn hollow, lamp columns on both sides lighting from above, warm even light, no daylight
- characters: Zeph, Sefa, Hesk, Ket, Bron, Pip
- mood: tense, then sorrow, then confront
- backdrop: 1440_the_standing_muster:1

## Beat
The relief formula is not a password. It is a handover: the words a relieving watch says to take a
post, so the standing watch can stand down. Sefa corrects the back half Hesk has carried in her mouth
since she was a child — two words — and it costs Sefa nothing and Hesk a great deal, and Hesk takes
it without a word and then makes Zeph give it back to her slowly until she has it right herself.
Then Zeph sings it. The game describes the singing rather than printing it: one long line carried in
a voice with a second tone under it, and rank by rank, front to back, thirty halberds come down to
the stone and thirty armoured figures step to the wall. It takes a long time and it is the worst
sound in the game. Then four of them come on anyway, because a crushed helm cannot hear a handover,
and nobody is triumphant about any of it.

## Dialogue
- Zeph {tense}: A handover has to be taken by somebody. That is why the paper on its own does nothing.
- Sefa: The relieving watch speaks. The standing watch stands down. Thirty words and a tune.
- Hesk: Which words did I have wrong.
- Sefa: Watch, not way. And the end of it is count them by. Not let them by.
- Hesk: ...
- Hesk: Reader. Say the back of it again. Slowly. I'll have it right before you sing it at them.
- Zeph: The post is relieved. The watch is spoken for. Stand aside and count them by.
- Hesk: The post is relieved. The watch is spoken for. Stand aside and count them by.
- Hesk {sorrow}: Forty-four years with that wrong in my mouth. — Go on. Sing it.
- Bron: Front rank's grounding them. ...Second rank.
- Hesk: Those four aren't moving. Two on the left. Two at the far end.
- Ket: Twenty-six relieved. Four not.
- Ket: Those four cannot hear a handover. Their helms are crushed. It is not correctable.
- Bron {confront}: Then we do it quickly.
