# Scene 0720: The Mouth

- type: talk
- location: a wide cut-stone shelf at the foot of the quarry face, an ancient squared hall-mouth capped by a huge brass and iron bore head, bunk rows crowded against the cliff, hanging lamps, dust in the air, no daylight reaching down
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Hesk, Ket
- mood: tense, then sorrow

## Beat
The lowest tier is the warmest place in the Basin and the worst place in it. No daylight reaches the
shelf; the lamps burn all day; the air tastes of the dust off the cap. Hesk is dying of the heat and
has been saying so since the gate, and Bron has been keeping a flat count, which is the only funny
thing on this tier. A woman on the end of a bunk row is feeding coins into the meter on her roof and
explains the arithmetic of it cheerfully, without one word of complaint, because she is good at it
and proud of it and has never once come up short. Ket stops in the middle of the gangway and says
the hum in the floor is not the city, and does not say what it is. Lyra asks the woman one question
more than the conversation wanted, and gets an answer that ends it — not angrily. The woman simply
does not understand what she is being asked, and goes back to the dial.

## Dialogue
- Hesk {tense}: Hotter in here than it was out on the salt. Who puts a bunk row against the warm wall.
- Bron: Nine.
- Hesk: Nine what?
- Bron: Nine times since the gate.
- Woman: Top row's the one you want, love. Heat goes up and it costs you the same as the bottom.
- Woman: Four nights at a go, me. Coin in, dial round, shut your eyes.
- Lyra: And when the four nights run out before the wage does?
- Woman: Drying shed at the end, with the stove in it. Nobody ever died of a chair.
- Woman: I've not been short. Nine years on this row, not one night, and you can ask anybody down it.
- Hesk: This is a kitchen with beds in it.
- Ket: The hum in this floor is not the city.
- Lyra {sorrow}: What would you do with it? If you did not have to buy the nights.
- Woman: Buy the nights. ...I don't know what it is you're asking me, love.
