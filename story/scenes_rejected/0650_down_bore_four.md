# Scene 0650: Down Bore Four

- location: a rough natural gallery whose whole back wall is packed hair-fine pale gold filament lying in strata, a plank walkway and handrail in front, a dropped lamp on the boards lighting upward, no daylight
- characters: Bron, Zeph, Ket
- mood: wonder, then dread
- staging: Down is to the right and forward, out is to the left and up. The filament face fills the right and back of every panel below; the party stands to the left of it, small, facing right

## Beat
Twelve minutes in a wire cage going down past lit gallery mouths with crews working in them, and the
noise stops before the light does. At the bottom the air is wrong in a way none of them can name:
dust hangs where it is put, a lamp flame stands dead straight, and there is a chord in the jaw rather
than in the ear. The end of the drift opens on a face of packed hair-fine filament lying in strata
like grain in wood, pale gold going white where it is dense, brightening very slightly in slow bands
that always travel the same way. It is not aware of them. It is not doing anything to them. Zeph,
who has talked for three chapters, goes quiet, and that is the loudest thing he does all act.

## Dialogue
- Zeph [1] {wonder}: Twelve minutes. Nine hundred feet. We have been past four crews and I have not heard one of them for the last three.
- Ket [2]: The lamp. Look at the lamp and not at the wall.
- Ket [2]: There is no draught at this depth. There is also no dust falling. Those are two different facts.
- Zeph [3]: ...
- Ket [4] {dread}: That is the store. That is the article the index at Braid had a word for.
- Zeph [5]: ...
- Zeph [5]: I have nothing. I had eleven things ready and I have nothing.
- Bron [6]: It's not looking at us.
