# Scene 1010: The Wall

- location: a low tram town of grey brick and sandstone in a shallow valley, washing lines strung between roofs, a tall dark ring tower standing above it, steam drifting flat, overcast morning, light from the left
- characters: Ket, Bron
- mood: ordinary, then not
- staging: The party comes in from the left of frame and faces right. The tram rails run along the left; the dark ring tower and the long sanatorium wall stand to the right and above. Everyone's attention travels right, up the street

## Beat
A town they have already slept in, seen properly for the first time. It is six in the morning and
four laundries are boiling at once, so the whole street is standing in white steam; a tram bell goes
somewhere out of sight; a dead tower three hundred feet high stands over the roofs with birds on it
and nobody in the town has looked up at it this century. At the top of the street is a long clean
brick wall with one gate in it. The party are warm, tired and unhurried; Ket stops dead at the
tower and says its name and the name of the person who sweeps it, and does not explain. Bron is
looking at the wall and has been since the street bent. Nobody says anything ominous about it.
