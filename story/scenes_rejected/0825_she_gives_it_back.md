# Scene 0825: She Gives It Back

- type: talk
- location: an open yard of sunken stone soaking pits in rows, hides pegged on drying frames along the wall, a wooden stirring paddle leaning, a pump on a stone base, hard low sun over the ravine lip from the right, morning
- characters: Bron, Zeph, Lyra
- mood: tense, then wonder

## Beat
Asking after a woman in a grey coat gets a straight answer from the first person they ask, and the
second, and the fourth. Half the town has seen her. She has been here three days, she goes to
houses, and what happens in a house is the house's business. The tanner's wife has a firm opinion
and states it the way you state the price of bark. Then a yard hand disagrees with her about which
day the woman went where, and the two of them have the argument out in front of the party while the
hides drip, which is how the player learns that this is not a rumour any more, it is ordinary town
gossip with a running dispute attached. Nobody is frightened of her. Two households have asked her
to come.

## Dialogue
- Tanner's wife {tense}: Grey coat, hair cut short by somebody in a hurry, no gloves on her in this weather. Aye. Three days now.
- Zeph: Three days. Where is she sleeping?
- Tanner's wife: Not my business. She goes to houses. What happens in a house is the house's.
- Yard hand: She went to the Mells first. Tuesday.
- Tanner's wife: Wednesday. She was at the Renners' Tuesday, I saw her on their steps with her boots off.
- Yard hand: It was Tuesday at the Mells, because the lime came Tuesday and I was stood here.
- Tanner's wife: The lime came Monday.
- Bron: Does anybody mind her being here?
- Tanner's wife: Mind? Two houses have asked her. The Renner boy wrote her a letter and nobody knows where you'd send one.
- Tanner's wife {wonder}: She gives it back. She doesn't make it up. There's a difference and everybody down here knows it.
- Lyra: Who told you the difference?
- Tanner's wife: Nobody told me. You watch a man come out of his own kitchen knowing his sister's name. You'd know.
