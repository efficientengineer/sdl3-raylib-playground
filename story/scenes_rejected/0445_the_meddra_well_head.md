# Scene 0445: The Meddra Well Head

- location: a stone ring tower with three of its upper rings cracked and strapped with iron bands, scavenged brass pipework patched along one side, a plank scaffold at its foot, a hand-cart of parts, flat grey morning light
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip, Ket
- mood: a complaint, filed
- staging: the ring tower stands on the right of every panel and runs off the top of frame. Ket works at a canvas of laid-out parts in the centre, facing right. The company stores clerk stands on the right of the canvas, facing left. Bron, Lyra, Zeph and Pip come up from the left and stay on the left, facing right

## Beat
A bone-white woman with four small lights down each temple is taking a company stores clerk apart
with numbers, and she is not raising her voice, and he is not getting away. She has been promised one
governor spindle for eleven years. She gives him the requisition date, the eleven annual
acknowledgments in order, and the exact tolerance, and he says he will chase it, and she tells him
where he last said that. When he goes she is back inside the machine before he has cleared the
scaffold. Then she notices four people who have been standing there for four minutes, states her own
condition the way another person would state the time, and asks what they want. Her face does not
act. Her hands do all of it.

## Dialogue
- Ket [1] {tense}: Requisition: ninth of Green, ten ninety-six. One governor spindle. Tolerance four thousandths.
- Clerk [2]: It's on the list. I'll chase it when I'm back at the office.
- Ket [2]: Eleven acknowledgments. One a year, on the ninth of Green, in that case, in order.
- Clerk [3]: I'll chase it.
- Ket [4]: You said that in Dust.
- Ket [5] {wonder}: You have been standing there four minutes. Say what you want, or move; I need the light.
- Bron [6]: Passage east. And somebody who knows what's under those hills.
- Ket [7]: Two things. I want four. Hold that, and keep your thumb off the face of it.
