# Scene 0688: The Warden Asks

- type: talk
- location: a long canvas relief tent open along one side on white salt, trestle tables in two rows under it with enamel bowls stacked, a tea urn on a crate, hard flat afternoon light from overhead, salt glare
- characters: Bron, Ivo
- mood: tense, then something quieter

## Beat
A very large, very slow-moving man with a cup much too small for his hands stands beside a tea urn
and asks Bron a series of questions he already knows the answers to: his full name, his hall, the
bill he cleared in Turn, whether he has eaten today. He does not threaten anything, he does not
mention a warrant, and he uses Bron's full name twice. He tells him, accurately and without any
emphasis at all, that there is a list going round with four names on it and that it is not his list.
Then he asks one question that is not procedure. Bron answers it easily and warmly, the way he
answers everything about home, and Warden Teach looks into his cup for one beat longer than a man
looks into a cup, and then asks whether he wants the other half. Nothing is revealed. Nobody notices
anything.

## Dialogue
- Ivo {tense}: Bron Sallow. Sallowgate hall, the Even Hand.
- Bron: You know that.
- Ivo: I do. Cleared a bill in Turn, up at the old works. Triple rate, burned seal.
- Bron: You know that as well.
- Ivo: I like hearing it from the man. People change their account of a thing and I like to know when.
- Ivo: Have you eaten today?
- Bron: There's been a lot on.
- Ivo: There has. Eat. There's a tent for it forty feet that way and nobody's queueing.
- Ivo: There's a list going about with four names on it. It came down this morning and it isn't mine.
- Bron: What does that mean, it isn't yours?
- Ivo: It means I didn't write it and I'm not working it today. Today I'm working the gallery.
- Ivo: Have you got family?
- Bron {sorrow}: Mother. Tellwater, house on the square with the well outside it. She's ten years gone. Good hands, terrible singer.
- Ivo: ...
- Ivo: Do you want the other half of that urn? It'll be cold by four and they'll throw it out.
