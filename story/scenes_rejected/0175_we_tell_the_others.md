# Scene 0175: We Tell the Others

- type: talk
- location: a cold cut-stone corridor deep underground with square tooling marks on dressed walls, a great stone door standing open onto darkness at the right, one guttering torch in an iron wall bracket, dry dust, no daylight
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip
- mood: tense

## Beat
Twenty feet back from the great door, out of the guard's reach, the four of them together again.
Bron and Lyra tell Zeph and Pip what happened, and saying it out loud makes it worse rather than
better. Zeph wants to go back and look at it and is outvoted twice, badly. Pip has already found
the way round: a side passage off the corridor with fresh chisel marks in both walls and boot
scuffs going in and coming out. Nobody remarks on that, because it solves their problem. The guard
has not followed and has not moved, and Bron says so in four words, and that is the only comment
anybody makes about it.

## Dialogue
- Pip {tense}: Say that again.
- Lyra: It could have cut us down when that door opened. It did not. It pointed us back up the corridor.
- Zeph: Back. Up. — I want to look at it.
- Bron: No.
- Zeph: I want to look at it from much further away.
- Bron: No.
- Pip: There's a way round. Side passage, twenty feet back. Fresh chisel marks, both walls.
- Pip: Boot scuffs going in and coming out.
- Bron: Bring them back. Four out, four back. We go round.
- Lyra: ...
- Bron: It's not following.
- Pip {tense}: Right. Me first.
