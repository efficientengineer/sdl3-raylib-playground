# Scene 1255: The Ossun Head

- location: a cathedral-sized machine hall inside the topmost stone ring, banks of brass valve gear and iron governor arms turning slowly, a gantry catwalk at mid height, steam venting low, flat cold light from a ring opening above
- characters: Ket, Bron
- mood: wonder, then wrong
- staging: The party stands on the bolted brass gallery on the left of frame and faces right into the machinery. The governor shaft and the valve banks fill the right of every panel; the ring opening is above. Everyone looks right and up

## Beat
Three hundred feet of stacked stone rings with a company gallery bolted round it, humming at a
pitch you feel in your teeth. Ket has not stood inside this machine since she was removed from the
post in 1082. She reads it the way a doctor reads a chest — the governor weights riding out, one
arm bent, the ring pitch a full tone above the figure she gave a schoolmistress three hours ago —
and states, without alarm and with numbers, that the well is being driven past its rating to cover
a draw that is on none of her paperwork. Nobody works out what the draw is; they only know the
number does not close. Then she announces her own state, flatly, the way she would announce a
pressure, and the governor lets go while she is speaking.

## Dialogue
- Ket [1] {wonder}: I had this post until ten eighty-two. I have not been inside the rings since they metered it.
- Bron [2] {tense}: What's it doing?
- Ket [2]: Two hundred and thirty-four in the second. At the school wall, three hours ago, it was two hundred and eight.
- Ket [3]: Nine over eight. A full tone, in three hours. The weights are riding out and that arm is bent.
- Hesk [3]: Bent by what.
- Ket [4]: By being driven past its rating. Something underneath is taking, and it is on no paper of mine.
- Hesk [5]: Then whose paper is it on.
- Ket [6]: I am at three lights. One on this side, two on the other. Plan around it.
- Ket [6] {confront}: — Off the rail. All of you, off the rail, now.
