# Scene 0665: Get Them Out

- type: talk
- location: a timbered gallery with props and lagging, laid tramway rail down the middle, an ore tub standing on it, a ventilation canvas hung across the roof, one lamp on a hook at the left, no daylight
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip, Ket, Hesk, Orrin, Ivo
- mood: confront, then work

## Beat
Field Superintendent Orrin Cadder is at the north gallery mouth with his coat off, running the
evacuation by name — he knows the crews' names, every one of them, and he uses them — and he puts six
strangers to work in the same breath as the rest of it, without once asking who they are, because
there are two hundred people below him and six pairs of hands are six pairs of hands. Warden Ivo
Teach is at the other side of the mouth doing exactly the same job with exactly as little ceremony.
He looks at Bron for three seconds, as he did at Tellwater, and then gives him the left file. Nobody
arrests anybody. Cadder tells one joke, badly, at the worst possible moment, and it works, and the
line moves. He counts the crews out loud as they come past him and the count is short.

## Dialogue
- Orrin {confront}: Mave, Ruel, both of you on the tub — Sesk, that's the wrong hand on that rope and you know it.
- Orrin: You six. You're not mine, I don't care. Left file to the cage, two at a time, and don't let them run.
- Bron: How many are down there?
- Orrin: Two hundred and eleven on the board. I have got a hundred and nine past me. You can do the rest of that sum.
- Ivo: Bron Sallow.
- Ivo: ...
- Ivo: Left file. Keep them walking. If one of them sits down, pick them up, they'll go again.
- Pip: That's it? That's all he's going to say?
- Ivo: That's all there is to say. There's a queue behind you.
- Orrin: Right, listen. Anybody who makes it up that shaft tonight gets a song out of me at the top.
- Orrin: That's why we're going up. That is the reason. Move.
- Hesk: Cadder.
- Orrin: Ninefold. Not now, and I'd say the same to my mother.
- Bron: Dov Ninefold. Is he on your board?
- Orrin {tense}: Dov. Up on the first cage with a cut hand and a sulk on. Tell his aunt. — Tarn. Ordo. Mave. Where's the rest of Ordo's four?
