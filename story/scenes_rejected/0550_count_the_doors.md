# Scene 0550: Count the Doors

- type: talk
- optional: yes
- location: a squared door-mouth in sandstone filled with fitted cut-stone blocks, an iron door-key ring set in the centre stone, worn steps in front of it, a stacked cairn to one side, flat noon light
- characters: Pip, Zeph, Ket, Hesk
- mood: a pleasant walk on a job that is not a job
- backdrop: 0505_the_terrace_landing:1

## Beat
A thousand feet up a cliff path on a job everybody already knows the answer to. Hesk walks it the
way she has walked it four times a year since she was eleven: name the door, name the hold above it,
check the fill, move on. Pip, who picks locks for a living, is professionally offended that nine
doors have no locks at all, and gets an answer she cannot argue with. Zeph counts the bands cut
above the door-mouths and finds the same rows over every one of them. Then they come up on the
Third, and Hesk stops talking.

## Dialogue
- Hesk {hope}: First. Ordo's hold. Fill sound, cairn standing, ring clear.
- Hesk: Second. Mave's hold. Fill sound. Cairn standing.
- Pip: Nine doors and not one lock. Not one. I want you to understand what that does to me.
- Hesk: A lock is a thing that opens. That is why there is not one.
- Pip: ...That's the worst answer I have ever agreed with.
- Hesk: My grandmother walked this four times a year. Her mother walked it. I was eleven the first time.
- Pip: And nothing's ever been wrong?
- Hesk: Nothing has ever been wrong.
- Zeph: The band over the Second is the band over the First. Same rows, same count, same hand.
- Ket: Fifteen rows on each. One cutter. About a week a door.
- Hesk {tense}: Third. Dov's hold.
- Hesk: ...
