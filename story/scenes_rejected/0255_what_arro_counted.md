# Scene 0255: What Arro Counted

- type: talk
- location: a tall stone ring tower standing whole and dark over a tram town, birds circling the upper rings, a swept stone apron at its foot, a broom and a stacked pile of squared stone chips, grey overcast morning light
- characters: Arro, Lyra, Bron, Orla
- mood: flat, and then it lands

## Beat
The birds are moved, not killed, and crated down the valley to where the feed is. Lyra asks the
question she cannot stop herself asking, and the answer is literal and devastating: it is the post,
the post did not end, he was not relieved, and he has the date he was not relieved. He thanks them
in a form of words that is plainly a maintenance log entry and says so. Bron asks whether he wants
anything brought up next time anybody comes, and he names one small cheap specific thing, and that
is the only thing he asks anyone for in the whole game. Down in the yard Orla pays on completion and
does not ask where they have been.

## Dialogue
- Lyra {sorrow}: Why do you sweep it?
- Arro: It is the post.
- Lyra: The well has not run since the year nine hundred and forty-four.
- Arro: Correct. The post did not end. I was not relieved.
- Arro: I have the date I was not relieved. Fourth of Rime, nine hundred and forty-four. Nothing came.
- Bron: The birds are down the valley in crates. They'll stop where the feed is.
- Arro: Apron clear. Upper ledges clear. Four hands, one morning, no damage to the stonework.
- Arro: That is the entry. I have said it out loud because you were standing here.
- Bron: Next time anyone comes up. Is there anything you want brought?
- Arro: A flat file. Second cut. Eight inches.
- Bron: Right.
- Arro: That is the whole list.
- Orla {hope}: Wash is clean. Six and two. I don't ask where anybody's been.
