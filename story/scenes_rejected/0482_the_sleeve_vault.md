# Scene 0482: The Sleeve Vault

- location: a stone vault lined with shallow brass drawers to the ceiling, an iron retrieval gantry on floor and roof rails standing in the middle, a control pedestal of valve wheels, lamps on stanchions, no daylight
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip
- mood: tense -> confront
- staging: the gantry and its rails stand at the centre and run up out of frame. Bron, Lyra, Zeph and Pip are on the left facing right. The doorway and the two annex guards are on the right, facing left. The gantry arm travels down the rail from the right

## Beat
The gantry wakes because something is out of place, and the something is them. It is not hostile and
it is not alive: it is a machine built to fetch a tray and put a tray back, and it has classified
four wet people as material in the wrong location. Two annex guards come in at a jog, see it running,
establish between themselves that nobody here is paid enough for this, and take up positions anyway.
The older one asks, genuinely and politely, whether the party would consider standing still until the
shift ends. The gantry arm comes down the rail at head height. Bron steps in front of Zeph. Nothing
about the machine has a face.
