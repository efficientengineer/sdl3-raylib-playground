# Scene 0835: Rope-Walk Supper

- type: talk
- optional: yes
- location: a long narrow stone alley with strung rope lines running its length at shoulder height, twisting posts and a hand wheel at one end, coiled hemp stacked against the wall, bars of light from above, midday
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip, Hesk, Ket
- mood: hope

## Beat
A lean-to at the end of a rope-walk, a fire pit, a pot, and a bad supper cooked badly. Zeph teaches
the party a Vess counting game that his line has played for four hundred years; Pip breaks it on the
third move, not by cheating exactly but by finding the hole in it; and Ket rules on the dispute with
total, literal, unhurried seriousness and ends the game forever. Then Hesk explains, at length and
entirely unprompted, the correct way to lay rope, and it turns out she is right, which annoys
everyone. Nothing happens. Lyra eats almost nothing and puts her plate down beside her where nobody
is looking; the player can see it and no character does, and no character ever mentions it.

## Dialogue
- Zeph {hope}: It is called Coin. You count. You do not repeat, and you do not say a number I have already said.
- Pip: Three.
- Zeph: Four.
- Pip: Four and a half.
- Zeph: You cannot — Ket. Ket, tell her.
- Ket: Four and a half is a number. It has not been said. It is legal.
- Zeph: It is a counting game.
- Ket: Then you have not defined the set. She can give you every number between four and five until morning and none of us will eat.
- Pip: I love her.
- Zeph: ...I will not be playing that again.
- Hesk: You're laying that rope wrong. Over and under, one hand's width, and turn the coil against the twist.
- Hesk: I'll show you the once. I'm not showing you twice.
- Bron: She's right.
- Zeph: She is always right. It is the most annoying thing about her and I am including the spear.
