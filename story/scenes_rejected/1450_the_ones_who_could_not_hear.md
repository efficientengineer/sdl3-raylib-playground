# Scene 1450: The Ones Who Could Not Hear

- location: a broad stone landing before double stone doors at the back right, stone weapon racks along the left wall, a shallow step worn hollow, lamp columns on both sides lighting from above, warm even light, no daylight
- characters: Pip, Hesk, Bron
- mood: confront, then sorrow
- staging: The party stands at the left of the landing and faces right. The doors, the wall line of relieved guards and the four fallen ones are at the right and back. Nobody stands over anything

## Beat
Four suits of armour lie on the flagstones, come apart, with nothing inside any of them: no bodies,
nothing to bury, nothing to say a rite over. The fight was a mercy and the panels must not be heroic
— no victory poses, no one standing over anything, no triumph anywhere. Along the walls the
twenty-six that were relieved are still standing at ease with their halberds grounded, watching
nothing, and they will stand there forever now, because nobody is ever coming to take a post back.
Hesk gathers the broken pieces and stacks them against the wall in the order they fell, squaring the
edges, taking far too long over it, and everybody lets her. Pip looks away down the hall and says she
is fine. At the end Hesk puts a number in her book, because she has no names to put in it, and says
it out loud. Lyra is not mentioned.
