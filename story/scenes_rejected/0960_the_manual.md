# Scene 0960: The Manual

- location: a small iron cabin with two bunks folded down, a chart table with weighted maps, a stove the size of a bucket, tin cups in a rack, one lamp swinging from the roof, night
- characters: Ket, Lyra
- mood: ordinary evening, then the floor goes
- staging: Ket sits at the left of the chart table facing right; Lyra sits at the right of it facing left. The open manual lies on the table between them and everything either of them looks at is that page

## Beat
Ket wants a diagram of a ring joint and opens the maintenance manual she has carried since before
there were years. She reads the facing page aloud while she looks for the figure, the way anybody
does — a fifteen-point check, numbered, in order, in the form of questions, to be run on a thing that
has been moved. By the fourth question Lyra has stopped moving. By the seventh she is saying it along
with her, half a beat behind, and she is not reading it. Ket stops. She does not know what she has
done and she can see exactly that she has done something. Nobody in this cabin explains anything. The
last thing in the scene is Ket asking where Lyra learned it, and the page still open under her hand.
