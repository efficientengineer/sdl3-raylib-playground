# Scene 0440: The Braid Hall

- type: talk
- location: a side room off a Vess chant-hall, a low plank ceiling, a chalked job board on the right-hand wall, a squat iron stove with a kettle on it at the left, a short ledger wall of ruled columns of small handwriting behind the board, lamplight from the left, evening
- characters: Bron, Pip, Mir
- mood: hope -> tense

## Beat
The only Even Hand hall in the Basin not run by humans: a side room off a chant-hall with a board, a
stove, and a ledger wall a quarter the size of Sallowgate's. Mir Lirr signs them in without a single
question, puts food in front of them before anyone mentions being hungry, and then tells them what
came down the rail about their Sallowgate bill, which she finds worse than anything the Pale have
ever done in this room. On the floor under the board is a sheet of paper that has been swept aside
twice and not burned: a job with no seal on it. She will not pin it, she is angry about it in a
completely level voice, and she is right. Then she says the other thing, and goes out, and leaves
the paper where it is.

## Dialogue
- Mir {hope}: Braid hall. Sign there, both of you, and the two outside when they come in.
- Mir: Sit. There is barley and there is fish and there is nothing else until the sixth.
- Bron: We can pay.
- Mir: Pay. You cannot, and I did not ask.
- Mir: Your Sallowgate bill came down the rail. It shows cleared. It also shows unpaid.
- Bron: We were paid. Tovin paid us on the spot, in full.
- Mir: Tovin paid you. The poster has not paid Tovin, and there is no seal on it to make him.
- Pip: So the hall's out the money.
- Mir: The hall is out the money. I have had the Pale in this room and I minded it less.
- Pip: There's a sheet on the floor under your board.
- Mir {tense}: There is, and there is no seal on it. The seal or no bill. I will not pin it and I have told the woman so to her face.
- Mir: I have swept it aside twice and I have not put it in the stove. Eat. I will be outside.
