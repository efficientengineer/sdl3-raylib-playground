# Scene 0470: The Drowned Stacks

- location: a tall stone hall with still water at chest height across its floor, stone shelving standing half submerged, a plank walkway lashed between columns, a lamp set on the planks lighting upward, no daylight
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip
- mood: wonder -> dread
- staging: the stair out is at the left and above in every panel. The flooded aisles run away down and to the right. The party faces right and down. Lamps are carried low on the left

## Beat
The company's catalogue stops at the third floor down. Below that the aisles keep going, under water,
with the shelves still loaded — a hundred years of Vess work that was never listed because listing it
would have cost somebody money. This is a library, not a tomb: nothing is ruined, nothing is broken,
everything is exactly where it was put. Zeph goes in up to his chest without waiting to be told, and
Bron has his hand out a half-second too late, and Zeph is happier than he has been in three chapters.
The water is dead flat. It takes the lamplight badly and gives none of it back.

## Dialogue
- Zeph [1] {wonder}: Their catalogue stops at the third floor. We are on the fourth.
- Zeph [2]: The shelves are still loaded. All of them. Nobody has ever been down here to empty them.
- Bron [3]: Zeph. Wait for the light.
- Pip [4]: I've been down a lot of holes. This is the first one with a filing system.
- Zeph [5] {dread}: A hundred years of us, under water, because listing it would have cost somebody money.
- Bron [6]: How far down does it go?
- Zeph [7]: Nobody knows. That is the entire point of it.
