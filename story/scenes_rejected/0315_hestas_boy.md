# Scene 0315: Hesta's Boy

- type: talk
- location: a small warm bakery, a brick oven arch at the back with its iron door open, a flour-dusted plank counter, a long wooden peel leaning by the jamb, firelight from the oven at the left, early morning
- characters: Ollo, Bron, Pip, Lyra, Zeph
- mood: hope
- backdrop: 0310_the_welcome:2

## Beat
The warmest room in the act. Ollo feeds them mid-sentence, apologises twice for things that are
not his fault, and talks about Bron's mother with real affection and no detail at all — a good
sort, a way with a queue, a terrible singer. He believes every word of it. Pip adopts him inside
ninety seconds, starts stealing bread immediately, and he lets her and then hands her more. The
small brass numbered badge at his collar is in plain sight the whole time and nobody looks at it,
including him. He speaks about the baker before him by name and in the past tense, cheerfully,
without once wondering where the man went. His last word is that Bron's house is still standing
and nobody has taken it.

## Dialogue
- Ollo {hope}: Sit down. Not there, that's the flour. There. Eat that while it's hot.
- Pip: I'm sitting where the flour is.
- Ollo: You are, and that's a coat ruined, and it's my fault.
- Bron: It isn't.
- Ollo: It is, though.
- Ollo: Your mother. Hesta. She was a good sort. She had a way with a queue, your mother.
- Bron: A way with a queue.
- Ollo: She'd have them laughing and they'd stop minding they were fourth. Terrible singer, mind.
- Zeph: Terrible. In what respect terrible.
- Ollo: In every respect. Flat as a plank and loud about it.
- Lyra: How long have you had the oven?
- Ollo: Eleven years. Denner had it before me, and Denner was better at it, and everyone here is too polite to say so.
- Pip: I've taken two.
- Ollo: Take three. The third one's the one you'll want later.
- Ollo: I'm sorry, I can't stand still and talk. It's the oven. She wants feeding whether anybody's hungry or not.
- Ollo {hope}: Your house is still up, you know. South row, third along, green door. Nobody's ever taken it.
