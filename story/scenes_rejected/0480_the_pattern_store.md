# Scene 0480: The Pattern Store

- type: talk
- location: a dry stone room above the water line lined with hand-cut index boards in ranks, a plank walkway ending at its door, a trolley of paper folders, one lamp on a bracket lighting from the right, no daylight
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip, Ket
- mood: wonder -> tense

## Beat
At the bottom of the stacks, above the water line, a wall of index boards cut by hand by people who
had no reason to be careful and were anyway. Zeph finds the entry and reads out what the Vess called
the thing under the hills before the company had a word for it. It is not a power source and it is
not a mystery: it is a store of rule sets, kept somewhere they could be run and re-run for ever,
because a rule that stops being run stops being right. Ket, who has been waiting a very long time for
somebody to say something accurate in her hearing, confirms it in one word and adds the item she came
for. Then Bron asks the only question he ever asks, and nobody in the room has an answer, and the
scene stops there.

## Dialogue
- Zeph {wonder}: Board nine. The archivists' own hand, before the Concession.
- Zeph: They did not call it the Seam. They called it a store of patterns.
- Pip: Patterns of what?
- Zeph: Rules. Weather. Seed stock. Soil chemistry. Where the water sits and how deep it sits.
- Zeph: And the towers. The nine towers are an entry on this board.
- Lyra: A store of rules. Not a store of power.
- Zeph: Not a source of anything. A store — and it runs what it stores.
- Zeph: "A rule that stops being run stops being right." Their words. That is why it is two thousand feet down.
- Ket: Correct.
- Ket: The array is in it. Nine towers, their governors, their tolerances, and the order they were to be brought up in.
- Ket: I have asked to read that eleven times.
- Pip: So the singing is—
- Zeph {tense}: It is being run. That is all it has ever been. It is being run.
- Bron: Then what is the company pulling out of it?
