# Scene 0220: The Man on the Bench

- location: a short brick platform beside laid rail, an iron bench bolted to the boards, a lamp post with an unlit lantern, a stack of crated freight under canvas, flat grey morning light from the left, steam drifting across
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip
- mood: pleasant, and then not
- staging: The rail and the trams are along the left. The bench and the seated man are at the right against the brick wall, the man facing left. The party come from the left and stay left of him. Everyone's attention is on the man

## Beat
A man in a company coat is sitting on the platform bench watching the trams come in. He is warm,
well fed, healthy and friendly, he makes eye contact, and he is having a nice morning. He says good
morning twice. He does not know where he is staying and is not troubled by it. Asked where he slept
last week he thinks about it agreeably, for some time, and then asks Lyra whether she is warm
enough. Nothing is wrong with him. A conductor greets him by the number at his collar and he greets
him back, and the tram pulls out, and he watches it go, entirely content. Lyra, whose whole job is
sitting with people, says nothing at all about it.
