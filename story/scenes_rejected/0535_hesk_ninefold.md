# Scene 0535: Hesk Ninefold

- type: talk
- location: stepped barley terraces walled in dry stone, half of them bare brown earth, hand-dug water channels and stacked hoes, a low stone hut at the field corner, hard clear morning light from the left
- characters: Bron, Pip, Ket, Hesk
- mood: cold wind, colder manners

## Beat
Hesk establishes in a dozen lines that she is right about nearly everything and pleasant about none
of it. She wants to know what four hunters and a keeper did at Sallowgate. Bron tells her straight,
including the part where they opened a door, and she takes that in with both hands. She asks his
name, and what she says about it he shrugs off without noticing that he has no answer to what comes
after it. Then she turns on Ket and is formally, exactly polite in a way that is visibly worse than
rudeness, and Ket answers her civilly, and it costs Ket something that nobody in the scene names.
Then Hesk tells them where to sleep, which is the first hospitality anyone on this cliff has offered.

## Dialogue
- Hesk {confront}: Four hunters and a keeper on my lane. What did you do at Sallowgate?
- Bron: Took a bill. Cleared an entrance. There was a company crew behind the door already.
- Hesk: Say the middle part again.
- Bron: We opened a door.
- Pip: In fairness it was mostly open when we—
- Hesk: It was shut a thousand years. Now it is not. Your name.
- Bron: Bron Sallow.
- Hesk: Sallow. That's not a name. That's a word off a map. Which hold?
- Bron: Tellwater.
- Hesk: Mm. Keeper. You are welcome on this terrace. You will not go near my door.
- Ket: Correct. I have not asked you for it.
- Hesk: Your people asked mine for a thousand years. The answer is written in my own hand and it has not moved.
- Ket: I have read it. Thank you for being exact.
- Hesk: The barn at the lane end is dry. Straw, and a stove you won't need. Eat before you sleep.
