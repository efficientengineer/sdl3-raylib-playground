# Scene 0865: Ask Her Anything

- type: talk
- location: a low vaulted stone cellar with a bedroll and a crate table, a survey satchel open on it, an oil lamp and a tin cup, a drain grate in the floor, warm lamplight from the left, no daylight
- characters: Maren, Bron, Zeph, Hesk, Ket, Pip
- mood: wonder, then tense
- backdrop: 0860_the_woman_on_the_crate:1

## Beat
Boots on the steps, a man coming down slowly because he is fifty-eight and it is dark, and thirty
seconds before he arrives. Zeph asks her everything a reader would ask and she answers all of it
plainly and none of it usefully, because she does not have the company's vocabulary and nobody has
ever offered her one: she does not know what she is, she knows what she can do. Hesk asks the only
question that matters to Hesk and gets a flat no, and there is no second tone under a human voice to
check it against, so believing her is a decision somebody has to make on their own. Bron asks where
she goes next and she answers a question a woman in Dunnow asked her about a dog an hour ago, and
apologises for it, and the apology is worse than the answer. Then she asks them, courteously, to go
up the steps. Bron does not move, so nobody moves. That is the party's choice and she does not make
it for them.

## Dialogue
- Zeph {wonder}: What are you?
- Maren: I don't know. I know what I can do. Nobody ever gave me the other half of it and I stopped waiting for it.
- Zeph: Then describe the doing. Exactly. I am extremely good at listening to exactly.
- Maren: I take somebody's hands — sorry, you said exactly. Skin on skin, both hands, and I give them their own back.
- Maren: It runs about a minute for an hour of them. Some of them are long.
- Zeph: And afterwards you still have it.
- Maren: No. Afterwards it's theirs and it isn't mine, and I couldn't make another one if you paid me. I wouldn't know how.
- Hesk: Have you ever done it to somebody who didn't ask?
- Maren: No.
- Hesk: ...
- Zeph {tense}: Hesk. There's nothing under her voice. She's human. There is no tone in her to go flat.
- Hesk: Then I'll make my own mind up. I generally have to.
- Bron: Where do you go after here?
- Maren: The dog was under the water butt. It had been under there the whole while and it was perfectly happy.
- Maren: ...I'm sorry. That was for somebody else and it was an hour ago. Please go up the steps. This next part isn't yours to watch.
