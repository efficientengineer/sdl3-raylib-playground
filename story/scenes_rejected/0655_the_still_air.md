# Scene 0655: The Still Air

- type: talk
- location: a rough natural gallery whose whole back wall is packed hair-fine pale gold filament lying in strata, a plank walkway and handrail in front, a dropped lamp on the boards lighting upward, no daylight
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip, Ket, Hesk
- backdrop: 0650_down_bore_four:4
- mood: dread, then nothing to do with it

## Beat
Zeph is happier than he has been all game and is measuring a wall with his hands. Ket reads the site's
own boards off the drift-end post — the draw head's rating, the branch's density, four years of
quarterly figures — and gives them flat, because to her they are maintenance and nothing else. The
second figure has been coming down for four years. She says what that means about the work in the
same voice she uses for a spindle. Then Lyra asks the question nobody has asked, and nobody in the
gallery can answer it: Ket does not know, and says so in those words rather than guessing, and Pip
says the thing she says when she is frightened. The dust goes on hanging exactly where it is.

## Dialogue
- Zeph {dread}: Four feet of it in the wall here. Eleven at the corner. I have it as one article, not a seam of separate ones.
- Zeph: Strata like grain. And it brightens, and the bands always run the same way. Always. I have watched eleven of them.
- Ket: The board is on the post behind you. They write it up every quarter and nobody takes it down.
- Ket: Draw head rated at four hundred. Branch density at the face, this quarter: one hundred and seventy.
- Ket: Four years ago it was three hundred and ten. It has come down every quarter since, and not evenly.
- Hesk: Meaning what.
- Ket: Meaning they are taking more out of it than is arriving. That is the work. That is what the work is.
- Ket: A thinner branch is a slower yield. They will sink deeper and they will widen the head. That is also the work.
- Lyra: What happens to the hole when there's nothing left in it?
- Ket: ...
- Ket: Not known.
- Lyra: Does anybody know?
- Ket: Not known. I will not guess at it. I have been wrong twice in a thousand years and both times I guessed.
- Pip {tense}: Right. Well. I've seen it. I've seen the famous wall. Can we go and stand in some weather now.
