# Scene 0890: The Tannery Yard

- location: an open yard of sunken stone soaking pits in rows, hides pegged on drying frames along the wall, a wooden stirring paddle leaning, a pump on a stone base, hard low sun over the ravine lip from the right, morning
- characters: Ivo, Maren, Bron, Pip
- mood: dread, then sorrow
- staging: Ivo stands at the left of frame in the gateway, facing right. Maren stands at the right facing left. Bron and Pip are at the yard gate in the foreground and do not come further in. Nothing moves toward anything

## Beat
The party comes round the corner at first light and stops dead, because Warden Ivo Teach has her.
No constables, no cart, no drawn weapon: a very big man and a tired woman alone in a tannery yard,
and she is not running and has made no attempt to. He says her full name, the way he says everyone's
full name. She says his back, with the timing exactly right, and her voice arrives doubled on the
second half of it. Then she tells him one small physical thing about the ninth of Fallow that he has
never told anybody, because there was nobody to tell it to and it was not worth telling — and it is
not an accusation, and she is not using it, and she apologises for it afterwards. He says nothing at
all. He stands there a long moment with his eyes shut, and then he steps out of the gateway and lets
her walk past him, exactly the way he stepped aside for Bron on a ridge road in chapter three. The
party understands this one no better than that one.
