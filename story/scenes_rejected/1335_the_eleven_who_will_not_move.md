# Scene 1335: The Eleven Who Will Not Move

- type: talk
- location: a plank sledge deck with a hand-crank drum at the stern, a bench of lashed crates, the mast foot and sheet cleats, a water barrel roped down, hard flat daylight from above, salt glare
- characters: Threnn, Domma, Zeph, Bron, Sefa
- mood: tense

## Beat
Two miles west of Braid on the laid rail, walking the line ahead of tonight's run, and there is a
sledge standing on it with eleven people aboard who will not move. They are not protesting and they
are not being stubborn: they are in the middle of the day's passage, a passage broken in the middle
is a passage lost, their line has held its record since year 700, and they have four hours to go.
They are perfectly calm about it, entirely immovable, and correct. Sefa offers a way out that is
lawful, costs nobody a fine and takes ten minutes, and she offers it in good faith and it would
take four hours of seven hundred years off them. Domma answers it properly, as bookkeeping, and
Sefa does not concede and is not sorry. Zeph says nothing at all for a long time. Then he asks one
question, and it is not about any of that, and he sits down on the deck.

## Dialogue
- Threnn {tense}: Rail. Yes, we are on the rail. We are also four hours from the end of today's passage. Both are true.
- Bron: How long have you been stopped?
- Threnn: We are not stopped. The sledge is stopped. Two hours and forty minutes since the crank went cold.
- Domma: Sit, if you are staying. There is deck, and it is warmer than the salt is.
- Threnn: Eleven sledges behind us tonight. We counted them twice while you were walking up.
- Sefa: Then hear the whole of my offer, because it is lawful and it takes ten minutes.
- Sefa: The order lets a named officer set occupants down at a siding and run the sledge on. Nobody is touched or fined.
- Sefa: And I will take the passage down in writing while you walk. Every word. I am fast and I do not lose words.
- Domma: Thank you. That is a real offer and I will answer it properly. Paper cannot be asked a question.
- Domma: If I drop a figure tonight, Threnn has it and puts me right before I am off the line. Your page would not.
- Threnn: And a passage broken in the middle is not held over. It is gone. Tomorrow we begin on the far side of a hole.
- Sefa: I have given you the lawful answer. You may refuse it. I am entitled to think it expensive.
- Zeph: ...
- Zeph: Where are you up to?
- Threnn: A river six hundred years out of the ground. The second fork, the four villages on it, and what each paid in six twelve.
