# Scene 0590: The Standing Post

- location: a low cut-stone chamber at the stair foot with a stone gate slab winched half up, iron chain hanging slack from a drum, a stone kerb worn hollow, one carried lamp lighting from the left, no daylight
- characters: Hesk, Bron, Pip
- mood: dread, then a fight nobody wants
- staging: The gate and the armoured figure are on the right of every panel; the party comes in from the left and stays on the left facing right. Everyone's attention is on the figure in the gap

## Beat
One complete hall-guard, correct in every particular, standing in the gap with its halberd level.
It gives the warning in the oath's words and does not move, because it will not strike first and it
will not let anyone by. Hesk steps out in front and says her one mangled line of the relief formula,
and the helm turns down toward her and waits — and then gives the warning again, because she has
only the back half of it. Her face after that is the worst thing in the chamber. They have to
exhaust it, which everybody standing there understands is not the same as winning.
