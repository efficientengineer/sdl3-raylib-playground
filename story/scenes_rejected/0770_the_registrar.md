# Scene 0770: The Registrar

- location: a small tidy office of shelved boxes, a desk with a green shade lamp, a chair for a visitor, reading lenses on a chain hung on a hook, a shuttered window behind, warm lamplight from the left, evening
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip, Ket, Hesk, Sefa
- mood: tense, then sorrow
- staging: Sefa and the desk are at the right of every panel with the shelved boxes behind her. The party comes in from the left and stands there. Zeph is at the front of them, and everyone's attention goes right, to her

## Beat
She has been waiting up. A flagged docket is a flagged docket and she reads everything that comes
across her desk, so the lamp is lit at three in the morning, her coat is still on, and the visitor's
chair has been pulled out square and left empty. She is tall and spare and entirely unafraid of six
armed people in a doorway, and she is very good company for about ninety seconds. Then she gives the
price. The money is small and slightly funny — the schedule was superseded eleven years ago and
superseded stock goes out by weight — and she is completely serious about it. The other half of the
price is that she would like to be asked for it by name, out loud, in front of his friends. Zeph
refuses. She does not argue, does not repeat herself, does not raise her voice, and does not move
her hand off the blotter. Nobody in the room helps him. He says it. She thanks him, correctly, and
goes to the shelf, and never once uses his name, and that is worse than anything she could have
done.

## Dialogue
- Sefa [1] {tense}: Come in. There is one chair and there are six of you, and I am not going to solve that for you.
- Sefa [2]: Your docket came up at five. I read everything that comes up. Which of you is paying?
- Zeph [3]: The Tellwater instrument. Ninety-three. And the schedules that go with it.
- Sefa [3]: Superseded in ninety-six. Superseded stock goes out by weight — four marks the pound, and that one is three pounds and a little.
- Bron [4]: Twelve marks and four.
- Sefa [4]: And one other thing, which is that I should like to be asked for it by name. Out loud. In front of your friends.
- Zeph [5]: No.
- Pip [6]: ...How long can you sit like that?
- Zeph [7]: Registrar Sefa Quill.
- Sefa [8] {sorrow}: Thank you. That is the correct form of it.
