# Scene 1115: Hesta's Boy

- type: talk
- location: a small warm bakery, a brick oven arch at the back with its iron door open, a flour-dusted plank counter, a long wooden peel leaning by the jamb, firelight from the oven at the left, early morning
- characters: Ollo, Bron, Pip
- mood: sorrow

## Beat
In Green this man put a flour-white hand on Bron's shoulder in the square and called him Hesta's
boy one degree too well. Now he is packing a bakery he never owned, and he knows that Bron knows,
and he apologises for it while feeding him, badly, twice, and cannot stop working while he talks.
He is not asking to be forgiven. Bron does not offer it. What they find instead is that the same
office handed them both the same page. Pip stands at the door and says nothing for a long time.

## Dialogue
- Ollo {sorrow}: You're back. Sit — not there, that's flour. There. Eat that while it's warm.
- Bron: I'm not hungry.
- Ollo: No. Eat it anyway. It's coming out of the oven whether anybody's hungry or not.
- Ollo: I had my hand on your shoulder in the square. They'd given me your mother's name that morning.
- Bron: I know.
- Ollo: I'm sorry for it. I've had six weeks to find a better sentence than that and that's still the one.
- Bron: They gave you a page.
- Ollo: A page and a badge. You get the page at Windrow and you read it on the cart coming up.
- Bron: So did I.
- Ollo: ...The oven wants feeding. Talk to me while I do it, I can't stand still and be looked at.
- Ollo: My posting says Denner. Role: Denner, baker. Eleven years of a man with one letter of a name.
- Ollo: There's a book of his in that drawer. Recipes, in a hand that isn't mine, signed D.
- Pip: Did you ever ask who he was?
- Ollo: I wrote to the office. They sent me a form for a lost item and I couldn't think what to put in the box.
- Ollo: The baker's got a posting closing on the 11th and nowhere to be on the 12th. He'll think of something.
- Ollo {hope}: My name's Ollo. It's been my name the whole time. It's just nobody's used it.
