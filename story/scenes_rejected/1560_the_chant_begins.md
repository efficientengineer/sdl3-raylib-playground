# Scene 1560: The Chant Begins

- location: a great oval chamber of fitted stone with concentric stepped tiers going down to a central floor disc, stone standing posts at the rim, a seam face filling the back wall, faint even light from that face
- characters: Ilven, Sem, Maren, Zeph
- mood: sorrow, then hope
- staging: The Vess line sits in a half-ring on the floor disc at the centre and left, facing right. Maren stands at the foot of the filament wall at the right, facing left toward them. The wall fills the back and the right. The gallery and the stair are above and behind at the left

## Beat
A record being laid down. No candles, no circle, no light, and nothing in the wall reacts to any of
it at any point. Eleven Vess sit down on cold stone in a half-ring and Ilven takes the keeper's
account first, because an Onn's is exact and dated and gives the rest of it a spine to hang on. The
keeper of the dark Dree well stood on her platform at Tellwater on the 9th of Fallow, 1093, and
watched the whole of it from dusk to first light, and has answered anyone who asked for fourteen
years, and in fourteen years one person has asked. She gives times and figures and no feelings at
all and it is unbearable. Then Maren begins, at the beginning, in order, name by name, and Zeph
answers every line back to her, and Ilven says one word between them. Five minutes gone. Eleven
hours to run.

## Dialogue
- Ilven [1] {sorrow}: The keeper first. An account with times in it gives the rest of it a spine.
- Sem [2]: Sem-Dree. Keeper of the Dree well at Tellwater. I was on the platform from dusk to first light.
- Sem [2]: Twenty-one forty, the run started. Twenty-two oh two, the relay answered. Twenty-two thirty-one, nine seconds.
- Sem [3]: Four hundred and seven in the town. Nobody fell. Two in the lane kept walking until they stopped.
- Sem [3]: I have answered this whenever I was asked. In fourteen years I have been asked once, this year.
- Ilven [4]: Taken. — Ostry. From the beginning, in order. Do not summarise and do not hurry.
- Maren [5] {hope}: Tremmel, Ilsa. Shift engineer, third season on the night run. She liked the winch house because it was warm.
- Zeph [6]: Tremmel, Ilsa. Shift engineer, third season on the night run. She liked the winch house because it was warm.
- Ilven [7]: Held. — Next.
