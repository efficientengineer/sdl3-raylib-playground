# Scene 1495: The Choir Stair

- location: a squared stone archway at the low end of the street, its stone bar lifted aside, a stair dropping into darkness beyond it, a threshold step worn hollow, a lamp column on the right lighting from above, no daylight
- characters: Bron, Ket, Sefa
- mood: dread, then tense
- staging: The archway and the stair going down are at the right and below; the lit street of Thurn is behind the party at the left and above. Everyone faces right and down toward the stair

## Beat
The bar is off the archway, lifted from the inside, the way everything down here has always been
done. On the third step down, folded and set neatly to one side where somebody would find it, is a
canvas travelling satchel with a road's worth of wear in it and nothing anybody needs. It was not
dropped. It was put down. That is the only foreshadowing of her choice in the whole game and nothing
in this scene may explain it. Ket reads a gauge against her new specification and gives the number
the player has been waiting for since the chapter opened: the branch under Thurn is thinning at a
rate she can measure, and the only thing in the Basin that size is the Long Bore. Sefa says the cold
true thing about the woman who has the chair. Behind and above them the street goes on being lit and
swept and full, and nobody in it is facing the archway, and nobody in it knows that anyone is leaving.

## Dialogue
- Bron [1] {dread}: Bar's off. Lifted from the other side.
- Bron [2]: That's hers.
- Sefa [2]: Folded, and set where it would be found. That is not a woman who dropped a bag.
- Ket [3]: The branch under this floor is thinner than it was yesterday. I have it at four parts in a hundred.
- Ket [3]: That is the Long Bore. Nothing else in the Basin is that size.
- Sefa [4] {tense}: Then she will not wait for it to go. She will cap it, with all of this still inside it.
- Bron [5]: How long have we got.
- Ket [6]: Not known. Days. Not weeks.
