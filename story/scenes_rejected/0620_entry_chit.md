# Scene 0620: Entry Chit

- type: talk
- location: a plank-walled office with a long counter, pigeonholes of paper folders behind it, a ledger open on a slope with a pen rest, a brass tally frame on the wall, flat daylight from a window at the left, morning
- characters: Bron, Pip, Zeph, Emmet
- mood: procedure, played straight

## Beat
The man behind the counter is the man from the folding table behind the ancient door in Turn. He
remembers them exactly and is neither frightened nor pleased about it; he is four clerks short and it
is twenty past eleven. He explains what can be released, to whom, on what form, and every word of it
is correct. Pip tries charm, then confusion, then a mark and a half laid flat on the counter, and he
declines all three courteously and writes the third one down. Then Zeph asks a question next to the
thing they came for, and Sark answers the question he was asked and the one he was not, accurately,
because that is what he does. He is not obstructing them. He would have it on the counter in four
minutes if they had the piece of paper, and he says so, and means it.

## Dialogue
- Emmet {tense}: Counter's for releases. A release wants the form, and the form wants a chit.
- Pip: A chit.
- Emmet: An entry chit. We have had this conversation.
- Pip: We have. There was a door in it.
- Emmet: There was. You had no chit then either. You were very consistent about it.
- Zeph: Entry to what, precisely. There is no door here. There is a counter and a wall of boxes.
- Emmet: Entry to the record. Same chit. The door was never the point. The ledger is the point.
- Pip: Say we were family. Of somebody on it.
- Emmet: Then you would bring a chit, and I would be delighted.
- Pip: Would a mark and a half go anywhere at all?
- Emmet: It would go in the day book. A mark and a half, offered at the counter, twenty past eleven. It's in now.
- Zeph: How far back does this counter go?
- Emmet: Seven years on site, current and closed. Older than that is Vantage. The Rim district payroll is here, if that is what you are circling.
- Emmet {hope}: Bring me a chit and I will have the page on this counter in four minutes. I am not being difficult. I am being quick.
