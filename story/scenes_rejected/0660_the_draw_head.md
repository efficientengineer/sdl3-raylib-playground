# Scene 0660: The Draw Head

- location: a shaft-bottom chamber with a brass draw head on a gimbal hanging above a stone floor, a runaway winch drum spooling cable, a valve pedestal, dust standing motionless, one lamp on a stanchion at the left
- characters: Bron, Ket
- mood: tense, then confront
- staging: The draw head hangs at the right of every panel above the stone floor. The winch house and the brake stand are on the left. The party and the crew face right toward the bell; the way out is left and up

## Beat
The winch runs away with nobody's hand on it. The night-shift crew chief does everything correctly
and in order — brake, clutch, cut the drive, call it up the shaft — and none of it changes anything,
and he keeps doing it, in order, from the top, which is the most frightening thing in the chapter.
The brass bell swings out of true on its gimbal and the air around it goes visibly still: the dust
stops, every flame in the chamber stands up straight, and a washer knocked off the stand hangs a
half-beat wrong on the way down. Nothing in this room has any intention. Nothing here can be beaten.
For the first time in twelve hundred years Ket says the word "stop" out loud to a machine.
