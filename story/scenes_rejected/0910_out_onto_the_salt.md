# Scene 0910: Out Onto the Salt

- location: a wire-fenced yard on white salt holding three survey crawlers nose to tail, a stencilled company plate bolted on the nearest one, a plank watch hut, one lamp on a pole lighting from the right, night
- characters: Bron, Pip, Hesk
- mood: comic theft, then scale
- staging: The yard gate is on the right and the open salt lies beyond it. The party moves left to right all through the scene, the crawler faces right, and everything anyone looks at is right of frame

## Beat
Thirty seconds of everything going wrong in the correct order. Pip is over the wire before Zeph has
finished counting, the boiler is warm because Ket said it would be, and the gate goes down flat under
the tracks with two watchmen running for the hut rather than for the crawler, because nobody is paid
enough to stand in front of eleven tons. Nobody is hurt. Then the yard is behind them and the noise
stops mattering, because there is nothing out here to hear it. The last picture is the point of the
chapter: a machine the size of a house on an absolutely flat white plain, and the party has a vehicle
for the first time in the game.
