# Scene 0450: Five Lights

- type: talk
- location: a stone ring tower with three of its upper rings cracked and strapped with iron bands, scavenged brass pipework patched along one side, a plank scaffold at its foot, a hand-cart of parts, flat grey morning light
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip, Ket
- mood: tense -> hope
- backdrop: 0445_the_meddra_well_head:6

## Beat
The party's first real conversation with an Onn, and it goes wrong in the way that makes you like
somebody. Pip makes the joke everyone makes and Ket answers it as a question, with the figure, and
then offers her the months as well, and Pip cannot get back out. Ket is not offended and is not
being clever; she is enjoying it, and she knows exactly how funny she is. Zeph asks the thing a
reader asks and gets a correction that has been filed a hundred times and never once answered. Bron
asks what she needs, because that is what he does with people, and gets a list with weights on it.
Then she asks what they need, writes it under the chain, and goes back to work.

## Dialogue
- Pip {tense}: So how old are you? Thousand? Give or take?
- Ket: One thousand two hundred and sixty-one.
- Pip: ...
- Ket: You may have the months as well. There are eight of them and I know which.
- Pip: ...I'm going to go and stand over there now.
- Lyra: She was joking.
- Ket: I know. It was a good one. The number is also true. Both things fit.
- Zeph: Does the Works know this well runs?
- Ket: The Works advertises the Basin's only working skywell. That is Ossun. It is not correct.
- Ket: I say so whenever I hear it said. Nothing has ever come of saying so. I say it anyway.
- Ket: I am at five lights. Three are out. You should plan around it.
- Bron {hope}: What do you need?
- Ket: Brass governor spindle, eleven ounces. Quarter-inch chain, forty feet, sixty pounds. Lamp oil, four gallons.
- Ket: And a hand on the scaffold who does not talk while I count. — What do you need? I will put it under the chain.
