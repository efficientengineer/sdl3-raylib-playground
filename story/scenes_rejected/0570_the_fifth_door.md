# Scene 0570: The Fifth Door

- type: talk
- location: a squared door-mouth in sandstone filled with fitted cut-stone blocks, an iron door-key ring set in the centre stone, worn steps in front of it, a stacked cairn to one side, flat noon light
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip, Ket, Hesk
- mood: a real argument

## Beat
Ropes and mortar tools laid out on the steps, and the argument that has to happen before anybody
touches them, and it is a real one. Ket states her terms in plain numbers, because they are all she
has. Hesk says shut it and put the stone back and tell nobody, and she is not wrong, and everybody
standing there knows she is not wrong. Bron's case is the Sixth Door, which Cadder is taking on a
lease whatever anybody votes: somebody should go down one of these on purpose, before it is done for
a wage. Lyra ends it by asking for the thing she came to ask for. Hesk loses. She will not put a
hand on the fill, she never says that it is right, and she stays.

## Dialogue
- Ket {confront}: The maintenance specification for the array is behind one of these doors. I would like to look at it.
- Ket: I am at five lights. There are six hundred and eleven of us and there will never be another. That is all of my argument.
- Hesk: Shut it. Shut it and put the stone back and tell nobody.
- Hesk: Ask, and be told no, and go down the hill. Your people did that for a thousand years and nobody died of it.
- Ket: Correct. Nobody died of it. Four thousand of us became six hundred and eleven while nobody died of it.
- Bron: Cadder leaves here with the Sixth on a twenty-year lease. That door opens whatever the council votes.
- Bron: Somebody should go down one of these meaning to, before it's done for a wage.
- Hesk: Meaning to is not a plan. It is a mood.
- Pip: If it isn't us it's a drill crew, and they won't count anything or write anything down.
- Hesk: Then it's them and not me. That is not nothing. That is the only thing I have ever had.
- Lyra: Hesk. May I say the thing I came up here to say?
- Lyra: I'd like to stop arguing and go in. I'd like that to be because we decided.
- Hesk: ...
- Hesk: I'll not lift one stone out of that fill. Not one block under my hand. Say you understand that.
- Bron: Understood.
- Hesk {tense}: You'll do it anyway. I'd rather be standing next to you when you do.
