# Scene 0260: The Climb

- type: talk
- location: a hill road of loose scree above a tram town, a dry stone wall going up on the right, wind-bent scrub, the town's lamps small below on the left and a dark ring tower black against the sky, cold failing light, night coming
- characters: Bron, Pip, Zeph, Lyra
- mood: cold, funny, and one joke that is not a joke

## Beat
The hill road up to the relay, in a wind that has teeth. Zeph and Pip are competing over which of
them is colder and both are losing. Bron is walking up a hillside at night in one sleeveless jacket
and is comfortable and is enjoying himself, and Pip demands an explanation, and then remembers the
story he told at the guild hall and makes the obvious joke. Bron does not flinch at it, because
there is nothing in him to flinch — he says that winter was a different thing, and that is all, and
nobody follows it up. The building has been visible for ten minutes and Pip has not seen it.

## Dialogue
- Zeph {hope}: My hands have stopped. I am reporting that, not complaining about it.
- Pip: My face has stopped. Beat that.
- Zeph: A face is not load-bearing.
- Pip: ...Bron. Bron, look at me. Where is your coat.
- Bron: Wearing it.
- Pip: That is a jacket with no sleeves in it.
- Bron: It's got a collar.
- Pip: You got *cold*? You lot sleep in snowdrifts for fun.
- Bron: That one was two nights. It was different.
- Pip: How much further?
- Lyra {tense}: We have been able to see it for ten minutes.
- Pip: I hate this hill, I hate that hut, and I hate that all of you could see it.
