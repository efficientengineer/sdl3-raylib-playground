# Scene 0598: The Hill Path

- location: a narrow goat path on a dry hill shoulder, dry stone scree and wind-bent scrub above it, the white salt flats opening far below, one lit headframe small on the crust, cold failing light from the right, dusk
- characters: Bron, Hesk
- mood: tense, then something like hope
- staging: The path runs from the right of the frame down and away to the left. The party walks left. The drop and the white basin are on the left; the hillside rises on the right

## Beat
Six of them on a goat path with the whole Basin opened out below: white salt to the horizon, a dead
ring tower, and one lit headframe with a town around it working a night shift. Hesk has her spear,
her key and a packed bag, and she has told nobody in the village that she is going, which Bron
notices and does not mention. He says out loud, for the first time, that they are going to go and
look at the company's hole instead of the Kell's. Hesk says that is what she came to watch. Below
them the headframe lights are small and busy and entirely ordinary, and the shift is working.
