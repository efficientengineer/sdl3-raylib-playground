# Scene 0715: Entry Chit

- type: talk
- location: a stone gate arch between tiers with an iron barrier down, a trestle desk with a lamp and a clipboard, a brass tally frame on the wall, a grey-coated constable's post stool, cold early morning light from the left
- characters: Bron, Pip, Lyra, Arden
- mood: tense, then hope

## Beat
First light at the Tier One road gate. The constable on the barrier is nineteen, his coat is two
sizes wrong, and he is so frightened of passing something he should not that he reads every line of
every permit out loud twice before he lets himself believe it. He apologises for the delay four
times. He cannot find the stamp on the back of the third sheet, and he is close to sending them
round to the day office, until Pip leans over and puts a finger on it. He is grateful in a way that
is hard to watch. He passes them, thanks them by name off the sheets, lifts the barrier himself
because the winch sticks, and tells them the steps are wet at the turn. Then he drops their docket
into a wire basket on the desk marked for Tier Five and reaches for the next cart. Nobody in the
party looks at the basket. Nobody in the party thinks about him again.

## Dialogue
- Arden {tense}: Six. Six of you, six permits, all the same date. That's — that's correct, that's in order, that's fine.
- Arden: Road permit, freight class, countersigned at Lomm. Road permit, freight class, countersigned at Lomm.
- Pip: You've read that one twice.
- Arden: I know. I do that. It's better than the other thing.
- Bron: What's the other thing?
- Arden: Passing something I shouldn't have. Sorry. Sorry, this is slow, I know it's cold.
- Arden: There's meant to be a stamp on the back of the third sheet and I can't get my eye on it —
- Pip: Under your thumb. The faint one.
- Arden: ...That's it. That's it. Thank you. I'd have had you standing here till the relief came at eight.
- Arden {hope}: Sallow. Quill. Rime. Ninefold. Ket-Ossun of Meddra. Sister Lyra. Go on through — mind the steps, they're wet at the turn.
- Bron: Thanks.
- Lyra: He was kind.
