# Scene 0290: What He Wanted

- type: talk
- location: a low chop-house room of dark plank walls, one long scrubbed table down the middle with the dishes cleared, a coal stove at the back, a steamed window, warm lamplight from a bracket at the left, night
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip
- mood: full, paid, and not sure what happened

## Beat
Plates cleared, money still sitting in the middle of the table, four people trying to work out
whether they have just been bought with the evidence in coins between them. Pip's position is that
he was nice. Lyra's is that he was nice and it worked and it is still working, at this table, now.
Zeph counts what they actually learned and it is smaller out loud than it felt: a name, an office,
a season. Bron will not let go of one small fact, which is that the season is moving east onto the
Tellwater road, and when Pip asks why he cares he tells her, flat and easy and warm, that it is
where he is from and he has not been back since he was thirteen. Somebody takes the money. Nobody
says the word bribe.

## Dialogue
- Pip {hope}: I liked him. I'm allowed to have liked him.
- Zeph: You are allowed. I liked him. That is the interesting part.
- Lyra: He was kind, he paid, and we've stopped being angry. That's a piece of work and it's still going on.
- Pip: It's money on a table. It isn't a trick.
- Zeph: What did we learn? Say it out loud. It is smaller out loud.
- Zeph: A name. An office. A season. I have it as three things.
- Bron: Four. The season's moving east.
- Zeph: East is a direction. It is not a thing.
- Bron: It's a road. This cut's nearly finished and the crews go on to the Tellwater road.
- Pip: Why do you care where they go?
- Bron: That's where I'm from. I haven't been back since I was thirteen.
- Pip: ...You've never said.
- Bron: You never asked. It's a town. It's got a well and a square and a fair in the autumn.
- Pip: ...I'm taking this, then. Nobody say anything.
- Lyra {tense}: Then we should go.
