# Scene 1535: The Only Bill We Ever Wrote

- type: talk
- location: the gallery rim of a great oval chamber of fitted stone, stone standing posts along it, stepped tiers dropping away below, a seam face filling the back wall, faint even light from that face
- characters: Sefa, Bron, Hesk, Pip, Ket, Ollo
- mood: tense, then hope
- backdrop: 1505_the_seam_face:1

## Beat
The logistics problem lands on the gallery rail about four minutes after it was created. A Vess
chant-line has to come a hundred and forty miles down a maintenance rail inside a day, from a town
that was emptied onto the seabed yesterday, and the only people in the Basin who move that fast are
hunters, and hunters do not move because somebody shouted. So Bron writes a bill on the back of a
survey sheet with Hesk's pencil: the work, the place, the fee. The fee is nothing and he writes
nothing on the line. Then he stops, because the third of the Four Lines is burned into the frame of
every board in the Basin and he is not carrying a seal. Sefa takes out the Registrar's seal of the
Clement Works — the one that turned a night into a resettlement — and presses it into the wax on a
bill asking for people to come and stop the Clement Works. Pip is gone up the stair before anyone
has agreed to anything.

## Dialogue
- Bron {tense}: Braid's a hundred and forty miles. The rail does it in under a day if nothing stops it.
- Sefa: Braid is empty. Four thousand of them are camped on the crust, and Ilven Tessen's line with them.
- Bron: Then somebody has to go and ask. Hunters move for a bill. Nobody moves for shouting.
- Pip: So write one.
- Bron: Bring a chant-line down the maintenance rail to the Ninth Door. Today. Fee—
- Bron: Fee: nothing.
- Hesk: The seal or no bill. That's the third Line and it's burnt into the frame at Sallowgate.
- Bron: I know what's on the frame. I've no seal on me.
- Sefa: I have.
- Sefa: Registrar, the Clement Works. This is the seal that turned a night into a resettlement.
- Sefa {hope}: Your bill asks nothing but questions. It is the best use I have ever found for it.
- Pip: Give it here. Nine minutes to the platform if nobody talks to me.
- Hesk: Write down who comes.
- Bron: There's a page for it already.
