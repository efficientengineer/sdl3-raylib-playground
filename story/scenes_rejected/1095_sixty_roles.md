# Scene 1095: Sixty Roles

- type: narration
- mood: administrative

## Beat
Chapter ending, over black, as the tram pulls out of Windrow. A company notice on the board at the
tram yard, read flat and correct in the Works' own register. Nobody speaks and nobody reacts. Two
short lines afterwards that state what a sealed roster is and do not editorialise about it. Then
the date.

## Dialogue
- Narrator {sorrow}: The Clement Works gives notice of the decommission of the Tellwater postings. Sixty roles.
- Narrator: Occupants will be reassigned against vacancies. The property will be closed and placed on a caretaker contract.
- Narrator: The roster will be sealed and the file transferred to the Registry at Vantage for retention.
- Narrator {dread}: A sealed roster is a roster nobody has to be shown. It is not destroyed. It is finished with.
- Narrator: After the 12th, the last paper saying who was in that town, and what each of them was hired to be, is in a box at Vantage.
- Narrator: Posted the 2nd of Dust, 1107.
