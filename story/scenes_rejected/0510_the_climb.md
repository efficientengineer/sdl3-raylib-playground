# Scene 0510: The Climb

- type: talk
- location: eleven hundred feet of cut sandstone steps zigzagging up a cliff face, the rail landing and the laid track shrinking away below, hard clear light, wind
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip, Ket
- mood: warm, breathless
- backdrop: 0505_the_terrace_landing:7

## Beat
Five people, one stair, and nothing to do but talk. Ket has been counting the steps aloud since the
bottom and will not be talked out of it. Zeph is six foot four and none of it is for this. Bron is
not breathing hard and has not noticed that this is offensive. Lyra asks Ket a polite question about
being tired and gets a precise, cheerful, extremely bleak answer, and the party's silence afterwards
is the joke. Pip changes the subject the only way she knows, which is by asking for a number.

## Dialogue
- Ket {hope}: Three hundred and nine. Three hundred and ten. Three hundred and eleven.
- Pip: Ket. I am begging you. Out loud. In front of people.
- Ket: You begged Bron for the last of the bread on Tuesday. This is the second time.
- Zeph: ...
- Zeph: I am six foot four. None of it is for this.
- Bron: Shorter steps. Don't look up. You'll get there.
- Zeph: You are not even breathing.
- Lyra: Ket. Do you get tired?
- Ket: Not the way you do. I have a reserve. It goes down, and the lights are how you read it.
- Ket: A stair costs more than standing. This one takes a part of a day I cannot put back.
- Ket: I have four hundred years of that left. Give or take. I do not give or take. You do.
- Pip: ...How many are left?
- Ket: Four hundred and nine.
