# Scene 1060: The File Room

- location: a windowless room of floor-to-ceiling pigeonhole shelving stuffed with paper folders, a rolling ladder on a rail, a bare table with one folder open on it, a hanging lamp overhead, night
- characters: Zeph, Hesk, Bron
- mood: cold, then worse
- staging: The party come in at the left of the long room and face right; the shelving runs away to the right; the lit reading desk stands at the far right end. Everybody's attention ends up pointing right, down the room, at the lamp that is already burning

## Beat
Eleven feet of shelving carrying one year and one town: four hundred boxes, in order, dusted once
a season. Maren's is twelve years thick and takes two of them to get it down. And at the far end
of a locked building at two in the morning, a lamp is burning over a reading desk, and on the
blotter under it, squared up, is one thin file with a docket slip clipped to its corner. Zeph reads
the slip from six feet away, recognises the hand and the initials, and stops dead. It was requested
eleven days ago by the Registrar and never sent back. Nobody in the room says why she would do
that. Nobody moves toward the desk.
