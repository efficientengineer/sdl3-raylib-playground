# Scene 0483: Overtime

- type: talk
- location: a stone vault lined with shallow brass drawers to the ceiling, an iron retrieval gantry stopped on its rails, a control pedestal of valve wheels, lamps on stanchions, no daylight
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip
- mood: tense -> hope
- backdrop: 0482_the_sleeve_vault:1

## Beat
The gantry is stopped and everybody is sitting down, including both guards, and nobody is dead. The
older guard has his back against a rack and is working out the overtime before he works out anything
else. Then he explains what he is going to write in the incident form, which is a cleaner and much
more flattering account than what actually happened, and Pip — appalled and deeply impressed — asks
why he would do that for them. He is not doing it for them. He is doing it for the shift, and his
reasoning is airtight and entirely local. Then he tells them which way to leave, and why.

## Dialogue
- Guard {tense}: Right. That's ten past. Overtime starts at ten past.
- Guard: Rate and a half after two, double after four. I'll be here till four filling in the form.
- Pip: What form?
- Guard: Incident. Three copies. What I'll write is: gantry ran on a stuck contact, two staff attended, no persons present.
- Pip: You're going to lie for us.
- Guard: No. I'm going to lie for the shift.
- Guard: An incident means an inspection. An inspection means somebody up the line takes an interest in Braid.
- Guard: Then they send us a new doorkeeper, and the new one is always worse.
- Second guard: The last one let the old ones sit inside when it rained.
- Pip: ...That's it? That's the whole argument?
- Guard: That's the whole job. — Go out by the water, would you.
- Guard {hope}: The door gets counted. The water doesn't.
