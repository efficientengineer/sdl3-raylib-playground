# Scene 002: The Guard

- location: a cold cut-stone corridor deep underground with square tooling marks on dressed walls, a great stone door standing open onto darkness at the right, one guttering torch in an iron wall bracket, dry dust, no daylight
- characters: Lyra, Zeph
- mood: dread
- staging: Lyra and Zeph stand on the left of the corridor facing right. The open doorway and the armoured figure standing in it are on the right, facing left toward them. Everyone's attention is on the figure in the doorway

## Beat
The great warded door grinds open on its pivot with nobody near it, and there is a hall-guard
standing in the gap: old segmented armour, a tattered red cloak, two small steady blue points in
the helm, absolutely still. Zeph is thrilled, because a thing that old opening itself is the best
day of his life, and he is already moving toward it to look at the shoulder plate, which is older
than the door it is standing in. Lyra puts an arm across his chest without taking her eyes off it.
It does not move at all. Bron and Pip are twenty feet back up the corridor, out of frame.

## Dialogue
- Zeph [1] {tense}: It opened by itself. Nobody has been near it.
- Lyra [2] {dread}: Zeph. That's old armour. Stand still.
- Zeph [3]: Old. You said old. That is nine hundred years past old.
- Zeph [3]: The shoulder plate is older than the door it is standing in. Two centuries older.
- Lyra [4]: Zeph. Step back.
