# Scene 1130: Tibb Orsk Rides In

- type: talk
- location: a neat sandstone town square with a raised stone well at its centre, swept flagstones, whitewashed housefronts with green shutters, a painted board over the bakery with no words, bright clean midmorning sun from the left
- characters: Tibb, Bron, Lyra, Pip
- mood: tense
- backdrop: 1105_the_postings_come_down:1

## Beat
Tibb has come two days up the Rim road on a borrowed mule to say a thing in person, because the
Even Hand does not send a thing like this by tram. The Pale went to the Sallowgate hall and
required it closed while there are wanted persons on the ledger, and Tovin-Caleth counted at them
until they went and stood outside instead. He is enjoying telling it and he is frightened, and he
covers the second one by asking whether anybody has eaten. He has bills stacking up that nobody
will take. He will be back for the fair. Nobody says Sela's name and he does not either.

## Dialogue
- Tibb {tense}: Two days on a mule. Don't laugh. It was the fastest thing on the Rim road that would have me.
- Tibb: The Pale came to the hall. Required it closed, while there's wanted persons on the ledger.
- Pip: Required.
- Tibb: Required. Six of them in the doorway. Very polite, hats off, the lot.
- Tibb: Tovin counted the bills out at them. Out loud. Nineteen out today. Then she said it twice more.
- Tibb: Then she told them the board eats at six and went back to writing up the wall.
- Bron: And?
- Tibb: And they stood about a while and then they went and stood outside instead. Hall's open.
- Tibb: Have you eaten? You look like you haven't eaten. None of you look like you've eaten.
- Lyra: I've eaten. Sit down, Tibb.
- Tibb: I've nine bills up that nobody'll take. I'd take all nine if I could stand in nine places.
- Tibb {hope}: I'm back for the fair, though. Man rides two days, he gets bread out of it.
