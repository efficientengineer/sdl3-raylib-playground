# Scene 0755: Say Her Name

- type: talk
- location: a plain hired office of cut stone over the workshop yards, a desk with a shaded lamp, a small iron stove with a half-full scuttle, two chairs, a shuttered window over the drop, warm lamplight from the left, evening
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip, Ket, Hesk
- mood: tense

## Beat
A rented room on Tier Two with a bad printed plan of the tiers weighted down on the desk. Pip has
walked the route twice and her half of the plan is good and concrete and gets them onto Tier Four
without anybody drawing anything. After that it stops, because the Registry does not check papers,
it checks signatures, against a register of signatures it wrote itself — you cannot forge your way
into that building, you can only be let into it. Zeph, who has been enjoying himself since
Sallowgate, stops talking for a long time in the middle of the room. Then he gives them the one fact
they need and refuses the rest of it in advance, and nobody pushes him, and nobody puts a hand on
him either. Hesk says the practical thing, which is that a thing like that gets worse if you sleep
on it, so they go tonight.

## Dialogue
- Pip {tense}: Right. Tier Four. Laundry hoist on the east face, up the cable gallery, out onto the school terrace. I've walked it twice.
- Pip: That's the easy half. Four to Five is a staff stair with one man on it and a door with a lock I like the look of.
- Bron: And the room at the top?
- Pip: The room at the top wants a signature.
- Zeph: It wants a signature they already hold. They keep a register of signatures and they check yours against it.
- Zeph: You cannot forge your way into that building. You can only be let into it.
- Hesk: Then who lets us in.
- Zeph: ...
- Zeph: The Registrar.
- Zeph: The Registrar is my mother. I have not said her name out loud since I was nineteen and I am not going to start in this room.
- Bron: All right.
- Hesk: Then we go tonight. Nobody sleeps twice on a thing like that.
