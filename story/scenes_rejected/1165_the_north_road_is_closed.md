# Scene 1165: The North Road Is Closed

- type: talk
- location: a straight dirt road leaving town between two low sandstone walls, wheel ruts, a leaning stone mile post, dry grass on the verges, flat overcast morning light, the town roofs low behind
- characters: Hanna, Bron, Lyra, Ondra, Arden
- mood: tense

## Beat
Morning of the third day, the party walking out with the roster book on a handcart. A thin cordon
across the road: six constables, a cart, and a sergeant who is polite, competent and bored. Kesk
states the position, quotes the schedule, and offers a genuinely reasonable compromise, which Pell
immediately agrees to do today. Then a runner calls her to the far end of town over an inventory
dispute about a stove, and she goes, because it is her job and the road is quiet, and she leaves
the road to the boy who is standing in it. Nothing in the scene is anybody's fault.

## Dialogue
- Hanna {tense}: Morning. That book's company property and it doesn't leave the town. That's the whole of it.
- Bron: It's a list of names.
- Hanna: It's personnel data, which is worse. I can't let it go and you can't carry it past me.
- Ondra: I can copy out anything on it that isn't personnel. Trades, dates, the building schedule. Today, if you like.
- Lyra: That's a fair offer.
- Hanna: It's the only offer on the road and it's mine, so I'd take it.
- Arden: If the — if the party would produce their papers. Please. Sir. Sister.
- Hanna: He's on a two-day commission. He's asked eleven people for papers this morning and read none of them.
- Hanna: ...What? Whose stove? It's a fixture. It stays. Tell him it's in the schedule and I'll be down.
- Hanna: It is never the big thing. It is always a stove.
- Hanna: Arden. Hold the road. Keep them talking and wait for me.
- Hanna: Nobody on this road has to do anything this morning.
