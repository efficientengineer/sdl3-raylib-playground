# Scene 0415: The Rails to Braid

- location: a Vess town on a terrace lip stepping down onto white dry seabed, narrow tall stone houses with shuttered openings, laid rail running to a flat horizon, a patched stone ring tower behind, morning sun from the left
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip, Cassa
- mood: arrival, high wind
- staging: the party stands on the left of frame facing right. The laid rail runs left to right through every panel. Braid, its terraces and the ring tower stand on the right and above. Cassa and her sledge are on the right, facing left toward the party

## Beat
A sail comes over the salt at speed with nothing under it but rail. Cassa Tessen brings a crank
sledge past them at full run, overshoots by fifty yards, cranks herself back, and is talking before
she has stopped moving. She wants passage money less than she wants an exact head count, and she
will not release the brake until she has said the number out loud. Behind her stands Braid: stacked
chant-halls cut down a cliff, rail sheds on the salt, and a ring tower three hundred feet high with
the air trembling faintly at its joints. Zeph has not told anyone he is from here and does not tell
them now. Bron is relieved. Pip is delighted. Lyra is watching Zeph.

## Dialogue
- Pip [1] {wonder}: That's a sail. There's no water and that is a sail.
- Cassa [2]: Too far — too far — hold on, I am coming back!
- Cassa [3] {hope}: Passage west? Passage west. How many of you?
- Bron [4]: Four.
- Cassa [5]: Four. One, two, three, four. Four. People say four and then they are six.
- Zeph [6]: ...
- Cassa [7]: Braid by dark. Hold the rope and not the rail. The rail takes fingers.
