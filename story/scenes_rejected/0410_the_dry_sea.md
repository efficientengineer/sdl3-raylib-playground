# Scene 0410: The Dry Sea

- type: talk
- location: a floor of white salt crust running flat to the horizon in every direction, two lines of laid rail going west, the terrace wall a low brown band far behind, hard flat morning light, no shade
- characters: Bron, Lyra, Zeph, Pip
- mood: wonder -> hope

## Beat
Four people who have never been out of one valley, standing on the floor of a sea. Pip is loud
because she is frightened and keeps describing the horizon to people who can see it. Zeph corrects
a distance twice, is wrong the second time, and concedes it with more grace than the situation
needs. Lyra wants to know what they are going to say to the first person they meet. Bron wants a
direction. Nobody says the name of the town they were run out of, and all four of them are careful
about it without appearing to be.

## Dialogue
- Pip {wonder}: There's nothing out here. I'm saying it out loud so somebody's said it.
- Pip: That's the horizon. That's also the horizon. That, over there, is the horizon as well.
- Zeph: Forty, you said. It is forty-one to Braid. Forty-one is not forty.
- Lyra: And what do we tell them when we get there?
- Bron: That we want passage.
- Lyra: They'll ask where we've come from.
- Pip: Up. We came from up.
- Zeph: ...Sixty-one. Not forty-one. I have had that figure wrong for years and I have said it twice today.
- Zeph: I would like it noted that I corrected myself, unprompted, and that I am still the only one of you who counts.
- Bron {hope}: The rails go west. We go west.
