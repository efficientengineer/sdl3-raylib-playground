# Scene 1555: Twenty Minutes Later

- type: talk
- location: a short plank platform on a cut-stone ledge beside laid rail, an iron lamp stanchion, a stacked pile of sleepers and spikes, a squared ancient doorway in the wall behind, lamplight from above, no daylight
- characters: Zeph, Ilven, Cassa, Bron
- mood: tense, then hope

## Beat
Twenty minutes later, same platform, same eleven people. Zeph does not ask them to take anything.
He asks to be taken into the line for the length of one record, as the bonded pair who answers it
back, which is the only way a chant has ever been laid down and the one request a first chanter
cannot refuse a Vess who can be checked. He is quieter and more formal than he has been in fifteen
chapters and there is no performance in any of it. Ilven tests his second tone with two questions
that are none of her business, and he answers both without hedging, and the tone holds through both.
Then Cassa gives the figure she has been dreading since the platform, and it is funny for exactly
one line and then it is not: eleven hours minimum, no stopping, and a passage broken in the middle
is a passage lost. Sefa is on the platform for all of it and says nothing and nobody looks at her.

## Dialogue
- Zeph {tense}: First chanter. I am not asking you to take anything.
- Zeph: I am asking to be taken in. For the length of one record. As the pair who answers it back.
- Ilven: Answered back. — That is a bonding, Reader. For the length of one record it is still a bonding.
- Ilven: Two questions. They are none of my business and you will answer them anyway.
- Ilven: Why were you put out of the annex?
- Zeph: I read four sealed sleeves I had not been given. I knew they were sealed. I would do it again tomorrow.
- Ilven: ...The tone holds. — Second. Are you asking because of the woman standing behind you?
- Zeph: No.
- Zeph: I am asking because there is a record and nobody has offered to hold it, and I can hold it.
- Ilven: ...The tone holds.
- Cassa: Eleven hours. That is the minimum, I have not rounded it, and I would have liked to round it.
- Cassa: You cannot stop. If you stop in the middle then the middle is gone and it does not come back.
- Zeph {hope}: Do I get to sit down?
- Ilven: No.
