# Scene 1545: Eleven Minutes of Empty Stair

- type: talk
- location: a long flight of broad cut-stone steps between dressed walls, iron handrails on both sides, a lamp stanchion at the head, cable coils stacked against the left wall, faint even light rising from below at the right, no daylight
- characters: Kerrow, Bron, Hesk, Sefa, Pip, Ket
- mood: confront, then sorrow
- backdrop: 1540_forty_one_charges:1

## Beat
The scene where the party loses the argument, and it is not close. Every objection they have, she
has already costed, and she has brought the working with her and offers it to anyone who wants to
read it. The record is a record and not people, and she quotes the rule better than they can, and
the one person in the party who could dispute it agrees with her out loud instead. Hesk refuses to
move, which is not an argument, and Kerrow says so without heat. And then the thing nobody can
touch: drawing thins a branch, a thin branch inverts, every large bore, no engineering anywhere in
it, and nobody standing on this stair has a plan that does not end with somebody else's town out in
the street at half past ten at night in about nine years. Nobody answers her. What happens instead
is that Pip takes her coat off and hands it over, because Kerrow is shaking and will not say so, and
the whole exchange about it is two words long.

## Dialogue
- Bron {confront}: Three hundred and forty living people over our heads.
- Kerrow: Six months to walk them out by hand, one at a time. I costed it coming down. I have hours.
- Sefa: And four hundred and eleven in the wall. You would put two thousand feet of hill on them.
- Kerrow: A record is not people. Nothing in it is alive and nothing in it is in pain. You know that better than I do.
- Sefa: ...I do.
- Hesk: I'm not moving off this stair.
- Kerrow: That isn't an argument, that's a position. I can outwait a position. I can't outwait a branch.
- Bron: Then take the bores out of the hills.
- Kerrow: Drawing thins a branch. A thin branch inverts. Every large bore. There's no engineering in it.
- Kerrow: Cap this one and in nine years it's Dunnow. Or Lomm. Or your hall, at half past ten at night.
- Kerrow {sorrow}: Give me a plan that doesn't end with somebody standing in a street, and I'll stand them down.
- Bron: ...
- Pip: Put this on.
- Kerrow: ...Emberrow?
- Pip: Emberrow.
- Kerrow: Thank you. Now I need eleven minutes of empty stair.
