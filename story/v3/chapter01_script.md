# Chapter one — reading script (generated, names substituted)


## Scene 0101: The Dummies

**Ottilie:** Here he goes. He is at the last one again.  
**Ottilie:** Watch its arm, Falke. Left. It always comes back on the left —  
**Falke:** I had it! I hit that thing eleven times!  
**Ottilie:** You hit it eleven times. It hit you once. That is the whole afternoon.  
**Falke:** My wrist is fine.  
**Ottilie:** Let go of it, then. I cannot look at a wrist you are holding.  
**Falke:** How is anyone meant to beat that thing? It hits back harder every time I hit it!  
**Ottilie:** I know. I have watched you find that out four times this week.  
**Falke:** Four?  
**Ottilie:** Four. Hold still. That is me finished until the evening.  
**Hart:** Again. From the gate.  
**Falke:** Again? I can't hold a stick!  
**Hart:** Tomorrow, then.  
**Falke:** Hart — say it straight. Am I ready or am I not?  
**Hart:** Not quite ready yet.  
**Falke:** That is what you said in the spring!  

## Scene 0102: The Errand

**Hart:** Ottilie. Eat here tonight. Sundown.  
**Ottilie:** You have asked me that every week for six years.  
**Hart:** And you have come every week for six years. Sundown.  
**Ottilie:** Sundown, then. I am at Linde's room until it gets dark.  
**Hart:** Falke. Town. Bread for three, and this bundle goes to the guild hall.  
**Falke:** What's in the bundle?  
**Hart:** The catch off the well winch. It sheared. I cut a new one.  
**Falke:** And the rest of it? That's a letter.  
**Hart:** Post. Give the whole bundle to the clerk and come home.  
**Falke:** It's sealed, and that's your mark on the seal. What's in it?  
**Hart:** Mine. Bread for three. Go.  
**Ottilie:** He will not tell you. He has never told me either, and I eat at his table.  
**Falke:** Then I'll ask the clerk when he opens it!  

## Scene 0103: The Counter

**Clerk:** Hart's bundle. Put it on the counter.  
**Falke:** The catch for the well winch is in there. And post.  
**Clerk:** The post I open now, so that you can tell him it arrived.  
**Falke:** He wouldn't say what was in it.  
**Clerk:** A job, and the money to pay for it. That is all a letter to me ever is.  
**Falke:** A job! Put it up on the board and I'll sign for it now.  
**Clerk:** The rule is that job sheets go up at dawn. Not tonight. Tomorrow, at dawn.  
**Falke:** Dawn. Then I'll stand here at dawn.  
**Clerk:** The board stays open all day. It closes on the last ring of the evening bell.  
**Falke:** One bell at the end of the day. I can hear that from the yard.  
**Clerk:** You can. I still cannot write your name in the book.  
**Falke:** Why not? I've trained four years!  
**Clerk:** No sword, no signature. An apprentice signs when his teacher says he is ready.  
**Falke:** My teacher is up a hill. How would you know what he says?  
**Clerk:** A hunter's first sword comes from his teacher. It is the sign. I look for the sword.  
**Stolz:** He looks for the sword, Falke. Mine took two years.  
**Falke:** Two years?  
**Stolz:** Two. I signed for the coast road job the morning after I got it.  
**Falke:** That's the best job on the board and it isn't even up yet!  
**Stolz:** Here. Four coin for your bread. Pay me back when you sign for something.  
**Falke:** I don't want your four coin, Stolz!  
**Stolz:** Take it. I would rather you owed me than went home short.  
**Falke:** I'll have a sword by the bell tomorrow and you can watch me sign!  

## Scene 0104: The Table

**Falke:** Job sheets go up at dawn. The clerk told me himself.  
**Hart:** Heard you the first time.  
**Falke:** And the board is open all day! I could sign and be gone by noon.  
**Hart:** You could not. You are not ready.  
**Falke:** I beat three of your machines! Three out of four!  
**Hart:** The fourth one is the one that is out on the road.  
**Ottilie:** He is right about the fourth one. You never wait, Falke.  
**Falke:** I hit it eleven times! What is there to wait for?  
**Hart:** It isn't about hitting it.  
**Falke:** Pass the bread.  
**Ottilie:** Both of you. He does not know what you meant, and you have not said it.  
**Hart:** Said it.  
**Falke:** What is that book you write in every night?  
**Hart:** Every machine, every day, and how it went. Eat.  
**Falke:** Four years of that. It's a book of me losing!  
**Ottilie:** Linde keeps one on me. Mine is longer than yours.  
**Falke:** Why would she keep a book on you? You've healed this town since I was small.  
**Ottilie:** I am an apprentice. She writes down what I can close and what I cannot.  
**Ottilie:** I am twenty-three. You are seventeen. We are both somebody's apprentice.  
**Falke:** What can't you close?  
**Ottilie:** Anything big. I run out of strength part way, and then I have to sit down.  
**Hart:** She was nine when Linde took her on. A fever took her mother and her father that winter.  
**Falke:** Nine?  
**Hart:** She tried to close it herself. Was not strong enough. Next door on her own ever since.  
**Ottilie:** Hart. He asked me what I cannot close.  
**Hart:** He did.  
**Falke:** I'm going out to the machines before it's light.  

## Scene 0105: The Night

**Falke:** Three out of four. He said it like it was nothing at all.  
**Falke:** Eleven hits. Eleven, and that arm was still up.  
**Falke:** Fine. I'll be out here before he is, and I'll stay out here all day.  

## Scene 0107: The Sword

**Ottilie:** That is the forty-first time. You have been at it since before it was light.  
**Falke:** Don't count today. Please don't count today.  
**Ottilie:** Left. Left! It is always the left, Falke. I have said so since you were twelve.  
**Falke:** My arm's gone. I can't swing at it again.  
**Ottilie:** Then don't swing at it. Stand there and watch the arm come.  
**Falke:** There's a click. The weight drops, and then the arm comes.  
**Falke:** It's been clicking at me all day.  
**Falke:** Come on, then. Come on!  
**Ottilie:** He turned it aside. He turned it aside and then he hit it.  
**Ottilie:** I had a very good thing ready to say to you and now I am not saying it.  
**Falke:** Say it!  
**Ottilie:** No. You would have it framed.  
**Hart:** Put the stick down. Both hands.  
**Falke:** Did you see what it did? The arm went nowhere!  
**Hart:** Saw it. Forty-one times I watched you walk into that arm.  
**Hart:** Take this. I made it the winter before last.  
**Falke:** The winter before last? You've had it the whole time?  
**Hart:** Finished and on its hook. Waiting for you to stop swinging.  
**Ottilie:** The grip is dark in the middle. He has had that down off the hook a hundred times.  
**Falke:** Why today? What changed?  
**Hart:** A hunter who only attacks dies out there. I was not burying Rabe's son as well.  
**Hart:** You're ready. A first sword comes from your teacher — show the clerk and he will let you sign.  
**Falke:** Sign? The sheets went up at dawn!  
**Ottilie:** Falke. That is the evening bell. It started while you were shouting at a machine.  
**Falke:** The board closes on the last ring. Hold the gate open!  

## Scene 0110: The Board

**Clerk:** You made it on the last ring of the bell.  
**Falke:** The last ring? I came the whole width of the town for that!  
**Clerk:** Then look at the board. There is one job sheet left.  
**Falke:** One? The board was full of job sheets at dawn!  
**Clerk:** The others went all day. Stolz signed for the coast road job at noon.  
**Falke:** At noon I was in the yard with a stick in my hand!  
**Clerk:** You were. You have a sword on your back now.  
**Falke:** Hart put it in my hands an hour ago. He said to show you.  
**Clerk:** Then I will write your name. The last job sheet is the water run.  
**Falke:** What does the water run pay?  
**Clerk:** Thirty coin. Two days out and two days back.  
**Falke:** Out to where?  
**Clerk:** Out to the *(descriptive)*. A jar of our well water goes there sealed, every year.  
**Falke:** What for?  
**Clerk:** No one has ever told me. It goes all the same.  
**Falke:** Who put it on the board?  
**Clerk:** You did. It came in yesterday, with Hart's post.  
**Falke:** I carried it in? I stood right here while you opened it!  
**Clerk:** You did.  
**Falke:** Hart? But the carters take the jar out. They take it every year.  
**Clerk:** There is no convoy this year. Every carter in the valley has walked west.  
**Falke:** Then who is paying the thirty coin?  
**Clerk:** Hart is. His own money.  
**Falke:** A man who walks that road alone dies out there.  
**Falke:** He was my father.  
**Falke:** I'll take it! Write my name down now!  

## Scene 0120: What The Road Is

**Falke:** You posted it. You put the water run in my hands and sent me down the hill with it!  
**Hart:** I did.  
**Falke:** You let me carry my own job into that hall and you never said a word.  
**Hart:** You were not ready. Yesterday you were not ready.  
**Falke:** So you sent it somewhere I couldn't reach it!  
**Hart:** Where anybody in the valley could take it but you. Then this afternoon you were ready.  
**Falke:** And now you want it back off me.  
**Hart:** I made that sword. I put it in your hand. I cannot take it off you again.  
**Falke:** Then tell me what's out there. You've never once told me what's out there.  
**Hart:** Twenty years your father and I carried that jar out together.  
**Falke:** Twenty years, the two of you.  
**Hart:** The twentieth year that road took my knee. I have not walked it since.  
**Hart:** The year after, Rabe walked it on his own. He did not come back.  
**Falke:** I was six, and no one has ever told me where he died.  
**Hart:** Out in the grass, north of the river. There are drapes in that grass.  
**Falke:** What is a drape?  
**Hart:** Pale blue skin, lying flat. It comes up and wraps whoever walks over it.  
**Falke:** And then what happens?  
**Hart:** Then you cannot move your arms, and somebody else has to cut you out.  
**Hart:** Two of us went out so that there was always a somebody. He went alone.  
**Falke:** Is that what the last machine is? The arm that comes back at you?  
**Hart:** Everything out there hits back. That is the whole of what I built it to teach.  
**Falke:** What's at the far end of it? I've never been past the bridge.  
**Hart:** A staircase the ancients built, coming down out of the cloud.  
**Falke:** Who are the ancients?  
**Hart:** The people who built it. They have been gone a thousand years.  
**Falke:** And we carry a jar of well water to a staircase.  
**Hart:** A woman lives under the bottom step. She takes the jar, once a year.  
**Falke:** What is the water for?  
**Hart:** No one in Halm knows. It has gone every year since before my father.  
**Falke:** Who is she?  
**Hart:** I never learned. Your father sat and talked with her, every year, for twenty years.  
**Falke:** About what?  
**Hart:** He never said and I never asked. Put the jar on her shelf and come home.  
**Falke:** I'm taking it.  
**Hart:** I know. Take Ottilie with you, and ask her yourself.  
**Falke:** She'll say yes before I've finished the sentence!  

## Scene 0130: Asking Her

**Falke:** Ottilie. I'm walking the water run west at dawn. Come with me.  
**Ottilie:** Yes.  
**Falke:** That was fast.  
**Ottilie:** My bag has been packed since the bell. Someone has to carry the bandages you will need.  
**Falke:** You packed it before I asked?  
**Ottilie:** I packed it because Linde told me to pack it.  
**Falke:** Linde wants you to go west?  
**Ottilie:** She gives full standing after a season of work away from home. I have never had one.  
**Falke:** A season away? You've healed this whole town since I was small!  
**Ottilie:** In this town. I have never been further than the bridge in my life.  
**Falke:** Four days is not a season.  
**Ottilie:** It is four days more than I have. She said to start.  
**Ottilie:** Two winters ago they carried a carter in off that road. I could not close what he had.  
**Falke:** What happened to him?  
**Ottilie:** He died on Linde's bench with my hands on him. I want to be out where it happens.  
**Falke:** You never told me any of that.  
**Ottilie:** You have never asked me anything until tonight.  
**Falke:** I'm asking now. Come west with me.  
**Ottilie:** I said yes the first time. Be at the gate before it is light.  
**Falke:** I'll be there before you are!  
**Ottilie:** You will not.  

## Scene 0150: The Road West

**Falke:** Where did you get that coat?  
**Stolz:** The lord of the dry city in the west bought it for me.  
**Falke:** You signed for the coast road job. I watched the clerk write your name down.  
**Stolz:** I did, and I sold it on to another hunter the same morning.  
**Falke:** You sold a guild job the day you signed it?  
**Stolz:** For eighty coin a week. That is what the lord pays a hunter.  
**Ottilie:** Paying you to do what, exactly?  
**Stolz:** To walk west with him. He is going to the mountain.  
**Ottilie:** What is at the mountain?  
**Stolz:** The ancients are. He means to ask them for rain on his city.  
**Falke:** People have asked the ancients for a thousand years! They have never answered anyone.  
**Stolz:** He has someone with him who can make them answer.  
**Ottilie:** Who has he got?  
**Stolz:** He did not say, and at eighty coin a week I did not ask.  
**Stolz:** You're carrying a sealed jar and I'm riding in a cart. Enjoy the walk!  
**Falke:** Thirty coin, and I signed for mine myself!  

## Scene 0160: The Fire

**Falke:** That was the worst day I have ever had. How many was that?  
**Ottilie:** Nineteen. And I patched you four times, twice for the same arm. Some things do not change.  
**Falke:** You saw the first drape, though. I waited for it and I turned it!  
**Ottilie:** You did. Two days ago you would be lying in that grass wrapped up like a parcel.  
**Falke:** Did you know my father? Properly, I mean.  
**Ottilie:** I was twelve, and he was the big one who came and went.  
**Ottilie:** I remember one thing about this run. He came home a day late off it. Every year.  
**Falke:** A day late, every year? I was six when he died and no one ever told me that!  
**Ottilie:** Hart used to stand at the gate and count the days.  
**Falke:** How do you even remember that? You were twelve.  
**Ottilie:** I had lost mine three years before. You notice whose father still comes home.  
**Falke:** Ottilie —  
**Ottilie:** Sleep. I wake you at the turn.  
**Falke:** Wake me early. I want to see it in the light.  

## Scene 0170: The Jar

**Shrine Woman:** Set it down there, beside last year's. She keeps the shelf for them.  
**Falke:** Who does? Who takes the water?  
**Shrine Woman:** The woman who lives under the bottom step. She was here before these houses were.  
**Falke:** What is her name?  
**Shrine Woman:** She has never given one. I have asked her for sixty years.  
**Shrine Woman:** The hunter who used to bring the jar sat with her a whole day. Every year, twenty years.  
**Falke:** A whole day.  
**Falke:** That's the day! He was never late coming home — he was here!  
**Ottilie:** Falke. There are papers nailed inside this door. Come and count them.  
**Falke:** They're carters' receipts. One for carrying the jar.  
**Falke:** Eleven of them. One for every year since he died.  
**Ottilie:** Read whose name is at the bottom of each one.  
**Falke:** Hart. It's Hart on all eleven. He paid a carter every single year.  
**Ottilie:** He never told you, and he never told me.  
**Falke:** Twenty years my father sat here and he never once said her name to me!  
**Ottilie:** He may not have had one to say. Look at last year's jar.  
**Falke:** The seal is still on it.  
**Ottilie:** Then she did not come for it. She has been gone a year and no one noticed.  
**Falke:** It goes up and up. Where does the top of it come out?  
**Ottilie:** In the cloud. I have been looking at it an hour and I cannot find an end.  
**Falke:** That old man in the square told me the *(descriptive)* has never moved.  
**Ottilie:** It has not. It has sat over the same roof his whole life.  
**Falke:** Ottilie — it is moving. It is moving west, right now!  
**Ottilie:** I see it. Listen — every bell in the village has started.  
**Falke:** Pack the fire. We're going west before it stops.
