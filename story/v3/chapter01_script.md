## 0130: The Counter  *(talk scene)*

> *No pictures — a talk scene: the guild hall of Halm in the afternoon: a long low stone room with a plank counter, an open double doorway on the street, and an empty job board on the wall be*

**Clerk**: The repaired part goes on the counter. Is there anything else?  
**Falke**: Yes. When can I take a job?  
**Clerk**: No sword, no signature.  
**Falke**: Say that plainly.  
**Clerk**: An apprentice signs the guild book when his teacher says he is ready.  
**Falke**: I've trained four years!  
**Clerk**: A hunter's first sword comes from his teacher. It is the sign, and I look for it.  
**Falke**: Then I'll buy one myself.  
**Clerk**: You do not buy one. You are given one. That is the whole of the rule.  
**Falke**: Stolz. Of course you're standing there.  
**Stolz**: He's right. Mine took me two years to get.  
**Stolz**: Job sheets go up at dawn and the board shuts on the last ring of the evening bell.  
**Stolz**: So don't be up a hill at supper time. Here — three coin for your bread.  
**Falke**: I'll have a sword before you've spent it!  

*The lane home at dusk goes past Ottilie's open door — one chair at the table, two coats on the hook — and then you sit down at Hart's table and look at the workshop book on the bench.*
