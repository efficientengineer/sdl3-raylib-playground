[Hart's yard, late afternoon. Ottilie is holding the guild book open. Hart is sitting on the bottom rung of the ladder with his bad leg straight out. Falke has just put down the practice sword.]
OTTILIE: [holds the book steady under his hand] Sign it, Hart. Two days west, two days back — and I have walked that road twice.
HART: [drags the book onto his knee and signs] Two days west. Jar on the stone shelf under the bottom step. Straight back.
FALKE: [takes the book off Hart's knee] Two days west, alone? Ottilie — come with me. You've walked that road twice and I've never left Halm.
OTTILIE: [snaps the book shut] Yes — my pack is already made up. He has never once asked me to come anywhere, and you asked!
FALKE: [grips the book to his chest] You're coming? Then tell me plainly, both of you — what is the Stair, and who takes the jar?
HART: [doesn't look up] Stair the ancients built. Woman lives under it, takes the jar once a year — don't touch the stone, don't pass her shelf.
FALKE: [tucks the book under his arm] A woman lives under it? Fine — we don't touch the stone, we don't pass her shelf. Ottilie, dawn!
