[The guild hall in Halm, early afternoon. One sheet is left on the board. The clerk is at the ledger desk. Falke has just come in.]
CLERK: [does not look up from the ledger] The escort is gone — Stolz signed it an hour ago and left at noon. One sheet is left, and Hart posted it.
FALKE: Gone? I was standing right here at the morning bell! What's the last sheet, then?
CLERK: [sets the pen down] The rule is the water goes to the Stair the same day every year. That day is tomorrow, so it leaves at dawn.
FALKE: [crosses to the board] Dawn? The water run out to the Stair — what does it pay, and who has to vouch for me?
CLERK: [opens the ledger to the sheet] The rule is a hunter must vouch for you. That rule is waived on this one. Thirty coin, two days out, two days back.
FALKE: [looks up from the sheet] Waived? Thirty coin and no hunter has to vouch for me — why would the guild waive that rule?
CLERK: [closes the ledger] There is no convoy this year. A man walking that road alone dies — that is what happened eleven years ago.
FALKE: [takes the pen off the ledger] Eleven years ago, on that road — that was my father. Write my name down. I leave at dawn.
