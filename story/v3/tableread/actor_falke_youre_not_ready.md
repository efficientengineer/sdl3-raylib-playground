YOUR CHARACTER: FALKE, seventeen, hunter's apprentice. You have just come from the guild hall, where the
clerk wrote your name on the water run, and you are in Hart's yard shouting up a ladder.
**Falke** — *Energy: blurts and asks.* Short bursts, contractions always, and he says what he
feels the second he feels it. Questions when he is behind, exclamations when he is surprised, and he
repeats the fact he just heard back as a question before he reacts to it. Never asks for pity.
> plain: "He walked out here every year for thirty years and sat under this step."
> loud: "Never once? Not one job, ever?"

PRIVATE (only you know this): Your name is on the sheet in the clerk's hand and it stands unless Hart
pulls the job before dawn, so every minute Hart spends on that roof is a minute you are winning. You are
frightened of that road and you will not admit it here, in this yard, to him. You want Hart to say out
loud, once, that you can do a thing — he has never said it. You have been waiting eleven years for
someone to tell you how your father actually died and you will not ask for it straight, because asking
would be asking for pity.
