[Hart's yard, late afternoon. Ottilie is holding the guild book open. Hart is sitting on the bottom rung of the ladder with his bad leg straight out. Falke has just put down the practice sword.]
OTTILIE: [holds the book steady under his hand] Sign it, Hart. The run leaves at dawn — that is fourteen hours, and I am the one who patches whoever goes.
HART: [takes the pen, signs without looking up] Two days west. Jar on the stone shelf under the bottom step. Then straight back.
FALKE: [takes the book, still breathing hard] Two days west, alone? Ottilie — come with me. You've walked that road twice!
OTTILIE: [closes the book on his signature] Yes — four days, not two: two west, two back. I am a healer, and Hart has never once asked me to come anywhere.
FALKE: [grips the book] Four days, not two? Yes! Hart — what is the Stair, exactly? Tell me plainly, before I walk it.
HART: [jerks his chin west, not looking up] Staircase the ancients built, woman living under it who takes the jar once a year. Don't touch the stone, don't go past her shelf.
FALKE: [shoulders the pack, looks west] A woman takes the jar once a year? Then I don't touch the stone. Dawn, Ottilie — west road. Thank you!
