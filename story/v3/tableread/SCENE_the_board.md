# Table read: "The Board"

## Situation (all actors know this)
The hunters' guild hall in Halm, early afternoon. The board is nearly bare. Stolz signed for the coast
escort an hour ago and left at noon; Falke was standing in this hall at the morning bell and missed it
anyway. One sheet is left on the board, and Hart posted it himself this morning, before the bell: the
year's bread run out to the Stair. The bread goes out on the same day every year and that day is
tomorrow, so it leaves at dawn — two days there, two days back, thirty coin. Every carter in the valley
who would have taken it has walked west to the lord's city for eighty coin a week, which is why the
sheet is on a guild board at all this year. The guild rule is that every job needs a hunter who will
vouch for you. The rule is waived on this one, because no hunter in this valley will vouch for a man on
the run that killed the last hunter who walked it, eleven years ago. That hunter was Falke's father.
Falke has one grain sack's worth of coin and needs work today.

NOTE FOR THIS TAKE: the last read never said out loud why there is no carter doing this job. Somebody in
this scene has to say, in plain words, that every carter who would have carried the bread has walked
west to the lord's city for eighty coin a week — and somebody has to say that Hart posted this sheet
himself, this morning.

## Facts someone must state plainly before the scene ends
1. The coast escort is gone: Stolz signed for it an hour ago and left at noon.
2. One sheet is left, and Hart posted it this morning.
3. The bread goes out to the Stair on the same day every year, that day is tomorrow, so it leaves at dawn.
4. Two days there, two days back, thirty coin.
5. The rule that a hunter must vouch for you is waived on this one job.
6. No hunter in the valley will vouch for a man on that run, because the last hunter who walked it died
   on that road eleven years ago.
7. Every carter who would have taken it instead is walking west for eighty coin a week.
8. The last hunter who walked that run was Falke's father.
9. Falke takes the job.

## Rules
Three to six lines total. One beat: Falke takes the only job on the board, and it is the job that killed
his father. Plain: after the clip a twelve-year-old can say what happened and why. Voice rides on
clarity — the clerk recites the rule then the fact, Falke blurts and repeats facts back as questions.
Energy: at least one question and one exclamation; interrupt with a dash where it is natural.
