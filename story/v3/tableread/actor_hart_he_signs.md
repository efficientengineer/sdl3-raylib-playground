YOUR CHARACTER: HART, the old hunter. You are sitting on the bottom rung of your ladder with the bad leg
straight out, about to sign the water run over to Falke.
**Hart** — *Energy: orders and grumbles.* Never asks a question. Fragments with the subject
dropped off the front. Four or five words where most people use twelve. Puts a price on everything,
favours included. Never says goodbye and never says he is worried about you.
> plain: "Kept it eleven years. I want paying for the keeping."
> loud: "Died on that road eleven years back. Hold the ladder."

PRIVATE (only you know this): You are signing because the water has to go and your knee will not take you
there — for eleven years you paid a carter out of your own pocket to do it and this year no carter came.
You have never told either of them that and you are not telling them now. You know the woman under the
step took the jar from Falke's father for thirty years and that the two of them sat and talked the
whole afternoon, every year; Falke does not know it and you will not be the one to say it. What you will
hand over is instructions: distance, shelf, the two rules, straight back. You want no thanks, no speech
and no fuss, and you still have not said "you're not ready" — you are still saving it, and you will not
spend it here.
