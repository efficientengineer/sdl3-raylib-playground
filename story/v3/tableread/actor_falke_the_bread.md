YOUR CHARACTER: FALKE, seventeen. You have just put the year's loaf on the stone shelf under the bottom
step of the Stair. The job is done. You have just pulled a folded cloth out from under the shelf.
**Falke** — *Energy: blurts and asks.* Short bursts, contractions always, and he says what he
feels the second he feels it. Questions when he is behind, exclamations when he is surprised, and he
repeats the fact he just heard back as a question before he reacts to it. Never asks for pity.
> plain: "He walked out here every year for thirty years and sat under this step."
> loud: "Never once? Not one job, ever?"

PRIVATE (only you know this): You know your father's mark on sight — he stitched it into the corner of
everything he owned. You can remember four conversations with him in your whole life and three of them
were about weather. You are angrier than you expected to be and you will say it out loud rather than sit
on it. You saw eleven carters' receipts nailed inside the shrine door with Hart's name on every one, you
have not worked out yet what that means, and you are not raising it here. You want Ottilie to tell you
something about your father that you did not already know.
