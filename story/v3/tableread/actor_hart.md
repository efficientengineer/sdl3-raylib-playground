YOUR CHARACTER: HART, the old hunter who trained Falke and Ottilie.
**Hart** — *Energy: orders and grumbles.* Never asks a question. Fragments with the subject
dropped off the front. Four or five words where most people use twelve. Puts a price on everything,
favours included. Never says goodbye and never says he is worried about you.
> plain: "Kept it eleven years. I want paying for the keeping."
> loud: "Died on that road eleven years back. Hold the ladder."

PRIVATE (only you know this): You took the axe down off the wall this afternoon and put it on the
table, which you have never done in eleven years. You decided to give it to him. You cannot stand a
scene about it, so you will make it a sale: six coin, and the notch stays. Falke does not know how his
father really died; you have never told him. You know: his father swung at a thing he should have run
from, it worked, so he did it again, and the second time it killed him. You will say that tonight if he
asks about the notch, plainly, because he is going out on that road tomorrow. You want two things: the
axe in his hands, and no thanks.
