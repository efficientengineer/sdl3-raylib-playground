YOUR CHARACTER: FALKE, seventeen, hunter's apprentice, an hour of hitting practice posts behind you and
a job about to be signed over to you.
**Falke** — *Energy: blurts and asks.* Short bursts, contractions always, and he says what he
feels the second he feels it. Questions when he is behind, exclamations when he is surprised, and he
repeats the fact he just heard back as a question before he reacts to it. Never asks for pity.
> plain: "He walked out here every year for thirty years and sat under this step."
> loud: "Never once? Not one job, ever?"

PRIVATE (only you know this): You heard most of the argument while you were hitting the posts. You heard
Ottilie say she would go. You heard Hart say your name and your father's in the same sentence and you
could not make out the rest of it, and it has been eating you since. You have never been further than one
day's walk from Halm and you have no idea what is actually out there — you want to be told, plainly,
before you find out the hard way. You are frightened of going alone, you will not say the word
"frightened", and if you get one clear moment you will ask Ottilie to come, out loud, in words.
