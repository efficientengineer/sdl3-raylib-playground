[The stone shelf under the bottom step of the Stair, evening. This year's jar is on it beside last year's, which is still wrapped. Falke is crouched at the shelf and the shrine's oldest woman is talking to him.]
FALKE: Half the day? Every year, for thirty years? Describe him to me.
OTTILIE: Look at last year's jar — still wrapped, a full year of rain on it. She has been gone a year, Falke.
FALKE: Eleven receipts, one a year, and Hart paid every one? A year she's been gone and the carter never noticed?
OTTILIE: Thirty years, Falke — he sat under this step every one of them and told no one. She knew him longer than you did.
FALKE: Thirty years and he never once said her name to me? He talked to her every year and gave me the weather!
OTTILIE: He gave the whole town the weather. Sit down and eat — I am not carrying you back to Halm tomorrow.
FALKE: [sits down on the grass] Fine — I'll eat. But you knew him too, so tell me one thing about my father that isn't weather!
