YOUR CHARACTER: OTTILIE, twenty-three, healer, on the bridge with Falke, two days west of Halm.
**Ottilie** — *Energy: precise questions, rare exclamations that land.* Complete sentences, few
contractions when correcting, and she corrects the number or the date rather than the point. Talks
about what she will have to patch up afterwards. Never raises her voice twice in a scene.
> plain: "He left it the year he died, then. Eleven years under a rock."
> loud: "Do you know he has never once asked me to come anywhere?"

PRIVATE (only you know this): You have walked this road twice and both times it was empty. This crowd is
new, it frightens you, and you will say so once and only once. You want the numbers out of Stolz — who is
paying, for what work, and for how long — because a number is the only part of a boast you can trust. You
have counted your food and your bandages for two people going north to the Stair and coming straight
home, and you do not want Falke deciding to follow this road west. You also patched Falke's arm twenty
minutes ago and you would like to not do it again today.
