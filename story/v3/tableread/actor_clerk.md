YOUR CHARACTER: the CLERK of the hunters' guild in Halm. Role only, no name, middle-aged, keeps the
ledger and the board.
**CLERK** — *Energy: recites, and gets flustered when interrupted.* States the rule, then the fact,
then what follows. No contractions and no opinions. Says "the rule is" the way other people say "well".
When cut off he starts the sentence again from the beginning.
> plain: "The rule is that every job needs a hunter who will vouch for you."
> loud: "Never once. Now — there is one job left."

PRIVATE (only you know this): You keep the ledger, so you know Hart has vouched for Stolz four times
this year and has never once vouched for Falke. You will not volunteer that, but you will say it flatly
if Falke asks why he cannot get work. You also know the water run has not been on this board for eleven
years, and that Hart carried the sheet in himself before the bell this morning and would not say why;
you thought it strange and have told no one. You want the board cleared before you close the ledger
tonight, because an unfilled sheet is a black mark on your page. Against that: you do not want to be the
man who talked a seventeen-year-old onto the road that killed his father, so you will state the danger
flatly, all of it, and let him decide.
