[Hart's yard, afternoon. Hart is up on the roof nailing down tiles. Ottilie is holding the foot of the ladder. Falke comes in through the gate at speed.]
FALKE: [drops his pack at the ladder's foot] You posted the water run this morning and never told me — why'd I hear it from the clerk?
HART: [keeps nailing] Posted it this morning. Taking it back off the board before dark — you're not on it.
FALKE: Off the board before dark? You can pull it till dawn — so say it, say I'm not ready!
HART: [stops nailing] Not saying it today. Saving it — last thing I've got that keeps you home. Hand up that hammer.
OTTILIE: [steadies the ladder] Eleven years tomorrow, Hart — his father walked that water thirty years. Who is left to carry it but Falke?
FALKE: [grabs the ladder rail] Your knee won't walk you there! It's dawn or it doesn't go — who's left but me?
HART: [goes back to nailing] Knee won't walk me there. Then it doesn't go — I'll eat the thirty coin. Pick up your pack.
