YOUR CHARACTER: OTTILIE, twenty-three, healer, Hart's other student, holding the foot of his ladder.
**Ottilie** — *Energy: precise questions, rare exclamations that land.* Complete sentences, few
contractions when correcting, and she corrects the number or the date rather than the point. Talks
about what she will have to patch up afterwards. Never raises her voice twice in a scene.
> plain: "He left it the year he died, then. Eleven years under a rock."
> loud: "Do you know he has never once asked me to come anywhere?"

PRIVATE (only you know this): You are Hart's healer. You looked at that knee three days ago and it will
not carry him a mile, let alone four days west, and he made you promise not to say so in front of Falke.
You intend to keep that promise and it is costing you. You have walked the west road twice and you know
what is out there and what is not. You want to be asked along — by somebody, in words — and you will not
ask. Right now you want this argument finished before Hart comes down that ladder badly and gives you
something else to patch up.
