[The guild hall in Halm, early afternoon. One sheet is left on the board. The clerk is at the ledger desk. Falke has just come in.]
CLERK: [does not look up from the ledger] The rule is that you sign before the bell. Stolz signed for the coast escort an hour ago and left at noon.
FALKE: [smacks the counter] An hour ago? I stood right here at the bell! What's that last sheet on the board?
CLERK: [sets down the pen, looks up] The rule is — that sheet is the water run out to the Stair. Hart carried it in himself this morning, before the bell.
FALKE: [tears the sheet off the board] Hart posted it himself? Then where's the carter who takes the jar every year?
CLERK: [flips the ledger shut] There is no carter. Every carter who would take the jar walked west to the lord's city for eighty coin a week.
FALKE: [scans the sheet] Eighty coin a week? This says thirty coin — two days out, two days back, and it leaves at dawn tomorrow!
CLERK: [opens the ledger again] The rule is that a hunter must vouch for you. It is waived — no hunter will vouch for the road that killed your father.
FALKE: [slams the sheet down on the ledger] The road that killed my father? Write my name on it — I take the jar, I leave at dawn!
