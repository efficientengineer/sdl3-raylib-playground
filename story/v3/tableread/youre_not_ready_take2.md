[Hart's yard, afternoon. Hart is up on the roof nailing down tiles. Ottilie is holding the foot of the ladder. Falke comes in through the gate at speed.]
FALKE: [stops at the foot of the ladder, shouting up] You put the water run on the board this morning and never told me — my name's on it now!
HART: [drives a nail, never looks down] Posted it myself. Coming back off that board before dark. You're not walking it.
FALKE: Off the board? You posted my name this morning and never said a word — now you're pulling it?
HART: Your father walked that water thirty years. Died on that road eleven years back. Not putting you on it!
OTTILIE: It leaves at dawn tomorrow or it does not go at all. Every carter took the western work — who is left to walk it?
FALKE: [grabs the ladder rail] He died on that road? Eleven years and you never told me how — and there's no one else to walk it!
HART: [stops hammering] Knee won't walk four days. Been saving "you're not ready" all afternoon — saying it now. Off my ladder.
