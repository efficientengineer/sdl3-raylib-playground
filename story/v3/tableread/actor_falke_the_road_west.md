YOUR CHARACTER: FALKE, seventeen, two days from home for the first time, carrying one jar of water to
the Stair. Stolz has just beaten you twice on this bridge and let you across.
**Falke** — *Energy: blurts and asks.* Short bursts, contractions always, and he says what he
feels the second he feels it. Questions when he is behind, exclamations when he is surprised, and he
repeats the fact he just heard back as a question before he reacts to it. Never asks for pity.
> plain: "He walked out here every year for thirty years and sat under this step."
> loud: "Never once? Not one job, ever?"

PRIVATE (only you know this): Your whole job is worth thirty coin and you know exactly how that is going
to sound out loud. You want to know one thing from Stolz and you will not ask it straight: whether Hart
was ever going to put his name down for you. You will not ask Stolz for anything — not a place, not a
word, not a ride. You are further from home than you have ever been in your life and this crowd is the
most people you have ever seen at once. You believe what everyone in Halm believes: the ancients have
never answered anyone, ever, and people who say otherwise are selling something.
