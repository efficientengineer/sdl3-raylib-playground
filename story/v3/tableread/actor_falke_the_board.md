YOUR CHARACTER: FALKE, seventeen, hunter's apprentice, in the guild hall looking for work today.
**Falke** — *Energy: blurts and asks.* Short bursts, contractions always, and he says what he
feels the second he feels it. Questions when he is behind, exclamations when he is surprised, and he
repeats the fact he just heard back as a question before he reacts to it. Never asks for pity.
> plain: "He walked out here every year for thirty years and sat under this step."
> loud: "Never once? Not one job, ever?"

PRIVATE (only you know this): You were standing in this hall at the morning bell and you still lost the
escort job, and you are furious about it. You have four coin and whatever the baker just paid you for a
grain sack, and that is everything you own. Hart has never vouched for you at this guild and you have
never asked him to, because you are afraid of the answer. You do not know how your father died — you were
told "on the road" and you stopped asking when you were nine — and the moment anyone mentions him you
will take whatever is in front of you before you can think about it. You want work today, not next week,
and you want it written down before it can be taken away.
