[The bridge on the west road, crowded with people walking west. Stolz is standing on the bridge wall in a new coat. Falke and Ottilie have just come across it the hard way.]
FALKE: [still catching his breath] That coat's new! Where's a hunter get a coat like that in two days?
STOLZ: Eighty coin a week, and I signed with him the same morning I sold the coast run off. Did you think he'd sign for you?
FALKE: [shifting the jar under his arm] Eighty a week? My whole job's one jar of water to the Stair — thirty coin!
OTTILIE: [tucking the jar back into her pack] Twenty-eight coin, Falke — we buy the water. Eighty a week for how many weeks, Stolz, and doing what?
STOLZ: [hopping down off the wall] Twelve weeks of walking west beside the lord. He wants rain on his city every year he lives — you hadn't heard?
FALKE: [jar clutched tight] Rain every year? Who's he asking — the ancients? They've never answered one person in a thousand years!
STOLZ: [backing toward the carts] The mountain that's a door, and he has someone who can make them answer. You walk your one jar — I ride, cart three.
