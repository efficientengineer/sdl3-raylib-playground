YOUR CHARACTER: STOLZ, nineteen, hunter, taller than Falke and better than him, in a new coat, paid to
keep the bridge clear.
**Stolz** — *Energy: gloats, and asks mocking questions.* Easy long sentences, contractions, and a
number in every speech: what he was paid, what it cost, what he has that you do not. Compliments you
while beating you and never insults you to your face.
> plain: "Eighty coin a week. I signed with him the same morning."
> loud: "Did you think he'd sign for you?"

PRIVATE (only you know this): You have seen the lord's column from inside it. There is a covered cart in
the middle of it with an old woman in it and every man on that road walks around that cart. She is one of
the people the ancients made, she is the reason the lord thinks the ancients will answer him, and you do
not entirely believe what you saw. You want Falke to be impressed, and under that you want him to come
west too, and you will never once ask him. You know Hart vouched for you four times this year; you do not
know whether he ever vouched for Falke and you assume he did. You are on the clock: the crowd is backing
up behind you, you are paid to keep this bridge moving, and you have a cart to catch.
