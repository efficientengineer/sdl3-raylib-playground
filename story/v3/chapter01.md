# CHAPTER ONE — Four

All names are tokens. See `NAMES.md`.

## Goal
Get to the guild hall before the good work is signed for.

## Play: Explore + first fight — the town (6 min)
Nine streets, a grain yard, a well. Talk to anyone; six of eleven townsfolk say your father's name
before yours. The bell has already rung once and the hall is full.
Something is knocking behind the grain shed. **Fight:** three shed-rats, dog-sized, charge in a line
and squeal when hit. Teaches attack timing.
**Find:** a whetstone in the yard, coins under the well bucket, a grain sack behind the rats.
**Unlocks:** the baker buys the sack; that's your starting purse and your first shop.

## Play: Chase — the rooftops (3 min)
A small girl lifts {{RIVAL}}'s purse while everyone is looking at the ledger, and runs. You can chase
or walk away; the clerk pins up the last unclaimed job either way.
**Chase:** two minutes of roofs and washing lines, no combat, one alley you can cut off if you looked
at the town map.
**Find:** the last job in the hall — the old halls on the east ridge, clear the entrance for a survey
crew. It has been unclaimed four months and the pay is bad.
**Unlocks:** {{THIEF}}'s face, which you will see again, and a purse you now have to explain.

## Goal
Get {{MENTOR}} to take the job.

## Play: Talk + climb — {{MENTOR}}'s yard (4 min)
Up the hill past the fence he made you rebuild twice. Six practice posts, all split at your height.
{{MENTOR}} is on his own roof with a hammer and a knee that has no business up a ladder.
{{HEALER}} is holding the ladder and will give you three optional lines about that before you shout
anything up at him.
**Find:** your father's hunting badge at the bottom of the water barrel. Taking it is optional and
{{MENTOR}} watches you do it from the roof.
**Unlocks:** the argument.

## Clip: You're Not Ready

## Play: Tutorial fight — the posts (4 min)
He comes down and makes you work while he and {{HEALER}} argue behind you, audible, in barks. Timed
swings on the six posts; the last one he moves.
**Mechanics:** teaches the guard-break window. Swing early three times and you lose, and losing only
costs you the good version of the argument — he takes the job regardless.
**Find:** hit all six clean and he gives you a hunter's whistle, which calls the party back to you
from anywhere on screen and keeps working all game.
**Unlocks:** his name in the guild book, under yours.

## Play: Explore + shop — {{MENTOR}}'s kitchen, supper (3 min)
Door propped open, pot on, the neighbour's goat in the yard where it should not be. He stays at the
table and comments on everything you touch.
**Find:** a folded map of the east valley in a hand that isn't his; a tin of his wife's buttons he
will not discuss; the axe, on the table, with a stone beside it.
**Shop:** he sells rope, a waterskin and bad advice. Money matters in the next clip, so spend
carefully.
**Unlocks:** the axe. Slower and much harder hitting, and the only weapon in the game that can break.

## Goal
Be at the gate before light.

## Play: Shop + explore — the gate, blue dark (3 min)
Frost on the rail, the baker awake and waving, a cart going out ahead of you with a man asleep on it.
Last chance to sell: the stable hand buys shed-rat hides at a price {{THIEF}} will later call criminal.
Walk to the gate and the game lets you turn around twice. Both times the yard up the hill is dark and
the ladder is still against the house.
**Unlocks:** leaving. You cannot come back to town this chapter.

## Goal
Reach the old halls on the east ridge.

## Play: Travel + fight — the ridge road (10 min)
Two days of uphill. First proper road: three fight encounters, a dead cart, a farm with the shutters
nailed shut from outside.
**Fight:** ridge dogs, packs of four, one hangs back and howls; the howler calls another dog every
two turns until it dies. Teaches target priority.
**Find:** a lantern and a half-written letter in the cart; a shortcut over the scree that skips one
fight and costs you a party conversation.
**See:** from the high point, your town behind you, a river that is far too low, and the {{TOWER}} in
the western haze with one light near the top. The camera holds on it for three seconds.
**Unlocks:** the ridge camp, where somebody has eaten your bread.

## Goal
Clear the entrance. That's the whole job.

## Play: Fight + clear — the entrance (6 min)
A building coming out of a hillside at the wrong angle, half-buried, warm to the touch. The rubble
across the mouth is the job: four minutes of clearing, and the corner of the screen tells you the job
is done.
Inside, a corridor with a floor too flat. Lights come on in the ceiling ahead of you, one, then the
next, then the next, and keep going after you stop.
**Fight:** hall-crawlers drop off the ceiling onto whoever is at the back and keep arriving until
someone smashes the cracked pipe. Teaches checking behind you.
**Find:** a bone-handled knife under the rubble, still sharp; a survey crew's stake, snapped.
**Unlocks:** the corridor. The hum enters the sound mix here, under everything, and stays.

## Goal
Find out what's still running down there.

## Play: Puzzle — the lights are going somewhere (10 min)
New mechanic: **Listen.** Hold the button and the screen desaturates, sound drops away, and the hum
shows as a direction and a pitch. Only {{HERO}} has it. Louder and lower means closer to whatever is
running. This is your compass for the rest of the game.
The lights keep running ahead into rooms with nothing in them. Doors unseal on empty stairwells and
seal again. Warm air moves through corridors the party has already cleared.
**Do:** three rooms of chasing the lights and finding them pointing at nothing — a store room, a
washroom, a stair that goes nowhere — then get {{SCHOLAR}} to the wall panel he can read.
**Find:** a soldier's coat from the west on the fourth landing, folded, with a ration tin and a coin
none of you know.
**Unlocks:** the panel above the inner door.

## Play: Puzzle — follow the search (6 min)
The lesson of the last hour was to work against the building. Now you do the opposite: the lights are
a search pattern, and the search is systematic, so it goes deeper every sweep. Stop clearing your own
route and walk where it walks.
**Do:** wait at junctions for the sweep, three times, timing it on Listen. Get it wrong and you spend
four minutes in a sealed room while it goes past.
**Find:** every room it checks has been checked before — scuffed floors, one door open on a made bed.
**Unlocks:** the inner door, which the building opens because it has not looked in there yet,
and walks in ahead of you.

## Play: Boss — the stone hound (5 min)
A guard animal the ancients built, awake nineteen days and furious about it. Armoured, slow, hits
the whole party.
**Mechanics:** it opens its chest to vent heat every third turn. Hit it then or hit it never.
{{HERO}} on Listen hears the vent coming one turn early, which teaches using Listen in fights.
**Drops:** a hot green shard worth more than the whole job, and {{SCHOLAR}} screams about selling it.

## Play: Explore — the big hall (5 min)
A room the size of the town. Pipes as thick as houses wound in something glowing a low steady blue,
all of it turning, all of it loud, and warm rain falling from a ceiling you cannot see and running
away down channels in the floor.
**Do:** cross the walkway. Two soldiers in western coats are on watch with a crawler they've been
feeding fat on the heat; they surrender the moment it drops.
**Find:** sealed orders on the soldiers. {{HEALER}} sets one of their arms and he tells you the name
on the seal. A control stand, switched on, chalk mark fresh beside the lever, tool crate open.
**Unlocks:** the way out, and a save point, because of what's outside.

## Goal
Choose a road.

## Play: Travel — the fork (2 min)
Half a mile down, in the rain, the road splits. East is two days home and a hot meal and a man on a
roof. West is the {{TOWER}}, and you can see it from here now, three lights, through weather that has
no business existing.
**Do:** the game lets you walk east about forty steps before {{HEALER}} says his name.
**Unlocks:** the rest of it.
