# writer3

## Scene 1 — The Board

[The guild job board. Twenty hunters, one good posting, and Bron's hand already on it.]

BRON: Cave wolves, three days out, forty mesetas — that's mine, I called it, I called it out loud.

CRAY: You called it to a wall, Bron. Walls don't sign contracts.

LYRA: Cray, let him have one. He's been standing here since before the lamps went out.

CRAY: Then he should've signed it. Hand it over and I'll put your name in the report. Second line.

BRON: I don't want the second line. I want the job.

CRAY: Fine. Tell everybody what Dorn calls you — say it loud enough for the back — and it's yours.

BRON: ...He calls me "not ready."

CRAY: Louder, hunter.

BRON: NOT READY. He says I'm not ready.

CRAY: There it is. [He signs the board, blows on the ink, and doesn't look up.] Thanks for the job.

## Scene 2 — The Axe

[Dorn's back room, one candle. A long pale mark on the wall where something used to hang.]

DORN: Sit down before you break something. Both hands out.

BRON: That's — that's Dad's axe. That's been on your wall my whole life.

DORN: It's been on my wall because I couldn't look at the floor it came off of.

BRON: Why now? I lost the board today. In front of her. In front of everyone.

DORN: I know. I heard you shout it. Whole street heard you shout it.

BRON: Then say it again, go on. Say I'm not ready.

DORN: You're not. Neither was he, and he went anyway, and I let him, and I carried what was left of him home in a sack that weighed nothing.

BRON: So you just keep saying it forever? That's the plan?

DORN: That was the plan, yeah. It's a coward's plan. [He puts the axe in Bron's hands and lets go.] Go get your job back.
