# Scene 1 — The Board

[Morning. Thirty hunters packed at the guild board. BRON has his elbows out, holding a spot he has held all night.]

BRON: Wolf contract, north road, forty coin — that one's mine, I slept on these boards, ask the dog.

CRAY: Then sleep lighter. [He peels the paper off the nail and holds it up so the back row can see.]

BRON: Cray. Give it here. I need this one. I need a real posting on my card before winter or Dorn won't send me anywhere but the henhouse.

CRAY: Fine. It's yours. Say the thing Dorn calls you — out loud, so everybody gets it — and I'll pin it back on the nail myself.

LYRA: Or you could both grow up before noon, and Bron, don't you dare open your mouth, he's not worth the breath.

CRAY: He's already opening it. Look at him. He wants it that bad. Everybody watch, this is the most honest thing that happens today.

BRON: He calls me — he calls all of us — it's just a thing he says, it doesn't mean —

CRAY: Louder.

BRON: NOT-READY. He calls me Not-Ready. He's called me that for four years and he has never once called me anything else.

CRAY: There it is. [He folds the contract into his coat and walks out through the laughing.] I never said I'd give it to you. I said say it.

[Lyra reaches for Bron's shoulder. Bron is already gone.]

# Scene 2 — The Axe

[Night. Dorn's front room, one lamp. A pale square on the wall where something has hung a long time.]

DORN: Shut the door, you're letting the cold in and my knee keeps a grudge.

BRON: I'm quitting. I came to say it to your face because my father would have said it to your face. I'm done being the joke of that room.

DORN: Sit down and hold this. [He puts a long bundle across Bron's knees.]

BRON: That's — this is off your wall. This is the one off your wall. That's my father's axe. You've had my father's axe on your wall my whole life.

DORN: Eleven years. Hung it too high for you to reach on purpose. Then you got tall, so I had to start being clever.

BRON: Why now? Why tonight, when I'm at my absolute worst?

DORN: Because I told your father he was ready. He asked me straight, same as you're about to, and I looked him in the eye and I said yes, and eight days later I carried his boots home and nothing else.

BRON: So say it. Say it once. Tell me I'm ready and I'll walk out that door and be worth something.

DORN: You're not ready. [Beat.] I'm going to say that to you every day you live, and you're going to hate me, and you're going to come home. Now take the axe.

BRON: You are the worst kind man I have ever met. [He picks it up. It's heavier than he thought.] Gods, he swung this all day? No wonder he was in a mood.
