# Scene 1 — The Board

[Noon at the guild board. Twenty hunters, one good job left on it.]

BRON: Move — move — Salt Hollow, cave drake, two hundred gold. That's mine, I've wanted a drake since I was nine.

[Cray plucks the pin off the board.]

CRAY: Then you should have grabbed it at eight.

BRON: Put it back, Cray. I'm asking nice, and I only do that once.

CRAY: Make me want to. Say the thing Dorn says about you — out loud, in front of all these people — and it's yours.

LYRA: Cray. Take the job and go. Don't take this too.

CRAY: He's the one who wants something. I'm just naming a price. Word for word, Bron.

BRON: ...He says I'm not ready.

CRAY: Louder. Half the guild came out here for their lunch.

BRON: HE SAYS I'M NOT READY!

[Cray pins the job to his own coat.]

CRAY: He says it to me too. I just stopped asking his permission. Somebody buy the boy a drink, he's had a day.

LYRA: Bron. Look at me. Not at them — at me. We're walking out the front like you meant to.

# Scene 2 — The Axe

[That night. Dorn's workshop. The axe is off the wall for the first time in years, lying on the table between them.]

DORN: Sit. Don't touch it yet.

BRON: I've never seen that wall without it. It looks naked. Like you shaved.

DORN: It's yours. It's been yours since you were six. I just kept it somewhere I could see it.

BRON: Six? I could have had this at six? I could have been somebody by now, Dorn. I could have been the somebody instead of the one who says it out loud at the board.

DORN: You'd have been buried by now.

BRON: Everybody says I'm not ready. You say it loudest and you say it first.

DORN: I say it to everyone. I only ever meant it about you. Your father was ready. Best I ever trained, and ready didn't save him — I was thirty feet back with this knee, I got there late, and I have been getting there late every night since.

BRON: ...So it was never about me. All of it. All these years.

DORN: It was always about me. You were just the one paying for it. I'm sorry, boy. That's the whole confession, I'm not doing it twice.

[Bron picks up the axe.]

BRON: It's heavier than I thought.

DORN: He said the same thing. Complained about it for twenty years, right up to the end. — Go on. You're not ready. Nobody is. Go anyway.
