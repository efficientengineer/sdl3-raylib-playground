# Scene 1 — The Board

[The guild hall. A crowd three deep at the job board. One slip left worth taking.]

BRON: Move, move, move — the ridge-wolves are mine, I've been standing here since the morning bell!

CRAY: [pulls the slip down without turning around] Then you stood a long time to lose.

BRON: Give it here, Cray. My mother's roof leaks, my belt's got no coin in it, and I need one job people talk about.

LYRA: Give it to him and I'll set your ribs free the next three times you break them.

CRAY: Lyra, you'd have to catch me getting hurt first. [raises the slip so the room can see it] Bron. Say what Dorn calls you and it's yours.

BRON: In front of everybody?

CRAY: That's the whole idea. It's no good quiet.

BRON: [to the floor] ...Pup.

CRAY: Louder. I want the back of the hall to know who's going up that ridge.

BRON: PUP! HE CALLS ME PUP! Now hand it over!

CRAY: [pockets the slip and walks] No. I only wanted the hall.

# Scene 2 — The Axe

[Dorn's cabin, late. A pale square on the wall where something has hung a long time.]

DORN: Shut the door. You're letting the cold at my knee.

BRON: I'm going after those wolves anyway. Tomorrow. Ahead of Cray, alone, and you can't stop me.

DORN: You're not ready.

BRON: You say that to everyone! You said it to Lyra last spring and she's twice the hunter I'll ever be!

DORN: [takes the axe down off the wall and lays it on the table] Then you'll be wanting this.

BRON: That's my father's. That's been up there my whole life.

DORN: Eleven years. I hung it where I'd have to look at it every morning, because I'm the man who told him he was ready.

BRON: He was the best there was. You say so yourself, every time you're drunk.

DORN: He was the best I ever trained and it didn't buy him one extra breath. So I stopped saying the word. To Lyra, to that prick Cray, to you. It was never about you being small, boy. It was about me being a coward at dawn for eleven years.

BRON: [lifts it, and sags] Gods, it's heavy.

DORN: Everything he carried was. Sharpen it tonight, and take the girl and the prick with you — you're still not ready, but three of you is nearly one hunter.
