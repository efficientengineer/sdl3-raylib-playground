## Ottilie — what she has in this draft

Her own, on screen: she sits on the wall between the two houses because it is her wall too; she calls
the machine's swings before they land and has done since he was twelve (she is the hint system, and
she is **right when both men are wrong** — "He is right about the fourth one. You never wait" at the
table, ignored by both of them, and proved the next afternoon); she keeps the count of his injuries
and corrects it, which is a joke that runs from `0101` to `0160`; the fourth machine's rope is set
below his shoulder because **she** uses it when the yard is empty (`hart_yard.machine_four`); the
town asks for her by name outside {{TOWN_HEALER}}'s door (`halm.healer_bench`); her limit is strength,
shown by one wrist costing her the afternoon; her stake in the road is her own — full standing needs a
season away and she has never been past the bridge — and she had packed before he asked; she is the
one who notices that the sword's grip is dark in the middle, which nobody else in the yard sees; and
her family is one plain fact said by {{MENTOR}}, never by her.

### Three options for deepening her further (owner to pick, or none)

**A — The second patient.** Give her one scene of work the player watches: a child's arm at
{{TOWN_HEALER}}'s bench during the day-one errand, done in four lines, fast and funny and completely
competent, with the mother asking for her by name and {{TOWN_HEALER}} not in the room. {{HERO}} stands
in the doorway and is not the point of the scene. Cost: one talk scene, no art.
*Gives her:* professional standing the player has seen, not been told.

**B — The mace, and why she keeps it quiet.** On the ridge camp, she takes the first watch and the
player controls **her** for ninety seconds — one fight, her mace, no {{HERO}}. Afterwards one line:
she has carried it since the winter the carter died on the bench and has never used it in front of
anybody who would make it a story. Cost: one play block, one line, no art.
*Gives her:* a second competence, and a reason for hiding it that is about her, not about him.

**C — The thing she wants said out.** One exchange, late, where {{HERO}} calls her family and she
does not let it go past: she asks him to say which of them he means and makes him name all three
houses. Four lines, no crying, and she ends it with a joke about the bandages. Cost: four lines in
`0160` or a new scene before the {{STAIR}}.
*Gives her:* the want the premise gave her, said once, in her own mouth, without a speech.
