# Requests to the location designer — from the story editor

Built by reading every `where:` line in `story/outline/act1-5.md` against the 227 keys in
`story/locations.md`. The file covers the great majority of them; what follows is only the gaps.
Grouped by how much they cost you and ordered by chapter.

Everything you decided in `story/notes/location-designer.md` section 1 is **accepted as canon**,
including the dressed-stone Halls with one below-shoulder light source, Thurn's fireless stone lamp
columns, the concentric Choir chamber against a raw seam face, ornament-free skywell rings, the broken
cradle, the brass yoke at two scales, Vess shuttered openings, Kell low flat-roofed stone, the shared
care-house dormitory image, blank painted company boards, Tellwater's drowned low ground, Tier Five's
glazed roof, Ostry Bar's spaced standing figures, and Emberrow's Kestrin stone. Scene `003` keeps its
own eighteen-word corridor line; every other Halls scene uses `halls_upper_corridor`. Your underground
time-of-day convention is confirmed. **Please also add the per-chapter index** you offered in your
section 4.7 — fifteen writers will look places up by chapter, not by region.

## 1. One canon correction

**The Ninth Door is the ninth door-mouth of Ninefold Terrace** (`story/canon.md` section 2) — Hesk's
own cliff, the one the party counts along in chapter 5. The Works' maintenance rail reaches it from
behind and below; the door itself opens on the terrace face, which is why people walk out into grey
daylight at `1595_the_doors_stay_open`. So `ninefold_cliff_wide`, `ninefold_door_sealed`,
`ninth_approach_gallery` and `ninth_inner_doors` are all the same cliff seen from two sides, and the
Ninth Door section should say so. **One key missing: the Ninth Door from outside**, standing open at
first light, with a line of people coming up out of it — `1595`, the last panel of chapter 15.

## 2. Missing keys, by chapter

### Chapter 1 — Sallowgate
- **the guild hall's upper bunk room** — `0115_the_last_hour`. Twelve bunks, eleven empty, one lamp, a
  window with the cold coming through it. The most important room in chapter 1 and the file has
  `even_hand_bunkroom` for the hunters' quarters; this is the same room played as a deathbed.
- **the fair-pole / lumber store** — see chapter 3 and chapter 11 below; first referenced here only as
  a type.

### Chapter 2 — Windrow and above it
- **a boarding-house room, three beds, a window that will not shut** — `0215_three_beds`. Recurs as a
  talk backdrop.
- **a chop-house with a long table** — `0230`, `0235`. Windrow's only warm interior that is not a
  laundry.
- **the Tellwater relay hut, two miles out** — *you proposed this and were right.* A single plank hut
  and a telegraph pole on an empty road. It is canon in the bible's timeline (22:40, the
  acknowledgment fails, a rider goes to Windrow) and it pays off enormously in `1575`/`1610` when the
  22:02 log is produced. **Please write it.**

### Chapter 3 — Tellwater
- **a lit kitchen window on a night square** — `0330_the_window`. A panel scene; one window, one lamp,
  an empty square.
- **the ridge road above Tellwater** — `0365`, `0370`, `0375`. Where the Pale stop them and the plate
  is confiscated. `tellwater_road_out` is the north road; this is the climb east.
- **the fair-ground pole store** — `0320` (optional). Also chapter 11's loft; one key would serve both.
- **the Fold** — *you flagged this as the one place with a strong reason to exist that you did not
  write.* Canon now names it: a shepherd's fold and drift camp in the high ground above Tellwater,
  lower case in dialogue ("the drifts above the fold"). **No scene is set there.** It is permitted as
  one establishing backdrop in chapter 3 or chapter 11, so that `0985_the_recitation` has a picture
  attached to it. One key, one panel, no interior.

### Chapter 4 — Braid
- **the annex index room**, in the drowned lower stacks — `0470`, `0475`. Dry, above the water line,
  and the one room in the flooded dungeon where you can read something.
- **a chant-hall back room** — `0487_domma_sath`. Low, mats, one stove, a line seated round one old
  woman. Recurs as the Act V chant backdrop and as `1335`/`1340`.

### Chapter 5 — Ninefold Terrace and the Sealed Stair
- **empty armour niches on the stair** — `0585_the_oath`. Three empty niches and one set of fallen
  segments. `sealed_stair_landing` may already cover it; if not, it needs to, because the scene is
  built on the emptiness.
- **the gate chamber and the hand-laid fill beyond it** — `0595`. The bottom of the dungeon: the stair
  does not go through, and forty feet of stacked fill is the image that closes the chapter.

### Chapter 6 — Saltmouth

- **the Semmet keeper's quarters** — `0640` (optional). `semmet_stump` is the exterior.
- **the relief tent and the yard of trestle tables** — `0686`, `0688`, `0691`. Where the act ends, and
  the only time the player sees the Works doing something that looks like care.
- **the Kettle cistern house from inside** — `0607_bad_water_at_kettle` (new scene). `kettle_cistern_house`
  exists; confirm it shows the inflow and standing water, because the job is in there.

### Chapters 7-8 — Vantage and Emberrow

- **an Emberrow tenement stair and landing** — `0845` (optional).

### Chapters 9-10 — the Flats and Windrow
