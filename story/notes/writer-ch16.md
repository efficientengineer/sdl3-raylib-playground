## Departures from the outline, and why

- **`1615`'s "eleven months later" is arithmetically impossible** and I did not use it. Kerrow took
  the chair on the 13th-16th of Dust 1107 (`canon.md` section 4); the epilogue runs to the 9th of
  Fallow 1108; and the outline sets the scene in "the following Rime", which is Rime **1107**, two
  months after she took the chair. I have used **eleven weeks**, which lands exactly in Rime 1107,
  keeps the game's eleven motif, and lets her be removed by the same clause and the same count she
  used on Crewe — which is a better line than the interval was. **Orchestrator: "Where everyone ends
  up" still says eleven months; one of the two wants changing.**
- **`1605`'s last line does not say "two days before the fourteenth anniversary."** The fourteenth
  anniversary of the 9th of Fallow 1093 is the 9th of Fallow **1107**, which is inside chapter 15;
  the epilogue opens on the 12th. The inquiry's first sitting is therefore the **16th of Fallow
  1107**, and the line reads *"Nothing in the Basin marked the ninth"* — same gesture, working
  calendar.
- **`1610` is 3+3+2 pages and swaps one shot.** Kerrow's `profile_flat` and Sark's `portrait_inset`
  would have put two squares side by side on page 2 (R4). Sark is now a `full_body_reveal`,
  deliberately the same shot the chapter gave Chairman Crewe on page 1 — the clerk and the chairman
  framed identically, which is the whole point of Emmet Sark. Ilsa Tremmel's eleven-year notebook is
  in panel 3 as a closed book beside Crewe's list; she is not named in a panel and so is not on the
  `characters:` line.
- **`1630` is 7 panels, not 6.** The outline's page 2 was `establishing_wide` / `object_insert` /
  `portrait_inset` — two squares (R4). Arro gets a `full_body_reveal` standing at the foot of a
  tower that is finally running with his hands empty, and the scene still ends on Ket's portrait at
  two lights. Bron and Pip are named in the street panel so the party is in their own epilogue.
- **`1655` mirrors `0125`'s shots on purpose.** Panel 1 is the same `over_shoulder` on the same
  board in the same burned-edged frame; panel 3 is the same `establishing_tall` of the ledger wall,
  which now has Lyra's barred line in it. Page 2 is `object_insert` / `two_shot` / `establishing_tall`
  for R3-R4; the outline's `portrait_inset` of Bron reading is gone because panel 1 already has him
  at the board.
- **`1655` cuts "Bron: Who posted it?" from the bible's sample exchange** so the scene lands on nine
  boxes as the outline requires. Tovin volunteers the poster and the seal unasked, which is the
  cleaner payoff of `0125` (where there was no poster and no seal and she said so).
- **`1610` is 10 lines** as outlined; Sefa's striking-off and Teach's acquittal share one box.
- **Nobody states the theme.** `1645` originally had Zeph say "that is what the checking is for".
  It is cut. The one-word correction does the work.

## Lines I am unsure of

- **`1640`, "Alive. Grown. Working."** A practical woman would ask about her son, and Bron cannot
  answer without either lying or telling her. This is the smallest true answer he has, and it is
  also the moment closest to breaking the outline's "they do not recognise each other". Cuttable in
  one box if the owner wants her never to ask.
- **`1620`, Cadder's joke.** It is supposed to be bad. It may be too bad. Only Dov reacts, as
  instructed, and nobody rescues it.
- **`1650`** deliberately does **not** have Bron tell Maren that the winter he keeps is hers. I
  considered it and rejected it: it is her own childhood, so it is not "the night" and not banned,
  but she would have to say *did I* and mean nothing by it, and the outline forbids her almost
  remembering. If the owner wants the knife, it is two boxes.
- **`1610`** has no verdict, no gavel and no vindication, per the outline. It may read as flat on a
  first pass. It is meant to.
