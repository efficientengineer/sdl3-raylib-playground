### The relief formula, complete — `1440_the_standing_muster`

**Printed to the player here for the first time, in two boxes, read aloud off the Registry
transcript by Sefa.** `1380` prints nothing, per `canon.md` section 2.

> **Sefa: The next watch is at the post. The word is carried. The count is kept.**
> **Sefa: The post is relieved. The watch is spoken for. Stand aside and count them by.**

- **Front half** (box 1) is new and is the part `0585` says nobody living has.
- **Back half** (box 2) is Hesk's inherited line corrected in exactly **two words**. Her version
  (`0585`, `0590`, verbatim) is *"The post is relieved. The way is spoken for. Stand aside and let
  them by."* The two errors are **way → watch** and **let them by → count them by**. Named as such
  on the page at `1445`: *"Watch, not way. And the end of it is count them by. Not let them by."*
- **It is a handover, not a password**: the relieving watch speaks, the standing watch stands down.
  Because it is a handover, a *reading* does nothing — Sefa reads it low to the party at the near
  edge of the landing and the Muster does not move. It has to be **sung at them**, which is Zeph at
  `1445`. That is the only reason `1440` can print it without ending the boss.
- **Never printed in full again.** `1445` prints only the back half, twice, because Hesk is having
  her own line corrected; Zeph's singing is described, not printed.
- **The formula is exactly thirty words**, fifteen each side, and `1445` says so on the page
  ("Thirty words and a tune"). See the conflict flagged for the orchestrator below.
- "The count is kept" / "count them by" deliberately rhymes with the doorwards' formula at `0565`
  ("we count it four times a year") and with Hesk starting a register at `1405`. **No character
  notices and no scene may point at it.** It is also not to be rhymed with the Tally or with
  "nine hundred tallies" (`canon.md` section 6).

## Departures from the outline, and why

- **Panel layouts.** Nine of the outline's page layouts break R3 (two neighbouring panels of the same
  scale) or R4 (a page of three with only two distinct shapes — usually two `object_insert`/
  `portrait_inset` squares side by side). Every scene keeps the outline's shots and its stated last
  image; only the page splits and one or two shot swaps changed:
  - `1405` is 3+4 (was 3+4 with two squares adjacent); `over_shoulder` moves to page 1.
  - `1415` is 3+3+2. The ceiling `establishing_tall` moves up to panel 3; the page ends on the
    `high_angle_down` of the street, which is the outline's stated last image.
  - `1450` drops the outline's `high_angle_down` and its final notebook insert. The notebook is in
    the `eyes_slit` (her eyes down on a page below frame) because **R9 rejects a panel that asks for
    a written numeral** and the number is spoken instead. An `establishing_tall` of the twenty-six
    relieved guards standing at ease was added, which the outline's note requires to be visible.
  - `1455` ends on the `object_insert` of Hesk's hand flat on the wall (the outline's last image);
    Bron's `profile_flat` became an `eyes_slit` for shape variety.
  - `1475` swaps the outline's second `object_insert` for a `high_angle_down` of the two dropped
    hammers, and ends on Ket's hand on the sheared casting.
  - `1490` is 3+2+3. The lamp insert carries the six hours; the outline's "Bron awake much later"
    `portrait_inset` is cut as redundant with the `high_angle_down`.
  - `1495` is 4+2 and ends on the `establishing_wide` of the street with nobody facing the archway.
- **`1440` is 8 lines, not 7,** because the formula needs two boxes of its own and Sefa's "at last a
  door that wants a document" is what earns her producing it.
- **`1440` prints the formula as a reading, not a delivery.** Hard rule 14 says the guards stand
  aside for anyone who says it correctly, so if Sefa said it *at them* the boss would end in the
  panel scene. She reads it off the page to the party at the near edge of the landing, flat, and
  nothing happens; Zeph's first sung word is what turns thirty helms. Zeph says so on the page: *"It
  is not a paper. It is a part."*
- **`1420` gives Sefa two lines.** The outline's beat requires Zeph to correct Sefa's "a few hundred"
  twice but leaves her off the speaker list. Correcting his mother twice without looking at her is
  worth more than moving the line to Pip.
- **`1425` is 16 lines, `1435` 15, `1470` 16, `1490` 10** — one or two over the outline in each case,
  all inside the scene, none of it padding. `1490` needs ten because an eight-panel scene needs eight
  reveal points.
- **`1490` prints question 1 only.** The canon table says Ket gives him 1-14 off the manual; printing
  fourteen questions in a nine-line scene would be the whole scene, and ch.15's `1525` owns the
  recitation. The fourteen are the six hours; question 1 is the last line, which is also Ket asking
  a man sitting in a broken cradle under a dead city where he is.
- **`1465` has no backdrop.** A talk scene's backdrop must name a panel in a panel scene, and chapter
  14 has no panel scene at Sallowgate. It is optional, so it plays on the hall's existing art.
