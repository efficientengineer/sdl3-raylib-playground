### The Sealed Stair inscription — `0575_the_sealed_stair`

The band is **shallow cut marks in fifteen ruled rows**, cut at hand height beside the stair and
also over the face of all nine door-mouths. Two of the fifteen questions are printed for the player
and nobody reacts. They are spoken by **`Narrator`**, not by a character, so that no one in the
party can be said to have read them aloud — and Zeph never speaks a question's words:

> Narrator [2]: What happened the day before this one?
> Narrator [5] {dread}: Is there anything you would rather nobody knew?

Verbatim questions 7 and 13 of `canon.md` section 1. Question 7 is printed over the insert of the
band; question 13 is printed over the insert of **Lyra's ungloved hand travelling along the marks**.
Lyra has no line in `0575` at all and nobody looks at her.

Zeph's two complaints, which carry the blank fifteenth:

> Zeph [2]: It isn't a warning. It's a list. Numbered, ruled, and cut by one hand.
> Zeph [4]: Fifteen entries. Fifteen is cut, and then there is nothing after it. The numbering is wrong.
> Zeph [6]: I was promised a warning and I have been given a form.

Hesk, who watches the stair and not the wall:

> Hesk [3]: Same band as the doors. All nine carry it. It has never had to mean anything.

**`0580_the_words_on_the_wall`** holds the line. Zeph names 13 and 14 by number only and never
quotes them; Lyra declines and does not lie:

> Zeph: And two of them are the same question. Not similar. The same, asked twice, one under the other.
> Lyra: Which two?
> Zeph: The thirteenth and the fourteenth. To a reader they are one question written out twice.
> Lyra: To a reader. Yes.
> Zeph: Have you ever seen a working procedure repeat itself?
> Lyra: I have.
> Zeph: ...And?
> Lyra: And I have.

"To a reader. Yes." is the whole of her admission. Between here and ch.9 she says nothing else.

### The hall-guards' oath — terms — `0585_the_oath`

Hesk recites the terms in two boxes. This is the wording ch.13 and ch.14 should quote:

> Hesk: Bar the way. Give warning. Do not pursue. Do not strike first.
> Hesk: And stand aside for a voice that knows the words.

Her explanation, which is the ch.5 rung of Ladder C and contains no word from the banned list:

> Hesk: The corps said one oath. Four times a day, for years, in a place that keeps what's said often enough.
> Hesk: The men are a thousand years gone. The oath is still being said. The only thing left to say it in is the armour.
> Hesk: It isn't interesting. It's a promise that outlived the men who made it. Those are two different things.

### Hesk's one mangled line of the relief formula — `0585`, `0590`

> **Hesk: The post is relieved. The way is spoken for. Stand aside and let them by.**
> Hesk: That's the back half of something. Nobody living has the front. It unbars a side passage and nothing more.

### The retelling of Bron's winter story — `0545_the_cold_walk`

Per DECISIONS D9, the one permitted retelling. Two boxes, shorter than `0130` and weighted on the
cold rather than the boredom, which is exactly the detail that is wrong:

> Bron: I got lost above Tellwater at nine. Two nights out, in the drifts above the fold.
> Bron: Second night I was shaking too hard to hold a stone. Shook the whole way down after she found me.

It keeps `0130`'s facts (nine, two nights, the drifts above the fold, shaking, a stone, hands that
found him, carried down) and adds nothing. It drops the boredom, the sheep scrape, the shouting and
the boots, so ch.9's `0985` can still land "you weren't cold, you were bored" against `0130`.
Nobody remarks on the wrongness: Hesk's answer is a full panel of silence and then "Eat your food."
Pip's closing line is an offer of food, not a comment.

## Departures from the outline, and why

- **`0520` is set in `ninefold_council_room`, not an open yard.** `locations.md` has no key for a
  council yard with a stone table, and the brief forbids inventing a description for a place with a
  key. The council room is better anyway: nine stone benches, a fire pit, and **nine iron door-keys
  hung in a row on the back wall** behind the man buying one of the doors. Spitting on his boot
  indoors is also worse. The Caleth stump on the skyline moves to `0545`, where it is the location.
- **`0520` page 2 is `eyes_slit` / `portrait_inset` / `establishing_wide`**, not the outline's
  `low_angle_menace` / `portrait_inset` / `establishing_wide`, which broke R3 (two close panels
  adjacent) and R4 (two shapes). Panel 3's `full_body_reveal` already gives Hesk from below.
- **`0545` is 10 lines, not 9, and its page 1 opens on `establishing_tall` of the Caleth stump.** A
  panel only shows when a dialogue line reveals it, so a 7-panel scene needs 7 reveal points. Same
  reason as chapter 1's `0190`. Page 2 is `two_shot` / `portrait_inset` / `eyes_slit` /
  `high_angle_down`; the outline's `profile_flat` of Hesk was dropped because it sat next to
  `portrait_inset` (two close panels) — `eyes_slit` carries "the camera stays on Hesk not saying
  anything for a full panel".
- **`0575` is 7 panels and 8 lines, not 6 and 7.** The outline's page 2 (`high_angle_down`,
  `object_insert`, `profile_flat`) has only two distinct shapes and breaks R4. Added
  `establishing_tall` of the band running down out of the lamplight as panel 7 — which is the
  outline's stated last image anyway — and the extra line is Hesk's "Wall side."
- **`0590` is 7 panels, not 6**, for R4 on both pages, and it ends on Hesk's profile rather than the
  guard. The outline's last image (the guard still standing) is already panel 2's `full_body_reveal`
  and the gameplay note covers the walk-back; Hesk's face is what the scene is about.
- **`0530` gives Hesk one line** ("I'll walk it with them"), because the outline's beat for `0530`
  requires it while the note on `0520` says her first spoken line is `0535`. I read the `0520` note
  as meaning her first *scene* is `0535`, and kept `0530`'s line to five words so `0535` still plays
  as her introduction. **Orchestrator: flag if you want it moved to the council speaker.**
- **`0570` argues from the Sixth Door lease only**, never from the company mortar, per canon section
  2 — the scene plays identically whether or not the player took "Count the doors".
- **`0525` is 15 lines** as the outline asks, with Cadder's whistle delivered through Zeph's
  reaction ("He is whistling. Four bars, and two of them are wrong"), since a talk scene has no
  picture to play it in.
