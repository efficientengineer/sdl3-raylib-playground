# Requests to the cast designer — from the story editor

Built by grepping every `who:` line in `story/outline/act1-5.md` against the 34 entries in
`story/characters.md`. Two lists: **A** is people the outlines put on screen in a **panel** scene with
no entry (they need a `look` and a reference sheet, or an explicit decision not to spend one); **B** is
named speakers in **talk** scenes with no entry (they need a portrait only); **C** is existing entries
that now contradict the outlines or `story/canon.md`.

Names, ages and peoples are settled in `story/canon.md` section 6 and are not up for renegotiation —
only the designs are. Handles follow your own rule: one word, not an English word, whole-word safe.

## A. On screen in a panel scene, no entry

### 1. `Ovey` — Recovery Chief Ovey
- **People / age:** human, unspecified (read as 40s-50s).
- **Role:** Clement Works recovery service. **The boss of chapter 8.**
- **Appears:** `0885_the_frame_on_the_handcart` (panel, ch.8), and as the fight that follows it.
- **What the outlines say he looks like:** steps down off a handcart with a clipboard under one arm;
  shot `full_body_reveal`, head to boots, low angle. Travels with **two frame-bearers** — Pale
  troopers, uniformed, bored, careful with the equipment, carrying a brass yoke in a case under
  canvas. Brisk, professional, genuinely believes the frame is a kindness.
- **The one instruction:** **he must not look like a villain.** He is the fourth reasonable person in a
  row to offer the party a reasonable thing. Clement Works senior cut, company black, no cuff band.
- **Priority: high.** He is a named bible character with a panel scene and no design at all.

### 5. `Nine` — a woman of Thurn

- **Priority: high.** Chapter 14's entire emotional load is Ollo talking to her, and she is the first

## C. Existing entries that now contradict the outlines or canon

1. **`Anneke` is Kell, not human.** DECISIONS D8.3. Her entry says `people: human` and gives her brown
   skin and brown eyes. Canon: **Kell, four foot eleven — the same height as her son, and neither of
   them knows — clay-warm skin, iron-grey hair that will not lie flat, amber eyes, bare arms in a hot
   room because Kell run hot, and she complains about the steam constantly.** Keep the faded rose-pink
   accent, the laundry apron, the flat iron and the chipped cup. **This is the one rewrite that
   matters**: she is in a panel scene (`1085`) and an epilogue scene (`1640`), and her height beside
   Bron's is the whole shot.
2. **`Sem` is at four of eight lights, not six.** `story/canon.md` section 3. She keeps a *dead* well;
   Ket at five must be the brightest of the four Onn at the start of the game. Change "six of the eight
   still lit" to **"two of the four lit on each temple"**. The panels that show her temple
   (`1145` panel 3, `1560` panel 2) have been corrected to match.
3. **`Tovin`'s status line** reads "at two lights and going down", which reads as two of eight. Her
   `look` is right ("only two lit on each side"). Make the status line say **two a side, four of
   eight**, so nobody writes her as dimmer than Ket.
4. **`Ket`'s `look` is drawn at five lights and she is at five for only six chapters.** The sheet is
   correct for chapters 4-8; chapters 9-11 are four, 12 is three, and 13-16 are two. Either add the
   variant to her notes or accept that the panel notes carry it — but a writer will otherwise ask the
   art tool for a five-light Ket in chapter 15. Flagging, not prescribing.
5. **`Zeph` and `Pip`** — the bible's open question 4 asks for the Vess rewrite and the care-house
   cosmetic pass. Your notes say both are done; this is only a marker that nothing else changed them.
6. **Nothing else contradicts.** Bron's and Lyra's `look` and `ref` lines are untouched, the accent
   colour map holds against every scene pairing in the outlines, and the handles used in Act V's
   `who:` lines match your headings exactly.
