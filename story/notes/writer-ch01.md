## Canon wording established in chapter 1

Later writers: copy these character for character.

### The Tally — the fifteen questions — `0115_the_last_hour`

Heard in full, once, in order, across ten dialogue boxes. Lyra asks; Merrit Tack answers three
times and stops. The boxes are grouped as follows and **the grouping is not canon — only the
wording and the order are**:

1. "Do you know where you are? Do you know who is holding your hand?" (Q1, Q2)
2. "What is your name? What name did your mother use?" (Q3, Q4)
3. "What did you eat last? What was the weather? What happened the day before this one?" (Q5-Q7)
4. "What did you do that you would do again? Who is owed something? Who owes you?" (Q8-Q10)
5. "What is unfinished? What should be told, and who to? Is there anything you would rather nobody
   knew?" (Q11-Q13)
6. "Is there anything you want said out loud now, while there are people?" (Q14)
7. "What do you want done?" (Q15)

Q15 is its own box and is the last line of the scene, said to a man who has stopped breathing.
Bron is outside the door and hears nothing.

### The guild's Four Lines, burned into the board frame — `0125_the_burned_seal`

The frame wording, from bible section 7, is four short phrases, one to a side, older than the paint:

1. **Pay on completion.**
2. **No patron.**
3. **The seal or no bill.**
4. **Bring them back.**

They are visible in `0125` panel 1 ("four short rows of scorched marks burned into it"). Each is
spoken once in the chapter, as a fact and never as a lecture:

- Line 1 — `0110_the_ledger_wall`, Tovin: "Pay on completion. You completed. Hold out your hand."
- Line 2 — `0195_under_the_seal`, Tovin: "No patron. That is the second Line. Nobody is paying you to be curious."
- Line 3 — `0125_the_burned_seal`, Tovin: "The seal or no bill. That is the third Line and it is burned into that frame."
- Line 4 — `0175_we_tell_the_others`, Bron: "Bring them back. Four out, four back. We go round."

The unwritten fifth Line is Tibb's joke in `0125`: "That's not a favour, that's a price."

### The contract — `p01_prologue`, `0125_the_burned_seal`

The bill's wording is **"Clear the entrance. Ask no questions."** at **triple rate**, with the
poster's seal burned out of it. Stated in the prologue as two sentences on their own line, and
carried by Tovin's `0125` exchange, which is the bible's, verbatim:

> Tovin: Triple rate. Burned seal. No questions.
> Bron: What's the catch.
> Tovin: I just said it three times.

### Emmet Sark's first words — `0180_the_tent`

> Emmet: Entry chit?
> Pip: A what?
> Emmet: Entry chit. Issued at the head of the path. You will have been given one.
> Pip: There was nobody at the head of the path.
> Emmet: No. That's the seven o'clock party.
> Emmet: Then you'll have to wait outside. There's a bench.
> Pip: There isn't a bench.
> Emmet: No. It was requisitioned.

### Bron's winter story — `0130_the_board_eats_at_six`

Told once, warmly, because Pip complained about the cold floor. Four boxes, verbatim:

> Bron: You want cold. I got lost above Tellwater when I was nine. Two nights out.
> Bron: First night I wasn't frightened. I was bored. There's nothing in the drifts above the fold. I counted stones.
> Bron: Then it went dark and I was shaking, and I sat in a sheep scrape and shouted at a rock till my voice went.
> Bron: She found me. Hands under my arms. Carried me the whole way down and told me off the entire way.
> Bron: Two nights lost, and what I remember is her saying I'd ruined my boots.

Contains: **bored before frightened**; **shivering** (the ch.5 plant); **the drifts above the
fold**; **hands and a voice, never a face**; **carried home**. Nobody reacts to it, and nobody
comments after. Chapter 9's `0985` should quote "you weren't cold, you were bored" against it.

## Departures from the outline, and why

- **`0115_the_last_hour` is 7 panels, not 6.** The outline's six broke R4 on both pages (three
  panels of two shapes each). Added `establishing_tall` of the bunk-room window as panel 6, which
  also gives the silence after question 13 somewhere to sit. Page 1's third panel became
  `eyes_slit` for the same reason; `profile_flat` moved out and `portrait_inset` keeps the last
  image the outline asked for.
- **`0125_the_burned_seal` is 7 panels, not 6**, for R4 again. Added `establishing_tall` of the
  ledger wall, which is where Tovin says the third Line.
- **`0180_the_tent` uses `full_body_reveal` for Emmet Sark.** The shot's own use is
  "introductions, villains, monsters". Pointing it at a duty clerk with a clipboard is the joke
  the chapter turns on.
- **`0190_the_walk_out` has 4 lines, not 2-3.** A panel is only revealed by a dialogue line, so a
  four-panel scene needs four lines or a panel never shows. Two of the four are "..." and
  "Morning." Silence is still effectively the version we get.
- **`0160_the_broken_post` page 2** is `eyes_slit` / `low_angle_menace` / `impact` rather than the
  outline's `portrait_inset` / `low_angle_menace` / `impact`, because two neighbouring close-scale
  panels break R3.
- **`0175_we_tell_the_others` carries the fourth Line.** The outline did not place it and the plant
  table requires it in ch.1. Bron says it as his reason for going round, which is also the
  structural plant for ch.5 and ch.14.
