## Canon wording established in chapter 4

Later writers: copy these character for character.

### The edges test — `0465_the_edges_test`

The demonstration is on a **stranger** (a Vess rail hand, Oren Ander), it works, and nobody points
it at Bron. The canon exchange, using the bible's section 14 example:

> Zeph: Good. And the day before that one?
> Rail hand: The day before. ...Nothing. I have nothing at all. Isn't that funny.
> Pip: That's it? That's the whole trick?
> Zeph: That's the whole trick. Nobody builds the day before. Why would you.
> Zeph: The archivists called it the edges. Coin.

The term in dialogue is **"the edges"**; the scene and the prose name is **the edges test**
(canon section 6). Nobody in chapter 4 ever says the Tally's question 7 wording, and no character
notices a resemblance — Zeph's phrasing is "the day before that one", never "what happened the day
before this one".

Also in `0465`, the world fact behind it: the rail hand's afternoon was a **year-end issue** —
*"They give us one each instead of the rise. All different. Mine was the fair."* (All different
because nothing is ever copied, hard rule 3.) He is pleasant, unbothered, knows where it came from,
and walks off whistling. Lyra's closer: *"What's your name?"* / *"Oren Ander. Why?"*

### The Vess second tone — `0420_cassa_tessen`

Planted as a party trick, in two boxes, by Cassa Tessen, after she catches one of Pip's small lies
for entirely ordinary reasons. Nobody draws a conclusion; Zeph says nothing all scene.

> Cassa: All guesswork, that. With one of mine there is no guessing. There is a second tone under this voice, a fifth below.
> Cassa: It goes flat when I lie and I cannot stop it. Yours has nothing under it at all. Human witnesses are exhausting.

Chapters 8 and 15: the operative facts are **a fifth below**, **it goes flat when they lie**, **they
cannot stop it**, and **humans have no second tone at all**, so a human cannot be checked this way.

Chant-lines, same scene (the ch.15 plant): *"Seventeen of us hold the same eleven hours between us.
Every evening we say them back. Nothing on paper."* Cassa's bond-pair is **Ilven**, seventy, first
chanter. Cassa acknowledges before she answers throughout ("Rail." / "Zeph.").

### Ket-Ossun's introduction — `0445`, `0450`, `0460`, `0480`, `0484`, `0488`, `0491`, `0492`

**Lights.** Stated once, flat and unprompted, at `0450_five_lights`:

> Ket: I am at five lights. Three are out. You should plan around it.

Five lit, three dark, for the rest of Act II. The `0445` panel line states "five of the eight lit";
`0492` states no count. Nothing lights back up.

**Her register.** "Correct" / "not correct"; exact unrounded figures; announces her own state; funny
by accident and leaning into it; pauses written as a short beat, never an ellipsis. Lines later
chapters may want:

- `0450`: *"The Works advertises the Basin's only working skywell. That is Ossun. It is not correct."*
  / *"I say so whenever I hear it said. Nothing has ever come of saying so. I say it anyway."*
- `0450`, on Pip's thousand-years joke: *"One thousand two hundred and sixty-one."* / *"You may have
  the months as well. There are eight of them and I know which."* / *"I know. It was a good one. The
  number is also true. Both things fit."*
- `0488_wick`, the Basin running down: **year 310**, six inches a year for eight hundred years, and
  **four feet a year for the last forty** — *"It is not a steady figure. It has never been steady."*
  Asked whether it will stop: *"Not correct."*
- `0480`: her confirmation of the pattern store is one word — *"Correct."* — and *"I have asked to
  read that eleven times."*

**Her terms for joining** — `0484_what_we_have`, stated like a contract, no warmth, no fate:

> Ket: I will sign four hunters onto the Meddra maintenance manifest. As my hands.
> Ket: They will take it, or they will refuse it in writing. To refuse it they must write down that Meddra runs.
> Bron: What are you going to want off us later?
> Ket: Thurn. The door-mouth, and the specification kept behind it. When a door opens I go through it first and I take it.
> Bron: Fine.

She does not mention the cradle. "Thurn" is spoken once, as a place-name.

**Tovin and Ket** — `0460_forty_nine_years`, four lines and a break:

> Ket: Forty-nine years.
> Tovin: Forty-eight. You are counting the year I was still on the hill.
> Ket: Forty-eight.
> Tovin: You are at five. Do not spend one on this.

Then Ket, to Bron, and Bron does not answer: *"How much do the four of you weigh?"* / *"Not now.
Have it by the sixth. I will want it in pounds."* The quarrel is never named.

**Friction with the four.** Pip: the age joke, lost. Zeph: asks the reader's question and is handed a
correction that has been filed a hundred times. Bron: asks what she needs and gets weights, which he
finds restful. Lyra: the only one who tries to smooth it and is told the joke was good.

## Departures from the outline, and why

- **`0440`/`0455`: the chapter-one bill was already paid in chapter 1.** Written scene
  `0195_under_the_seal` has Tovin paying eleven marks and two less the tenth — ten marks and one —
  on the spot. The act-2 outline was drafted before that and has Tovin walk to Braid to pay it. Kept
  the beat, changed the money: **the poster never paid the hall.** Mir's "cleared but unpaid" is
  about the poster, which is why she finds it worse than the Pale; and Tovin walks three days to
  hand back **the hall's own tenth (one mark and one)**, because a hall that pays a hunter out of its
  own box and keeps a cut has become that hunter's patron, and that is the second Line. It costs the
  hall the winter money `0125` established. **Orchestrator: confirm this substitution** — it is the
  only reading I found that keeps ch.1, `0440`, `0455` and the Four Lines all true.
- **`0491_night_on_the_salt` does not retell the winter story** (DECISIONS D9.1). Bron answers
  Lyra's one-question-too-many — *"What's the first thing you remember?"* — with a different warm,
  well-told, edgeless childhood morning (a stool, a stove, ice on the window, counting to eleven).
  The winter is not referred to at all; the glancing reference the assignment allows is not spent,
  so ch.5's `0545` has it clean. Ket's throwaway is keyed to that morning instead: Bron places it at
  about five years old (**1089**) and Ket says 1089 was the softest winter in sixty years, four
  above at Tellwater, nothing froze. Nobody reacts. If a later writer needs 1089 for something else,
  change both figures together.
- **`0445`: Ket does not announce her lights here.** Canon section 3 says the count is stated once,
  at `0450`. The `0445` panels show it; she says it at `0450`.
- **Panel counts raised for R4** (a page of three panels needs three different panel shapes):
  `0415` is 7 panels (4+3), not 6 — added `full_body_reveal` for Cassa's arrival, which is her
  introduction and the only shot in the scene that is tall. `0425`, `0445`, `0470` and `0492` are 7;
  `0470` and `0492` gained one panel each, and in `0425` the slate board moved from panel 2 to the
  last panel so the outline's closing image survives.
- **`0481` panel 3 is `eyes_slit`, not `portrait_inset`.** Four panels of one wide and three squares
  break R4. Lyra's eyes tracking the card and going back to the start is the same beat, colder.
- **`0482` panel 6 is `full_body_reveal`, not `over_shoulder`,** for the same reason. The shot's own
  prompt says "single standing figure"; the panel puts Zeph half behind Bron's shoulder anyway,
  because Bron stepping in front of him is the image the scene is for.
- **`0487` ends on Pip, not on the sleeve.** Three square panels in a row break R4 and R3, so the
  hands closing round the grey thread became panel 2 and Pip's profile closes it. The scene is
  purpose-tagged "character (Pip)", so this reads as a gain rather than a loss, but the outline's
  last image is available again if the panel count goes to five.
- **`0410`: Zeph's wrong distance is his second correction, not his first,** so the concession has
  something to land on. He never says he is from Braid.

## Lines I am unsure of

- `0465`, Zeph: "The archivists called it the edges." It is the only time the test is named out loud
  in the chapter, and it may be one word too tidy. Cutting it costs nothing; the trick still lands.
- `0455`, Tovin's filthy line ("Your bunks are over your stove. In my hall they have the decency to
  be quiet about it") is milder than `0110`'s. Mir's reaction is written in the beat only, so the
  owner can pitch it either way without touching the dialogue.
- `0481`, the file reference "R, ten ninety-three, four". 1093 is the year of the night, which a
  sharp player may notice a chapter or two before it means anything. It is deniable, but say the
  word and I would make it a neutral number.
- `0491`, Ket's two throwaway figures. They are meant to be catchable only on a second run. If they
  read as a nudge, cut the second line and leave "Ten eighty-nine was the softest winter in sixty
  years" hanging on its own.
