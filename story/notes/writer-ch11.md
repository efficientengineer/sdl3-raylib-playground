## Canon wording established in chapter 11

Later writers: copy these character for character.

### The exchange — `1150_the_fifteenth_question`

Said at dusk at the fair ground, at the end of an argument she loses on the merits, with the
trestles going up around them. Three boxes, in this order, nothing between them:

> Lyra: Fifteenth question. Ask me the fifteenth.
> Bron: I don't know it.
> Lyra: You will.

She is then called away mid-thought to hold a trestle up (Ollo: "Sister! This end, before it goes
over!" / Lyra: "Coming."). **It is not a goodbye and must never be replayed as one.**

The rest of `1150` establishes, in her driest register, the ch.9-11 rung of ladder H:

> Lyra: They're a check. Somebody wrote them so a thing would survive being moved.
> Lyra: I'm extremely pleased about it. Two hundred hours, and I'd had it the wrong way round the whole time.

Bron recites **11, 12, 13, 14** from habit in that scene and stops. That is the whole plant.

### The Tally in the road — `1175_the_hour`

**Asked in the road: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, and 15.**
**Never asked: 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14.** There was no time, she told him to take the last one,
and nobody in the party notices or mentions it afterwards. Chapters 12-15 may rely on this: Bron has
now *asked* eight of the fifteen and been taught all fifteen.

Order on the page (grouped exactly as `0115` groups them; the wording and order are canon, the
grouping is not):

1. Lyra, on herself, out of habit: "Do you know where you are. ...The north road out of Tellwater.
   Do you know who is holding your hand." — and she cannot go further.
2. "Bron. Take the hour." / "I don't know the fifteenth." / "What do you want done. That's the
   fifteenth. Now start at the top."
3. Bron: Q1-2 → "The road out. You, and Pip."
4. Bron: Q3-4 → "Lyra. She used the whole of it. Every time." (her mother's name is never given)
5. Bron: Q5-7 → "Bread. Too much of it. It was warm and there was no wind at all." / "The fair.
   ...Take the last one now."
6. Bron: Q15 → **her answer, and the last thing she says:**

> Lyra: Somebody write the time down. They'll ask for it at the house.

"The house" is the Order of the Late Hour's house at Sallowgate. Nothing else is bequeathed, asked
for, or explained. The gold sun medallion passes in an object insert with no line on it (panel 5).

### What Bron carries, and how he describes it

**He does not describe it, in this chapter or at the end of it.** The only trace of the hour in his
mouth is administrative, at `1185`, when Hesk is taking down times and Zeph cannot get the number
out:

> Bron: Nine forty. It goes in the letter. That was asked for.

That is the whole of it: a time, given in the passive, because it was asked for. Ch.12's `1225`
("I learned it off someone") and ch.15 own anything larger. Per `1185`, the hour of the shot is
**09:40**, on the 9th of Dust.

### The chapter's clock

Nothing on screen contradicts `canon.md`. The narration opens on the **6th of Dust**; the party
arrives on the **7th** (`1105`); the house-by-house work runs the 7th and 8th; the fair is the night
of the **8th** (`1155`-`1160`); the road is the morning of the **9th** (`1165`-`1175`); `1180`'s two
days are the 10th and 11th; the postings close on the **11th** and `1195` is that dusk. Only the
2nd, the 11th and the 12th are ever said out loud.

## Departures from the outline, and why

- **`1145` panel 7** is the flooded hollow east of town at first light, not "the sheep pasture, flat,
  filled in". `locations.md` has the pasture drowned on purpose in 1093 (`tellwater_low_ground`) and
  ch.3 plays there; an unflooded pasture would contradict both.
- **`1175` has 15 dialogue lines, not 10.** Eight of the fifteen questions and their answers do not
  fit in ten boxes at 120 characters, and grouping them any tighter than `0115` does would make Bron
  sound like he was hurrying her. Nothing was added: there is no line in the scene that is not a
  question, an answer, or a silence.
- **`1150` is 18 lines and `1160` 18, against 14 and 16.** Both carry frozen wording (the Tally
  quiz; Ollo's joining). I cut every line that was not doing work and stopped there.
- **`1170` has 8 lines, not 7.** A panel only appears when a line reveals it, and panels 5 and 6 are
  after the shot, when nobody speaks. They are revealed by "..." from Bron and from Pip.
- **`1105` last panel** keeps the outline's `eyes_slit`; Pip took the "they're queueing" line so all
  four of the outline's speakers speak in eight boxes.
- **`1140`** ends the Jory Tass fight with Hesk beating him with his own contract (trestles are
  plant, plant is inventory, inventory is the clerk's) rather than with money. The brawl still
  happens; he is not killed, he is sent to argue with Ondra Pell, who has tea.
- **`1195`'s `characters:` line lists only Bron and Ollo**, the two named in panels. Zeph, Hesk and
  Tibb speak but are not drawn individually, and listing them only produced R6 warnings.

- Wen Arden is never spoken to after the shot, never named by a party member, never redeemed and
  never punished. Ollo's apron goes round his shoulders in panel 7 of `1175` with no line attached.
- No device, frame, strand, glass, light or transfer in `1175`. The only object is the medallion and
  it is an insert with no dialogue.
- `1195` does not show the medallion and nobody says where Bron keeps it.
- Ollo does not take the badge off, and nobody remarks on the badge after `1120`.
- Nobody says Sela's name in `1130`, and nobody says Lyra's after `1175`.

## Lines I am unsure of

- **Lyra's answer to question 15** — "Somebody write the time down. They'll ask for it at the house."
  It is small, practical, and not about the party, as the outline requires, and `1185` pays it off
  immediately. The risk is that it reads as too flat for a last line; the alternative I rejected was
  an instruction about somebody else's deathbed, which promises a scene chapters 12-15 would have to
  pay.
- **`1175`, Lyra: "The fair. ...Take the last one now."** She is triaging her own Tally, which is
  exactly what two hundred hours would teach a person; but it is also the one place in the scene
  where she manages the moment, and a reader could hear it as her making it bigger. Cut the second
  sentence and Bron simply jumps to fifteen; the scene survives either way.
- **`1160`, Lyra's last joke: "You'd already packed."** It is meant to be so dry that a first-time
  player misses it. It may be so dry that everybody misses it.
