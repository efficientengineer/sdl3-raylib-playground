## 1. Her voice — page vs should-be

- **She is an audience, not a second teacher.** On the page she still translates people to each
  other (Falke to Hart, Hart to Falke, Distel to the player). She came for the show. She rates it,
  she doesn't referee it.
- **She never explains a thing she isn't holding.** She'll read a wound — nine holes, healed shut —
  because that's hers. She would not brief anybody on the Mohn, the machines, or what Hart means.
- **Warm, never soft.** Two lines on the page go sincere ("I eat here every night", "since you could
  walk"). She says the warm thing by being there, by having a bag packed, by taking Distel. The
  sentimental version is the one she'd swallow.
- **Her jokes land on the number, not on the wording.** "It hit you once." is her. "It isn't the
  same hill." is a writer. Anything you could put on a poster, she wouldn't say — that's the joke
  of "You'd have it framed."
- **Fewest words in the room.** Falke blurts, Hart grunts, the clerk recites; she is loose, short
  and unhurried. Two facts in one of her lines is one too many.

## 4. The moment that is hers alone

**The second coat, at the top of 0180, before "We're coming."** Pure action, no line, no new scene.

```
🖼 first light on the hillside — Ottilie pulling a second coat out of her bag and putting it round
   Distel's shoulders from behind; Distel still looking up the hill; Falke watching the sheep
Ottilie (neutral):
Distel: I'm going up to find out what was done to him. I don't need either of you.
Ottilie (resolve): We're coming. Walk in the middle, you're asleep on your feet.
```

Why it is hers: `halm.ottilie_house` is a **mandatory** examine — one chair at the table, two coats on
the hook — and nobody comments on it, then or ever. This pays it off without a word. She packed the
second coat before she knew where they were going, she puts it on a stray she met four hours ago, and
"walk in the middle" is the same instruction twice, once with her hands. No one remarks on it.

**Alternate, if the coat is too much for the art budget:** at supper, on Hart's "You've eaten alone
enough" — she does not answer it. She gets up and lays the third place herself, then takes it. One
textless `Ottilie (smile):` box over the action. Right now she answers that line with a joke about a
spoon, which is a deflection written for her; letting her move instead is the same deflection played.

**Most alive — don't touch:**
1. `0150-12/13` — "I had a very good thing ready to say and now I'm not saying it." / "No. You'd have it framed."
2. `0180-22` — "We're coming. Walk in the middle, you're asleep on your feet."

**Worst three:**
1. `0160-18` — "I've heard of your people all my life. No one here has ever seen one." Encyclopedia
   entry, delivered over the head of the fourteen-year-old it is about, in a register she never uses.
   → **"No one in Halm has ever seen one of you."** (said to Distel, one fact, 40 chars).
2. `0140-10` — "He means it, Hart. He'll be out there in the dark hitting a post." She narrates the
   next scene so the player won't miss it, and it edges on coaching-by-proxy. → **cut to
   `Ottilie (laugh):`** — a textless box. Falke's own line already says he's going.
3. `0140-8` — "He won't tell you. He's never told me either, and I eat here every night." Two facts,
   and the second is the one thing she would never state. → **"He's never told me either."**

Runner-up, flagged rather than ranked: `0180-20`'s "Nine of them, all the same size, healed shut years
ago." The nine is the chapter's hook and the healer is the right person to read it — but three
observations in one box is the narrator talking, and `SMELLS.md` warns off integers doing the work of
observation. **"Nine. Healed shut years ago."** keeps the count and gives the picture its job back.
