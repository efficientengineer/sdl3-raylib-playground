### The recitation — `0985_the_recitation`

Maren's first-person telling. Built from `0130`'s exact facts (nine, two nights, the drifts above
the fold, bored before frightened, counting stones, the sheep scrape, shouting at a rock until the
voice went) but **deliberately not word for word**, because she is remembering and he was issued.
The wording the rest of the game should quote back:

> Maren: I was nine. Two nights out in the drifts above the fold. The first night I wasn't cold. I was bored.
> Maren: I counted stones. There was a dead bird in a drift on the way up, and its wings were open.
> Maren: Then it went dark, and I sat in a sheep scrape and shouted at a rock until my voice went.
> Bron: Anyone could know that.
> **Maren: Nobody knows that. You never told it. It's too small to tell.**

Bron's version says "I wasn't *frightened*"; hers says "I wasn't *cold*", which is the Kell plant
(`0545`) firing without anyone pointing at it. Hesk's one line is four words: **"Kell don't feel
cold."**

Maren's day before: *my father put a dog down and I wouldn't speak to him at supper.* Her day after:
*I was sent back up with grease for a shepherd's boot, and I cried the whole way.* She offers Bron
either one and he has neither. Her forward line, which chapter 10 destroys, is: **"If there's more
of you, it's there."**

## Departures from the outline, and why

- **`0985` is 15 dialogue lines, not 10, and 8 panels in 3 pages of 3/3/2.** Ten boxes cannot hold
  the recitation (3), the "nobody knows that" exchange (2), the day before and the day after (3),
  Hesk's four words, and both apologies plus Windrow. Every
  line is short and one thought. Bron still speaks four times and never about how he feels; Hesk has
  one line; Lyra has none. The outline's `over_shoulder` and `portrait_inset` of Maren were dropped
  and replaced by a `two_shot` of Pip and Hesk watching Bron and not Maren, which the outline also
  asked for; `low_angle_menace` of Bron from Maren's seated eye level does the confrontation
  geometry and `high_angle_down` closes it, so the shot count did not grow.
- **`0985` is set at the fold above Tellwater, not above Ostry Bar.** The outline says Maren got
  lost "above Ostry Bar"; `canon.md` section 6 says the Fold is above Tellwater and that "Bron got
  lost there at nine; so did Maren, **which is the same sentence**." Canon beats the outline, and
  the identical place is what makes her use of *the drifts above the fold* land a beat before he
  does. **Orchestrator: confirm.** If Ostry Bar is wanted instead, only the phrase in her fourth box
  changes, but the recognition gets weaker.
- **`0985` does not use the optional "I don't have the carrying".** `outline-act3.md` item 2 marks
  it optional and warns not to build on it; the bible's own sample exchange for this scene ends
  "Then someone carried you", so she has the carrying. Hard rule 6 stays untouched.
- **`0910` is 7 panels, not 6.** The outline's page 1 (`establishing_wide`, `impact`,
  `object_insert`) has only two shapes and breaks R4, and its page 2 put two medium-scale panels
  side by side (R3). Panel 1 became `establishing_tall`, the gate shot moved up to page 1, and a
  `portrait_inset` of Pip driving was added so page 2 has three shapes.
- **`0960` is 5 panels in 2 pages of 3/2**, not the outline's 3/2 with two squares on page 1. The
  outline's `portrait_inset` of Lyra with the cup is folded into panel 1's acting line; `eyes_slit`
  carries her. The medallion insert and Ket's question are page 2.
- **`0970` is 2 pages of 2/3**, not 3/2: the outline's page 1 was `establishing_wide` +
  `two_shot` + `portrait_inset`, which is two shapes.
- **`0975` ends on Ket's face rather than the lamps on the floor**, because the scene is about her
  two hundred feet of silence, and because the outline's page 2 broke R4 as written.
- **`0995` is 6 panels in 3 pages of 2/2/2**, and the final `establishing_wide` carries both of the
  outline's last two images at once (the mile of rings, the small grey figure walking away, the dust
  trail). `establishing_tall` forbids characters, so the walking figure could not go there.
- **`0925` does not use the optional Kettle callback.** The outline permits Nessa to mention the man
  who lived in her cistern house if the ch.6 bill was cleared, but also requires the scene to play
  identically for a player who never took it. I left it out rather than write a branch; add it as a
  separate optional line if the systems writer wants the flag.
- **Dur-Sarn is never named.** He has no `characters.md` entry, so per the brief the standing Onn at
  `0930` are drawn with the "Standing Onn of the muster" extra and nobody approaches them.
