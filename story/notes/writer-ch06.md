### The payroll page's line — `0692_the_payroll`

Chapters 7 (`0780`) and 14 (`1470`) quote this. It is printed **once**, in one box, spoken by Bron,
who takes the page out of Zeph's hands and finishes the line Zeph would not finish:

> **Bron [5] {sorrow}: Ardo, N. — role: Hesta Sallow, widow, one son — posting closed 1097.**

Verbatim from `canon.md` section 6 / the act-2 outline. The year is written **1097 in digits**,
because he is reading a page; every other year in my chapter is spoken out in words ("ten sixty-one",
"eleven oh-three"), which makes this one line sound like a document and not like speech.

Zeph's abandoned half, one box earlier, is the only other place any of it appears:

> Zeph [3]: Ardo, N. — role: Hesta —
> Zeph [3]: ...

The rest of the page, as the party reads it (ch.7 and ch.11 may reuse any of this):

> Zeph: Rim district. Tellwater station. Sixty names, and every one of them has a posting number against it.
> Zeph: Marrick, O. — role: baker. Three-year posting. Renewed, renewed, renewed.
> Zeph: The well-keeper has no number. She is the only line on this page who is not an employee.

**Sixty names, sixty posting numbers, one entry with no number (Sem-Dree), and a role note against
twelve of them** — that twelve is mine and is invented; it exists so that "role note" is a category
on the page and not a thing that happens only to Bron. Change it freely; nothing depends on it.

Hesk's closing line, which deliberately is **not** "word off a map":

> Hesk: Fold it and put it inside your coat. It'll say the same thing in an hour.

### The closing of "Bad water at Kettle" — `0607_bad_water_at_kettle`

The bill posted at `0240` is cleared here and nowhere else. Nessa Tull's fixed lines:

> Nessa: You'll be off the board, then. Three days I walked to put that up, and the tram fare on top of it.
> Nessa: **Four marks. Stand still while I count it. I'm not counting it twice for you.**
> Bron: Take two back. It was an hour.
> Nessa: **Pay on completion. That's your line, not mine, and I'll not be the one who broke it. Four.**
> Nessa: Four marks out of my box, for an hour. The meter has the eleven of us at nine marks a month, every month.
> Nessa: For water that fell out of the sky onto my own roof and went down my own channel into my own tank.
> Nessa: **Here. Did the hall at Sallowgate ever get my second letter?**

**The fee is four marks and the meter is nine marks a month across eleven residents.** That monthly
figure is mine and is invented; it does not contradict `0240`'s fourpence the gallon, and the last
line deliberately repeats `0240`'s "the water fell out of the sky onto my roof" word for word, which
is the one thing about the scene she has not changed in four chapters. Ch.9's `0925` revisit should
assume nothing except that she is still there and still angry about the rate. Nobody answers the
question about the second letter and nobody may answer it later either.

## The one place I departed from the outline on content

**`0607`: the fouling.** The outline says "a dead salt-crawler wedged in the inflow", but a
salt-crawler is a freight vehicle (`locations.md`, VEHICLES), so it cannot be wedged in a water
channel. The bible (section 7, side contract 1 — higher authority than the outline) says the fouling
is **a man who has slept in the cistern house for nine years because it is the only building he
recognises, and the fouling is him washing.** I have written the version that keeps both and breaks
neither:

- the physical work is the blocked inflow and eleven residents' worth of standing water, an hour of
  filth, exactly as the outline stages it;
- **the man is there and does not speak.** Nessa mentions him the way she would mention a leaning
  post, Pip asks where he is from, and Nessa says she asked twice, he does not know, it does not
  bother him, so she stopped asking. Nobody in the party looks any further and nobody runs the edges
  test on him.

That keeps the outline's four speakers (Nessa, Bron, Pip, Hesk) and its twelve lines, keeps Nessa
free of plot, and still lights up what the bible wants lit: *the resettled are everywhere and nobody
looks.* **Orchestrator: if you want the bible's version fully on screen, he needs three or four lines
and a fifth speaker; if you want the outline's version exactly, cut the two Nessa lines about him and
the scene still works.**
