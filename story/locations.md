# LOCATIONS — *The Fair Copy*

The visual bible of the Sallow Basin. One canonical, drawable description per place, so the world
looks the same in every scene whoever writes it.

## How to use this file

1. Find the place. Find the sub-location key that matches your shot.
2. Paste the sentence after `- location:` in your scene file, **verbatim**, without the key.
   `- location: sandstone terraces stacked in four bands up a dry hillside, ...`
3. Paste or adapt the place's **Staging default** into your `- staging:` line so screen direction
   stays the same across every scene set there.
4. If your scene needs a room that is not here, write the closest key and add a note in
   `story/notes/location-designer.md` rather than inventing a second description of the same place.

**Rules every sentence here already obeys, and yours must too.** Only what is visible: architecture,
materials, light source and direction, two or three fixed props, time of day. No style or rendering
words. No readable words, painted names, or legible papers. Nothing that needs deep perspective — the
style is flat layered backdrops, so places are described as stacked bands, faces, and rows.

**The material language of this world.** Sandstone, salt crust, cut stone with square tooling marks,
brass valve gear, glass thread in paper sleeves, canvas, clipboards, iron stove pipe, rope, tallow
lamps, laid rail. Never: castles, taverns, thatched cottages, banners, portcullises, stained glass.

**The company's presence, in order of how bad things have got:** a brass meter box on a wall → a
canvas tent with a folding table → grey-coated staff with clipboards → a painted company board with
no words on it → mortar in an old doorway → a bore head.

# REGION 1 — THE RIM HILLS

Sandstone terraces two and three hundred feet high, cut stone underneath, cold dry air, hard light.
Everything is stacked: terraces, roofs, fields, doors. Base palette is buff and ochre against a sky
that goes green before it goes dark.

## Sallowgate (ch. 1, 2, 11, 14, 15)

The Basin's frontier town and the game's home. Poor, steep, older than the company and aware of being
bought. Warm stone, cold air, water on every roof and a meter on every tap.

- location (sallowgate_wide): sandstone terraces stacked in four bands up a dry hillside, flat roofs each carrying a round stone cistern, steep stepped streets between them, low morning sun striking from the left, cold clear sky
- location (sallowgate_wide_dusk): the same stacked sandstone terraces at dusk, roof cisterns black against a cold green sky, lamps lit in a few windows, brass meter boxes bolted beside each cistern tap, smoke going straight up
- location (sallowgate_stepped_street): a narrow stepped street cut between sandstone housefronts, shuttered windows, a handcart chocked on the steps, a company meter box on a wall bracket, hard midday sun down the right-hand wall
- location (even_hand_hall_exterior): a squat converted water-house of pale sandstone on the second terrace, a wide arched door propped open, an iron stove pipe through the roof, a chalked board beside the door, late afternoon sun from the right
- location (even_hand_hall_main): the guild hall ground floor, bare sandstone walls, a black iron stove burning at the left, a pinned job board on the right, behind it a floor-to-ceiling wall of ruled columns of small handwriting, lamplit evening
- location (even_hand_board): close on the guild job board, a burned-edged timber frame, pinned paper bills curling at the corners, a chalk stub on a string, a broken wax seal, stove light from the left in a dim room
- location (even_hand_ledger_wall): a sandstone wall covered floor to ceiling in narrow ruled columns of small handwriting, some lines struck through with a single ruled bar, a lamp on a bracket lighting it from the right, night
- location (even_hand_bunkroom): a low upstairs room under bare roof beams, twelve narrow iron bunks in two rows, packs and boots stowed beneath them, one small square window at the end, grey dawn light entering from the left
- location (even_hand_bunkroom_night): the same upstairs bunk room at night, twelve iron bunks with eleven stripped bare and one made up, a tallow lamp on a stool at the left, a small square window ajar, cold air
- location (even_hand_kitchen): a cramped back kitchen with a sandstone hearth, a blackened pot on an iron arm, a scrubbed plank table with mismatched stools, bunches of dried beans on hooks, warm stove light from the left, night
- location (sallowgate_cistern_yard): a bare sandstone yard under a raised stone cistern on squat pillars, a brass meter box and padlocked tap at waist height, empty buckets stacked against the wall, flat overcast noon light
- location (sallowgate_room_above_hall): a small plastered attic room, a cot, a survey satchel hung on a nail, a plank shelf of folded maps, a low square window looking down over flat roofs, early morning sun from the left

**Palette.** Sandstone buff, dust ochre, cold slate blue, iron black, stove ember orange, bone white.
**Light.** Outdoors: hard low sun from the left in the morning, from the right after noon; no shadow
anywhere at noon. Indoors: the stove on the left, one small square window high on the back wall.
**Landmarks.** The burned-edged job board. The ledger wall behind it. The hall stove that is never
let out. Roof cisterns with brass meter boxes.
**Staging default.** Facing into the hall: the door is behind the camera on the left, the stove on
the left wall, the board and ledger wall on the right, Tovin-Caleth in front of the board. Outdoors
the hill rises to the right, the Basin drops away to the left.

## Windrow (ch. 2, 10, 12)

A relay town that works for the tram or for the sanatorium and does not ask which. Steam, wet stone,
washing lines, and the dark Vintry well standing over the whole place like a chimney nobody uses.

- location (windrow_wide): a low tram town of grey brick and sandstone in a shallow valley, washing lines strung between roofs, a tall dark ring tower standing above it, steam drifting flat, overcast morning, light from the left
- location (windrow_tram_yard): a gravel yard of laid rail and turnouts, a squat brick engine shed with open doors, a water crane and a coal bunker, cinders underfoot, grey afternoon light from the right, wet ground
- location (windrow_tram_platform): a short brick platform beside laid rail, an iron bench bolted to the boards, a lamp post with an unlit lantern, a stack of crated freight under canvas, cold blue dusk, lamplight from the right
- location (windrow_laundry_row): a row of open laundry doorways in a wet brick wall, steam rolling out at ankle height, copper tubs and mangles just inside, washing lines overhead, late afternoon sun striking down the street from the left
- location (windrow_laundry_doorway): one laundry doorway close, wet brick jamb, steam pouring past a hanging sheet, a copper tub and a scrubbing board inside, a chipped enamel cup on the step, low warm sun from the left, evening
- location (windrow_bench): a public bench of grey planks against a brick wall, a bent lamp post beside it, pigeons on the cobbles, a folded coat left on the seat, flat grey noon light with no shadows
- location (windrow_boarding_room): a narrow upstairs lodging room with three plank beds in a row, a washing line of damp shirts strung across it, a sash window propped open on a broken catch, grey morning light from the left
- location (windrow_boarding_parlour): a boarding-house front parlour of grey brick and plaster, four mismatched armchairs round a small black stove, a plank table with a tin tray of cups, a net-curtained window, flat overcast daylight from the right, midday
- location (windrow_chop_house): a low chop-house room of dark plank walls, one long scrubbed table down the middle crowded with dishes, a coal stove at the back, a steamed window, warm lamplight from a bracket at the left, night
- location (quiet_house_front): a plain three-storey boarding house of grey brick, a scrubbed step, a black-painted door, window boxes of dead herbs, a boot scraper by the jamb, cold morning light from the left, frost on the step
- location (quiet_house_kitchen): a narrow boarding-house kitchen, a black range with a kettle on it, a long scrubbed table with eight chairs, cups hung on hooks, one small window over the sink, warm range light, late night
- location (quiet_house_upper_room): a small boarding-house bedroom upstairs, an iron bedstead against the right wall, a black stove with a full scuttle beside it, a chair with a folded coat, one sash window shuttered, warm stove light from the left, night
- location (windrow_sanatorium_wall): a long blank wall of grey brick topped with iron spikes, a single barred gate with a lamp bracket above it, a gravel road along its foot, cold blue evening light from the right, no windows

**Palette.** Wet grey brick, slate roof blue, steam white, tram-yard rust, soot black, lamp amber.
**Light.** Overcast and flat outdoors most of the time; steam catches what light there is. Indoors, a
range or a stove is the only source. Everything is damp and reflects a little.
**Landmarks.** The dark Vintry ring tower over the rooftops. Washing lines strung across every street.
The sanatorium's blank wall at the top of town.
**Staging default.** The tram line and the yard are on the left; the hill with the dark well and the
sanatorium wall is on the right and above. Characters arriving come from the left.

## The Clement Works sanatorium, Windrow (ch. 10)

Clean, warm, well run, and the worst building in the game. Nothing here looks like a prison, which is
the point. Doctor Marsh is proud of it. Maren lived in it for twelve years behind an unlocked door.

- location (sanatorium_gate): a barred iron gate in a long blank brick wall, a gravel drive beyond it, a gas lamp on a bracket, a painted company board with no words on it, cold morning light from the left, frost
- location (sanatorium_corridor): a long scrubbed corridor of cream-painted plaster, a polished linoleum floor, numbered doors in a row on the right, an iron radiator under a high window on the left, flat daylight from the left, midmorning
- location (sanatorium_ward): a warm ward of eight iron beds in two rows with white covers, a stove at the far end, a jug and basin on each locker, tall windows down one wall, afternoon sun falling across the floor from the right
- location (sanatorium_day_room): a wide warm day room of cream plaster with forty plain chairs in loose rows, a stove with a guard rail, a shelf of identical enamel cups, tall windows down one side, flat daylight from the left, afternoon
- location (sanatorium_file_room): a windowless room of floor-to-ceiling pigeonhole shelving stuffed with paper folders, a rolling ladder on a rail, a bare table with one folder open on it, a hanging lamp overhead, night
- location (sanatorium_holding_stock): a cold storeroom of shallow wooden drawers pulled half open, each tray holding rows of paper sleeves with glass threads inside, a brass tally frame on the wall, one lamp above the table, night
- location (sanatorium_marens_room): a small clean room with a cot, a plain chair, a stack of forty identical notebooks on a shelf, a window with no bars looking at a brick wall, grey daylight from the right, morning
- location (sanatorium_holding_frame): a tiled treatment room built around a brass yoke the size of a doorway on a floor gantry, padded straps hanging loose, a winding drum and a bank of valve wheels, harsh lamplight from above, night

**Palette.** Cream plaster, scrubbed grey linoleum, hospital white, brass yellow, iron black, one cold
green in the tiling.
**Light.** Tall windows with flat daylight from one side by day; hanging lamps directly overhead by
night. No torch or fire anywhere in this building.
**Landmarks.** The pigeonhole file wall. The shallow sleeve drawers. The brass yoke on its gantry.
**Staging default.** The corridor runs left to right across the frame; doors and rooms open off the
right. The file room is always entered from the left. Staff stand between the party and the door.

## The hill relay station and the survey cut (ch. 2)

Two small dungeons on one hillside above Windrow. The relay station is a hundred years older than the
company and the company is using it anyway. The cut above it is raw and still working.

- location (relay_station_exterior): a small square blockhouse of cut stone on a bare hill shoulder, a lightning mast and sagging wire, a sandbagged door, a cracked stone cistern beside it, wind-flattened scrub, hard low sun from the right, morning
- location (relay_station_interior): a single stone room with a plank bench of brass switchgear and dials, coiled wire on hooks, a wax cylinder machine with a brass horn on the table, dust in the air, lamplight from the left, no window
- location (relay_station_loft): a cramped roof space over bare joists, shelves of cylinder cases in rows, a collapsed canvas cot, a hatch open to the room below, one shaft of daylight entering from a broken slate at the right, noon
- location (survey_cut_exterior): a raw stepped cut sliced into a sandstone hillside, spoil heaps and plank walkways, a canvas company tent lashed at the top step, a standing derrick with brass gear, flat dusty afternoon light from above
- location (survey_cut_rig_floor): a plank working floor at the bottom of a sandstone cut, a cutting rig of iron frame and brass valve gear standing over a shaft collar, coiled hose and a barrow, harsh low sun raking in from the left, evening
- location (survey_cut_camp): a company camp on a hill terrace at night, three canvas tents in a row, a folding table with a lamp and a clipboard, a stack of crated cores, lamplight pooling downward, cold sky behind

**Palette.** Sandstone buff, spoil-heap grey, company canvas olive, brass yellow, iron black, cold
sky teal.
**Light.** Open sky, no shelter. By night, one lamp on a folding table doing all the work.
**Landmarks.** The lightning mast and its sagging wire. The brass horn of the cylinder machine. The
cutting rig's valve gear.
**Staging default.** The hill rises to the right; the cut and the shaft are downhill to the left. The
party comes up from the left. The crew chief stands between the party and the rig.

## Tellwater (ch. 3, 11, 15)

Four hundred houses, sixty people, and every window washed. The most frightening place in the first
act because it is beautiful and there is nobody in it. It should read as a model of a town rather
than a town: too tidy, too even, too new a coat of paint on old stone.

- location (tellwater_square_day): a neat sandstone town square with a raised stone well at its centre, swept flagstones, whitewashed housefronts with green shutters, a painted board over the bakery with no words, bright clean midmorning sun from the left
- location (tellwater_square_dusk): the same swept square at dusk, the stone well black against the paving, lamps lit in three windows out of forty, empty benches along the walls, a dark ring tower above the roofs, cold green sky
- location (tellwater_lit_window): the swept square at night, the raised stone well black at its centre, one lit kitchen window across forty dark housefronts throwing lamplight over the frosted flagstones from the right, the dark ring tower above
- location (tellwater_lane): a clean empty lane of identical sandstone cottages with green shutters and scrubbed steps, a hand pump on a stone plinth, a rope washing line with nothing on it, hard afternoon sun from the right
- location (tellwater_bakery): a small warm bakery, a brick oven arch at the back with its iron door open, a flour-dusted plank counter, a long wooden peel leaning by the jamb, firelight from the oven at the left, early morning
- location (tellwater_fair_ground): a flat grassless field at the town edge, four bare timber fair frames standing in a row, trodden circles of ground, a coil of bunting rope on a post, low evening sun from the right, long shadows
- location (tellwater_dree_platform): a stone platform at the foot of a tall dark ring tower, swept clean, a plain broom leaning against the stonework, a stack of stone chips squared off, flat grey daylight from the left, morning
- location (tellwater_road_out): a straight dirt road leaving town between two low sandstone walls, wheel ruts, a leaning stone mile post, dry grass on the verges, flat overcast morning light, the town roofs low behind
- location (tellwater_fair_pole_store): an open-sided timber pole store at a fair ground, bare fair frames stacked in racks along one wall, a plank loft above with coiled bunting rope on it, trodden bare ground outside, hard afternoon sun from the right
- location (tellwater_ridge_road): a stony road climbing a ridge above a sandstone town, dry-stone walls on both sides, a leaning stone mile post, the flooded hollow and low roofs below on the left, hard low sun from the right, late afternoon
- location (tellwater_relay_hut): a single plank hut alone on a dirt road across dry hill country, a telegraph pole with two wires beside it, a padlocked door, a water trough, hard flat afternoon light from overhead, the horizon empty
- location (tellwater_posting_office): a small sandstone office on the square with a plank counter across it, a trestle desk set outside the propped door, a rack of pigeonholes behind, a tally board with chalk marks, flat grey noon light from the left
- location (tellwater_roster_room): a back office of whitewashed stone behind a counter, an iron company strongbox bolted to the floor, shelves of paper folders in ranks, a plain desk with a stamp block, one high window, flat daylight from the right, morning
- location (tellwater_posting_yard): a walled yard of swept flagstones behind a sandstone office, a two-wheeled handcart standing on its shafts, stacked empty crates under a canvas sheet, a water butt at the wall, low evening sun from the right
- location (tellwater_south_row): a row of identical sandstone cottages along a swept lane with green shutters, the last house at the end standing apart with its door open, scrubbed steps, a hand pump, flat grey daylight from the left, morning
- location (tellwater_empty_house): a bare cottage room with a swept plank floor, one wooden chair against a whitewashed wall, an empty hearth with no ash in it, a cupboard door standing open, cold daylight from a window at the left, morning
- location (tellwater_low_ground): a shallow flooded hollow east of town, still brown water over drowned fence lines, the broken roof of a shed above the surface, reeds at the edges, cold flat daylight from the right, no wind
- location (tellwater_decommission_street): a lane of sandstone cottages with doors standing open, handcarts loaded with furniture and crates, canvas sheets over stacked goods, a folding table with a clipboard at the corner, grey noon light

**Palette.** Sandstone buff, whitewash, shutter green, clean paving grey, dark-well slate, cold sky
green.
**Light.** Bright, clean, even, and slightly too generous. Tellwater is the best-lit town in the Rim
Hills and that is the joke.
**Landmarks.** The raised stone well in the square. The green shutters. The dark ring tower over the
roofs.
**Staging default.** The well is at the centre-right of the square; the bakery front is on the left;
the road out runs to the right of frame. The party arrives from the left in every Tellwater scene.
**Note.** `tellwater_relay_hut` is the telegraph hut two miles out on the Windrow road — the one that
lost the acknowledgment on the night of the 9th of Fallow. It is not `relay_station_exterior`, which
is the older blockhouse on the hill above Windrow.

## The drowned bore (ch. 3)

Extraction Bore One, flooded on purpose in 1093 to make a flood true. Everything here is under water
or has been. Sound of dripping, no other sound.

- location (drowned_bore_shed): a half-sunk timber bore shed in standing brown water, its roof broken open, a winch drum rusted solid above the waterline, a plank ramp sliding in, flat grey daylight from the left, morning
- location (drowned_bore_stair): a square cut-stone stair descending into still water, the steps disappearing three treads down, an iron handrail bolted to the wall, a hanging lamp on a hook at head height, darkness below, night underground
- location (drowned_bore_gallery): a flooded cut-stone gallery with water at waist height, brass pipe runs along the right-hand wall, a floating plank raft roped to a ring bolt, one lamp on the raft lighting upward, no daylight
- location (drowned_bore_sump): a round flooded shaft bottom churning slowly, brass valve gear half submerged around the walls, dull grey glass threads turning in the water, a broken gimbal ring canted over, one lamp above at the left
- location (drowned_bore_plate_niche): a close stone alcove above the waterline, a brass maker's plate screwed to the cut stone, its rivets green, a rusted bracket beside it, dripping water, one lamp from the right, no daylight

**Palette.** Drowned brown water, wet cut-stone grey, rust orange, verdigris green, lamp amber, black.
**Light.** One carried lamp only, always below head height so it throws light up the walls. No
daylight past the shed.
**Landmarks.** The rusted winch drum. The brass pipe run along the right wall. Dull grey spent threads
in the water.
**Staging default.** Down is always to the right and forward; the way out is left and up. The lamp is
carried on the party's left so faces are lit from that side.

A shepherd's fold and drift camp in the high ground above the town. Lower case in dialogue. **No
scene is set here** — this is one establishing backdrop so that the ch. 9 recitation has a picture
attached to it. One key, no interior, no people.

- location (fold_camp): a dry-stone sheep fold on high open ground, a low shelter of stone and canvas against its wall, a stone water trough, a coil of rope, wind-flattened grass, hard low sun from the left, early morning

**Landmarks.** The ring of dry-stone wall. The canvas shelter against it. Wind-flattened grass.
**Staging default.** The fold wall runs across the frame; the high ground rises to the right, the

## Ninefold Terrace (ch. 5)

Nine sealed door-mouths in one cliff face, a doorward household directly above each, barley fields
dying in steps down the slope. Proud, poor, and quietly already beaten. This is the most beautiful
place in the Rim Hills and it should be drawn as a single tall face.

- location (ninefold_cliff_wide): a sheer sandstone cliff face with nine squared door-mouths in a row along its foot, a small flat-roofed stone house on the ledge above each one, stepped barley fields below, early sun from the left
- location (ninefold_fields): stepped barley terraces walled in dry stone, half of them bare brown earth, hand-dug water channels and stacked hoes, a low stone hut at the field corner, hard clear morning light from the left
- location (ninefold_door_sealed): a squared door-mouth in sandstone filled with fitted cut-stone blocks, an iron door-key ring set in the centre stone, worn steps in front of it, a stacked cairn to one side, flat noon light
- location (ninefold_door_mortared): the same squared door-mouth with grey company mortar packed into the joints, fresh drill dust on the steps, a brass survey pin driven at the threshold, hard raking light from the right, afternoon
- location (ninefold_fifth_door): a squared sandstone door-mouth standing open on black darkness, its cut-stone fill stacked aside in numbered rows, a mortar tool and a coil of rope on the steps, low evening sun from the left
- location (ninefold_doorward_house): a low stone room with a stone hearth, a long trestle table crowded with bowls, tools hung on every wall, a broad doorway looking out over stepped fields, firelight from the left, evening
- location (ninefold_council_room): a bare stone hall with a ring of nine worn stone benches, a shallow fire pit at the centre, nine iron door-keys hung in a row on the back wall, high slit windows, flat afternoon light from above

**Palette.** Sandstone buff, dead barley brown, dry-stone grey, doorward coat dark green, copper Kell
hair, hearth orange.
**Light.** Hard clear low sun across the cliff face, from the left in the morning. Indoors, a hearth
at the left and a broad doorway of white daylight behind.
**Landmarks.** The row of nine squared door-mouths. The iron door-key ring in each fill stone. Dry
stone field walls stepping down the slope.
**Staging default.** The cliff and the doors are on the right, the fields and the drop on the left.
Anyone arguing for the company stands downhill, on the left.

## The Sealed Stair (ch. 5)

Behind the Fifth Door. Cut stone, dry, and colder than the surface. The stair goes down further than

- location (sealed_stair_head): a squared cut-stone doorway inside the cliff, dry dust on the threshold, a stone stair dropping away beyond it, two empty iron sconces on the jambs, one carried lamp lighting from the left, no daylight
- location (sealed_stair_flight): a straight stair of square-cut stone treads between close dressed walls, a shallow rope groove worn into the right-hand wall, a broken stone lamp shelf at head height, one carried lamp from below, dark
- location (sealed_stair_marks): a dressed stone wall covered in shallow cut marks in fifteen even ruled rows, a rubbed-bare patch at hand height below them, dust on the floor, one lamp held close from the right, no daylight
- location (sealed_stair_landing): a wide cut-stone landing with a barred stone doorway at the back, three empty armour niches in the left wall, fallen iron segments on the floor, a guttering torch in a bracket on the right, no daylight
- location (sealed_stair_gate_chamber): a low cut-stone chamber at the stair foot with a stone gate slab winched half up, iron chain hanging slack from a drum, a stone kerb worn hollow, one carried lamp lighting from the left, no daylight
- location (sealed_stair_fill): a dressed-stone hall packed to the roof with squared blocks laid by hand in courses, a narrow gap at the top left unfilled, dust on the last course, one carried lamp from the right, no daylight

**Palette.** Cut-stone cold grey, dust ochre, torch orange, guard-armour iron, red cloak, black.
**Light.** One carried lamp, or one guttering wall torch on the right. Everything above head height is

**Landmarks.** The fifteen ruled rows of cut marks. The worn rope groove in the right-hand wall. Empty

## Cadder Step (ch. 5, epilogue)

The richest Kell village in the Basin, and the only one where the fields have been let go because
nobody needs them. Bunkhouses, a clinic, a school with glass in the windows, and an open door-mouth
with a drill head standing in it. Everything here is newer than everything around it.

- location (cadder_step_wide): a terrace village of new timber bunkhouses in straight rows on a sandstone shelf, a squared door-mouth standing open at the cliff foot with a derrick over it, abandoned field walls below, clear morning light from the left
- location (cadder_step_street): a graded gravel street between plank bunkhouses with tin roofs, a standpipe with a brass meter box, a stack of new crates under canvas, laid rail curving away at the end, flat noon light
- location (cadder_step_school): a single-room schoolhouse of new plank walls with four glazed windows, a stove pipe through the roof, rows of small benches inside, a plain painted board with no words, afternoon sun through the glass from the right
- location (cadder_step_clinic): a clean plank-walled clinic room, two iron cots with white covers, a glazed cabinet of bottles, a scrubbed table with an enamel basin, a stove at the left, flat daylight from a window at the right, morning
- location (cadder_step_leased_door): a squared sandstone door-mouth propped open with iron shoring, a drill head of brass and iron standing in the gap, spoil heaped to one side, a canvas tent beside it, hard raking sun from the right, evening

**Palette.** New timber yellow, tin-roof grey, gravel white, company canvas olive, brass yellow,
sandstone buff.
**Light.** Open, hard, and unshaded. Indoors, real glass windows, which nowhere else in the hills has.
**Landmarks.** The derrick over the open door-mouth. The glazed schoolhouse windows. Straight rows of
identical bunkhouses.
**Staging default.** The cliff and the open door are on the right; the dead fields fall away left.
Company hardware always occupies the right of frame here.

# REGION 2 — VANTAGE, THE COMPANY CITY (ch. 7, 10, 12)

Forty-one thousand people cut into a quarry face over a hall-mouth, six tiers and three hundred feet
top to bottom. The only warm place in the Basin, and it is metered. Draw it as stacked horizontal
shelves of lit windows in a stone face — never as a receding street.

**The whole city**

- location (vantage_from_below): a three-hundred-foot quarry face cut into six stacked terraces of stone buildings, lit windows in every band, cable lines running up the face, a capped shaft head at the foot, cold blue dusk, warm window light
- location (vantage_face_day): the same six-tier quarry face by day, pale cut stone and iron stair flights zigzagging between tiers, cable towers on each shelf, smoke standing over the top tier, hard morning sun striking the face from the left
- location (vantage_stair_flight): a steep open iron stair bolted to a cut-stone face, a landing with a bench and a lamp post, laundry strung under the flight above, the drop falling away on the left, grey afternoon light from the right
- location (vantage_staff_stair): a deep square stone shaft with a plain iron stair zigzagging down it, a lamp bracket at each landing, a coil of hose on a hook, a handrail bolted to cut stone, lamplight from above, night
- location (vantage_gate_checkpoint): a stone gate arch between tiers with an iron barrier down, a trestle desk with a lamp and a clipboard, a brass tally frame on the wall, a grey-coated constable's post stool, cold early morning light from the left

**Tier One — the Mouth (ch. 7, 13)**

- location (tier_one_mouth): a wide cut-stone shelf at the foot of the quarry face, an ancient squared hall-mouth capped by a huge brass and iron bore head, bunk rows crowded against the cliff, hanging lamps, dust in the air, no daylight reaching down
- location (tier_one_bunk_rows): a long low shed of triple-stacked plank bunks with canvas curtains, a central gangway of duckboards, a coal stove at the far end, a row of tin basins, one hanging lamp overhead, night
- location (tier_one_care_house): a clean plastered dormitory of twenty small beds in two rows, a warm iron stove at the centre, a shelf of identical enamel cups, a barred high window, flat lamplight from above, early evening
